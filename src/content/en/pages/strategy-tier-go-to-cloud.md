###### STRATEGY TIER

# Go to Cloud

The solution **Go to Cloud** We guide companies throughout their cloud migration and adoption journey, from diagnosis and readiness assessment to architecture, governance, security, and FinOps. Our goal is to ensure a secure, scalable, and financially efficient transition, allowing the organization to fully leverage the elasticity and resilience of the cloud.

What challenges does it solve?

- On-premises environments are expensive, rigid, and not very scalable.
- Lack of standardization and governance across multiple clouds.
- Security risks during or after migration
- Manual and unobservable operations
- Uncertainty regarding costs, consumption, and operational optimization.

Benefits for your business

- Cloud environments aligned with global best practices.
- Significant reduction in operational costs and waste.
- Governance and security implemented from day one.
- More efficient operations with FinOps and automation.
- Increased resilience, performance, and speed of delivery.
