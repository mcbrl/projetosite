# Privacy Policy

## **What data do we collect?**

We collect data such as name, email, phone number, CPF/CNPJ (Brazilian tax ID), IP address, location, browsing history, and preferences. This information may be provided directly by you or collected automatically on our websites through cookies and analytics tools.

## **What do we use your data for?**

We use your data to:

- To provide our services;
- I will contact you;
- Send technical and institutional content;
- To improve our products and platforms;
- To comply with legal and safety obligations.

## **Based on what?**

We process your data based on consent, contract execution, legitimate interest, or compliance with legal obligations, as provided for in the LGPD (Law 13.709/2018).

## **Using Cookies**

We use cookies to improve your experience, analyze browsing, and display relevant ads (remarketing). You can configure your browser to block cookies if you wish.

## **Safety**

We have adopted technical and organizational measures to protect your information, such as encryption, access control, and monitoring. Your data is stored in secure environments with restricted access.

## **Sharing**

Your data may be shared with technology partners and suppliers, always under agreements that guarantee confidentiality and security. In the case of international transfers, we comply with the legal requirements of the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law).

## **Your rights**

You have the right to:
– Access your data;
– Correct information;
– Request deletion;
– Revoke consent;
– Knowing who we share your data with.

## Data Protection Officer (DPO)

Person in charge: Fábio Freitas — dpo@vertigo.com.br
Communication channel between Vertigo, data subjects, and the ANPD, pursuant to Article 41 of the LGPD.

## **Updates**

This policy may be updated periodically. We recommend that you consult it whenever you visit our channels.
