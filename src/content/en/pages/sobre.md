###### Mentality

## Vertigo Driven Mindset

The Vertigo Driven mindset is about accepting that all transformation is uncomfortable and that's part of the game.

It's about operating with clarity, intelligence, and speed in highly complex scenarios.

We are not seeking comfort.

We are prepared to evolve continuously, make conscious decisions, and transform challenges into real progress.

Here, attitude matters more than intention. And culture only makes sense if it takes us forward and to the right place.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/www.flaticon.com-11-3.svg)

### Leadership

We act with critical thinking and strategic vision. We question, propose, and take responsibility for creating new paths and strategies.

• Think about the impact generated, not just the delivery of the task or meeting the deadline.

• To question, suggest, and provoke in order to generate value, proposing better solutions and paths forward.

• Anticipate needs: identify opportunities and act before being asked.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/www.flaticon.com-11-4.svg)

### Collaboration

We build together, with respect, exchange, and genuine engagement. Collaboration means combining talents to deliver the best experience for the customer, both internally and externally.

• Act with respect for people, differences, and ideas.

• Sharing knowledge, experiences, and lessons learned to strengthen the whole.

• Seek alignment, build consensus, and make decisions based on what is best for the team.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/www.flaticon.com-11-6.svg)

### Efficiency

Being efficient means knowing how to prioritize, using AI and data purposefully, and transforming intelligence into impact.

• Don't make impulsive decisions: use analysis, technology, and context.

• Eliminate unnecessary complexity and deliver the essentials with excellence.

• Using AI, automation, and digital tools to scale results, not tasks.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/www.flaticon.com-11-5.svg)

### ATHLETIC

Making mistakes is part of the process, and correcting them quickly is too. That's how we evolve. We transform discomfort into fuel to move forward.

• Experiment, prototype, and apply learnings with agility and courage.

• Use Individual Development Plans (IDPs), feedback, performance reviews, and daily conversations as real tools for growth.

• Taking responsibility for one's own development, seeking to learn and putting it into practice continuously.
