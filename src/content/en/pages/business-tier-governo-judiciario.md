- Low transparency and difficulties in making public data available.

- Complete digitization of essential services
- Cost reduction and increased administrative efficiency.
- Unified portals and platforms focused on the citizen.
- Enhanced transparency with standardized public data.
- Modern architecture preparing the institution for the future.
