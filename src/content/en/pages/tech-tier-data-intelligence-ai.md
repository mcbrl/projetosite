# Data, Intelligence & AI

Our solution **Data, Intelligence & AI** We bring together offerings that structure data, create robust pipelines, apply analytical intelligence, and operationalize AI at scale. Through Graph Database, Data Engineering, Machine Learning, Predictive AI, private LLM, and analytics automation, we ensure that companies extract real value from their data with security and governance.

What challenges does it solve?

- Scattered, unstructured, and non-standardized data
- Difficulty in generating reliable and actionable insights.
- AI models that don't reach production.
- Lack of governance and risk of undue data exposure.
- Poor performance in complex queries and in-depth analyses.

Benefits for your business

- Organized, governed, and ready-to-use data
- In-depth insights, including graphs and advanced analytics.
- Operational models that accelerate decision-making.
- Secure AI infrastructure compliant with the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law).
- Reducing operational costs through intelligent automation.
