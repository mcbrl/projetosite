###### Allegations

## Reporting Channel

Vertigo believes that ethics, respect, and transparency are fundamental pillars for building a healthy and trustworthy work environment.

The Whistleblowing Channel was created to ensure that employees, partners, customers, and suppliers have a safe and confidential means to report conduct that may violate ethical principles, internal policies, or applicable laws.

Support can be provided via email [**at comite.integridade@vertigo.com.br**](mailto:comite.integridade@vertigo.com.br) or through the form on this page.

**How does the channel work?**

Through this channel, it is possible to register **complaints, reports, or questions** related to inappropriate behavior, fraud, harassment, conflicts of interest, misuse of company resources, or any practice that goes against Vertigo's Code of Conduct.

The information submitted is treated with absolute confidentiality. All reports are analyzed with **secrecy, impartiality, and independence** by the **Integrity Committee** , which evaluates each case and takes appropriate action according to the severity of the situation.

**Important**

The Whistleblowing Channel **should not be used** for emergency communications, requests for technical support, or administrative matters.

Complaints made in bad faith, containing false or unfounded information, may result in **disciplinary action and legal liability**.

For complaints, suggestions, compliments, requests, and constructive criticism, please use the appropriate page [**by clicking here**](/en/ombudsman/).

Your contribution is essential so that we can continue building a Vertigo that is honest, ethical, and respected.
