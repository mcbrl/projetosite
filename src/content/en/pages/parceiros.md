###### PARTNERS

# Who supports our solutions?

Our technology partnerships strengthen the architecture of the solutions we deliver, ensuring interoperability, security, and scalability in critical environments.

![Vertigo Gitlab Partnership](/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/vertigo-parceiro-gitlab.png)

With GitLab, Vertigo brings AI-powered DevSecOps to engineering processes, with pipelines, code quality, and software delivery all on a single platform.

![Partner\_Badge\_-\_Certified\_PNG\_Chip](/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/Partner_Badge_-_Certified_PNG_Chip-rugkz7eenyfiidhlxzoadpi5p26z8u946ddhqgcg6g.png "Partner_Badge_-_Certified_PNG_Chip")

As a Certified Partner of the Claude Partner Network, Vertigo brings Claude to production in mission-critical environments with certified teams.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/vertigo-parceiro-aws.png)

With AWS, Vertigo provides cloud computing support based on best architectural and operational practices.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/vertigo-parceiro-blip.png)

With Blip, Vertigo helps automate and personalize your customer service with intelligent, AI-powered conversations.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/vertigo-parceiro-digibee.png)

With Digibee, Vertigo supports systems integration through an iPaaS platform that reduces complexity and increases speed.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/vertigo-parceiro-hashcorp.png)

With HashiCorp, Vertigo helps automate and protect its infrastructure in cloud and data center environments.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/vertigo-parceiro-kong.png)

With Kong, Vertigo guides its API and microservice management in hybrid and multicloud environments with a focus on security and performance.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/vertigo-parceiro-liferay.png)

With Liferay, Vertigo creates relevant digital experiences and improves its customer journey.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/vertigo-parceiro-mirantis.png)

With Mirantis, Vertigo helps run applications natively in any environment and accelerates cloud delivery.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/vertigo-parceiro-neo4j.png)

With Neo4j, Vertigo guides the modeling of relationships and the generation of complex insights through the storage and processing of data in graphs.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/vertigo-parceiro-vee-code.png)

With VeeCode, Vertigo helps orchestrate and govern cloud environments with a low-code platform to modernize IT.
