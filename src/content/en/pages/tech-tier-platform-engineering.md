###### TECH TIER

# Platform engineering

Our solution **Platform engineering** We structure internal platforms that enable technology teams to deliver software autonomously, securely, and quickly. Through an Internal Developer Portal (IDP), combining GitOps, CI/CD, microservices, Kubernetes, observability, and SRE, we create standardized environments that reduce complexity and support Fast Flow at enterprise scale.

What challenges does it solve?

- Teams overwhelmed with complex infrastructure.
- Lack of standardization between environments and pipelines.
- Excessive dependence between teams to deliver software.
- Low reliability and frequent production problems.
- Difficulty in scaling microservices and modern architectures.

Benefits for your business

- Team autonomy with a complete IDP (Individual Development Plan).
- Faster, safer, and more observable deployments
- Drastic reduction in delivery time.
- Standardized and governed environments
- Scalability and operational resilience from the ground up.
