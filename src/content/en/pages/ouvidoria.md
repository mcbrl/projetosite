###### Ombudsman Office

## Customer Service Channel

This channel is exclusively for registering and forwarding feedback such as complaints, suggestions, compliments, requests, and constructive criticism, ensuring transparency and efficiency in handling requests.

The Ombudsman acts as a second instance. Before registering your complaint, we recommend that you use the primary service channels of the responsible area. If the issue has not been resolved or requires further analysis, the Ombudsman is available to provide support.

Customer service inquiries can be made via email [**at ouvidoria@vertigo.com.br**](mailto:ouvidoria@vertigo.com.br) or through the form on this page.

**Important**

The Customer Service Channel should not be used for technical support requests. For complaints, please use the appropriate page [**by clicking here**](/en/whistleblower-channel/).

Vertigo's Ombudsman's Office exists to guarantee transparency, respect, and continuous improvement in our processes and relationships.

Your participation is essential for strengthening our organizational culture.
