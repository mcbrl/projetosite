When we talk about a DevSecOps culture and APIs, Zero Trust security fits perfectly within the context, as it is an approach that promotes security throughout the entire application development process.

Zero Trust security is an approach based on the premise that all devices and users must be verified and authenticated before accessing resources; none of them will have free access to information before passing through a security layer.

Companies that care about the privacy of their data embrace the [DevSecOps culture](/en/DevSecCops-in-API-Development%3A-4-Digital-Security-Tips-for-Your-Application/) , placing digital security at the center and as a cornerstone of their projects.

Therefore, all development is shaped by the guidance of the [DevSecOps team of experts](/en/us/) , thereby increasing application security and preserving sensitive data.

Has your company already implemented Zero Trust security alongside a DevSecOps strategy?

Check out this article we've prepared to answer your questions about Zero Trust security and show how it can be incorporated into an efficient DevSecOps strategy.

## Why adopt Zero Trust Security?

If your company is concerned about data security, it should consider implementing tools that promote Zero Trust security.

In times when cybercrimes are frequently committed and cases of company professionals leaking information, intentionally or not, the need for an efficient DevSecOps strategy is highlighted.

Furthermore, implementing Zero Trust practices can reduce costs for companies because, in addition to making development processes more secure, it decreases the risk of operational disruption, which would cause significant problems and high expenses.

It's worth mentioning that for internal control purposes, this practice also serves to provide remote access even to partners, who need assurance that their operations will not be disrupted.

## Zero Trust security for APIs, can I use it?

Yes, API development becomes much more secure through the implementation of Zero Trust security.

DevSecOps serves as a central point for defining the strategies to be used in [API](/en/API-security%3A-check-out-the-top-10-security-flaws-in-OWASP./) development , with Zero Trust as another element that strictly and effectively controls the access that collaborators will have.

An [API](/en/API-security%3A-check-out-the-top-10-security-flaws-in-OWASP./) needs to be integrated with another application to then perform its designated function, which makes the whole process a very careful step-by-step procedure, requiring strict control over permissions.

## DevSecOps culture is the foundation of everything.

Think about your digital security strategy from the start; DevSecOps is the guiding principle that IT leaders need to follow.

Thinking about DevSecOps culture means defining security policies using the collaborative principles of [DevOps](/en/5-programming-languages-%E2%80%8B%E2%80%8Bfor-DevOps/) , which represents considering the entire project lifecycle with greater reliability and delivery with less risk.

In this way, development teams are able to apply good security practices to the construction of applications, providing a solid foundation for customers.

Just like in DevOps, solutions should involve collaboration and integration between teams at all stages, ensuring security and privacy in project execution.

Therefore, think about your security strategy holistically, gather the best DevSecOps professionals, and keep in mind that investing in digital security is saving money.
