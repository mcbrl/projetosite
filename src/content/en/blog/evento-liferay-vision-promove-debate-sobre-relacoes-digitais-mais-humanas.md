Vertigo sponsored the Liferay Vision digital event, promoted by Liferay, between June 15th and 16th, 2021. The event, open to the public, brought together influencers and industry professionals who discussed the theme of more connected and human Digital Experiences.

It was the perfect opportunity for exchanging experiences and networking through virtual booths and individual meetings with companies specializing in digital transformation.

## Vertigo unveiled its new website and portfolio of digital solutions.

During the two-day event, the marketing and sales teams welcomed guests to our exclusive booth. Visitors had the opportunity to explore our new website, download institutional materials, learn about success stories in the public and private sectors, and hold individual meetings with our team.

## Lecture with Andrea Iorio, former director of Tinder, Ricardo Rocha, CEO of Luiza Labs, and guests.

Through interaction with the audience via chat, the guests gave lectures, sharing real-life cases from their experiences and challenges faced during their professional careers.

The first day began with a lecture by actress Denise Fraga, who addressed the theme of human connections in digital times. The actress revealed the challenges faced by theater and performing arts professionals within the digital world, especially in the current pandemic scenario.

Bryan Cheung – CEO of Liferay – gave the presentation “Less technology, more mindset” and Ricardo Rocha – CEO of Luiza Labs – closed the day by reflecting on people at the center of the strategy, sharing his experiences leading Softbox and Luiza Labs and the importance of human relationships throughout the journey.

On the second day of the meeting, Andrea Iorio, former director of Tinder and L'Oréal, opened the event with a reflection on the key skills for digital transformation in organizations. Actor and director Miguel Falabella closed the meeting with the Masterclass “Reinventing the present: develop attitudes for success”.

In total, Liferay Vision 2021 featured over 4 hours of live content, streamed across 3 simultaneous tracks, with more than 15 speakers interacting with event attendees through the exchange of experiences.
