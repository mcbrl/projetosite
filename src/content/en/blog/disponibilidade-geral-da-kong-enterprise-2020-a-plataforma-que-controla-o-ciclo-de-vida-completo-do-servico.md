The general availability of the latest version of Kong Enterprise 2020 has been announced. At last year's Kong Summit, the creators of this solution expressed their vision for the service control platform as a **tool that serves as an intelligent information provider for the enterprise** . Now, it has been announced that they have taken the vision of the service control platform a step further, thus enabling organizations worldwide to manage and optimize the entire service lifecycle.

## A unique solution

Kong presented a single **A solution designed to optimize current application modernization needs.** Through intelligent automation and governance, designed for the entire API and service lifecycle, customers can count on comprehensive service management, from pre- to post-production, across all platforms, protocols, and deployment standards. 
Furthermore, KE 2020 concluded the first vision phase to create an intelligent service control and information flow management platform across the enterprise, aiming for a holistic view to encompass the entire lifecycle of an API or service.
KE 2020 represented the most ambitious and feature-rich corporate launch to date.

## Decentralized architecture

KE 2020 was created to enable organizations to innovate rapidly while maintaining governance. While legacy API management platforms hinder these efforts due to their inability to support decentralized architectures and work across multiple platforms, enabling automation is likely to help organizations...s be Completely modernized, adopting decentralized architectures, CI/CD practices, and automating workflows.

## Freedom to build

KE 2020 expands the commitment to giving developers the freedom to build in the best way possible. In addition, it provides management teams with tools to ensure efficiency and governance. With KE 2020, organizations of all sizes can automate the security, communication, and orchestration of APIs and microservices across platforms, as well as deployment standards and transport protocols.

## How works

### ![](/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Vertigo-Post-Blog-Kong_Capa-copia-2.png)

Keep reading to get a preview of some of the exciting new features in this release. We'll detail how these features can help you go beyond simple API management to intelligently control the entire service lifecycle.

## Kong Studio for designing and testing integrated services.

Built on the recently acquired Insomnia, Kong Studio provides a robust set of tools for optimizing the design and testing of REST and GraphQL services.
Furthermore, Kong Studio simplifies development workflows and eliminates tedious tasks by providing the ability to generate code snippets in multiple languages, automating tests through OpenAPI, integrating them into CI/CD workflows via Git Sync, and enabling response inspection beyond JSON and XML. 

## Multiprotocol support for gRPC, GraphQL, and Kafka.

Kong's multi-protocol support facilitates the adoption of emerging service platforms. This is because it has the ability to unify communications across REST, gRPC, GraphQL, and other services. *streaming* With data, Kong's customers can unlock new service platforms, use cases, and architecture paradigms without the risk of silos.
Furthermore, Kong's native support for gRPC and REST, combined with seamless integrations with Apollo GraphQL and Apache Kafka servers, allows users to effortlessly apply consistent policies across all services to minimize friction and maximize control.

## Visual service map with Kong Brain

Expanding on the features revealed last year, the latest version of Kong Brain introduces a powerful Service Map, which helps organizations understand their entire service architecture through an elegant visual interface. Kong Brain automatically generates and updates the Service Map to provide a real-time view of your entire service architecture. For customers also using Kong Immunity, the Service Map can also expose real-time anomaly alerts, as well as one-click problem investigation within the same interface.

## Enhanced immunity alerts and Kong user interface.

With KE 2020, Kong Immunity was also enhanced, providing a dedicated user interface to investigate anomaly alerts and track resolution status. Furthermore, with intuitive filtering and sorting capabilities, users can analyze immunity alerts in real time to focus their attention on the most critical issues. With Kong Brain, users can also display immunity alerts on the Service Map and easily conduct searches to understand specific endpoints exhibiting anomalous behavior. 

## Enhanced collaboration and integration with Kong teams.

Kong Teams provides enhanced collaboration and security capabilities, as well as integration, to allow KE users to easily scale their deployments across the organization. With Kong Teams, KE users can easily segment users into workgroups, implement policies across entire environments, and quickly onboard new users while ensuring compliance. 
Role-based access control and workspaces allow users to assign administrative privileges and easily grant or limit access privileges to individual consumers, entire teams, partner companies, and environments on the Kong platform. [Learn more about Kong.](/en/kong/)

## Major updates to Kong Manager and Kong Developer Portal

KE 2020 introduces significant improvements to Kong Manager and the Developer Portal to deliver a significantly updated user experience. Both products provide simplified permissions and simpler management workflows, as well as greater flexibility to facilitate maximum utilization. The latest version of the Developer Portal makes customization even easier and reduces integration friction to provide an optimal developer experience, both internally and externally.
