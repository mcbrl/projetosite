Febraban [**Tech 2024**](https://www.febrabantech.com/inscricao) is approaching and promises to be an unmissable event for everyone who wants to transform their business and stay up-to-date on the latest technological innovations in the financial sector. And this year, [**Vertigo Tecnologia**](/en/) and [**Digibee**](https://www.digibee.com/pt/) will be present as official sponsors.

## What to expect from Febraban Tech 2024?

Febraban Tech, one of the largest technology and innovation events for the financial sector in Latin America, will take place on June 25, 26 and 27 at the Transamérica Expo Center in São Paulo.

With a program rich in lectures, workshops, and networking opportunities, the event is an excellent chance for industry professionals to connect with the latest technological trends and solutions.

### Vertigo Technology & Digibee: Transforming Businesses

Vertigo **Tecnologia** and **Digibee** will be present at booth D103, showcasing their innovative solutions that can transform your business. Both companies are recognized for their expertise in technology and innovation, offering solutions that optimize processes and increase operational efficiency.

- **Vertigo Technology**With extensive experience in API integration solutions, Cloud Native, and best practices. [**DevOps**](https://materiais.vertigo.com.br/case-multinacional-seguros) Through digitalization, Vertigo Tecnologia helps companies transform digitally, improving connectivity between systems and processes.
- **Digibee**Specialist in integration platforms as a service ([**iPaas**](/en/5-benefits-of-Digibee-IPAAS/)Digibee facilitates the integration between systems, applications, and data, promoting a more agile and connected operation.

### **Benefits of Visiting the Vertigo Tecnologia and Digibee Booth**

At booth D103, you will have the opportunity to:

- Get a close look at the innovative solutions from Vertigo Tecnologia and Digibee.
- Participate in live and interactive demonstrations.
- Talk to experts and get answers to your questions about how these solutions can be applied to your business.
- Explore success stories and understand how other companies are benefiting from these technologies.
