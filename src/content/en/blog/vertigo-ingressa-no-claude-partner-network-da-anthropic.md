Vertigo is in the process of joining Anthropic's Claude Partner Network, the official partner program of the American company responsible for the Claude artificial intelligence model. Joining the program's Services Track marks another step in the consultancy's expansion into generative AI projects in complex corporate environments, focusing on the financial and insurance sectors.

## A program that requires more than just intention.

The Claude Partner Network does not operate as an accreditation by membership. To join the Services Track, the company needs to demonstrate a certified team, a track record of delivering results to real clients, and at least one public reference that the client themselves agrees to endorse. Anthropic conducts the validation of each criterion directly.

This qualification model was crucial for **[Vertigo](/en/#sobre)** . With over 25 years of experience in technology projects in mission-critical environments and a portfolio of more than 500 delivered projects, the consultancy meets the program's requirements. The application process is underway and reflects the company's positioning in the face of the advancement of generative AI in the Brazilian corporate market.

## What does the partnership mean for customers?

Formalizing the partnership expands Vertigo's access to Anthropic's in-depth technical resources, including specialized support during implementations and priority access to updates to the Claude model. For the consultancy's clients in the financial and insurance sectors, this translates into greater execution capacity in initiatives that require generative AI with governance, security, and integration with existing systems.

Anthropic is expanding its presence in Brazil, and Vertigo positions this partnership as part of a structured strategy: to serve organizations that want to operate with generative AI in production, and not just conduct pilot projects. This type of client requires robust architecture, governance built in from the start, and the ability to integrate with legacy environments—competencies that the consultancy already practices in other technological contexts.

## Real-world implementation in regulated sectors

In sectors such as finance and insurance, bringing generative AI to production involves decisions that go beyond the technology itself. Compliance with the **[General Data Protection Law (LGPD)](https://www.gov.br/esporte/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/lgpd)** , traceability of automated decisions, output control, and integration with legacy APIs are requirements that need to be resolved before operations begin, not adjusted after the first incident.

It is in this context that Vertigo structures its work with generative AI. The partnership with Anthropic reinforces this capability and signals to the market the consultancy's commitment to delivering solutions that support, scale, and operate within the regulatory standards required by the sectors it serves.

### **How to take the next step with generative AI** 

Joining the Claude Partner Network represents the first stage of a long-term commitment to Anthropic technology. Vertigo will continue to develop its generative AI practice based on ongoing projects, focusing on measurable results and sustainable operation for its clients.

Organizations in the financial and insurance sectors evaluating generative AI initiatives can contact Vertigo to understand how to structure this process with governance and true integration from day one.
