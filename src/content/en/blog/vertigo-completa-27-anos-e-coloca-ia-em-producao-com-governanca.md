The technology market is experiencing a paradox. Artificial intelligence has never been discussed so much, yet it has never been so difficult to distinguish those who truly put AI into production from those who merely test the rhetoric. The question leadership should be asking is no longer "shall we use AI?". It's about who is putting this technology into production with governance, security, and accountability for the outcome. This distinction separates companies that embrace AI as a competitive advantage from those that accumulate experiments without return.

## **27 years of applied criteria, now with AI at the center.**

Vertigo reaches its 27th anniversary precisely at this turning point. For almost three decades, the company has applied the same criteria to different waves of technology: first systems integration, then platforms, then cloud. Now, with AI at the heart of its operations, the criteria remain the same: delivery measured in concrete results, not promises.

This benchmark relies on three measurable indicators: certified professionals, deployments running in production, and clients willing to publicly endorse the company. It's the same benchmark Vertigo applies internally before bringing any solution to market. Today, the company reports 100% of its employees are AI certified, more than 45 agents are in production, 4 hours saved, and R$480 in savings over the last six months.

## **A journey in five phases, with a solution for every moment.**

All of this experience is supported by a structured method in five phases: Discover, Empower, Deploy, Sustain, and Intelligence. This method recognizes that each company starts from a different point of maturity and brings together, in each phase, the solutions corresponding to that stage of the journey.

- **Discover:** **[VAIM (Vertigo AI Maturity Model) diagnosis](https://diagnosticoai.vertigo.com.br/)** and an AI program structured on foundation, capability, and direction.
- **Empower:** Masterclasses on Team Topologies and AI Fluency programs prepare teams for the strategic use of AI with a tool-agnostic approach. When a company doesn't yet have a defined solution internally, Vertigo recommends Claude from Anthropic, as Vertigo is registered with the Claude Partner Network.
- **Deploy:** Driven by Claude, implementing AI agents, Vertigo Software Lifecycle, and modernizing legacy systems with AI.
- **Sustain:** AI governance and the FDE as a Service model, which support secure and traceable production operations.
- **Intelligence:** Knowledge intelligence and data and machine learning practices that transform the mature use of AI into a long-term competitive advantage.

There is no single starting point, because AI maturity varies according to each organization's history of investment in data, integration, and digital culture. The first recommended step is the VAIM diagnosis itself, which maps in a few minutes where the company is, what the main gaps are, and where to start safely, before any major investment in technology.

## **27 years of history, a new page under construction.**

Here's to another 27 years!
