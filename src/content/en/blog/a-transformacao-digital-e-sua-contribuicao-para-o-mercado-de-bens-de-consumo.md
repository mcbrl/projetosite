Organizations that have the infrastructure, automation, and agile processes to accelerate their market entry are increasingly ahead of those that haven't yet invested in Digital Transformation.

Delivering services and applications at the speed that the audience demands is absolutely necessary for success. Long, complicated, and restrictive software development and release cycles are no longer acceptable.
To adopt [DevOps](/en/5-necessary-changes-to-adopt-DevOps-in-your-company/) It's mandatory, as business innovation is its main benefit, ahead of improved developer productivity. Now it's about getting the solution right and how to scale it across the company.
It is necessary to find ways to facilitate this transition to technology. Consumers have been keeping pace with market changes, and privacy and trust are indispensable characteristics for managers concerned with the evolution of their business.

In this sense, the implementation of automated platforms, such as ERPs, opens the doors to governance and offers new possibilities for the consumer goods sector.

##
**Optimizing the experience**

The quality and price of a product are no longer the only criteria that transform users into passionate customers of a brand. Consumers expect a... **The buying journey should be seamless, prioritizing the simple and objective resolution of any problems, as well as being secure and reliable.**.
Improve the *Customer Experience* It's about understanding and valuing all stages of a customer's loyalty journey, since a deep understanding of your audience allows you to deliver highly personalized results.
Consumer goods companies need to view this scenario as a great opportunity to redefine processes based on... *insights* extracted by their [digital platform solutions](/en/mobile-intranet-portal-digital-platform/), which aim to build sustainable and increasingly close relationships with the public.

##
**General Law on Data Protection (LGPD)**

Stored data is a central part of the relationship between company and customer, especially in a post-Digital Transformation era. In other words, the channels selected by the consumer must be able to ensure the integrity and security of the information provided.
It is no coincidence that the [General Law of Data Protection](/en/How-can-Devsecops-culture-help-in-the-process-of-adapting-to-the-LGPD-%28Brazilian-General-Data-Protection-Law%29/) It was created to support security and transparency in commercial relations.

**Renovation of existing infrastructure**

Companies that lack confidence in their infrastructure and management resources to effectively serve customers need to start worrying. **agile and open infrastructure** It cannot be put off until later.

##
**Automation as a pillar for success**

Enhanced security, faster application deployment and *downtime* Reduced usage is a top priority in management and automation strategies. Just consider the cost and business implications of a single application failure or security breach if improvements are not made.
**Reduced risks,** ***Customer Experience*** **and enhanced innovation** These are the three main benefits that consumer goods organizations expect from modernizing, managing, and automating their infrastructure.

##
**Importance and evolution of the CIO**

With Digital Transformation, the *IOC* The Chief Information Officer has gained more prominence in the corporate world and is now considered responsible for the revolution in the consumer goods industry.
Its position adds another strategic characteristic, which is to direct high-value-added investments in technology to all types of the company's business. With the constant change in how users consume, it is vital to have a **strategic vision in consumer behavior,** also considering applications within the company.
As a leader in Digital Transformation, the CIO sets goals and objectives to make this movement as efficient as possible and focuses on articulating and connecting the strategic business objectives with the specific objectives of the IT sector, always seeking to offer the best possible digital strategy.

##
**Conclusion**

The consumer goods sector plays an important marketing role in economic terms, filling an unprecedented commercial space in Brazil. The search for methods capable of providing personalized, contextualized, relevant, and simplified shopping experiences is in line with the arrival of Digital Transformation and its operational benefits.
**Certain strategic questions and analyses are important in a company's evolution, especially when it comes to aligning technological development with other areas of the business:**

- Understanding IT objectives and goals and how well they align with the business strategy. Technology is present in all areas of the company, and it's important to have objectives that encompass them.
- Being aware of emerging technologies and how they can positively impact the business, as well as promoting best practices for their adoption;
- Understanding how the company and the IT sector are organized to maximize innovation across all areas;
- Knowing how to measure the return on investment in innovation and technology is crucial. Having monitoring tools and indicators that provide accurate information on the level of Digital Transformation is essential.

In addition to adapting new methodologies for DevOps success, it's also necessary to address cultural changes consistently. Therefore, it's essential to prioritize:

- Modernization of infrastructure;
- Automation and infrastructure management;
- Overcoming ongoing cultural resistance.
