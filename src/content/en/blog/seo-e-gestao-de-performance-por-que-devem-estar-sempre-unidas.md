An online presence is a reality for most companies, and those that are still outside this scenario are already seeking options for their inclusion, usually opting for digital marketing strategies such as social media, sponsored links, and SEO.
Many, however, forget the importance of solutions that optimize the results of these strategies, such as performance management for websites, portals, and e-commerce in general.

> If digital channels are a source of revenue for you, pay close attention to managing your business performance, whether it's web-based or mobile.

## What is performance management?

All websites ranked by Google, Yahoo, Bing, and other search engines are constantly evaluated by bots to determine the site's relevance, which directly influences search ranking.
In this case, most people immediately think of SEO, right? However, website performance is one of the most relevant factors, as well as content and mobile performance. See this indicator within your Google Analytics; it will be under "Behavior," see the image below:

Performance management also influences the user experience, which, if poor due to long website and internal page loading times, may lead users to abandon the site. The question is: Can you identify, with certainty, what is causing your website to become so slow that users abandon their purchase/browsing?
Therefore, performance management encompasses all the monitoring carried out by the company to ensure that the website is not penalized by search engines or becomes uninteresting to users due to long waiting times. In other words, this involves both the IT and Ecommerce teams, as certain actions by the infrastructure or systems personnel can significantly improve the number of logged-in users without harming website performance, page load times, speed of the purchase button, and other factors.

## What is the relationship between performance monitoring and SEO?

When it comes to improving search engine ranking, many companies focus solely on SEO and forget about managing website and internal page performance, losing positions for important keywords without even understanding where this penalty from search engines comes from.
Thanks to these factors, SEO and performance monitoring are two strategies that must go hand in hand for a company to achieve the desired results from its investment in digital marketing strategies.
It is important to know which are the best tools and strategies for developing efficient performance management, providing:

- Better results for the website;
- Optimization of user experience;
- Real-time monitoring of the user experience;
- Real-time identification of bugs within the platform;
- Knowing in real time: who they are, where they're from, what device they're using, and where they are on my website.
- To improve the website's ranking in search engines.

## How to define a performance management strategy?

Performance management should help identify who is visiting the website, whether users or tracking bots, and what experience is being provided on the pages. Some tools allow you to identify the volume of user and bot traffic and even know which search engine has the highest frequency of visits to the site.
The performance monitoring strategy should include:

- Using tools that allow you to identify the information most relevant to the business;
- Creating dashboards by area of ​​interest, meaning that IT and Marketing want to see different information;
- A team committed to transforming shortcomings into opportunities for improvement;
- Frequent monitoring of any changes that may occur in the website's performance;
- Constant innovation to keep up with the changing demands of search engines.

Nowadays, even a few milliseconds can make the difference between an excellent, mediocre, or bad experience.
In this scenario, another factor cannot be ignored: the website's mobile experience! Googlebot-Mobile is there all the time crawling the [mobile website performance](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/o-que-voce-precisa-saber-sobre-estrategia-mobile/) And a poor result in this aspect has a definite influence on the site's ranking.

## Why invest in performance?

Search engines, especially Google, are increasingly focused on providing users with truly positive experiences. A recent test conducted by Google placed a red label saying "Slow" before addresses that appeared in search results but likely weren't aligned with an efficient performance management strategy. This type of "alert" should become more frequent, preventing users from wasting time on bad internet experiences, which ultimately harms the entire business.

## Conclusion

Companies, technology professionals, and digital marketing experts must constantly update their knowledge to prevent low budgets or misinformation from negatively impacting their online business performance.
It is necessary that, just as performance management is essential for SEO results, the [professionals from these two areas are aligned.](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/5-acoes-para-aumentar-a-colaboracao-na-sua-empresa/) and with common goals to identify problems in order to optimize business results.
