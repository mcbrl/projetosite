## What is a chatbot?

Chatbots are APIs that behave like "virtual assistants," ready to interact with the user at any time if so determined.

Imagine you need virtual customer service from a company. Sometimes, depending on the volume of calls, there are no human agents available, and chatbots triage the requests until a human employee can assist you. In some situations, the customer's problem is resolved through API integration, without needing to speak to anyone.

Chatbots allow companies to program automatic questions and answers, simulating communication similar to a human conversation.

Implementing this integration with chatbots on your company website or even on social media allows for a much wider reach in approaching customers. Furthermore, it provides an important tool for *community management* with a personalized service standard, even facilitating lead generation.

## Increase the impact of your sales.

Generating and qualifying leads is not an easy task, but it is essential. Chatbots can be the right-hand of sales and marketing teams, through routines that deliver quality content such as ebooks, whitepapers, etc., in an automated way. Direct communication with the lead is important to improve both sales and customer retention.

Often, leads browse the website looking for specific products, or sometimes they don't even know exactly what they want to buy, but are entertained and looking at the options presented. At this point, the chances of sales increase if your website has a proactive chatbot to make an approach, something very similar to department stores.

Promoting offers, offering products, and answering questions from users visiting your website is perfectly possible through chatbot API integration. With Tag Chat, you can also use the chatbot to recover abandoned shopping carts, notify your customers about upcoming payment deadlines, and send them WhatsApp updates on their order status.

Poor customer service can negatively impact your business or even customer retention. By using a chatbot correctly and configuring it properly, it's possible to resolve requests automatically.

## B2B strategies applicable via chatbots

With the popularization of messaging solutions, technology that once seemed distant from the reality of Brazilian companies has become an indispensable, yet very powerful tool in the competitive market. Thanks to the versatility of chatbots, it's possible to apply various strategies to boost sales. Here are some of them.

### Integrating chatbots with CRM.

Throughout this article, we've already understood that chatbots are synonymous with agility, so what would happen if they were integrated with a CRM? Let's find out.

Imagine that the questions answered by the lead are analyzed by the bot and then allocated within the system. Each answer from the lead will serve as a parameter for qualifying them and thus, automatically distributing them, for example, among specific members of the sales team.

If a lead fills in the information and still has questions about a company product or service and wants to receive more information, the CRM will start sending nurturing emails with relevant information to help close the sale.

This strategy reduces customer service wait times because it allows for automation and guides leads to a faster decision-making process through interaction with the chatbot.

### Marketing actions

The marketing field is one of the biggest users of bot solutions, as they allow the creation of landing pages focused on an offer or product, where the user provides their information so that someone can get in touch.

Many landing pages are created solely through forms, but it's possible to increase user engagement by asking qualifying questions through a chatbot, culminating in a CTA for conversion.

Depending on the customer's responses, the chatbot can direct specific material to them, which facilitates the transition through the sales funnel and allows other areas to act in closing the sale.

### Triage for human care

Depending on the complexity of the product, it's possible to answer 100% of questions through the chatbot, but there are others with greater complexity that require human agents to answer the remaining questions.

In this way, it's possible to create an option to migrate the automated service so that a person can finalize the interaction. Chatbots can and should facilitate the customer journey, and if the customer wants to speak with a person, this option should always be clear.

There are companies that automate chatbots in isolation from other strategies, but the most important thing is that integrations are always done with attention to every detail, with a holistic view of the business model.
