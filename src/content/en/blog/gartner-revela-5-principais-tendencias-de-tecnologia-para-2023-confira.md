Gartner , [a](https://www.gartner.com/en) company specializing in technology consulting, has mapped out the main technology trends for 2023. Organizations that want to get ahead of the competition and stay aligned with the best solutions on the market need to keep up-to-date on the main innovations in the sector.

We can highlight that [cloud computing](/en/Cloud-IT-infrastructure%3A-why-should-I-automate-it/) continues as a strong trend, and through it, several other services also benefit and stand out on the list. ## The importance of key technology trends

When discussing technology trends, it's good to know that companies seek to position themselves in the market and have a competitive edge. Considering this factor, it's important to observe what experts in the field recommend.

[Cloud solutions](/en/AWS-in-Brazil%3A-Cloud-solution-optimizes-infrastructure-and-expands-mobility./) remain a cornerstone of innovation strategies for companies. Migrating your infrastructure to the cloud is a process that requires specialized implementation [partners .](/en/solutions/cloud_native/)

Below are some of the trends indicated by Gartner, and see how they can benefit your company.

## Cloud platforms

According to Gartner, by 2027, more than 50% of companies will have their platforms in the cloud to accelerate their business initiatives.

According to the consultancy, implementing a [cloud infrastructure](/en/Terraform%3A-Automate-your-cloud-infrastructure-in-code./) can enhance a company's business model, as the technology enables the integration of solutions that were previously implemented in isolation.

Adopting cloud infrastructures, such as those from [AWS](/en/Cloud-computing%3A-5-reasons-to-use-AWS-in-your-company./) , provides greater organizational agility, speed of innovation, optimizes results, and strengthens a company's image in the market.

## Integration between Superapps is emerging as part of technology trends.

According to Gartner, it is estimated that by 2027, more than 50% of the global population will be actively using superapps.

Superapps are fully customizable applications that aggregate various types of services, which can include processing, financial transactions, or even a complete online platform that can be used in commerce.

Furthermore, superapps possess the capability for independent communication, enabling direct interaction with their customers.

## Adaptive AI Applications

Following the trend of adopting cutting-edge technologies, Gartner indicates that by 2026, approximately 25% of companies developing AI-based engineering solutions will have systems operating through adaptive AI models.

The main advantage of implementing AI within your operation lies in the ability to quickly develop, deploy, and adapt it to different business environments.

Given the complexity of the engineering and the high demand for rapid commercialization, it is crucial to develop less rigid AI engineering pipelines or build AI models that can automatically adapt in production.

## Metaverse is a new technology trend.

Given the new technological trends for the coming years, the metaverse continues to grow. Gartner indicates that by 2027, more than 40% of large organizations worldwide will use a combination of Web3 and spatial computing in metaverse-based projects aimed at increasing revenue.

The metaverse is a technology that utilizes combinatorial innovation made up of various themes and trends within a virtual environment.

Individually, these trends are designed to provide new opportunities and challenges for organizations across a broad spectrum of industries and use cases.

## Companies will continue to apply observability.

According to a study by Gartner, by 2026, approximately 70% of organizations that have successfully implemented [observability will have greater agility in decision-making.](/en/projects/resilience-observability/)

The monitoring method used in traditional IT has several drawbacks that hinder the growth of companies.

One of the main reasons is precisely the lack of scalability and intelligent analysis in the development environment, in addition to the inefficient detection of problems, which consumes a lot of time.

Companies that implement observability tend to achieve better results due to the constant monitoring of each stage of the development process.
