Cloud computing has recently emerged as one of the top technology trends for 2023, according to a [study by Gartner](/en/Gartner-reveals-5-key-technology-trends-for-2023---check-them-out%21/).

But why is investing in cloud computing solutions so important in 2023? There are some good reasons we can highlight for companies that want to optimize results and have more security in their infrastructures.

When it comes to cloud computing, we're talking about a great opportunity to manage risks, develop business strategies, and also provide greater flexibility for development teams.

Check out the blog post we've prepared and learn why your company should invest in cloud computing. Happy reading.

## Why should companies invest in cloud computing?

The pursuit of greater data security and privacy remains a highly relevant topic within the information technology sector.

Faced with constant attacks, [investments](https://www.convergenciadigital.com.br/Cloud-Computing/Nuvem-vai-sustentar-alta-de-5%25-nos-gastos-com-TI-apesar-da-recessao-global-61768.html) in cloud computing solutions are increasing despite the global recession. After all, what would the interruption of your company's services for a few hours, days, or weeks represent?

IT leaders and managers seek [partners](/en/solutions/cloud_native/) to enhance their delivery capabilities, but without compromising security best practices and also to make better use of technical and financial resources.

Therefore, check out some reasons why your company should adopt cloud computing solutions in 2023.

## 1 – Reduce Costs by Investing in Cloud Computing

The first point on our list concerns the cost reductions your company will experience by investing in cloud computing.

When an [AWS infrastructure](/en/AWS-in-Brazil%3A-Cloud-solution-optimizes-infrastructure-and-expands-mobility./) is implemented, the company saves on hardware and software resource costs. Furthermore, maintenance and electricity costs are reduced when maintaining a fully cloud-based infrastructure.

## 2 - Increase data security and privacy

It is possible to implement an automated backup routine for your AWS environment, allowing you to have control of the situation and, in case of any problem, to restore the environment in its entirety.

Amazon itself provides a service to assist in the backup process, AWS Backup, a managed and centralized platform for backing up various AWS services, such as Amazon EBS, EC2, EFS, FSx, RDS, DynamoDB, and Storage Gateway volumes.

## 3 – Optimize your IT team's time

Implementing cloud solutions allows IT teams to make better use of their resources, enabling them to dedicate their efforts to other areas that are also important to the business.

Through the adoption of [DevOps](/en/DevOps-tools%3A-5-tips-for-you-to-use-in-2022/) culture , teams from different areas can collaboratively promote actions aimed at a work routine with a lower error rate and greater clarity in the distribution of their functions.

## 4 – Implement API integration

Integrating applications with various APIs is a comprehensive task and a major challenge for companies. By implementing cloud computing solutions, teams will have greater integration and analysis capabilities at their disposal, thus generating increased productivity.

Cloud infrastructures create the [opportunity to containerize](/en/DevOps-and-Kubernetes%3A-an-efficient-and-complementary-duo./) the system, thus increasing system efficiency and providing the possibility of replicating the same configuration model in various locations.
