## Are you going to build a chatbot? Define your strategy.

A chatbot is simply a tool for providing solutions. Therefore, it's necessary to consider what type of problem(s) its integration will solve. You need to have a very clear objective regarding the purpose of your automation.

Regardless of the industry your company operates in, it is committed to solving a specific type of problem, providing solutions for customers, and generating business value.

What path is taken to deliver the solution to your customers? Are there any bottlenecks? Is it possible to optimize the processes?

For example, if your company is in the [insurance business](/en/Digital-transformation-in-the-insurance-sector-and-the-use-of-chatbots./) and wants to automate customer service for answering questions, sending quotes, and collecting information to generate a policy, your chatbot will need to be fed with each business variable through a list of functionalities.

The strategy adopted must be advantageous for both sides, both for the company implementing the solution, which will gain efficiency, and for the customers, who will benefit from better service.

Preparing the groundwork through in-depth studies of where your company can improve may not be a simple task; it may be necessary to admit that the work being done is not yet up to the level that customers need.

Mapping and understanding this is not a sign of weakness; on the contrary, it is an important step towards strengthening oneself and delivering better solutions.

After completing this step, it's time to understand if adopting bots is worthwhile for your company, or the best way to implement it.

## Does it make sense to adopt a chatbot in my company? Where would I implement it?

After understanding the steps your company needs to take to implement a chatbot and clearly seeing what kind of solutions you need to apply alongside this tool, it's time to find out if adopting this automation makes sense or how it will work.

Among the various possibilities that a chatbot can provide are the sale of products and services, clarification of doubts about products, registration of lead information, and understanding of the stage of the sales funnel.

However, it is not advisable to eliminate the possibility of human interaction, as each client has a unique case and sometimes it is necessary to speak with a real person to resolve more complex problems or problems that have not yet been identified.

On which customer service channel will the chatbot be implemented? Website, app, social media? All channels? It's necessary to understand and apply automation in the places that are right for your business.

If your company offers differentiated service to newer, older, or VIP clients based on spending, automation may require subtle differences in approach.

### Some practical chatbot integration possibilities

- **Companies that operate in the sale of products:** Integration with inventory control systems and payment options is possible.
- **Opening services:** If your company has a business model that allows for the opening of support tickets/service requests, it's possible to integrate with Zendesk, Groove, and others.
- **Appointment scheduling:** If your business is a clinic, you can schedule appointments through integrations with Google Calendar.
- **Funnel stage analysis:** RD Station is a great alternative for integrating with a chatbot.

## Communicating well is fundamental.

The best communication is that which gets straight to the point, is clear, objective, and leaves no room for doubt.

When we use very long sentences, it makes conversation between the user and the chatbot difficult. The conversational experience should be light and easy to understand for both parties.

It is important to avoid using highly technical terms, prioritizing words that are easy for the customer to understand and that promote inclusive communication.

There is a possibility that the chatbot may not understand the phrase written by the user, as it may contain grammatical errors, abbreviations, internet terms, or slang. Therefore, it is important that the solution is able to understand these variables and return the closest possible answer to the correct solution.

Another important point: often the client is in a hurry, stressed, or doesn't have time to read long texts, so get straight to the point.

Your company is responsible for guiding the interaction between the chatbot and the customer. Plan the customer journey so that they are strategically guided through the various stages of the sales funnel.

## Do tests and more tests.

After implementation, it's important to continue testing, and this should be an ongoing exercise. Compare metrics before and after using the chatbot to measure performance before and after its adoption.

It's also important to let different users and teams do the testing, not just the developers; this helps when it's necessary to identify any bugs.

Gradually, you will be able to understand if your customer base has grown, if engagement has improved, and if your business has become more profitable.
