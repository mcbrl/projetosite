Data privacy is a recurring topic for any company concerned with the digital security of its organizational and customer data, and the public sector is no exception.

Preserving data privacy is a constant challenge for organizations; after all, with the growing demand for new technologies, such as cloud migration, a number of precautions are necessary.

The public sector is undoubtedly one of the segments that has suffered the most from technological lag in recent years, resulting in a shortfall in the adoption of best practices in digital security. However, this scenario is changing with the advancement of digital transformation in the sector.

The Federal Government, through initiatives such as [Brasil Mais Digital (More Digital Brazil](/en/digital-transformation-in-the-public-sector/) ), has been implementing efforts to increase the digital maturity of public services offered by municipalities. Initiatives such as the implementation of Conecte SUS (Connect SUS), the Digital Work Card, and other functionalities and services are some examples of this movement.

Furthermore, the Ministry of Economy recently announced the creation of a guidance document with best practices for data protection and privacy.

We prepared this article to demonstrate a little more about the guide and also the importance of an efficient digital security strategy for the public sector.

Good reading.

## The digital security guide was developed to comply with the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law).

This [initiative by the Federal Government](https://www.gov.br/economia/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2022/junho/economia-lanca-guia-de-orientacao-sobre-privacidade-e-seguranca-da-informacao-voltados-a-orgaos-publicos) is part of the Privacy and Information Security Program, and its objective is to increase the maturity level of government agencies in managing the protection of personal data and information security measures.

[It is worth mentioning that numerous data breach](https://valor.globo.com/empresas/noticia/2022/06/21/portal-de-compras-do-governo-expe-dados-pessoais-de-empresrios.ghtml) cases are still common within the public sector, thus exposing some vulnerabilities within the sector.

Therefore, actions like those taken by the Ministry of Economy are necessary to implement a culture of data management that is planned, serious, and responsible.

To access the document, [visit the](https://www.gov.br/governodigital/pt-br/seguranca-e-protecao-de-dados/PPSI) Privacy and Information Security Department page of the Digital Government Secretariat, on the Privacy and Information Security Program (PPSI) page.

According to the Digital Government website, the PPSI (Program for the Prevention and Protection of Information Security) is a set of actions to improve privacy and information security, developed within the scope of the disciplines of governance, people, methodology, technology, and maturity management, implemented concurrently and incrementally in the federal Public Administration.

## Digital security begins with a well-defined DevSecOps strategy.

The constant hacker attacks on the systems of various Brazilian public bodies, such as the Superior Court of Justice, the Government Procurement Portal, the Ministry of Health, Conecte SUS, the Government of the Federal District, among others, expose the fragility of digital security within the public sector.

These cases reinforce the need for modernization actions and [DevSecOps](/en/devsecops-ransomware-protection/) best practices on the part of the authorities, and for them to urgently recognize the importance of investing in information security.

As the digitization of public services continues to increase, the necessary measures for data protection must also keep pace with this demand.

Therefore, [DevSecOps](https://materiais.vertigo.com.br/ebook-empresa-segura-com-devsecops-hashicorp) encompasses various phases in the development of a strategy, from the software development lifecycle to the final delivery stages.

This practice helps develop teams that seamlessly integrate security and compliance functions into their regular workflows, incorporating the necessary steps into the CI/CD continuous integration and delivery pipeline.
