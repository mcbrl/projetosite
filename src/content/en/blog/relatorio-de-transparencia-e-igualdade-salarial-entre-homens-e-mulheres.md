In accordance with the provisions of Law 14.611, of July 3, 2023, Vertigo Tecnologia publishes the Transparency and Salary Equity Report between Women and Men.

This topic is very significant and extremely important for society as a whole, and we are happy to practice equal pay and uphold the respect that women deserve. Without the contribution and talent of these professionals, we would not have achieved the success and reached where we are today.

## Women play a significant role in Vertigo's leadership.

The diversity of perspectives and skills that women bring to our team is fundamental to our continued innovation and growth.

Currently, 47% of Vertigo Tecnologia **'s leadership is composed of women** , demonstrating the total contribution of women to the company's growth, which is a source of pride for us.

Check out the [**transparency**](/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Relatorio-de-Transparencia-e-Igualdade-Salarial-de-Mulheres-e-Homens-1o-Semestre-2024.pdf) and equal pay report for men and women. The data refers to the first half of 2024.
