Vertigo Tecnologia announces that, starting in January 2025, Gustavo Paiva will be the company's new Chief Financial Officer and Head of Governance. With over 20 years of experience in various areas, he previously served as Vertigo's Head of Finance and now assumes the mission of strengthening corporate governance and driving the company's sustainable growth. His consolidated career and expertise in managing financial indicators will be fundamental for Vertigo to advance its strategic objectives and further distinguish itself in the market.

## **Challenges and Opportunities in the New Management**

In his new position, Gustavo will face the challenge of monitoring economic and tax policies that impact the cost of capital and identifying opportunities for the company to remain competitive. In addition, he will need to be aware of global trends, such as digitalization and sustainability, and monitor geopolitical risks that could affect the business. Strategies such as acquisitions and partnerships will also be on the radar to strengthen Vertigo in the market.

## **Innovation and Governance in the Digital Age**

Internally, Gustavo will be responsible for ensuring compliance with the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law) and optimizing processes through artificial intelligence, seeking operational efficiency and cost control. The integration of new technologies and adaptation to digital transformation will be central challenges, requiring a balance between innovation and governance. With a strategic vision, his management will be essential to consolidating Vertigo as a benchmark in innovation and sustainable growth.

Vertigo [**Tecnologia**](/en/) wishes Gustavo success in this new challenge, which will guide the company down a new path to success!
