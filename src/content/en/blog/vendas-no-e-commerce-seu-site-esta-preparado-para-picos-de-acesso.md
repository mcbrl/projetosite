For many e-commerce businesses, sales during special dates and promotions are crucial for annual revenue. Therefore, significant investments are made in digital media during these times. The best discounts and conditions are developed to attract more customers using sponsored link strategies, precisely to increase website traffic, in addition to other strategies. But will this be enough for what you're planning?

## Should promotional activities involve only the Marketing and Sales teams?

Imagine this situation: you own a physical store and you advertise a big sale. You manage to attract a large number of customers and your place is packed! Perfect, right? But this prevents you from serving all the customers, and some are left without being served. It's not so bad, though, because you managed to serve a portion of them.

Conclusion: It's likely that not everyone spent the amount you had projected, right? And that will directly affect your projected revenue for that month.
Now consider the same situation, but looking at your e-commerce business. Your platform, just like the physical store space, also has limitations, but this time they are technical limitations that need to be understood and respected! The advantage is that it has scalability, meaning it adapts to changes according to each scenario. And even if this happens quickly, especially if your operation is based on cloud solutions. With the right infrastructure and planning, your e-commerce becomes a powerful sales tool, suitable for your audience's traffic, even during periods of higher visit volume. Yes, talk to your IT staff to understand the technical limitations of your platform, and here's why.
At the same time, e-commerce has the disadvantage that without the proper infrastructure or planning, however temporary, your company risks not being able to serve ANY customers, that is, NO sales! Yes, all it takes is for the website to go offline or the purchase button to stop working, and I bet you know a story of a company that has already gone through this.
Therefore, at this point, you're probably wondering how to ensure the success of your platform during peak times, and I hope so! It's not uncommon for companies to ignore these limitations and end up paying dearly for it, whether it's having the website down, a button that doesn't start the purchase process, problems with the Java programming, database failures, and so on.

## **So, how can I ensure that all visitors have access to the website?**

The example makes it clear that when planning an e-commerce campaign, you need to think beyond the obvious! Don't just focus on content and design, but question how the infrastructure is being planned, whether it will handle the projected traffic volume. And if possible, monitor the user experience in real time. Therefore, good Marketing and Sales efforts will always be necessary, but they won't be enough if the digital platform isn't up and running and generating sales, right? It's essential to involve the Information Technology (IT) team in planning these campaigns. They will be able to help design an environment that can support this peak traffic.
The Marketing and IT teams should be responsible for forecasting, together with other teams, the amount of website traffic and projected sales for the period, for example. This way, tests will be carried out. This ensures that all customers can easily make their purchases, even on peak access days, meaning the "Black Friday" project will be a success!
Another reason for the IT team to participate in action planning is cost reduction. With a longer timeframe and the thorough analysis provided by these professionals, investments to maintain the stability of your website tend to be lower, since they can be better negotiated and understood. Not to mention that prevention is cheaper than having to hire someone or some tool at the last minute to solve a problem, or even pay overtime to get the website up and running in the middle of the night.

## **Conclusion**

The success of an online promotion depends, among other factors, on the alignment of the marketing team with the IT team. Don't take unnecessary risks or leave the effectiveness of your website to chance. This planning guarantees the success of your sales project and the achievement of your campaign goals.
