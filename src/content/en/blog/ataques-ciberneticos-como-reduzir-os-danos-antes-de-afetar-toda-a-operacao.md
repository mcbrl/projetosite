With the constant increase in digitalization, cybersecurity has become a major concern for businesses. Every day, new threats and vulnerabilities emerge that can result in significant losses for organizations, including the loss of sensitive data, downtime, damage to brand reputation, and increased costs.

Cyberattacks can occur in various forms, including phishing, malware, ransomware, and denial-of-service attacks, and are capable of negatively impacting a company's IT operations.

These impacts can be devastating for an organization, and the losses are often difficult to calculate. Beyond the financial costs involved, there is also damage to reputation and loss of customer trust. A company that has suffered a cyberattack can take years to regain market and customer confidence. Therefore, it is crucial that companies adopt preventative measures to minimize the risks of intrusions and prepare to deal with the effects of an attack.

DevSecOps is a practice that seeks to integrate security from the beginning of the software development lifecycle, allowing companies to identify and resolve potential vulnerabilities before they even become a problem. Furthermore, DevSecOps also helps companies maintain compliance with security regulations such as LGPD, GDPR, PCI, and HIPAA.

One of the tools that has stood out in optimizing DevSecOps is HashiCorp Vault, which offers advanced solutions for managing secrets and protecting sensitive data.

In this article, we will explore how cyberattacks can impact a company's IT operations and how DevSecOps can be optimized with HashiCorp Vault. We will also present some measures that companies can take to reduce the damage from these attacks before they affect the entire operation.

Our goal is to provide valuable information to business decision-makers and help them take preventative measures to keep their IT environments secure.

## Impacts of a cyberattack on your IT operations

A cyberattack can have a devastating impact on a company's IT operations. Imagine the chaos that can ensue from the loss of valuable data, system downtime, and disruption to daily operations. It's a stressful and costly situation that can negatively affect the company's reputation and customer trust.

Furthermore, it can have even more serious consequences, such as the loss of intellectual property, violation of customer privacy, and even identity theft. These problems can lead to a significant loss of money, time, and resources, as well as negatively impacting the company's image and credibility.

This type of attack can lead to potential fines and lawsuits, especially if the company is not in compliance with data privacy and security regulations. This significantly impacts the company's cash flow and its ability to invest in new projects or hire new talent.

According to an IBM study, the average time required to identify and contain a security breach is approximately 280 days, with an average cost of US$3,86 million.

To make matters worse, the number of cyberattacks is increasing every year. Criminals are becoming increasingly sophisticated in their tactics, and companies need to be prepared to face this growing threat. Unfortunately, many companies still do not have adequate security measures in place, which makes them vulnerable to attacks.

Therefore, it is crucial that companies understand the risks associated with cyberattacks and take steps to protect their systems and data. Prevention is always the best option, but if an attack occurs, it is important to have a contingency plan and a team specialized in cybersecurity to minimize damage. Do not underestimate the severity of a cyberattack – the consequences can be devastating.

## How to mitigate the damage from an attack before it cripples your entire operation.

With the growing threat of cyberattacks, it is crucial that companies take preventative measures to minimize damage before it's too late. Here are some strategies that can be implemented to reduce the losses from an attack before it even impacts the entire operation:

- **Establish an incident response plan:** An incident response plan is essential for a quick and effective response in the event of an attack. It should include the procedures to be followed and the responsibilities of each team member.
- **Update your software and hardware regularly:** Keeping software and hardware up-to-date is crucial to ensuring that known vulnerabilities are patched and that systems are protected against new threats.
- **Train your team:** A well-trained team is essential for preventing and responding to cyberattacks. It is important that all company employees know how to recognize the signs of a potential attack and how to report suspicious activity.
- **Monitor your network constantly:** Constant network monitoring is essential to detect suspicious activity and identify potential threats before they can cause damage.
- **Make regular backups:** Regularly backing up critical data is an important security measure in the event of a cyberattack. This helps ensure that the company can continue operating, even when data is lost.

Collaboration between different areas of the company, such as IT, information security, and risk management, is also crucial to ensure effective protection against cyberattacks. By adopting these measures, it is possible to significantly reduce the damage caused by an attack.
