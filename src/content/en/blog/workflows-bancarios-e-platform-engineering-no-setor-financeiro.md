## What are Bank Workflows?

Banking workflows are organized work flows that structure the internal and external activities of a financial institution. They connect systems, people, and processes in operations such as account opening, credit analysis, transaction processing, and regulatory compliance.

These workflows ensure that tasks are performed efficiently, safely, and in compliance with regulations.

#### **Examples of Banking Workflows**

A common example is opening a bank account, which includes steps such as submitting documents, identity verification, credit analysis, and approval. Another relevant example is loan processing, which involves application, data validation, risk assessment, and disbursement of funds.

Despite being essential, many banking workflows still face challenges, such as reliance on legacy systems, manual processes, and a lack of flexibility. This is where [**Platform Engineering**](/en/Journey-to-the-cloud-with-Veecode-platform-and-Kong-Gateway/) comes in.

## What is Platform Engineering and what is its role in banking workflows?

Platform Engineering refers to the construction and management of internal technological platforms that integrate different systems and provide a standardized foundation for automating and optimizing banking workflows. These platforms create a scalable, efficient, and secure environment, essential for handling the volume and complexity of modern [**financial operations**](/en/financial-sector-graph-database/).

#### **How does Platform Engineering Impact Banking Workflows?**

In the context of banking workflows, Platform Engineering works by connecting legacy systems to modern solutions, enabling seamless and automated flows. It digitizes manual tasks with tools like RPA and provides a scalable infrastructure capable of handling increasing transaction volumes.

Furthermore, Platform Engineering ensures regulatory compliance by integrating policies directly into workflows.
