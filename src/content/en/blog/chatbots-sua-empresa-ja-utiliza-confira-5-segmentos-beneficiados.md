## Automating customer service has come to address shortcomings.

A few years ago, customer service was provided through call centers, which resulted in long waiting times, a drop in the quality of the approach, and difficulty in understanding the customer's problem; in other words, it was almost always a stressful process for the company and especially for the consumer.

Manual customer service processes tend to be time-consuming; after all, humans can't work at the same pace as API-based automation because we get tired and can make mistakes.

After years of numerous consumer complaints, various sectors have adopted omnichannel strategies to improve communication with their customers, developing plans aligned with marketing tools to enhance engagement and generate more leads. Let's examine how the implementation of chatbots has helped different sectors.

### Chatbots in the Financial Sector

The financial sector is one of the biggest beneficiaries of the digital transformation revolution in recent years.

According to a report by [Juniper Research](https://www.juniperresearch.com/press/mobile-banking-users-to-reach-2-billion-by-2020) , chatbot solutions will provide savings of US$2 billion per year between 2016 and the end of 2022.

It's no wonder, after all, that technologies are being implemented to improve security and customer service, addressing what have been some of the most important issues for customers in recent years.

Through the implementation of chatbots, present in [Vertigo Tecnologia's solutions](/en/solutions/) , it is possible to segment customers through credit scoring, manage budgets, recommend the best alternatives to the customer through the use of artificial intelligence, and provide financial consulting for managing cash flow.

### Chatbots in the Industrial Sector

We are currently in Industry 4.0, also known as the Fourth Industrial Revolution, which is nothing more than the implementation of cutting-edge technologies within this segment, which also includes automation through chatbots.

The use of automation through chatbot APIs has several advantages for the industrial sector, such as training employees on how to use machinery and learning processes in a customized way.

Another possibility is mapping faults in equipment and applications that need to operate around the clock. In this scenario, chatbots can manage error detection and perform corrections.

Digital transformation is rapidly advancing to further strengthen the sector. Discover more possibilities for using chatbots to improve customer relationships.

### Utilities sector chatbot

The utilities sector refers to companies that integrate the sectors of production, transportation, electricity, gas, water, and other essential services.

Because it deals with basic services linked to customers' daily lives, the utilities sector has a high demand for debt negotiation through channels like WhatsApp and Messenger, something that was previously done through a lengthy telephone service process. Automated customer service via chatbot APIs is already a reality for many companies in the sector.

Automation through bots allows users to gain increasing autonomy in decision-making, as chatbot APIs are quite accessible to a large part of the population, especially through smartphones. Thanks to digital transformation, chatbot customer service is becoming increasingly intuitive and integrated into users' daily lives, proving to be powerful tools within the utilities sector.

### Chatbots in the healthcare sector

The healthcare sector has also been impacted by the use of chatbots to automate processes and customer service for greater efficiency.

Through the implementation of omnichannel strategies, patients can schedule appointments and undergo virtual triage for more accurate referrals when booking tests, thus improving the user experience.

As in other sectors, the use of chatbot integrations provides cost reduction for companies, in addition to shorter service queues, 24/7 availability, and error reduction.

### Public sector chatbots

The use of chatbots in the public sector has also gained momentum as agencies modernize to keep pace with the digital transformation process.

We can highlight the use of bots in providing basic services to citizens, such as:

– To provide information regarding important dates, indicating when a document will expire or when it will need to be renewed.

– Effective service in public agencies, directing the user to the correct departments after cross-referencing initial information, and also providing the names of those responsible for a given area.

– Information regarding the issuance of duplicate documents or verification of outstanding debts to any agency, such as fines.

Citizens now have access to information on public health guidelines, SMS alerts for storms in at-risk areas, interaction with government social media, and easy access to ombudsman services in case of complaints.
