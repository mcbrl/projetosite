## **How do companies use DXPs?**

For over a decade, we've seen companies leverage the capabilities and flexibility of Liferay DXP to transform themselves into truly digital businesses. While DXPs are undeniably powerful, their flexibility can present the challenge of figuring out exactly what a company wants to achieve with them. In this ebook, discover the 7 most common use cases where organizations benefit from a digital experience platform.

## **Self-Service Portals for Customers**

Self-service may be the first step towards more modern customer experiences, but if companies want to establish a foundation for truly exceptional experiences, they will need to unify the points of interaction into a single portal solution.

When customers have a unified place to download resources, access digital services, and resolve their issues without having to contact a representative, they are not only more satisfied with the service provided, but they are also more willing to return and use the portal.

With a unified solution for its customers:

- Reduce calls to customer support through self-service features, lowering the cost of service.
- Offer personalized experiences based on the behavior, roles, and preferences of your audience.
- Integrate existing systems and services to deliver a streamlined customer experience.

## **Making the customer experience easier**

For more than 80 years, EATEL served residential and commercial customers in the United States. But they knew that if they wanted to continue offering excellent customer experiences, their digital channels needed an upgrade.

To modernize this experience, EATEL built a responsive customer self-service portal for:

- To provide a comprehensive view of the customer relationship.
- To offer self-service features that provide an interface to various back-end systems.
- Customize for future changes and evolving service offerings.
- Reduce calls to the call center by 30%.

## **Websites for B2B Sales**

To better meet the complex needs of B2B buyers, sellers must be able to support their customers throughout the entire journey, not just up to the point of purchase. But pure-play digital commerce platforms will not be able to support post-purchase experiences.

When sellers instead rely on technologies designed to address the entire shopping experience, they can:

- Manage the entire experience, from first contact to post-purchase, all on a single platform.
- Increase cross-selling and upselling by providing personalized content and product recommendations.
- Empower your sales teams with digital tools to support, nurture, and grow each account.

## **Simplifying B2B orders**

With customers in over 40 different countries, Tag, a global end-to-end marketplace production partner, needed a solid foundation for its commerce systems, as existing legacy systems were hindering its ability to truly deliver the experience its customers were looking for.

By implementing B2B commerce on a single platform with Liferay, Tag was able to transform its sales processes:

- Connecting and managing systems, applications, and data more easily.
- Simplifying complex B2B requests through self-service capabilities.
- Reducing system management and customer service costs.

**Modern Intranets:** No matter where your employees work, they are the foundation for the excellent customer experiences you want to offer.

Business leaders understand that, to provide better employee experiences, they must be able to offer solutions to employees that:

- Increase organizational efficiency using custom forms, workflows, and functions.
- Offer support to global and remote employees from any device.
- Empower your employees to easily find and share information.

## **Providing Better Employee Experiences**

To be a better supplier of advanced materials, Materion needed to connect more than 2.600 employees. But they were hampered by outdated tools and processes. Employees struggled to find the content, policies, and information they needed.

The new intranet built on Liferay saw a 20% increase in sessions in just four months. Now employees are able to:

- Access the information you need on a single platform.
- Consume content such as podcasts, videos, and infographics.
- Communicating globally, fostering a culture of collaboration.

## **Integration Platforms**

The desire to provide better digital experiences brings great responsibilities in managing all the systems and applications necessary for this task. While organizations may be determined to digitally transform their businesses, this goal is often hampered by legacy and siloed technologies.

Organizations have been able to overcome these challenges by using a robust digital experience platform to:

- Unify disparate systems with APIs, web services, and integration points.
- Leverage previous investments by integrating with existing and legacy systems.
- Simplify IT management by reducing the time and costs of systems management.

## **Everything in a single technology stack.**

Due to the ever-changing nature of digital business, the QAD IT team understood that its role was becoming not only about managing technology but also about understanding the needs of its employees, customers, and partners, providing solutions to help them achieve their goals.

However, QAD's IT team had struggled in the past with a combination of disparate solutions and was now seeking a single solution to bring together its intranet, website, content management system, and future solutions.

QAD adopted the Liferay platform to:

- Establish a go-to-market strategy with new solutions quickly, such as a customer portal and online customer forums.
- Implement new features, such as greater customization, in existing solutions.
- Take advantage of a flexible and evolving platform to meet constantly changing IT needs.

## **Partner Portal/Extranets**

As an external tool for suppliers, partners, and agents, extranets still need to provide continuity in the customer experience, even when they work through third parties. But only 35% of companies have effective training and development for their channels.

Channel partners don't have to be a neglected, secondary character. Instead, they can stand out and help the company succeed with a tool that allows them to:

- Increase sales more effectively with customized dashboards and content.
- Support for large global networks with granular roles and permissions.
- Encouraging its adoption through easy self-service experiences.

## **A partner for success**

With seven different, long-standing partner portals, Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) was unable to provide the digital experiences needed to empower its more than 170.000 partners worldwide.

By offering partners a single global entry point through Liferay, HPE is able to:

- Customize home pages and dashboards based on complex permissions and roles.
- Integrate the portal with all services and tools available in the partner ecosystem.
- Scale efficiently as the partner base continues to grow.

**Locations**

Websites are the gateway to your company's digital experience. The website experience will either encourage users to go further or seek a better solution.

Companies need to be able to quickly create the personalized experiences their customers are looking for. These websites not only need to be user-friendly, but also:

- Easy for business users to edit and manage, relieving the burden on IT teams.
- Quickly customizable to accelerate go-to-market.

Its existing systems were unable to provide the same level of flexibility and personalization for the company's digital experiences.

To overcome this challenge, AGIA used [Liferay](/en/Liferay-DXP-for-insurance-companies/) to:

- Launching over 300 websites in just 9 months.

## **Campus Portals**

Effective remote learning goes beyond simply providing relevant material online. Educational organizations will need to learn how to facilitate excellent digital experiences that make it easier for students, parents, and staff to learn and connect.

Elevate the user experience by creating campus portals that are capable of:

- Provide customized dashboards and websites based on granular permissions.
- Easily integrate with third-party applications and older legacy systems.
- Protecting confidential documents and information.

## **Education beyond physical borders.**

With over 170.000 international students from England to Singapore, the University of London wanted to give remote students an engaging experience to create a sense of community.

Using Liferay, the university developers were able to:

- Manage content and applications across five websites on a single platform.
- To empower business users to manage pages and content without IT assistance.
- Offering customization to make the portal even more meaningful.
