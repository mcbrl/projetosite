One of the best ways to know if your business is delivering results and what critical points need improvement is by monitoring activities, including the online performance of the applications used in the company. However, this management is often neglected amidst the daily rush.
It may not seem like it, but postponing the monitoring of online performance will cost your business dearly in the future. If you're still hesitant or don't believe this activity is that important, keep reading our article and understand a little more about its importance.

## **What is the purpose of online performance monitoring?**

Application Performance Monitoring (APM) is a suite of solutions that helps you manage the performance and availability of applications. With APM, you can monitor processes to diagnose problems and gain valuable insights that improve team productivity and, consequently, increase your business profitability.

## **What are the benefits of monitoring online performance?**

There are several benefits to monitoring your business's online performance, as this tracking will allow for better management of processes and customer service, as well as a comprehensive view of the business environment.
Below, we will list some of the main advantages of implementing this management:

### **1\. Deep understanding of the environment**

One of the main benefits of monitoring your business's online performance is having the ability to... [to understand the environments in depth](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/5-beneficios-da-virtualizacao-para-testar-sua-api/) Internal and external aspects of the applications. With this management, you can monitor how the application is running and how it is performing. [used by users](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/4-dicas-para-aumentar-o-engajamento-na-sua-api/).
All this data is valuable for better planning and organizing business demands, optimizing processes, functions, and working hours.

### **2\. Process and team optimization**

Once you have an understanding of the application environments, there is no longer a need to maintain a bloated team of diverse people to validate and monitor the systems.
Since this entire process is automated and you already have enough data to understand application demand, it becomes much simpler to allocate resources and teams to essential and specific activities.
In summary, your team will perform at a higher level and processes will be highly optimized, reducing business costs.

### **3\. Greater customer satisfaction**

Increasing the satisfaction of internal and external customers is one of the main objectives of managing and monitoring the online performance of applications. This is because more efficient systems generate higher-performing teams, since employees don't need to waste time on details and repetitive tasks.
This scenario leads to better deliveries to customers, increasing satisfaction levels and building healthier, more lasting relationships.

### **4\. End of the “war rooms”**

When there is no organization and control, whenever a problem arises in a critical system, tempers flare and accusations begin to be exchanged between team members, the famous "blame game". Typically, the first action is to set up "war rooms," bringing together in the same room those responsible for all areas involved (Database, Network, Application, Storage, Security, etc.).
In addition to creating a hostile work environment, these situations result in a huge loss of time that could be used more productively.
When an APM solution is implemented, failures are flagged by the system itself, focusing attention on the solution rather than assigning blame. This allows you to assign one team member to solve the problem, while the rest can focus on other activities or even on other tasks. [collaborate on innovations and improvements](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/5-acoes-para-aumentar-a-colaboracao-na-sua-empresa/) for the area and for the company.

### **Conclusion**

Not monitoring your business's online performance is synonymous with wasting time, losing customers, and losing money. It may seem like just a detail, but when there's no control over what's happening, the company starts to fall short in delivery and process optimization.
