Thanks to contributions from the open-source community, the use of Kubernetes has flourished. Its popularity is so great that some professionals claim every company should use it. But what is the appeal of Kubernetes, and why has it become so popular?
Kubernetes has extensive features, which adds power and complexity. This can be overwhelming and a real obstacle for those who are starting to use it. At this point, some questions arise:

**It is complicated?**
Piloting a state-of-the-art jet comes with a lot of responsibility. If you've never done more than play a flight simulator, this can be downright daunting.

**Is it ready for production?**
Everyone's talking about Kubernetes these days, but it only really emerged in the enterprise market in recent years. Many companies have a habit of not being "early adopters," meaning they don't consider the immediate adoption of a release, because they prefer to wait to learn about all the new technologies or even learn from the mistakes and problems of others. Therefore, creating a Kubernetes deployment on your own means solving challenging problems without enterprise support or even support from the technical community.

**Do I have the people and skills that are needed?** 
Most IT teams are still learning how to use Kubernetes. If this is the case for you, it means you will need to hire professionals with the necessary experience.

**Does it automate and replace my IT work?**
Kubernetes automates many tasks related to application infrastructure and allows developers and DevOps teams to handle the... [infrastructure as code](/en/Infrastructure-as-code%3A-a-competitive-advantage-in-retail./).

**Is it just a passing fad?**
Some technology approaches had a brief moment of great attention, but faded into obscurity. Kubernetes is popular now, but will it be in 5 years?
Despite these questions, Kubernetes offers numerous advantages, including increased productivity, scalability, automation, and configuration, among others. In this article, we will focus on the benefit Kubernetes brings by reducing cloud complexity. Enjoy your reading!

## It offers more security.

Although it has several advantages, Kubernetes may have some drawbacks. [security challenges, such as container compromise.](/en/The-importance-of-container-security-and-microservices-for-insurance-companies./) and attacks on infrastructure. However, there are several built-in security features to mitigate these concerns.
Is your *stacks* They are protected with a comprehensive selection of security measures, including automated encryption, private logs, isolation policies, and scanning. Organizations can also manage security policies for... *pod*, *Transport Layer Security*API authorization and authentication, as well as restricted access to other secure clusters.
Also, there are [security solutions](/en/The-importance-of-container-security-and-microservices-for-insurance-companies./) available, such as the **Vault, a Hashicorp tool** used by Vertigo in projects where it was necessary to adopt it for additional security or to meet specific needs.
Vault tightly controls access to secrets and encryption keys through authentication against trusted identity sources, such as Kubernetes. This allows for granular authorization of which users and applications are permitted to access secrets and keys.

## Reasons why you might start using Kubernetes today.

**It doesn't have to be complicated.**
Tools like Docker facilitate the integration and use of Kubernetes for operations, allowing development and operations teams to use it as a standard orchestration platform.
**You can start without knowing everything.**
You don't need in-depth knowledge or certifications to get started. Docker provides a highly available and secure Kubernetes configuration out of the box, offering the controls and features you need. As your team's skills grow, they can gain full control over advanced configurations and settings.
**It helps operations teams grow professionally.**
Kubernetes helps expand the role of IT operations in an organization. With Kubernetes and Docker, you can provide a complete platform for your developers that works on any machine or in the cloud.
**Kubernetes has a mature ecosystem.**
All major cloud providers support Kubernetes. Hundreds of solutions, from storage, infrastructure, monitoring and alerting to security, IoT and AI.
