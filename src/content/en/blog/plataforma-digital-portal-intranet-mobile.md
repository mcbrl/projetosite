If you don't know **what a digital platform is** , rest assured that you use them for almost everything in your life. Conceptually, **digital platforms** are a business model that allows producers and consumers to connect, enabling them to access this environment and interact with each other, seeking to create some exchange value.

Nowadays, we can do many things using devices connected to the internet. It is through **digital platforms** that it is possible to perform everyday tasks, such as paying bills and buying products.

In this article, we will explain why digital platforms are becoming increasingly valuable assets for companies. Through them, many companies are building a competitive advantage, developing the ability to communicate and relate in a differentiated way with their target audience.

The concept of a **digital platform** has been evolving. Previously, it was more focused on the developer experience, ensuring optimal usability within the environment. Conversely, it was quite limited from the user's perspective.

The concept evolves into a **Digital Experience Platform** . This new approach follows the path of **Digital Transformation** , [focusing more on the user experience](/en/digital-marketing-information-technology-customer-experience/) and striving to be as efficient and interactive as possible.

According to **Liferay** – a company with which we are [a Platinum Partner –](/en/Vertigo-Liferay-Platinum/) **a Digital Experience Platform (** DXP) is an emerging category of enterprise software that seeks to meet the needs of corporations undergoing **Digital Transformation** , with the ultimate goal of providing better experiences to their audiences. **DXPs** can be a single solution, but are often a suite of products that work together.

## **Portals**

A portal is a web-based platform that collects information from different sources and presents it in a single interface, providing users with the most relevant information according to their context.

We are increasingly demanding consumers, and product creation follows a growing number of criteria. Personalization is one of the mandatory characteristics. A portal is no exception to this rule and needs to allow personalization in various ways. Because, the more it aligns with the audience's needs, the better the product.

A portal needs to have a modern and user-friendly interface, both for developers and administrators. The easier it is to handle and interact with an application, the faster that solution will gain user acceptance.

It also needs to have many native functionalities and the ability to integrate with other market solutions, contributing to the creation of exceptional **digital experiences** . A portal can be seen as the central strategy for generating new business, communicating with your audience, publicizing your purpose, among other functionalities.

There are different types of portals. Let's see them below!

### Customer portals

Allow your customers to conduct transactions, access documents, contract new services, and obtain information online. Companies such as Avianca, Smiles, Azul, Santander, the Rio de Janeiro City Hall, and others are using the [**Liferay DXP**](/en/Liferay-DXP-features/) digital experience platform as a portal platform to generate new business.

### Partner portals

It helps people, partners, or agents to be more effective by offering them personalized and contextualized information. For example, if you are an insurance company, you can create a portal for your brokers or your network of service providers, with specific information by product line, geographic region, and customer segments. Yes, this is possible.

## **intranets**

Depending on the size of your company, [you will need an intranet](/en/What-should-your-intranet-include) . They are important for creating a **healthy digital environment** that encourages collaboration and innovation. In simple terms, intranets are networks similar to the internet, but for private use, exclusively for the company.

Intranets are essential for increasing productivity, but they are often underestimated and not used effectively due to poor design during implementation. This impacts managers' perception of quality and productivity, not to mention the lack of training and updates for employees.

Investing in this type of solution can improve the efficiency of your activities and have a direct impact on your business results. Who hasn't ever searched for the most up-to-date version of a particular document? Or looked for a presentation that was archived in their email? Or couldn't find the template to create that business proposal? Archiving problems are frequent, but they can be solved with the organization that an [intranet has the potential to offer](/en/5-advantages-of-using-an-intranet-in-your-company.).

A corporate intranet offers several benefits:

**Accessibility** : Intranets should be easy to use and provide valuable information and insights for your team. Every intranet should have a clear function of facilitating daily routines. If it's a burden for your team, something is very wrong!

**Productivity:** It helps your employees make decisions, create effective content, share information and ideas with the rest of the company, and digitize workflows. This is very valuable because we live in the knowledge age!

**Integration:** When your intranet integrates with back-end systems (CRM, Management Software, HR Systems), it tends to eliminate silos and becomes the central hub for finding solutions to business operations.

## **Mobile Apps**

![](/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/mobile-apps-768x577-1.jpg)

The use of mobile devices to access online content is constantly increasing. Therefore, it is essential to pay special attention to how **digital experience platforms** behave on these *devices* . There is no single best way to create or launch your mobile strategy. What matters is finding the method that best suits your needs, considering your company's scope, timeframe, and available investment and maintenance budget.

Creating a native experience, tailored to each mobile operating system, or even a responsive web experience, and the ease of building these applications is another important point. When thinking about your **Digital Experience Platform strategy** , also consider the product roadmap. You absolutely cannot forget to optimize your UI for navigation on mobile devices.

Just as important as thinking about **User Experience** is thinking about [monitoring digital performance](/en/monitoring-the-digital-experience/) , paying attention to the numbers and indicators of your online environment. So, observe, evaluate, and measure the growth of interactions and engagement coming through this type of device.
