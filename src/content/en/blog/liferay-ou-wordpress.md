A very common characteristic of comparisons is to analyze the pros and cons of each option and recommend the one that offers the most advantages to the reader. But what if the compared solutions don't compete with each other? This is the case with Liferay and WordPress. Each has a specific purpose, and in this post we'll help you understand the characteristics of each. Want to know which one best suits your company's needs? Check it out!

## **Understanding WordPress**

WordPress is the CMS ([Follow this link to learn more about CMS.](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/portal-e-cms-qual-a-diferenca/?utm_source=Blog&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=LiferayOuWordpress)It's the most widely used system in the world, and not by chance. Besides being free, it's easy to install and offers a large number of customization options, such as templates (ready-made website models) and plugins that can even transform a website into an e-commerce site.
Despite this, WordPress's main focus remains content, and the most common use of this CMS is for creating blogs of all types. It's very easy to start publishing on the platform, and the various plugins add important functionalities, such as comment moderation and spam protection.
Furthermore, the global WordPress community is one of its greatest assets, and its active support is responsible for the platform's constant development. This level of effort is evident from the documentation, available in multiple languages, to the creation of new features and integrations.

## **Understanding Liferay**

O [Liferay](/en/partnerships/Liferay?utm_source=Blog&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=LiferayOuWordpress), on the other hand, is a portal [(Click on this link to learn more about the Portal)](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/portal-e-cms-qual-a-diferenca/?utm_source=Blog&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=LiferayOuWordpress) Focused on creating business solutions. As a portal tool, its functionalities include: building websites (in various languages), web applications, collaboration platforms (such as an intranet), creating content management systems, access permissions by user type, public and private pages, article editing permissions, Portlets, native integration with ERPs, databases, and more.
Unlike WordPress, which is widely used for casual websites and can adapt to certain professional user requirements, Liferay is a portal that meets much more complex business and organizational needs, without sacrificing flexibility and ease of use.

## **What is the best option for me?**

After quickly understanding the purpose of each application, it becomes easy to see that they are, in fact, completely different and do not serve the same audience—nor were they created for the same needs. WordPress is a great option for blogs and content channels, especially simpler ones, or even online stores starting their operations in a more modest way.
O [Liferay](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/criacao-de-portal-conheca-3-plataformas-open-source/?utm_source=Blog&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=LiferayOuWordpress), in turn, is more robust precisely because it is a portal tool. Its target audience is companies that need fast, easy-to-adopt, and simple solutions.
