The Liferay platform has been named a leader in Digital Experience Platforms for the tenth consecutive time in Gartner's Magic Quadrant, one of the world's most respected technology consulting and advisory firms. 

In its report, Gartner evaluated 14 digital experience platform providers based on the criteria of Completeness of Vision and Ability to Execute.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/Liferay-Post_Gartner.png)

### **What is Gartner's Magic Quadrant?**

These quadrants are composed of a range of companies, divided into four categories: Challengers, Niche Players, Visionaries, and Leaders.
For this ranking, Gartner experts analyze, for each company, items such as global presence, business vision, customer portfolio, satisfaction index, and the technical aspects of the platform. This expertise allows many companies to make decisions based on the consultancy's data.

### **Liferay's key findings according to Gartner.**

Liferay stood out among participating providers in terms of customer experience, open-source benefits, and extra features. Learn more:

- Liferay has a good understanding of the DXP market. Its product and packaging strategy is well-suited to the use cases of customer, partner, and employee experience.
- Customers benefit from Liferay's active open-source community, which continuously contributes to the codebase and encourages the use of the open-source version;
- Liferay offers strong features for application extensibility and integration, which appeal to IT buyers;
