When we talk about an iPaaS, we are referring to an Identity and Access Management (IAM) platform that offers companies a complete solution for managing user access to company resources.

With iPaaS, companies can control who has access to which resources, when, and how, which is essential in a scenario where cloud migration is increasingly common. The iPaaS platform is cloud-based and offers a wide range of security features, including multi-factor authentication, password management, identity management, user provisioning, and much more.

Therefore, solutions like [**Digibee's iPaaS**](/en/partnerships/digibee/) are highly scalable and can be easily integrated with other security and identity management solutions, in addition to allowing for faster implementation due to their low-code approach. We've prepared this article to demonstrate 5 benefits of Digibee's iPaaS and show how it can be useful for your company. Happy reading!

## What is Digibee iPaaS?

Digibee iPaaS is an integration platform as a service that simplifies the connection between different systems, applications, and data. Built for the cloud, it promotes fast, secure, and scalable cloud migration and system integration, breaking through the traditional complexities that often delay IT projects.

With its low-code approach, it democratizes the ability to innovate, allowing professionals with diverse levels of technical skill to contribute to integration efforts.

### 2\. Significant Reduction in TCO

Digibee's iPaaS offers a cloud-native solution that modernizes legacy systems and reduces the total cost of ownership (TCO). This is achieved by minimizing physical infrastructure, reducing the need for specialized maintenance, and leveraging reusable components, which together drastically lower development and operational costs.

The strategy of using reusable components is another fundamental pillar of this solution, allowing IT professionals to maximize efficiency in developing new features and [**integrating systems**](/en/API-integration-creating-connectivity-in-the-industrial-sector/).

### 3\. Rapid Adaptation to Organizational Changes

With reusable components and a low-code approach, the Digibee iPaaS facilitates adaptation to rapid organizational changes. Whether through expansions, downsizing, or strategic reorientations, the platform ensures that integrations can be adjusted or expanded with minimal disruption and effort.

This adaptability not only speeds up the implementation of organizational changes, but also reduces the costs associated with these transitions, since the reuse of components decreases the need to develop new solutions from scratch.

Unlike other iPaaS solutions, which require a steep learning curve or are geared towards non-technical users without adequate supervision, Digibee iPaaS is accessible to any developer with basic JSON knowledge, enabling adoption in a matter of days.
