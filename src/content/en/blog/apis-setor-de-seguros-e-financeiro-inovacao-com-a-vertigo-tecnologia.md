The insurance and financial sectors increasingly rely on systems integration to offer complete, secure, and customer-centric digital services. This integration is only possible thanks to APIs, which act as connectors between institutions, platforms, and digital ecosystems.

However, despite its central role, API management remains a significant pain point for banks, insurance companies, and fintechs. High costs associated with proprietary gateways, lack of governance, low visibility into usage, and security risks are frequent challenges. In many cases, the absence of control over APIs transforms a strategic asset into a point of vulnerability.

In this scenario, Vertigo Tecnologia stands out as a partner to help the insurance and financial sectors overcome these challenges. With its Kong-based [**VeeCode API Manager**](/en/Veecode-platform/) **offering ,** the company combines centralized governance, automation, and observability to transform APIs into a driver of innovation, security, and efficiency.

## **Key pain points in API management in the insurance and finance sectors.**

### **Excessive costs of proprietary gateways**

Many financial institutions and insurance companies still use expensive proprietary gateways. While robust, these models tend to create vendor lock-in, costly contracts, and limited customization flexibility.

This approach consumes financial resources that could be directed towards innovation, while simultaneously limiting the adaptability of institutions. The result is an expensive, rigid environment that is ill-prepared for rapid change. In this respect, open-source or community-based alternatives offer significant advantages, allowing for greater control, scalability, and cost optimization.

### **Lack of governance and centralized monitoring**

Another recurring challenge is the lack of centralized governance over APIs. Often, different areas of the institution develop and publish APIs without clear standards, resulting in a fragmented ecosystem that is difficult to monitor.

This lack of governance compromises not only efficiency but also security. Without centralized visibility, it becomes difficult to identify who consumes each API, monitor suspicious access, or apply consistent security and compliance policies. In a regulated environment such as the insurance and financial sectors, this gap can generate severe risks.

### **Complex integration between legacy and modern systems**

Financial institutions and insurance companies operate with critical legacy systems that were not designed to support modern integrations. This makes API implementation more challenging, requiring extra effort in adaptation and support.

Without the right strategy, each new integration can become an additional risk, as well as increasing operational complexity. At this point, efficient API governance is essential to allow legacy and modern systems to coexist securely and scalably.

## **How to transform APIs into a competitive advantage**

### **APIs as catalysts for innovation in the insurance and financial sector.**

When managed effectively, APIs cease to be merely technical integrations and become catalysts for innovation. They enable the creation of new products, agile partner integration, and the delivery of personalized customer experiences.

In the [**insurance sector**](/en/liferay-dxp-in-the-insurance-sector/) , for example, APIs enable real-time quotes, policy customization, and integration with digital marketplaces. In financial institutions, APIs allow for instant payments, integrations with digital wallets, and new open banking models.

### **Benefits of centralized and scalable governance**

Centralized governance is fundamental to transforming APIs into strategic assets. This means defining development standards, consistently applying security policies, and monitoring the entire API lifecycle on a single platform.

This model reduces risks, increases efficiency, and ensures regulatory compliance. Furthermore, it provides scalability: new APIs can be developed and published more quickly, always with visibility and control.

### **Secure and standardized APIs as the foundation for trusted digital ecosystems.**

API standardization is another crucial point. Without clear standards, each integration becomes an isolated effort, increasing the risks of inconsistency and security breaches. With standardization, institutions can create more reliable, scalable, and future-proof digital ecosystems.

These ecosystems strengthen relationships with customers and partners, ensuring seamless interoperability and data protection in every transaction.
