Last Wednesday, May 04th, 2022, the second Cloud Native Sessions meeting took place, a free digital event organized by Vertigo Tecnologia. The digital meeting brought to the debate the importance of connectivity in API management and highlighted the benefits of this practice in large companies.

The presentation featured Min Kyung Chung, Senior Customer Experience Manager at Kong, who explained to the attendees the importance of automating API deployments with APIOPs.

Also present and contributing to the prestige of the event were Bruno Badini and André Fernandes, respectively CEO and CPO of Vertigo Tecnologia.

## The benefits of APIOps were discussed at the event.

During the presentation, the speaker used a fictional case study to address the pain points and situations of companies that do not yet use API integration, demonstrating the business benefits of adopting this practice.

Min Kyung highlighted the speed of automation throughout the delivery chain, demonstrating how APIOps (DevOps practice applied to API integration) helps the various teams within a company, from onboarding and development to operations.

The speaker concludes by stating that the APIOps approach delivers higher quality within companies' development processes, generating automated governance, security, and greater consistency.
