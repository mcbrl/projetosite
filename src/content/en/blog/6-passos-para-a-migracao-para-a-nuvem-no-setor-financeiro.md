## Understanding the Need for Cloud in the Financial Sector

Migrating to the cloud allows financial institutions to improve operational efficiency, reduce costs, and increase security. The adoption of [**cloud solutions**](/en/solutions/cloud_native/) in the financial sector is driven by several factors, including the need to adapt quickly to market changes, manage large volumes of data, and meet customer demands for faster and more reliable services.

## Initial Migration Phase: Understanding the Concept

#### **IT and Application Modernization**

Digital transformation in the financial sector can be divided into two main phases: IT modernization and application modernization. IT modernization involves the transformation of systems, infrastructure, workloads, and processes, while application modernization utilizes cloud-native services and systems to create a more agile and intelligent approach to application development.

#### **Benefits of the Cloud for IT**

The cloud offers an ideal environment for IT modernization, allowing IT teams to focus less on operational demands and more on strategic decisions. The elastic infrastructure provided by cloud providers, such as [**AWS**](/en/AWS-cloud-migration%3A-key-tips-for-success/) , allows for the conversion of fixed IT investments into variable costs, improving cost-effectiveness through pay-as-you-go models.

## Preparedness for Migration: Connecting Migration and Modernization

#### **Establishing the Connection**

Understanding the migration process is crucial to maximizing the benefits of the cloud. Migration should be seen as the starting point for IT modernization. However, the migration and modernization process is not linear and must be strategically planned to minimize downtime and maximize efficiency.

#### **Modernization Strategy**

When planning a migration, it is essential to build a cloud-based operational foundation that helps the team continuously manage, monitor, and optimize cloud resources. Tools and services needed for the migration should be carefully selected, prioritizing the workloads that will benefit most from the cloud infrastructure and cloud-native services.

## Six Steps to Migrating to the Cloud in the Financial Sector

#### **1\. Cloud Mode Selection**

Choosing the appropriate cloud model (public, private, or hybrid) is a critical step. For the financial sector, the private cloud is often the best option due to security and compliance requirements.

#### **2\. Cost Assessment**

Before migrating, a detailed cost analysis is essential. This includes considering the costs of storage, data transfer, and cloud resource utilization.

#### **3\. Guarantee of Data Protection**

Data protection is crucial for cloud usage in the financial sector. Implementing appropriate security measures, such as encryption and two-factor authentication, is fundamental to ensuring data integrity and security.

#### **4\. Phased Migration Strategy**

Planning the migration gradually, starting with less critical applications and data, allows for a smoother transition and reduces the risk of significant disruptions.

#### **5\. Performance Tests**

Performing performance tests before full migration is vital to ensure that the new infrastructure meets the performance and scalability requirements of the financial sector.

#### **6\. Monitoring and Optimization**

Following migration, continuous monitoring and optimization of the cloud-based infrastructure are essential to detect problems and improve the performance of cloud resources in the financial sector.

## Key Technologies Used in Private Cloud in the Financial Sector

#### **Virtualization**

Virtualization allows multiple operating systems and applications to run on a single physical server, resulting in hardware cost savings and greater operational efficiency.

#### **Cloud Storage**

Cloud storage offers significant advantages, including reduced storage costs and improved data security.

#### **Software Defined Networks (SDN)**

SDN enables centralized and programmatic network management, increasing network efficiency and security in the financial sector.

#### **Automation**

Automating tasks reduces the need for human intervention, increasing efficiency and minimizing the occurrence of errors.

#### **Containerization**

Containerization [**allows**](/en/Containerization%3A-6-common-security-mistakes/) applications to run in isolated and portable environments, improving the effectiveness and security of applications.

#### **Safety**

Cloud security technologies, such as encryption and two-factor authentication, are essential to ensure data integrity in the financial sector.

## Challenges and Considerations Before Migrating to the Cloud

#### **Cloud Computing Experience**

Selecting the right partner for cloud migration in the financial sector is crucial. Lack of experience can result in high costs and unsuccessful migrations. It is essential to choose a partner with proven expertise in migrations in the financial sector.

#### **Migration without criteria**

Migrating projects to the cloud without proper assessment and planning can lead to significant problems. It is important to adopt a structured approach and consider alternatives such as deploying cloud-native applications.

#### **Preliminary Application Assessment**

Properly evaluating applications before migration helps determine the most appropriate approach and avoids subsequent performance and compatibility issues.

#### **Planning the “Landing Zone”**

Properly designing and implementing "landing zone" environments is essential to ensure security and compliance during migration.

#### **Identifying Bottlenecks**

Identifying potential bottlenecks after migration is crucial to avoid performance issues and delays. Detailed planning helps map dependencies and ensure a successful migration.

#### **Cost Assessment**

Assessing all costs associated with migration, including indirect and residual costs, is essential to ensure the financial viability of the project.

### **Cloud Native Maturity Level Assessment**

Assessing your company's cloud-native maturity level is an important step in understanding where to begin implementing cloud-native solutions. Assessment tools, such as the Cloud Native Assessment, can provide valuable insights into the current state and next steps for digital transformation.
