**Vertigo Tecnologia and Neo4j have formed a partnership that will offer the market graph-oriented data analysis solutions for the development of high-performance applications.**

**Rio de Janeiro, September 2023**

[Vertigo Tecnologia](/en/) , a Brazilian Digital Transformation consultancy with over two decades of experience, has formed a partnership with [Neo4j](https://neo4j.com/) , a leading company in Graph Databases & Analytics.

Neo4j offers a graph-oriented database that allows you to store and query data in this form. It offers solutions such as the Neo4j Graph Database, Neo4j AuraDB (a database-as-a-service platform), and Neo4j Graph Data Science (a library for analyzing graph data).

With embedded Generative AI and Machine Learning technologies, Neo4j is currently used by 75% of Fortune 500 companies and is widely employed across various sectors for analyzing and discovering insights from relational data.

The partnership between Vertigo Tecnologia and Neo4j aims to empower companies to face the growing challenges in the world of data analytics and application development using the Neo4j Enterprise Version offering.

Therefore, the partnership between the companies aims to offer the market efficient and high-performance applications, regardless of the size or complexity of the data involved.

*“The partnership with Neo4j adds data science and analytics expertise to our portfolio. Our collaboration aims to provide clients with a powerful tool to drive reflection and strategic decision-making. Together, we are ready to face challenges and create high-value opportunities.”* **Bruno Badini – CEO of Vertigo Tecnologia**

*“The partnership with Vertigo will provide companies in various segments with access to real-time transaction processing using Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, as well as intuitive data visualization, enabling them to develop anti-fraud solutions, supply chain management, 360-degree customer views, and much more, based on an understanding of connected data and its context,”* comments **André Serpa, Vice President of Latin America at Neo4j**.

The collaboration between [Vertigo and Neo4j](/en/partnerships/neo4j/) represents an important step for companies seeking a competitive advantage through deep link analysis of their data and the development of high-performance applications.

### About Neo4j

Neo4j, creator and leader in Database Graphs & Analytics, helps organizations find hidden relationships and patterns in billions of data connections in a deep, easy, and fast way. Customers leverage the structure of their connected data to uncover new ways to solve their most pressing business problems, from fraud detection, 360 customer experience, knowledge graphs, supply chain management, personalization, IoT, network management, and much more – even as their data grows.
