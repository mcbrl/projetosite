The Kong Summit 2021 took place on September 28th and 29th. The digital event, sponsored by Vertigo Tecnologia, brought together technology professionals from various global companies and promoted discussion on the tools needed to engage organizations in the digital transformation process, showcasing the innovations that are driving the global market.

Professionals from AWS, American Airlines, Vanguard, Lytx, National Australia Bank, and many other companies spoke about technology trends and how smart solutions are applied across various sectors.

It was the perfect opportunity to exchange experiences and expand networking through virtual booths and with the staff of each sponsor.

Vertigo made its presence felt with a virtual booth, where it showcased its entire portfolio through marketing materials, as well as fostering strong interaction with event participants, answering questions and presenting solutions via chat.

## Lectures on various technological solutions were the focus of the event.

**Day 1 –** The first day of the event was marked by talks on microservices security, customer experience with cloud-native standards using EKS, GitOps and APIOps, unification of commerce APIs with Kong solutions, and other topics within the universe of digital transformation.

**Day 2 –** The second day featured talks on scalable API delivery with APIOps and Kong at Lombard Odier. The virtual sessions covered Kong's strategies for migrating legacy systems to a modern security concept.

The lectures were led by technology professionals from large companies and included audience interaction through virtual booths.
