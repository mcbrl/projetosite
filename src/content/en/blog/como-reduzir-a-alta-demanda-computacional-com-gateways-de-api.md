In today's landscape, companies are constantly seeking ways to optimize their processes and reduce costs without sacrificing the quality of their services and products. To achieve this, they often need technological solutions that automate tasks and scale their business. In this context, API gateways have become increasingly popular among companies, especially those with systems and applications that communicate with other external systems.

It is important to highlight that high computational demand can bring numerous problems, among them: increased errors, slow task execution, difficulties in customer service, service unavailability, and even customer loss. Furthermore, increased demand can lead to higher infrastructure costs, as it may be necessary to invest in more servers, storage capacity, and processing power.

Another example of how high computational demand can negatively affect system performance and availability is when an API receives a very large number of requests in a short period of time. When the infrastructure is unable to support the demand, the API can become unstable, leading to system failures and outages. This can cause financial losses, loss of credibility, and even damage the company's reputation.

The solution to this problem is not simply to increase the processing capacity of the infrastructure. This can generate excessive and unnecessary costs for the company. It is important to seek more efficient alternatives that can be scaled according to demand.

A viable solution to reduce the high computational demand on APIs is the use of API gateways. These tools act as an intermediary layer between user requests and the systems that process those requests. This allows requests to be balanced and distributed more efficiently, avoiding system overload and ensuring better performance.

In this article, we will discuss the impacts of high computational demand on companies' IT operations, as well as the advantages that API gateways can offer when used correctly.

## Impacts of high computing demand on your IT operation.

High computational demand can significantly impact a company's IT operations. This is because, in many cases, the existing infrastructure is insufficient to support the large volume of requests the company receives. As a result, system performance can be affected, leading to slowdowns, service interruptions, and, in extreme cases, even system unavailability.

Furthermore, high computing demand can significantly increase a company's infrastructure costs, as it requires investment in more robust equipment and scalability solutions. This can be especially detrimental to smaller companies, which may struggle to bear these additional costs.

In short, the impacts of high computational demand on your IT operations can result in:

**Reduced performance:** When computational demand is high, there may be a reduction in system performance, making them slower and less responsive. This can negatively affect user experience and the efficiency of the IT team. A study by the performance monitoring company AppDynamics found that slow performance can cause a 25% drop in customer satisfaction and a loss of up to US$2,5 million in annual revenue for a typical company.

**Network congestion:** High computational demand can cause network congestion, making it difficult for users to access systems and services. This can affect team productivity and cause user frustration. IDC research also revealed that excessive workload can lead to a productivity loss of up to 60%. In numbers, this means that an employee who normally takes one hour to complete a task may take up to 1,5 hours to complete it when systems are overloaded.

**Increased costs:** As computing demand increases, it may be necessary to acquire more hardware and software resources to meet the demand. This can increase IT operating costs, including the acquisition of new equipment, software licenses, and infrastructure expansion. According to a study by the consulting firm Gartner, a lack of processing capacity can lead to a 10% to 20% increase in IT costs. Furthermore, IDC found that companies experiencing excessive workload on their IT systems can face a 37% increase in operating costs.

**System overload:** When computing demand is too high, systems can become overloaded, leading to failures and service interruptions. This can result in data loss, downtime, and user dissatisfaction. According to PagerDuty's State of IT Operations report, 54% of companies reported more than five unscheduled outages in the last 12 months. Furthermore, an IDC study found that companies lose an average of $100.000 per hour of system downtime.
Security risk: When computing demand is high, systems can become more vulnerable to cyberattacks, especially if the IT team cannot keep up with the demand and implement adequate security measures. According to ISACA's State of Cybersecurity report, 50% of companies reported an increase in cyberattacks during the pandemic. Furthermore, a study by the Ponemon Institute found that the average cost of a single data breach is US$3,86 million.
Need for scalability: To meet high computational demand, it may be necessary to implement scalability solutions to ensure that systems can handle traffic and load spikes. This may require investments in scalability technologies such as cloud and virtualization.

Based on this data, we can conclude that this type of problem negatively affects the user experience, which can lead to decreased customer satisfaction and even lost revenue. This is because, in many cases, high computational demand can generate slow response times or errors in task execution, which can frustrate the user.

Process automation is a major concern for companies that want to remain competitive in the market. With this implementation, it's possible to automate various processes, from communication between different systems to data management and report creation. This allows the company's IT team to focus on more strategic and important tasks for the business, increasing efficiency and reducing the time spent on operational activities.

Another major advantage of using API gateways is the ability to scale the business more efficiently. By implementing this tool, the company can easily integrate new applications and services into its ecosystem, allowing the IT team to be more agile in creating solutions to meet business demands. Its use also enables the creation of new partnerships and integrations with other companies, expanding business opportunities and generating new revenue streams.
