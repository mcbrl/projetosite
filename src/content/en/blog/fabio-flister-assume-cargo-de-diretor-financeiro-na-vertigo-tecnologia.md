## Internal movement generates high expectations for the next 3 years.

After 2 years as Financial and Administrative Manager at [Vertigo Tecnologia](/en/Vertigo-Liferay-Platinum/) , Fábio Flister assumes the important role of Financial Director of the company.

With a degree in Business Administration and Finance from UERJ, Fábio Flister has extensive professional experience, having worked at companies such as IRB – Brasil Resseguros, HP, and Itautec, before joining our team.

## Chief Financial Officer faces new challenges at Vertigo.

Among future challenges, the new Chief Financial Officer will be responsible for the continuous improvement of corporate governance, treasury, and internal controls. Furthermore, they will lead new strategic projects aimed at the company's growth, impacting all levels (operational, tactical, and strategic) of Vertigo.

Overwhelmed with emotion and proud of the journey so far, Flister expressed his gratitude: *“I want to express my immense gratitude to the FIN/ADM/DP team. They are always motivated, and our relationships are always filled with goodwill. Let's continue together on this journey of challenges and knowledge. Thank you very much!”*

## Vertigo Tecnologia's CEO projects an increase in administrative and operational capacity.

According to our CEO, Bruno Badini, the move is surrounded by great expectations and demonstrates a strong investment in the professionalization of internal leadership. *"The expectation is to prepare Vertigo for the next 3-year growth cycle, increasing our operational, administrative, and financial capacity, both in Brazil and abroad* ," he reveals.

## Vertigo believes that human capital is the key to success.

Stories like Fábio Flister's are commonplace at Vertigo and confirm that human capital is the key to the company's success. We invest in and support our employees in their professional development, because opportunities arise for everyone who is committed and dedicated to achieving the company's goals.

We are a team that values ​​human relationships and believes in the potential of our employees.
