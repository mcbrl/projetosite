The [financial sector](/en/Cloud-native-solutions-for-the-financial-sector%3A-the-challenges-of-digital-security./) is known for being a major driver of intelligent and innovative solutions in the market, prioritizing the digital experience and security of customer information through various APIs. Among the latest developments, services such as Pix and Open Finance stand out, expanding the range of financial transaction possibilities for customers.

With the advancement of digital transformation in the sector, customers are becoming increasingly integrated with technologies and migrating to digital financial services in search of convenience, but without losing focus on security.

Given this scenario, what would be the main reasons for using APIs within the financial sector?

We prepared this article to address 5 reasons why your financial services company should implement solutions with API integration.

Good reading.

## Use APIs for greater security.

Financial services need to guarantee the complete security of their clients' data, since any failure could lead to the misappropriation of large sums of money and a considerable financial loss.

Cases like that of Banco Pan, which confirmed the leak of customer [data , raise a red flag, urging the sector to continue developing new digital security strategies.](https://g1.globo.com/economia/noticia/2022/04/15/banco-pan-confirma-vazamento-de-dados-de-clientes-mas-nao-informa-quantos-foram-expostos.ghtml)

Therefore, data sharing through APIs is carried out using very specific security protocols.

The security protocols used by APIs limit application communication, providing only the access necessary to perform the task.

Security managers are able to gain full access and control over information and create a secure, encrypted environment.

The advancement of technology and digital transformation have brought possibilities for improving DevSecOps strategies, with some APIs developed exclusively focused on digital security.

## Create new business opportunities.

Through API integration, it's possible to expand your company's solutions in the financial sector to a wider range of services.

It is possible to implement updates for various payments and transactions, providing options that allow customers to make investments in an easy and secure way.

Issuing payment slips, loan options, insurance integration, and other service options can be implemented through APIs.

Therefore, integrating APIs allows companies to have more opportunities to acquire customers, thus enabling them to compete with rivals or perhaps even stand out in the market through exclusive services developed on demand.

## Use APIOps to improve the quality and lifespan of APIs.

Through an implementation that combines concepts from GitOps and [DevOps culture](/en/DevOps-in-the-financial-sector%3A-an-opportunity-to-make-the-market-more-competitive./) , [APIOps](/en/Cloud-Native-Sessions%3A-Connectivity-in-API-Management-is-the-topic-of-the-second-meeting./) is able to drive the entire lifecycle chain in the development, design, and deployment of APIs to ensure the quality of project specifications.

Through APIOps, it's possible to implement policies for managing APIs while simultaneously maintaining communication with the rest of the team, sharing information about all the changes that have been implemented.

In this way, the API development cycle becomes more consistent and efficient for the teams, thus increasing performance for any updates and also improving the quality of deliverables.

## Gain greater efficiency in financial services and applications.

The use of APIs allows for the integration of company operations; therefore, various routine tasks that are performed manually can be automated and updated as needed, thus reducing the time spent on unnecessary actions.

Implementing API integration in the financial sector allows for greater application efficiency and also reduces task execution time.

Through this time saving, teams can focus their efforts on other fronts to plan new business strategies or improve their own applications, thus generating a routine of improvement through agile methodologies.

## Optimizing communication

Through API integration, it's possible to improve internal communication between departments, fostering a DevOps culture, and also enhance the company's customer service channels.

Through a continuous workflow, companies can achieve greater agility in accessing information and implementing solutions quickly.

Furthermore, APIs are capable of promoting improved communication between people, not only by transforming organizational culture, but also through the ability to generate new service channels.

Through customer service channels, such as [chatbots](/en/Does-your-company-already-use-chatbots-Check-out-5-sectors-that-benefit-from-them./) , API integration allows companies in the financial sector to develop an omnichannel customer service strategy.
