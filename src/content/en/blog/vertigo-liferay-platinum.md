Vertigo Tecnologia is once again proving that Brazilians also know how to lead high-performance Digital Transformation projects. We were the only Brazilian company recognized as a **Platinum Partner.** This is the highest title in **Liferay's partner hierarchy,** alongside the world's largest IT consulting firms**.**

“We are the oldest **Liferay partner in Latin America, and we are also the company with the most success stories in Brazil. So we are very proud of this recognition,” says Bruno Badini, CEO of Vertigo.**

Liferay is a leading Digital Experience Platform (DXP) and has been a Leader in Gartner's Horizontal Portals quadrant for seven consecutive years **.** *Liferay* DXP **is** the ideal solution for omnichannel projects, delivering integrated solutions for Portal, Intranet, Mobile, and numerous functionalities that enable seamless integration of the entire customer journey.

A good example of the platform's robustness is the globally acclaimed case... [Carioca Digital](/en/Vertigo--a-liferay-from-Rio-de-Janeiro--digital./)In this project, Vertigo developed an online service and citizen engagement platform for the City of Rio de Janeiro. The project was disruptive, helping more than 6 million residents of Rio access over 21 city services in a much simpler, faster, and safer way.
