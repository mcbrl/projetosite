Implementing cloud computing solutions is an important step in a digital transformation strategy. By using AWS, companies have access to a widely used service that is synonymous with efficiency.

Therefore, when IT leaders take the first step toward adopting [cloud computing solutions , they need to plan the move with well-defined](/en/Cloud-native-solutions-for-the-financial-sector%3A-the-challenges-of-digital-security./) [DevSecOps](/en/devsecops-5-steps-to-implement/) strategies to avoid compromising applications in the future.

Adopting AWS represents choosing a service that stands out in the market, but it's worth mentioning that some precautions are necessary to use the solution safely and extract the best results for your company.

We've prepared 5 tips to help you increase security in your company's AWS environment. Check out our tips and learn how to protect yourself. Happy reading!

## The importance of adopting a solid digital security strategy.

Following the trend of adopting cloud solutions, the reality is that many companies, as they modernize their infrastructure, also become vulnerable. This scenario is evidenced by the fact that most [hacker attacks](https://itforum.com.br/noticias/maior-parte-de-ataques-de-hackers-vieram-de-nuvem-no-ano-passado/) in 2021 came through vulnerabilities in the cloud.

This type of action is expected; after all, being attacked almost always requires being connected to a network.

Despite the alarming scenario, it's worth mentioning that the [benefits of adopting a cloud infrastructure](/en/Cloud-computing%3A-5-reasons-to-use-AWS-in-your-company./) far outweigh the risks, thus justifying the investment in cloud-native solutions.

AWS provides various ways for your company to reap the benefits of a cloud solution while protecting itself from the risks inherent in taking the step toward digital transformation.

Check out some tips to increase the security level of your Amazon Web Services environment.

## Manage your AWS access credentials.

It is essential to have a well-defined security policy for your AWS environment, because the platform does not allow users to recover lost passwords.

or stolen, since many data breaches occur in password recovery cases.

Therefore, if you are the AWS account administrator, keep your login credentials in a secure location. It is recommended that you configure an IAM user with administrator permissions, so the root user can securely perform routine tasks while restricting access to other users.

## Define an automated backup strategy for your cloud environment.

When a database is compromised, access to information often becomes unavailable, and operations may be halted for an indefinite period, causing significant financial impact and jeopardizing your business.

Therefore, defining a backup strategy for your AWS environment is essential so that you have control of the situation and, in case of any problem, are able to restore the environment in its entirety.

To help with this movement, Amazon itself offers a service to assist in the backup process, [AWS Backup](https://aws.amazon.com/pt/backup/) , a managed and centralized platform for backing up various AWS services, such as Amazon EBS, EC2, EFS, FSx, RDS, DynamoDB, and Storage Gateway volumes.

## Keep your AWS account contacts up to date.

Any direct contact with the Amazon Web Services team is made through the information defined within the AWS platform's own management console.

As indicated by [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/pt/blogs/aws-brasil/top-10-dicas-de-seguranca-para-aperfeicoar-na-sua-conta-aws/) , all email addresses should be configured to be delivered to an "alias" or mailing list, so that AWS alert and contact messages are delivered to more than one user.

## Implement DevSecOps and track the development lifecycle.

Adopting a DevSecOps culture and monitoring the entire application development process, including the design of security strategies, is essential for achieving better results with your AWS environment.

It's worth mentioning that each employee within the IT team has a role to play in helping the company achieve the best results, as well as being committed to ensuring that security policies are respected.

A collaborative culture is essential for good safety practices to be disseminated and implemented by each team member, regardless of hierarchical level or role, all within their respective areas of expertise.
