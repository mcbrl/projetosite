When we talk about cloud computing, Amazon Web Services (AWS) stands out among various solutions for the cloud computing segment.

Launched in 2016, [AWS is the leader in Gartner's 2021 Magic Quadrant](https://aws.amazon.com/pt/resources/analyst-reports/gartner-mq-cips-2021/?pg=WIAWS) as a specialist technology consulting company. Amazon's cloud solution is the most widely adopted in the world and offers hundreds of services to data centers, private companies in the insurance, financial, and public sectors, among others.

Using AWS provides service optimization through a cutting-edge and consistent solution, which also generates cost reductions that can be allocated to other areas to optimize the digital transformation journey.

Check out the article we've prepared and see 5 reasons to start using AWS in your company.

## Why use AWS as a solution for businesses?

Adopting cutting-edge solutions to implement cloud computing within your company is a fundamental strategy for maturing the digital transformation process.

Along these lines, AWS has been recognized as a leader in cloud computing solutions by Gartner and stands out as a benchmark in this segment. 

Here are 5 reasons to implement AWS and drive digital transformation within your company: 

### Increase security in application development.

The [AWS solution](/en/partnerships/aws/) stands out with its best quality practices in its products, featuring constant security updates, which is a key factor for companies concerned with digital security in their operations.

Therefore, Amazon Web Services provides 24-hour support from experts and, in addition, also includes a firewall that limits access and increases flow control, offers multi-factor authentication, and encrypted data storage.

### Utilize hybrid cloud computing services.

The hybrid cloud is a solution that provides greater flexibility for businesses, in addition to solving digital security and privacy issues.

A [hybrid cloud](/en/Cloud-native%3A-do-you-know-the-difference-between-the-types-of-clouds/) is the combination of more than one public or private cloud, providing unified management, orchestration, integration, and portability of workloads.

By using AWS, companies have the flexibility to develop security policies, blending local and remote storage.

### Gain scalability in your projects.

Based on the practice of using a hybrid cloud, the scalability of AWS stands out.

Through customized structures, it's possible to optimize space and mitigate extra expenses within companies, bringing the business closer to the ideal for each corporation's reality.

Through solutions that champion scalability, it's possible to achieve positive results through solid and sustainable strategies.

### Improve the management of financial resources in IT projects.

When scalability is achieved within cloud computing projects, there is consequently better management of financial resources within the company.

Therefore, by using Amazon Web Services, it will be possible to open up space for other areas of action in the field of digital transformation, because with balanced resource management, it is possible to invest in new technology projects.

Leaders who invest in [digital solutions](/en/solutions/) that are benchmarks of quality and effectiveness are able to achieve better results across the board.

### Enjoy greater flexibility with AWS.

A major advantage of AWS is the flexibility it offers your company. By implementing Amazon's solution, you can maintain some programming practices and systems already in use by your teams, and there will also be room to migrate to other solutions if that's better for your project.

AWS provides a highly user-friendly and flexible IT infrastructure, allowing it to be used in conjunction with other platforms according to your needs. 

Furthermore, Amazon's solution enables your IT team to migrate existing SOA (Service-Oriented Architecture)-based solutions to the cloud, further enhancing scalability.
