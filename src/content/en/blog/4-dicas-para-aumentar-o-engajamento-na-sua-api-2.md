Technological development is enabling the integration of a multitude of systems that previously seemed unlikely to connect. One of the catalysts for this movement is **APIs** and the strategies implemented with them.

#### **|** [*Click here if you don't already know about APIs.*](/en/What-is-API-Understand-it-in-a-simple-way./)

With this advancement, innovations cease to be merely interesting and become necessary, as modernity drives new business models.

Developers and users seek simple solutions that are user-friendly and can be easily integrated into different platforms. This applies to online services such as [HubSpot](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/overview) , [PagSeguro](https://pagseguro.uol.com.br/v2/guia-de-integracao/api-de-notificacoes.html#!rmcl) , and [Google Maps](https://developers.google.com/maps/) ; banks; and complex systems responsible for vital business tasks, such as CRMs.

Therefore, it's important that API development is optimized so that integration with new systems happens without major problems. Here at Vertigo Tecnologia, for example, we use APIs to improve the quality of **KM de Vantagens (a loyalty program** ). The results were excellent.

You already fully understand how important APIs are to your business. For this reason, we've gathered 4 great tips in this post to improve your API creation and facilitate your business growth. Read on!

## **Supercharging your APIs**

Before action comes planning. A good study avoids rework and makes your procedure more precise. Therefore, design and decide how your API will handle calls, how you will document the functionalities, and define the limitations and scope of use. These are crucial factors to consider during the creation process.

Remember: Just as important as creating your **API is monitoring what's happening with it** . If the documentation is inconsistent, strange, or vague, you risk confusing users and even affecting your existing or potential user base.

Let's explain the concepts in more detail. Check it out below.

### **1\. Create good documentation.**

An important factor in developing a good API is to facilitate the user experience – which in this case also includes the developer – so that they can take advantage of the benefits offered by your API. That's why documentation is essential. It's what shows how the functions and responses to your API calls work.

At this stage, you can even create simple tutorials so that the developer can begin to better understand what they can achieve with what you are offering them. Create clear documentation, see how other companies are creating this documentation, be transparent, and use simple communication.

Just as important as having clear documentation is testing to verify that everything is correct. It may seem obvious, but it doesn't always happen. Another tip is to test with other programmers to ensure that the documentation actually provides satisfactory information. This encourages the use of the API and helps the platform grow.

### **2\. Be Consistent**

Consistency in development is what will allow programmers to intuitively understand functionalities whenever changes or improvements occur. This is more than just an important aspect for assisting your API user;

Just like *software* , an API needs to be updated to ensure it remains secure and stable. Large companies, such as Facebook and Apple, have a habit of constantly updating their APIs. Sometimes this causes a major headache for developers trying to figure out how to keep their platforms functioning the same way.

That's why consistency is important! Always make improvements to your API while maintaining a language that follows the same standard and works intuitively. This way, you prevent other programmers from having difficulties maintaining its use and abandoning integration with your platform.

### **3\. Create understandable error messages.**

During the integration of different platforms using your API, some errors are likely to occur. This type of problem is normal and can be easily resolved if the developer can identify the problem and work to fix it. However, for this to be possible, you need to help them with explanatory error messages or code references in your documentation.

Simply creating an "ERROR" or "FATAL ERROR" response during testing will take up a significant amount of programmers' time, preventing them from finding specific problems by analyzing the code. Map the main errors and make this visible in your documentation.

### **4\. Plan for the long term.**

It's noticeable that some APIs are robust and perfectly meet the immediate needs of a service or system, even without long-term planning. It's necessary to plan for evolution, to design something rigorous right from the start. This might inhibit the potential growth of that API, but having a roadmap can give it a much longer lifespan.

When developing your API, it's essential to analyze the current capabilities of your platform and understand the different integration methods available today. This allows you to meet demands quickly and efficiently. It's also important to think about the future!

Ask yourself: What are your plans for improving your platform? Do you plan to offer new features? What will you need to offer in the future?

Knowing this, it's possible to develop your API in an optimized way so that it will be easier to incorporate new elements in the future, and to facilitate the work of developers when new features are made available.

## **CONCLUSION**

To achieve a high initial adoption rate for your API, it's essential to make it user-friendly, both initially and in the future. Therefore, do everything you can to ensure developers can easily understand your API and create solutions without significant problems.

By following these steps and offering consistent, high-quality support, your API will become the most widely adopted and loved. Developers will be able to easily create new solutions, and your platform will be used more and more.
