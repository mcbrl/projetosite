In the Portuguese technology market, cloud migration has become a crucial topic for companies seeking efficiency, scalability, and reduced operating costs.

The possibility of leveraging the benefits of the cloud, such as elasticity, agility, and ease of management, has attracted the attention of IT managers and leaders. However, even with the growing recognition of these benefits, many organizations still resist this transition due to fears and doubts about the impacts and the real value of this change.

Maintaining on-premise infrastructure, in an increasingly competitive market environment, can limit companies' ability to innovate and keep pace with the rapid rate of technological change. With the growing demand for agile digital solutions, resisting migration to the cloud can put companies at a disadvantage compared to competitors who have already adopted this approach.

Furthermore, a lack of technological updates can create security vulnerabilities, increasing the risk of cyberattacks and compromise of sensitive data. Legacy applications may not be prepared to face current security challenges, leaving companies exposed to potential threats.

Another factor to consider is scalability. With resistance to migration, companies may miss the opportunity to scale their resources according to real-time business demands. The cloud offers the flexibility to adjust processing, storage, and network capacity as needed, allowing companies to handle traffic spikes or seasonal demands without having to invest in additional on-premise infrastructure.

Faced with these challenges, organizations need to carefully evaluate their technology strategies and consider the impacts of resistance to cloud migration on their operations and results. Adopting a Cloud Native approach can be a solution to overcome these challenges, allowing companies to create scalable, resilient, and highly available applications aligned with current and future business needs.

According to the consulting firm Gartner, more than 70% of organizations have already migrated, even if only partially, their infrastructure to the cloud. This trend is expected to gain even more momentum in the coming years.

Statistics clearly indicate the growing adoption of cloud migration as an imperative for modern businesses. However, it is essential that organizations consider not only the financial and technological aspects, but also the impacts on applications beyond the operational level. Compatibility with technological innovations, agility, and cybersecurity are fundamental elements that can be leveraged by adopting a Cloud Native approach.

By understanding how this strategy can solve the challenges faced by companies, they will be better prepared to fully leverage the potential of cloud computing and secure a leading position in the constantly evolving technology market.

## Ignoring cloud migration can increase costs.

By resisting cloud migration, companies may face significant impacts on their operational costs. The option of maintaining on-premise infrastructure can result in high expenses for server maintenance, data centers, and specialized teams. This traditional approach becomes a financial burden, requiring investments in hardware, software, and frequent upgrades, limiting the ability to invest in innovation and new initiatives.

Furthermore, this resistance results in wasted resources, since cloud computing offers the flexibility to adjust capacity according to real-time business demands. The inability to scale dynamically can lead to oversized or underutilized infrastructures, generating unnecessary costs. While the cloud allows for more efficient use of resources, maintaining on-premises infrastructures can lead to underutilization of servers and other assets, representing additional expense for the company.

The agility provided by cloud computing is another essential factor to consider. In the case of resistance, companies lose the opportunity to access computing resources quickly and on demand, delaying important projects and strategic decisions. The ability to access resources immediately is valuable in a competitive business environment, allowing companies to adapt quickly to market changes and secure their competitive advantage.

Not migrating can also lead to security and compliance risks. Cloud service providers invest heavily in security measures to protect their customers' data, which can be more difficult to achieve with on-premise infrastructures. Remaining in on-premise environments leaves companies more susceptible to security breaches and compliance issues, resulting in additional costs related to data recovery and reputational damage.

Embracing cloud migration is not just a technological choice, but also a strategic decision to boost the competitiveness and innovation of companies. By resisting change, organizations risk becoming stuck in an obsolete technological environment, unable to keep up with market demands and customer expectations. The agility and flexibility offered by the cloud are essential to ensure the relevance of companies in a constantly transforming business landscape.

In other words, resisting cloud migration means higher operating costs, wasted resources, lack of agility, and greater vulnerability to security risks.

## What are the impacts on applications beyond the operational ones?

Companies that resist migrating to the cloud may face significant challenges with their legacy applications and infrastructure. According to recent research from Gartner, worldwide end-user spending on public cloud services is projected to grow 20,7% in 2023, reaching a total of $591,8 billion. This trend reflects the continued adoption of cloud computing as a bulwark of security and innovation amidst economic uncertainty.

All segments of the cloud market are projected to show growth in 2023, driven by the continued adoption of cloud computing as a fundamental platform for the digital transformation of businesses.

According to projections, Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) will see the highest growth in end-user spending in 2023, at a rate of 30,9%. Platform as a Service (PaaS) is expected to follow suit, with a projected growth of 24,1% over the same period. These trends highlight the growing relevance of cloud solutions for business strategies, providing the agility, scalability, and efficiency needed to address potential challenges.

By not taking advantage of the benefits of the cloud, companies can become stuck with on-premise infrastructure that is not compatible with the scalability and technological innovations needed to keep pace with the rapid changes in the market, resulting in slow applications, poor performance, and unsatisfactory response times, harming the user experience and internal productivity.

The data reinforces the importance of adopting agile and scalable cloud solutions to ensure the necessary speed in times of constant change. With the cloud, many service providers also offer a broad set of technologies and policies to strengthen security ecosystems against potential threats, allowing companies to save substantial amounts of money on data backups and disaster recovery.

Therefore, it is essential that companies consider the negative impacts of resisting cloud migration, in addition to the opportunities for operational optimization. By embracing cloud solutions such as Cloud Native, organizations can ensure compatibility with scalability and technological innovations, while strengthening their cybersecurity posture, guaranteeing a more promising and efficient future for their businesses.

## How can you tell if Cloud Native solves your problem?

Cloud Native, with its agile and innovative approach, has emerged as a highly advantageous solution for companies seeking to unlock the full potential of cloud computing. By adopting it, organizations can enjoy a range of significant benefits that go beyond simply migrating to the cloud.

One of the main advantages is scalability, allowing applications to grow and adapt dynamically to business demands. The elasticity provided by the Cloud Native approach ensures that computing resources are allocated efficiently, avoiding waste and guaranteeing optimized performance at any scale.

Driving innovation and development speed, Cloud Native enables companies to deliver new features and updates quickly and efficiently. The modular, container-based architecture facilitates continuous deployment and integration of new technologies, making applications more agile and flexible to keep pace with market changes.

Resilience is also a key characteristic of Cloud Native. By designing fault-tolerant applications, it ensures high availability and reliability, minimizing the impact of potential outages and guaranteeing business continuity even in adverse situations.

This approach fosters a culture of collaboration and innovation within IT teams. By adopting DevOps practices and automation, companies can break down barriers between development and operations, accelerating the development cycle and improving operational efficiency.

Not to mention the long-term cost savings mentioned earlier. While initial adoption of Cloud Native may require investments in training and infrastructure modernization, the long-term benefits are significant. Operational efficiency, reduced waste, and optimized use of computing resources result in operational cost savings and a higher return on investment.
