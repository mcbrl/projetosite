We are partners with [Liferay](/en/partnerships/Liferay?utm_source=BlogVertigo&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=LiferayGartner) And we are pleased to announce that for the sixth consecutive year, the [Liferay](/en/partnerships/Liferay?utm_source=BlogVertigo&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=LiferayGartner) she was appointed leader within the [Gartner's 2015 Magic Quadrant for Horizontal Portals](https://www.liferay.com/pt/about-us/awards/gartnermq-portals) which is a consulting firm respected worldwide. A [Liferay](/en/partnerships/Liferay?utm_source=BlogVertigo&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=LiferayGartner) It is the only open-source company to appear in the top quadrant, both for vision and ability to execute.
Gartner reports that a transformation is underway toward horizontal portals; these types of systems are now being designed to be the central access point for users' digital experiences, but in a personalized way. Liferay continues to build and refine... [Liferay Portal](/en/partnerships/Liferay?utm_source=BlogVertigo&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=LiferayGartner), which is their main product, as the intention is to provide a scalable, enterprise-ready platform for creating personalized user experiences.
