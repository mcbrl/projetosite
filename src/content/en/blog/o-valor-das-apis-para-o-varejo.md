We live in a digital reality where APIs are gaining prominence. They are becoming increasingly important within the new Digital Economy, especially for those who want to start a business and bring innovation to companies and markets. In this content, we will talk about API strategy for Retail. Keep reading to learn more!

In today's world, APIs are like Lego pieces that adapt to digital structures of different natures, in different markets and at different times!

Thanks to an API like [Google Maps](https://developers.google.com/maps/?hl=pt-br) , for example, transportation services have undergone a true revolution, even allowing for the later emergence of [Uber](https://developer.uber.com/) . Thanks to location APIs, customers can find products and services that are closest to them. But how can these APIs generate transformations in retail?

The adoption and use of APIs has become increasingly common in companies, especially in organizations operating in the Business-to-Business (B2B) market. They have become increasingly important because they allow new applications to connect or even log in using social networking services such as Facebook, Twitter, and Google. Companies like Buscapé, Google Shopping, and Decolar only exist because of APIs. The fact is that there are many advantages to using APIs.

That's the topic of today's post, and we're going to look a little more at their innovation in the retail sector.

## New business models

APIs enable the creation of new business models and new ways to monetize a product or service. One model that has attracted considerable attention in Brazil is that of Lomadee. Lomadee is a marketing company belonging to the Buscapé Company group, which brings together the largest e-commerce companies in Brazil, such as Americanas, Submarino, Magazine Luiza, Nike, Walmart, Dafiti, Extra, FastShop, and Fnac—to name just a few. Through its APIs, it allows any company to sell its clients' products, receiving a commission for each sale made.

Lomadee's model has enabled the emergence of multi-million dollar companies such as Baixou, SaveMe, Busca Desconto, and Desconteiro. It's important to remember that these companies don't need to deal with inventory, payments, or logistics. Everything is processed by the suppliers, who, through Lomadee, know which companies are generating sales of their products.

## **APIs in online retail**

Online retail has been one of the leading examples of API usage in Brazil and worldwide. It's no coincidence that e-commerce providers are increasingly integrating third-party systems into their solutions. There's no need to create a new payment system; simply integrate one of the existing solutions, such as [PayPal](https://developer.paypal.com/) , [PagSeguro](https://dev.pagseguro.uol.com.br/modulos) , and [Moip,](http://dev.moip.com.br/) among dozens of others. There's also no need to create a system for calculating shipping costs and tracking product logistics; the Brazilian postal service ( [Correios)](https://www.correios.com.br/para-voce/correios-de-a-a-z/sigep-web-gerenciador-de-postagens-dos-correios#tab-1) already provides an API that, when integrated with your solution, does this for you.

Furthermore, it's possible to integrate a multitude of applications into your platform, such as management systems – [TinyERP](https://tiny.com.br/info/api-desenvolvedores) – visitor analytics like [Google Analytics](https://developers.google.com/analytics/?hl=pt-br) , competitor monitoring like [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/api-documentation/) , and even reward and scoring systems like WoowUp. By using APIs to integrate third-party services, vendors gain agility, shorten their product development cycle, and reduce costs, since they don't need to "reinvent the wheel."

The truth is that retail is one of the commercial sectors that most needs to adapt to changes in consumer behavior for various reasons, especially when it comes to purchasing behavior. It is precisely in this sector that contact with the end consumer has a direct impact on almost the entire production chain, and these retail companies already know this!

![](/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/submarino_Marketplace.png)

## Marketplace

Taking advantage of the fact that no one is ever completely disconnected from their smartphone, many companies are investing in e-commerce initiatives, especially focusing on [mobile](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/o-que-voce-precisa-saber-sobre-estrategia-mobile/) and the opening of Marketplaces as a way to innovate and guarantee more sales due to the ease and convenience provided for new partners and the end consumer. Even more traditional companies are entering this new sales logic, and APIs are the main solution for structuring these changes. Many companies are adopting the strategy of [first creating the API and then worrying about the device](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/primeiro-a-api-e-depois-o-device/).

Today, more and more companies, such as startups, large e-commerce businesses, and fintech companies, are emerging or reinventing their businesses thanks to APIs. This is because APIs allow for flexibility and adaptation to various types of businesses.

Just to give you an idea, companies like [Cnova](https://desenvolvedores.cnova.com/api-portal/) , [B2W,](https://api-sandbox.bonmarketplace.com.br/docs/) and Magazine Luiza have been investing resources since 2010 to improve their digital channels through APIs. Thanks to this movement, these retail giants have been developing various innovation processes that allow for savings in resources and time, as well as ensuring greater operational versatility. It's worth remembering that all these companies are increasingly improving their own marketplaces.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/API_Cnova.jpg)

Retailers are increasingly aware that the migration to e-commerce, including the mobile environment, is irreversible. However, more than just focusing on digitizing stores, it's necessary to develop an attractive and engaging user experience, and [monitoring that experience](https://hml.blog.vertigo.com.br/por-que-devo-monitorar-a-performance-online-do-meu-negocio/) is extremely important. This is perhaps the biggest challenge for those working to develop new services using APIs.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/API_B2W.jpg)

## Services and tools

It's important to highlight that this reinvention of retail isn't happening only in product retail. In fact, companies in the financial sector are also undergoing this restructuring driven by the *API-First concept.* Banco Original, a retail bank, is a good example of a company investing heavily in this IT area. Other banks and investors are following the same path. It's worthwhile to research and learn more.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/API_Banco-Original.jpg)

Other tools are emerging during this revolution that **Fintechs** are causing in the market. New companies are appearing, and they are all using APIs as the basis for creating their business models.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/API_SmartBot.jpg)

## **Technology lovers**

For the most tech-savvy, APIs have become the "apple of their eye." This concept is enabling the launch of countless software programs and applications, opening up new avenues in the market.

APIs allow applications to communicate with each other without user knowledge or intervention. They have become a secure way to interact, enabling the emergence of many new businesses, as well as the improvement of existing services. Yes, it is possible to envision a new business model for your current business through APIs.
