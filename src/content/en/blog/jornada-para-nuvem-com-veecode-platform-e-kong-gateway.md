In this context, the use of APIs (Application Programming Interfaces) becomes essential for the integration and automation of processes. ## **The Power of Kong Gateway**

### **What is Kong Gateway?**

Kong Gateway is an essential tool for modernizing IT management, enabling teams to adopt a DevOps culture applied to the development, testing, and delivery of APIs, known as APIOps. It provides access to a wide range of plugins and functionalities for API management, such as simulation, observability, and alerting.

### **Benefits of Kong Gateway**

1.  **Access to Plugins**: Facilitates the integration of additional features.
2.  **API Simulation**API testing in controlled environments.
3.  **Observability**Integration with Loki, Prometheus, and Grafana for monitoring.
4.  **Alerts**Incident notification system.

## **VeeCode Platform: Leveraging the Kong Gateway**

### **What is the VeeCode Platform?**

VeeCode Platform is a complete platform engineering solution that maximizes the use of Kong Gateway. Using the Backstage-based platform, VeeCode offers customized automations that simplify APIOps practices, reducing the learning curve for teams.

### **Features of the VeeCode Platform**

1.  **API Management**Advanced tools for creating, managing, and monitoring APIs.
2.  **Project Templates**Predefined structures to accelerate development.
3.  **Infrastructure as Code**Infrastructure automation through code models.
4.  **Kong Vitals**Monitoring the health and performance of APIs.
5.  **CI/CD Events**Continuous integration and API delivery with Argo CD and APIOps.
6.  **Kubernetes Cluster Information**Cluster management and monitoring.
7.  **Pull Request History**Detailed log of changes to the code.
8.  **Integration with DevPortal**Integrated developer portal for support and documentation.

## **DevPortal: Improving the Developer Experience**

### **What is DevPortal?**

DevPortal is a self-service solution for developers and business partners, offering clear and organized documentation for APIs. Through DevPortal, users can discover and utilize APIs more efficiently.

### **DevPortal Features**

1.  **Self-Service Experience**Autonomous access to resources and documentation.
2.  **Support 8x5 and 24x7**Ongoing support for developers.
3.  **Service Dashboard**Centralized view of available APIs and services.

## **Security with Kong Gateway and VeeCode Platform**

### **The Importance of Security in APIs**

Security is a critical aspect of API management, as APIs often expose sensitive information. Kong Gateway offers a variety of customizable security features to protect APIs against potential threats.

### **Security Features**

1.  **Integration with Hashicorp Vault**Secure management of secrets and credentials.
2.  **OIDC Authentication**Robust authentication to protect access.
3.  **Access control**Detailed access rules for APIs.

## **Platform Engineering: Concept and Applications**

### **What is Platform Engineering?**

[**Platform engineering**](/en/Why-use-platform-engineering/) is an approach that aims to create an efficient and automated technological infrastructure to support software development and operation. This discipline involves creating internal platforms that provide tools, services, and standards for developers, facilitating continuous delivery and management of complex systems.

### **Benefits of Platform Engineering**

1.  **Process Automation**It reduces the need for manual intervention.
2.  **Standardization of Tools and Methods**It promotes consistency and efficiency.
3.  **Scalability**It facilitates the growth of IT infrastructure.
4.  **Security and Compliance**Ensures that recommended safety practices are followed.
5.  **Improved Collaboration**It increases the efficiency of communication between development and operations teams.

## **How does the VeeCode Platform fit into Platform Engineering?**

### **Integration with Backstage**

VeeCode Platform utilizes Backstage, an open-source tool developed by Spotify, to create a robust internal platform. This allows for customization and automation of processes, reducing the administrative burden on developers and enabling them to focus on creating value for the business.

### **APIOps Automation**

The VeeCode Platform simplifies the adoption of APIOps, allowing teams to implement DevOps practices in API development and management without facing significant learning curves. With integrated tools such as Argo CD, Prometheus, and Grafana, the platform offers a complete solution for continuous delivery and **[API monitoring](/en/5-questions-to-ask-your-API-gateway/)**.
