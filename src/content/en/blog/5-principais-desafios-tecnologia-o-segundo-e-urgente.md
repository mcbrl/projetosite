Thinking about the future of IT is a constant challenge for managers worldwide; staying connected to key trends with the potential to change the routines of these professionals and revolutionize internal processes is essential if your company wants to remain competitive.

So, we've listed the main IT challenges for you to keep an eye on.

## **To develop products that integrate wearables into the daily lives of their users.**

Just as mobile technology has become universal and is still becoming so for many companies, the trend is for wearable gadgets and other devices to become part of our daily lives. Regardless of whether you operate in B2B or B2C, the IT department that can develop solutions that integrate these devices and simplify the lives of its users will come out ahead. There are system integration solutions for this, businesses emerging to serve mobile device users, processes being integrated for these devices, and even the creation of [new business models.](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/estrategia-de-monetizacao-das-apis/)

There is enormous potential in the application monitoring market and another in the [API market](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/entenda-o-que-e-uma-api/) . Getting ahead here will make a big difference in your company's processes and revenue. Data shows that over [70% of Brazilian companies](http://computerworld.com.br/mais-de-70-das-empresas-brasileiras-usam-apis-em-suas-estrategias-de-apps/) already work with APIs.

## **To dedicate oneself to information security.**

IT managers increasingly need to pay attention to the number of existing threats to the security of their clients, servers, and processes, and thus develop prevention and analysis policies that can anticipate attacks and even failures with the potential to compromise their business.

Security solutions combined with real-time monitoring and analysis methods are an effective way to identify potential threats and make the necessary corrections in time to ensure privacy, stability, and prevent damage to your business model.

In 2018, monitoring is gaining momentum, and highlighting the benefits of monitoring your systems will be essential.

## **Convincing customers to increasingly adopt cloud computing.**

[The cloud will not be forgotten in 2018](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/solucoes-em-nuvem-4-razoes-para-adotar/) , quite the contrary! Managers must strive to convince their clients of these benefits before it's too late and too much time or money has already been lost by not using this solution. Projects that utilize the benefits of cloud computing have shorter development cycles, can have lower costs, and offer the scalability to grow with your business, adapting quickly to the new needs of your processes.

Being someone who understands the importance of and champions this tool makes all the difference when it comes to convincing your clients of the benefits of the cloud and moving digital businesses into the future.

## **Ensuring application performance**

Controlling, analyzing, and understanding how your users interact with the solutions offered will be increasingly important in the IT and Marketing environment. [Application monitoring](/en/partnerships/dynatrace) is the easiest way to know exactly what is happening with your applications, what your users are doing – in real time – and thus discover flaws in your software in advance, that is, before major problems occur.

Choosing a tool capable of monitoring application health is essential for a smooth workflow, as it can highlight key problems and analyze metrics related to the use of your systems and corporate infrastructure. This allows for performance adjustments that will help you offer satisfactory solutions and reduce the number of user complaints, which can lead to decreased revenue.

With application monitoring, it's possible to gather precise metrics about complex systems and evaluate their usability and performance, making improvements as needed.

## **Using technology to revolutionize customer service.**

We constantly talk about artificial intelligence because of its potential to change how the industry views even the simplest things, like customer service. The "chatbot revolution" is the next big thing in the field, automating and improving how your company handles its customer service interactions.

Thinking of ways to reduce the distance between your company and its customers is a way to improve relationships – all within the reach of technology. Companies like [B2W](http://exame.abril.com.br/negocios/noticias/b2w-compra-plataforma-de-venda-por-whatsapp-e-instagram) and [Whirlpool](http://exame.abril.com.br/negocios/noticias/whirlpool-atendera-clientes-via-whatasapp) are looking at these solutions.

## **Conclusion**

Now more than ever, it is important that CIOs not only create innovation but also disseminate its possibilities, acquiring a broad vision of their industry and becoming accelerators of growth and productivity in their respective areas. One of the main IT challenges for 2016 is to reinvent itself.
