In this post, we're going to talk about our [Liferay Price Registration Record](https://materiais.vertigo.com.br/ata-de-registro-de-preco_liferay?utm_source=BlogVertigo&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=AtaLiferay) This is an instrument managed by the Brazilian Mint (CMB). The intention of this document is to facilitate the acquisition of products and services that have already gone through a bidding process; in other words, for those who will adhere to the instrument, it generates savings in time and money.
This instrument allows other government institutions to acquire services and products of the same nature. [It's worth remembering that our minutes are Federal.](https://materiais.vertigo.com.br/ata-de-registro-de-preco_liferay?utm_source=BlogVertigo&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=AtaLiferay).
Cost-effectiveness should always be the primary motivation and argument for those who are going to adhere to a Price Registration Agreement. Let's see what the regulations say:
"Article 22. Provided that the advantage is duly justified, the price registration record, during its validity, may be used by any body or entity of the federal public administration that did not participate in the bidding process, with the consent of the managing body."
Although the paragraph above specifically mentions the federal public administration, state and municipal governments can also apply the same interpretation.

[![](/wp-content/uploads/2015/09/the-best.png)](https://materiais.vertigo.com.br/ata-de-registro-de-preco_liferay?utm_source=BlogVertigo&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=AtaRegistroPreco&_fs=87bb9e17-c4e8-4975-b9a7-feb2e4aad195)

Here are three reasons why you should use this Price Registration Agreement:

- ### **Up to 45% cheaper than the current market value.**

    We were able to offer this amount because our minutes are not updated according to Liferay's new pricing table.

- ### **Federal Minutes**

    With this agreement, other spheres, whether at the Municipal, State or Federal level, can adhere to this instrument.

- ### **Function Point Measurement**

    All work measurement is metricized in relation to the acquisition of Function Points.
