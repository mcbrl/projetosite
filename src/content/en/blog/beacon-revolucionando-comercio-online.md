The concept of IoT is reshaping how we interact with systems, people, and "things." Several sectors are being impacted by these changes, and one sector that is experiencing a significant impact in the Internet of Things landscape is online shopping and e-commerce. Connected devices, from cars ( [Conect Car](http://www.conectcar.com/) ) to smart appliances, are transforming inanimate objects into potential sales and communication channels. Out of milk in your refrigerator? Don't worry – a smart refrigerator can place an order online and manage your purchases, and this is already a reality. In today's article, see how the Internet of Things is revolutionizing retail. Read on:

## How will the Internet of Things influence e-commerce?

The world of e-commerce continues to grow rapidly: analysts suggest that the number of online purchases will [increase by 50% by 2018.](https://www.internetretailer.com/2015/03/31/number-global-online-shoppers-will-grow-50-2018) Similarly, the Internet of Things has shaken up the way we consume and has become a source of increased revenue for many companies. By 2020, there will be at least five online- connected objects for every smartphone user , according to research by Google.

With the Internet of Things, there are a large number of connected devices bridging the gap between the user and technology. From health-tracking Fitbits to smart TVs, the potential impact of connected devices on e-commerce is enormous . Here are some reasons, according to Google itself:

## Better inventory management

Online devices will help e-commerce companies simplify their operations. For example, tracking and handling inventory will become more agile with products connected and traceable in real time. Such data can be used to notify managers when inventory is low, which products are stuck in inventory, and for how long.

## New marketing opportunities

The rise of online devices allows companies to gain more insight into their customers' behavior. Their daily routines, purchase history, and product preferences can be tracked and used in more targeted marketing campaigns. This is why, as consumers, we are beginning to notice an increase in personalized advertising among the emails we receive daily and the offers made to us.

## It increases overall efficiency.

The Internet of Things allows an e-commerce company to connect with customers in a way that was previously impossible. For example, data about technical failures and product defects can be automatically sent to the seller, ensuring a quick response. Sometimes, this happens even before the user realizes that their appliance has malfunctioned or stopped working.

## Responsive websites

Nokia [Networks](http://networks.nokia.com/innovation/internet-of-things) is a major player in the Internet of Things, and as a result, we are now seeing a significant shift in how mobile websites are developed and designed to exchange information with various devices. A positive user experience is essential, especially for e-commerce sites. And in the near future, when the Internet of Things becomes predominant, developers will work on developing applications to leverage every possible place for users to make or schedule purchases. This will ultimately lead to smarter and more responsive websites, capable of offering personalized browsing experiences.

The marriage between the Internet of Things and e-commerce is only just beginning, but the way goods and services are bought, delivered, and consumed looks set to change forever.
