## Challenges in Systems Integration

The connection between diverse systems, platforms, and technologies presents significant complexities for companies, ranging from compatibility issues to challenges of scalability, flexibility, and information security.

In this scenario, [**Digibee iPaaS**](/en/5-benefits-of-the-Digibee-ipass/) emerges as an innovative solution, offering an effective way to overcome such obstacles. Designed to facilitate systems integration, Digibee iPaaS provides organizations with the ability to unify their IT operations in a cohesive and secure manner, directly addressing the common challenges encountered in this process.

Check out 5 ways to overcome the complexities of systems integration, based on an [**article written**](https://www.digibee.com/blog/5-integration-pain-points-that-inspire-change/) by the Digibee team of experts.

### **1 – Supplier Lock-in**

Before solutions like Digibee, many companies dealt with a rigid, monolithic legacy integration system for a surprisingly long time. While it was acknowledged that more modern alternatives, such as iPaaS, existed, the cost of change was incredibly high, requiring a significant commitment of time and resources.

***"Legacy Application or System:*** *An information system that may be based on outdated technologies but is critical to daily operations. Replacing legacy applications and systems with systems based on new and different technologies represents one of the greatest challenges for information systems professionals"* ( [**Gartner**](https://www.gartner.com/en/information-technology/glossary/legacy-application-or-system#:~:text=An%20information%20system%20that%20may,IS\)%20professional%27s%20most%20significant%20challenges.) )

Although any transition to a new system requires effort, modernizing your integration strategy with iPaaS offers one of the highest returns on investment for your business.

Ultimately, contemporary Integration Platform as a Service (iPaaS) technology acts as the glue that connects all your applications, providing an agile and composable IT environment. With new [**AI-powered system integration**](/en/How-can-AI-optimize-DevOps/) migration capabilities , customers using Digibee iPaaS evolve in record time.

### **2 – System Updates**

The pain of updating legacy integration systems is at the top of the list for most Digibee customers. These workflows are time-consuming and manpower-intensive. The process also imposes system downtime and other business disruptions. It's an expensive and relentless cycle, necessary just to maintain the status quo.

According to [**Digibee's 2023 State of Enterprise Integration report**](https://marketing.digibee.com/state-of-enterprise-integration-report-2023) , upgrading legacy infrastructure is among the top ten integration goals for businesses in North America.

Furthermore, according to the report, 26,70% of companies want to use iPaaS to upgrade their legacy infrastructure.

With Digibee's modern iPaaS, every time a customer accesses the system, they are guaranteed to use the latest version of the technology. We maintain our cloud-born platform without requiring any investment of resources or time from our customers. All bandwidth previously tied up by legacy workflows and system updates is reallocated to more important tasks.

### **3 – Accumulation of IT Projects**

Legacy integration systems and backlogs of IT projects go hand in hand. With software developers spending most of their time simply maintaining the existing system, special projects and innovation initiatives are put on hold, hindering innovation and the company's ability to grow and thrive.

Another finding from the Digibee report shows that most respondents estimate that up to 60% of their delayed IT projects depend on systems integration support.

Once customers adopt Digibee's [**iPaaS**](/en/What-is-IPAAS/) , they overcome IT backlogs in record time, freeing up bandwidth and budget to invest in innovation.

### **4 – Limited Resources**

Companies burdened with a legacy systems integration product often need to invest heavily in ongoing training and certification for their development team. Limited by budget, most companies opt to train only a subset of developers, allowing only a portion of the team to perform the work.

Maintaining such a specialized team also increases the costs of hiring and retaining talent, as reflected in a recent study comparing MuleSoft's legacy requirements with Digibee's integration platform.

### **5 - API Overload**

With the growing [**popularity of APIs**](/en/The-role-of-APIs-in-the-digital-transformation-process-of-society./) , it's not surprising that API calls account for 83% of internet traffic, an impressive figure. However, companies that rely on legacy integration systems face a huge challenge: the underlying structure of custom coding and point-to-point integration is complex and difficult to manage. As a result, integration teams spend a significant amount of time mapping old code and rebuilding integrations.

Thus, the number of enterprise technology applications used by companies now exceeds 1.000, but integration between these applications is limited. Less than 30% of these applications are integrated, as Forbes points out in its warning about the 'Frankenstein Stack' of Data Systems. This scenario highlights a critical opportunity for improving systems integration.

Digibee customers address this overhead much more efficiently by leveraging the low-code visual interface and seamless workflows to enable rapid API creation. This frees integration teams from time-consuming manual work. Digibee simplifies the complexity of [**API integration**](/en/API-integration-creating-connectivity-in-the-industrial-sector/) , allowing your API strategy to be expansive and not limited.
