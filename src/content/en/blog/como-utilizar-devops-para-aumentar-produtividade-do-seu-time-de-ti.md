To expedite the delivery of tasks that arise among Information Technology professionals. This is one of the main objectives of **DevOps** . The practice aims to optimize the work of software developers and operations and infrastructure professionals.

This is how, with development and infrastructure professionals working towards common goals, results are achieved for each project that the IT sector delivers.

In this article, you'll learn how to use **DevOps** to increase your IT team's productivity. Check it out and enjoy reading!

[Learn here what DevOps is in detail.](/en/What-is-DevOps/)

## **DevOps enables more frequent production deployments and reduces errors.**

Unlike traditional models, where professionals worked in a way that conflicted with the interests of different teams, **DevOps** aims to harmonize IT work within a company. This is because the integration and communication between software developers and infrastructure operators become more fluid.

Through tools, trust, and shared responsibility, **DevOps** has made it possible to deliver demands consistently and in an organized manner. This results in a higher frequency of production and delivery, with a lower chance of professionals making mistakes. This is due to automated testing, provided by various tools such as Jenkins and Hudson.

Large companies around the world use this technology, such as Facebook, Spotify, Walmart, and Netflix.

## **The 4 pillars of DevOps**

**DevOps** can be characterized by four pillars: culture, automation, evaluation, and sharing. These pillars are segmented as follows:

### **Culture**

With the cultural shift, not only do teams become more collaborative, but the new assignments and tools empower project teams, putting an end to team friction.

With this change in behavior, a healthy relationship develops between the areas in pursuit of a common goal: improving deliverables.

### **Automation**

Through **DevOps** , it's possible to automate the entire project delivery pipeline, from initial setup to production, releasing new system functionalities multiple times a day with control and monitoring. Previously, IT professionals wasted a lot of time worrying about various technical and bureaucratic obstacles to delivering a product. With **DevOps,** this is no longer necessary, as releases need to undergo numerous tests before being delivered.

Now, professionals have greater control and can monitor to see if everything is going according to plan. The main concern is with the code.

### **Review**

With **DevOps,** it's also possible to analyze the results and see where potential errors are, allowing improvements to be made quickly.

In this way, it is possible to understand how an application is functioning by performing various types of measurements, with a wide variety of tests.

### **Sharing**

As already mentioned, **DevOps** enables good communication within the team, making it possible to give feedback and optimize the work of professionals.

## **How DevOps will increase the productivity of your IT team.**

### **IT projects will be completed in less time.**

With **DevOps,** sharing and engagement tools are essential, making production more efficient. This reduces the time required to develop an IT project.

### **Better use of resources and processes.**

As professionals will no longer work in isolation, the need to redo work or readjust will be minimized.

This is because everyone works together, thus reducing the delivery effort and allowing for better use of resources and processes.

### **Proactive intervention**

Since the entire team is involved in the same project, it will be possible to give opinions in a more informed way and solve problems more quickly.

With this proactive intervention, costs and time spent will be optimized, since flaws can be identified more quickly. This happens because, instead of professionals delivering the entire project at once, they will work focusing on small deliverables.

It can be concluded that **DevOps** is here to transform the IT world, driving change and integrating the common goals of development and operations professionals.

Furthermore, with **DevOps** , companies need to invest in knowledge and updates constantly so that projects become increasingly efficient.
