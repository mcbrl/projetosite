When we talk about Terraform, we are referring to an open-source software tool capable of generating "Infrastructure as Code" (IaC), a solution created by HashiCorp.

Vertigo Tecnologia has already implemented Terraform for international clients, such as the [successful case of Privacy Dynamics](https://materiais.vertigo.com.br/case-privacy-dynamics) , an American startup specializing in data protection, which was able to replicate its IaC environment for its clients in a simple and straightforward way.

But let's learn a little more about the possibilities of Terraform Cloud.

Terraform is a tool that enables developers to use a configuration language called HCL (HashiCorp Configuration Language) to declaratively describe the desired state of the cloud or on-premises infrastructure for running applications.

Therefore, Terraform allows you to easily provision infrastructure in multi-cloud or on-premises data centers. Furthermore, it's possible to reconfigure the infrastructure securely and efficiently in response to configuration changes. For this reason, Terraform has become one of the most popular infrastructure automation tools available today.

HashiCorp recently implemented a list of updates to Terraform Cloud, with features that even include a free plan; see more details in this article.

Companies considering implementing a solution that utilizes a hybrid or multi-cloud environment will be very interested in learning about and trying Terraform, which is part of Vertigo Tecnologia's [portfolio of solutions .](/en/partnerships/hashicorp/)

## Advantages of automating your infrastructure with Terraform

One of the main goals of cloud infrastructure provisioning is to make it replicable through code, which gives DevOps teams greater freedom to plan and allocate resources within workflows.

HashiCorp's Terraform is a tool in Vertigo Tecnologia's portfolio, with a team of experts prepared to solve complex problems for companies.

By adopting infrastructure as code, it's possible to follow an implementation and automation methodology, using tools that facilitate the secure and modern creation, modification, and management of the infrastructure.

In this way, it is possible to obtain better quality services, reduce costs and achieve significant gains, all while maintaining safety and excellence in delivery.

## Terraform Cloud received important updates and a free plan.

HashiCorp has announced [important updates](https://www.hashicorp.com/blog/terraform-cloud-updates-plans-with-an-enhanced-free-tier-and-more-flexibility) to Terraform Cloud, along with an expanded free plan. The free plan now provides access to premium features for up to 500 managed resources, including security features, and offers unlimited users and applications.

In addition, features such as Single Sign-On (SSO), policy as code, execution tasks, and Terraform Cloud Agent have been added, aiming to strengthen digital security and meet the needs of small teams and organizations that are starting to use Terraform.

These updates were based on user and customer feedback and bring significant benefits to Terraform Cloud. Now, businesses of all sizes can take advantage of premium security features on the free plan, while paid tiers offer upgrade paths for more advanced use cases and enterprise support.

## The Cloud Standard and Cloud Plus plans also received updates.

Within Terraform's paid plans, we have **Terraform Cloud Standard** , which is highly recommended for organizations starting to use Terraform, as it offers essential features for team management, scalability, security, and support.

Terraform **Cloud Plus** offers everything at the Standard level, plus additional features for continuous infrastructure management.

### For customers already using Terraform Cloud.

Just one important piece of information: according to HashiCorp, if you are already using Terraform Cloud Free, there will be no change to your account. However, to take advantage of the new features of the free tier, you [will need to activate them](https://app.terraform.io/) in your Terraform Cloud account.

Additionally, if you are a Terraform Cloud Team or Team & Governance customer, your existing plan will remain unchanged. However, you can migrate to the new Standard plan to take advantage of the new features. To do so, log in to your HashiCorp Cloud Platform (HCP) account and access the TFC Organization Plan and Billing tab.

If you don't have an HCP account, you can create one on the [HashiCorp Cloud Platform portal](https://portal.cloud.hashicorp.com/sign-in).
