**Customer Experience** is a term that is part of the daily lives of leaders in the world's leading companies. It's a key theme emphasized in many **digital transformation manuals** worldwide.

> [Click here to learn what Digital Transformation means for Vertigo Tecnologia.](/en/Digital-transformation%3A-are-you-ready/)

Regardless of the sector, **customer experiences** that meet the needs of the target audience and help convert them into customers are a crucial part of commercial success. However, regardless of the nature of the business, valuing companies is often difficult. This complicates determining their value and making fine adjustments that improve ROI (Return on Investment).

The concept of **Customer** **Experience** is gaining traction at a time when competition between companies is increasingly fierce. Furthermore, consumers are empowered, with diverse ways to consume and gather information about what they can purchase.

Considering these facts, check out below some ideal points for you who want to improve your company's relationship with its customers.

## **How does your customer perceive your brand?**

![](/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/441-768x480-1.jpg)

What's going wrong? What's working well? What new things can be created? These are questions that companies concerned with Customer Experience are constantly chasing answers to.

Customer Experience is about the interactions your consumer/client has with your brand. Improving this contact depends on a number of factors. However, in general, it revolves around improving the understanding of customer needs.

### **Customer Satisfaction Scoring**

The answer to improving your customer service can be found in your company's past experiences. Therefore, keeping records is essential for controlling your operations. Detailed reports are necessary to understand weaknesses and areas for potential improvement.

This analysis can yield insights and even generate new business opportunities for your company.

### **Customer review channels**

This point is very similar to the previous one. However, by creating channels for customers to give their opinions, your company significantly increases the flow of knowledge. Remember that idea that "whoever sees a situation from the outside, sees it better"? That's the gist of it.

Review channels can be integrated into your homepage, with buttons that allow users to rate a product. You can also create an email address for suggestions. The intention is to open up ways for your customers to communicate with your company.

### **Employee engagement at the company**

The first people who need to be convinced of the quality of a company's service or product are its employees. Having engaged employees is fundamental for a company in several aspects, ensuring a more efficient corporate team.

It's necessary to invest in hidden research and performance evaluations. This will allow you to better understand your team. The result is the ability to equip and train your team as needed.

### **First-call resolution rates**

Having a problem with a company is usually a double inconvenience. One is the failure/defect of the service/product, the other is the difficulty in resolving the problem with the company's customer service. You know the saying "Shit happens"? Well, that's it. Although many companies are full of rules to ensure their services are perfect, mistakes happen.

An effective customer service department is more likely to resolve customer issues during the first call or online chat. The higher the first-call resolution rate, the more effective your service will be.

### **Net Promoter Score**

Net Promoter Score (NPS) is a metric that aims to measure customer satisfaction and loyalty to companies. Companies and organizations of all sizes use this method because it is practical and effective. It consists of periodic online surveys conducted with customers.
