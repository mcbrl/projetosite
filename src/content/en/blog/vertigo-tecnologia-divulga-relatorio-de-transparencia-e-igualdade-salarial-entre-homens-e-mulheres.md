In accordance with **Law 14.611, of July 3, 2023** , Vertigo Tecnologia makes public the **Transparency and Salary Equity Report between Women and Men** , referring to the second half of 2025.

This issue is fundamental for society and for organizations seeking to align values ​​of respect, diversity, and social responsibility. For Vertigo, equal pay is a commitment that goes beyond legal compliance: it reflects our culture, which values ​​the talent and competence of all people, regardless of gender.

## **Diversity as an engine for innovation.**

The presence of women in all areas of the company, including leadership positions, reinforces our pride in having different perspectives and experiences that drive innovation and the continuous growth of Vertigo Tecnologia.

In addition to professional recognition, we have implemented clear and transparent compensation criteria, based on career and salary plans, years of experience, teamwork skills, proactivity, and goal achievement.

## **Commitments and actions for inclusion**

The report also highlights actions that strengthen diversity and inclusion, such as:

- Support for the sharing of family responsibilities for both sexes;
- Policies for hiring women in different contexts, including Black women, women with disabilities, women experiencing violence, or women who are heads of households;
- Incentives for the promotion of women to management and leadership positions.

These practices are part of Vertigo's strategy to build a fair, inclusive, and increasingly representative work environment.

## **Transparency that builds trust.**

The publication of this report is another step in reinforcing our commitment to transparency. We believe this path is essential to consolidating Vertigo as a leading player in the technology sector, not only for the innovation we deliver, but also for the values ​​we practice every day.

Check out the [**Transparency and Equal Pay Report for Women and Men – 2nd semester of 2025**](/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Vertigo_RelatorioIgualdadeSalarial_2025_2.pdf) , available on the Emprega Brasil Portal, with official data from eSocial and RAIS.
