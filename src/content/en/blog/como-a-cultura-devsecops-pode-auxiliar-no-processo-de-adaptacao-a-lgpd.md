Data is the most valuable asset of the 21st century, and therefore its security deserves utmost attention, especially when it comes to the development of technological solutions. Thus, it is no coincidence that the subject has never been discussed so much, and so much has been invested in systems and applications that keep organizational data confidential. Furthermore, with the entry into force of the [GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)](https://gdpr-info.eu/) in Europe and the future enforcement of the [LGPD (General Data Protection Law)](http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm) in Brazil, a shift is occurring in how individuals and companies approach data security.

With this in mind, we will address some issues related to the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law) and show how DevSecOps culture can contribute to the process of adapting to the new data security and protection law.

Come on?

## **How will the LGPD affect companies?**

Following the trend in other countries, the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law) represents a major shift in the protection and security of personal data in the country. Under this law, individuals will take control over how their data is collected and processed.

And, in cases of infractions committed by organizations, they are subject to sanctions ranging from a warning, with a deadline for adopting corrective measures, to fines for non-reconciliation that can reach 2% of revenue (limited to R$ 50 million) and even the partial or total suspension of their activities. 

Therefore, it is no longer possible to neglect the security of personal data in your company. Compliance with the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law) and other data security laws has become a top priority for software development companies, as they often interact with the personal data of thousands of people worldwide. 

If you are a company, regardless of your revenue or number of employees, that handles any personal data (name, identity, date of birth, location, biometric records, or any educational, financial, medical information, etc.), you must comply with the LGPD standards.

## **DevSecOps and LGPD**

One of the major questions raised by the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law) is how to comply with its rules when it comes to developing systems and technologies, ensuring the privacy and security of personal data. However, the answer is simpler than you might think: DevSecOps!

Ultimately, [DevSecOps](/en/devsecops/) means considering application security and the security infrastructure involved in the project from its inception, within a [DevOps](/en/What-is-DevOps/) approach to agile development.

The DevSecOps culture places security practices not only at the end of the software development lifecycle, but at every stage of the process to ensure a secure and smooth workflow from end to end. In other words, it's more than just a software development model; it's also a culture for driving change through continuous integration and continuous delivery, with security as a non-negotiable condition.

## **Because DevSecOps addresses data security needs.**

In general, DevSecOps can help companies comply with the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law) for the following reasons: 

- Ensures that your software meets all data security standards;
- It promotes safety as a collective responsibility for everyone in the organization;
- Its focus is on delivering software as quickly as possible with maximum attention to security;
- It streamlines the storage, processing, and collection of data that can ensure the proper agreement;
- It presents a logical, strategic, and potential approach to software development with security as a key component;
- It has the ability to produce excellent results when building modern software with maximum quality and speed. 

## **Containers and the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law)**

When we address data security, specifically using containers, they contribute to companies working in accordance with the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law) for the following reasons:

- **Protecting confidential data through vulnerability detection:** Through continuous scanning, it is possible to measure the vulnerability risks of confidential asset records, as well as implement effective remediation early in the process;
- **Detect, alert on, and respond to suspicious container-related activity:** Behavioral analytics of running containers allow organizations to automatically respond to suspicious activity, block and contain attacks;
- **Access control for confidential assets:** User access permissions to container resources are managed and controlled automatically, based on the company's access policy;
- **Demonstrate compliance:** Demonstrating compliance to controllers and supervisory authorities is a way to avoid or minimize fines in case of violation. 

Finally, we can affirm that the concept of DevSecOps is yet another important ally in building a security strategy across the entire organization, ensuring that the software produced is developed securely from its conception, which proves extremely necessary when we talk about working in accordance with the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law).
