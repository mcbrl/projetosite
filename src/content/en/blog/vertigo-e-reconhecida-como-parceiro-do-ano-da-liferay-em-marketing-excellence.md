Vertigo was named Liferay's Partner of the Year 2025 in the Marketing Excellence category, an award that recognizes partners who develop integrated, strategic marketing programs with measurable results for Liferay solutions. The award was presented on February 3, 2026, during Liferay's Sales Kickoff event in Vienna, Austria, and was received by Bruno Badini, CEO of Vertigo, and Vinicius Siani, CTO, who were present at the event representing the company.

This recognition reflects not only the execution of successful campaigns, but primarily the ability to deeply understand market needs and build strategies that connect technology, business, and people. For Vertigo, the award represents the validation of an approach that places customer proximity and channel integration at the heart of its go-to-market strategy.

## **What is the Marketing Excellence Partner of the Year award?**

The Marketing Excellence Partner of the Year award is given by Liferay to partners who demonstrate excellence in creating and executing marketing initiatives that generate qualified demand and real business impact. The evaluation considers the positive impact on business results, the ability to expand markets, and the demonstrated commitment to joint marketing and sales initiatives.

Recognition goes beyond tactical execution. Liferay seeks to identify partners who understand market dynamics, develop consistent narratives, and are able to translate strategy into concrete results. Winners are selected through a rigorous process that evaluates not only the volume of actions, but primarily the quality, coherence, and impact of the campaigns developed.

## **The strategy that led to recognition**

The work recognized by Liferay was developed throughout 2025 and focused on the Brazilian insurance sector, a market undergoing accelerated digital transformation that demands robust solutions for complex challenges.

### **Integrated campaign for the insurance sector**

Vertigo developed a campaign that integrated different touchpoints, creating a consistent and relevant narrative for business and IT leaders in the insurance sector. The strategy included participation in CQCS Insurtech & Innovation, the leading innovation forum for the Brazilian insurance market, as well as the creation of Insurance Tech Leaders, a proprietary event produced by Vertigo in partnership with Liferay and aimed at industry executives. The initiative also involved publicizing the **[success story of CNP Seguradora](https://materiais.vertigo.com.br/case-como-a-vertigo-tecnologia-ajudou-a-cnp-a-escalar-sua-opera%C3%A7%C3%A3o-digital)** and producing specialized content on the Vertigo blog.

These touchpoints were designed to mutually reinforce each other, building a solid and relevant market presence. The approach was guided by an Account-Based Marketing (ABM) strategy and personalized communications, which allowed Vertigo to align its messages with the real priorities of decision-makers. This proximity, combined with close collaboration with Liferay and face-to-face engagement with the target audience, was fundamental to the success of the initiative.

## **What does this recognition represent?**

For Vertigo, the Marketing Excellence award validates the conviction that integration, focus, and proximity are the pillars of marketing strategies that truly generate impact. In a market saturated with generic messages and disconnected campaigns, building consistent and relevant narratives makes all the difference.

This recognition also reinforces Vertigo's position as a strategic partner for organizations seeking not only to implement technology, but to transform digital capabilities into sustainable competitive advantages. The ability to deeply understand the challenges of regulated and complex sectors, such as insurance, and translate them into effective communication strategies is a key differentiator highlighted by this award.

Fernando Areias, Chief Operating Officer of Liferay, highlighted the importance of partners to the company: “At Liferay, partners are integrated into the core of our operations, representing much more than just a market channel. These awards are a testament to the remarkable results our partners have delivered and their unwavering commitment to our shared future.”

## **Regarding the Vertigo and Liferay partnership**

The partnership between Vertigo and Liferay is based on shared values ​​of innovation, technical excellence, and a commitment to customer success. Liferay is globally recognized for its flexible, open-source Digital Experience Platform (DXP), which enables organizations to build, manage, and scale powerful solutions, from marketing and commerce websites to customer portals and corporate intranets.

Vertigo acts as a strategic partner of Liferay in Brazil, combining deep platform knowledge with expertise in regulated sectors and mission-critical environments. This combination allows us to deliver solutions that not only meet complex technical requirements but also generate real and measurable business value.

Vertigo is a technology consultancy that transforms digital intelligence into business results in complex and mission-critical environments. With experience serving large companies, financial institutions, insurance companies, and government agencies, Vertigo connects strategy to execution to help its clients operate with greater speed, predictability, and control.
