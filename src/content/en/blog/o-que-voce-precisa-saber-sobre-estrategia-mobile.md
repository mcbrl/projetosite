## MOBILITY AS A STRATEGY

How is your company planning to adopt a digital strategy, and is mobile part of that initiative? Some data shows that more than half of web traffic in the US and more than a third of global traffic comes from mobile devices. Is your website prepared to take advantage of these benefits and changes in consumer behavior?

It's crucial to have a long-term mobile strategy. Choose a platform that offers both a responsive design strategy and stability in information transfer.

Don't forget, entering the mobile services and products market allows you to quickly expand your range of business partners.

## Cloud first, mobile first

The union of cloud computing with mobile applications has changed the way we handle information. Today, people check all kinds of data on tablets and smartphones, relegating computers to secondary devices in their daily lives. As a result, companies need to modify their business strategies to include new ways to reach their customers.

A successful mobile strategy takes into account the advantages and disadvantages of mobile websites, apps, and hybrid products. Focusing on the distribution of mobile web apps allows for a broader and simpler approach. With a responsive page, it's possible to reach virtually every type of user. However, if this type of solution doesn't require a cross-platform update strategy, it may not "win over" the user as easily as a native app.

An app-focused approach can more easily gain engagement. Native apps have greater access to device resources, such as the camera, contacts, and email. However, a cross-platform mobile strategy has a higher investment cost. Each mobile platform has its own framework, with its own APIs and update methods.

## Mobile strategy options

Websites are practically mandatory for any modern company. They allow a company to be seen by a large audience on tablets, smartphones, and computers. Furthermore, web apps are a simple alternative for those looking to reach diverse users without needing to create code for each system.

Native apps offer a good user experience. With access to features such as GPS, contact lists, and even the accelerometer, it's possible to create a more powerful mobile tool that's in sync with the phone. However, creating applications for multiple platforms is a major challenge that requires a greater investment in skilled personnel.

Regardless of your company's choice, it should prepare to have a mobile strategy. Today, more than a third of web traffic comes from mobile devices. In the coming years, this number is expected to double with the popularization of smartphones.

With a long-term strategy, companies can create good mobile websites. They should make use of best development practices, such as responsive layouts and always secure connections.

The better a company's ability to offer a good user experience, the greater its customer loyalty will be.
