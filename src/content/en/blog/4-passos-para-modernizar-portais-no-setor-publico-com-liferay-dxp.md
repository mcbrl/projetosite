Modernizing institutional portals in the public sector is a fundamental strategy for institutions to offer faster and more accessible services to citizens. Within this context, the Liferay DXP platform stands out as a complete solution to facilitate the digital transformation process, allowing the integration of legacy systems, strengthening security, and offering a better user experience. 

However, for efficient modernization, it is important to follow some specific best practices so that the transition process is successful and generates maximum value for public institutions. 

In this article, we present the essential practices for achieving effective modernization with Liferay DXP in the public sector.

## 1- Integration of Legacy Systems and Modernization of the Digital Infrastructure

In the public sector, most institutions still operate with legacy systems, which are old technologies developed to meet the demands of decades ago. These technologies limit flexibility and hinder the integration of new systems and resources, in addition to increasing operational costs due to complex maintenance. 

Modernization, in this context, implies replacing or integrating these old systems into a modern and scalable platform. [**Liferay DXP**](/en/liferay-dxp-7-4-all-about-the-digital-experience-platform/) facilitates this integration process, enabling disparate systems to connect and exchange information in real time. This allows the institution to centralize data and processes, promoting more efficient management aligned with current needs.

System integration not only improves operational efficiency but also provides greater flexibility for the public sector portal to adapt to new demands. Through this data centralization, Liferay DXP allows information to be accessed more quickly and accurately, offering a more consistent experience for citizens and employees. 

With a modern digital infrastructure, institutions can incorporate new technologies more easily, which translates into constant innovation and the possibility of offering new digital services to the public. In this way, the Liferay DXP platform not only helps overcome the challenge of legacy systems, but also prepares institutions for more agile and integrated management.

### Using APIs to Connect Legacy Systems

Liferay DXP stands out for its integration capabilities, allowing legacy systems to be connected through robust APIs that support different data types and formats. This centralized connection facilitates data transfer and simplifies information management, reducing the need for separate access points and streamlining the workflow of IT teams. 

With [**APIs**](/en/5-questions-to-ask-your-API-gateway/) , public sector portals can consolidate different data sources, making navigation smoother for users and allowing crucial information to be accessed and updated reliably and efficiently.

### Benefits of Interoperability in the Public Sector

Interoperability between systems ensures that information is always accessible and up-to-date, which is essential for improving transparency and user experience. Public institutions can offer a single, centralized portal that concentrates information relevant to citizens, eliminating data fragmentation and reducing errors. 

Compatibility with modern systems reduces the risk of failures and strengthens the security of the institutional portal, increasing the public sector's ability to react quickly to new needs and demands from citizens.

## 2- Security and Compliance in Liferay DXP

Information security is a priority in the public sector, especially due to the volume of sensitive and confidential data that these institutions handle daily. However, maintaining legacy systems can make protecting this information more difficult, as these systems often have vulnerabilities that do not comply with modern security standards. 

Implementing a robust platform like [**Liferay DXP**](/en/Liferay-DXP-SEO/) allows institutions to ensure data security more efficiently through advanced access control and authentication tools. These measures ensure that only authorized users can access confidential information, reducing the risk of attacks and data breaches.

In addition to its security features, Liferay DXP helps public sector institutions comply with regulations such as the [**General Data Protection Law (LGPD)**](/en/How-can-Devsecops-culture-help-in-the-process-of-adapting-to-the-LGPD-%28Brazilian-General-Data-Protection-Law%29/) , required for the protection of personal data. The platform allows for regular audits and continuous monitoring of activities, ensuring that the portal is always in compliance with current regulations.

With Liferay DXP, institutions can implement stronger security protocols and maintain constant vigilance over data access. This monitoring helps protect the integrity of information and strengthens citizen confidence in the security of public services.

### Data Protection with Authentication Tools

Liferay DXP implements multi-factor authentication capabilities, requiring a combination of passwords, tokens, and even biometrics to ensure that only authorized users can access sensitive information. 

This level of protection drastically reduces the risk of unauthorized access and ensures that security practices comply with regulations. With robust access control, public institutions can customize security by role, allowing for more flexible and secure management of user permissions.

### Compliance with Safety Regulations

To meet the requirements of the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law) and other data protection regulations, Liferay DXP offers continuous monitoring and activity logging capabilities. These features help identify and mitigate potential threats and violations, facilitating audits and enabling a rapid response to any security incident. 

Furthermore, Liferay DXP allows institutions to perform security updates quickly, ensuring that the portal is always protected against new vulnerabilities.

The complexity and fragmentation of many public systems result in confusing and frustrating navigation, which can drive users away and create a negative experience. 

With Liferay DXP, it's possible to create interfaces that adapt to the specific needs of different user profiles, ensuring that the portal is easy to use and intuitive. The platform also allows the portal's content and functionalities to be customized according to each user's profile and needs, providing simpler and more efficient navigation.

Beyond usability, digital accessibility is a fundamental aspect for the inclusion of all citizens, regardless of their physical or cognitive abilities. Liferay DXP offers several accessibility features, such as text-to-speech options for the visually impaired and improved color contrast for easier viewing. These features are essential for the portal to be accessible to everyone and promote digital inclusion. 

By investing in a high-quality user experience, public institutions not only improve citizen satisfaction but also strengthen the image of a modern and innovative administration.

With Liferay DXP, public institutions can adapt the portal interface to offer a personalized and intuitive experience for each user profile. Personalization also simplifies navigation, allowing citizens to quickly find the information and services they need.

### Digital Accessibility Optimization

Digital accessibility is a priority for the public sector, and Liferay DXP facilitates this implementation with native features. From text readers to contrast adjustments, the platform ensures that all citizens, regardless of their specific needs, can access public services autonomously and intuitively.
