Salesforce – the world leader in the SaaS CRM market – announced this week the acquisition of Mulesoft – a leading API connectivity platform – for a whopping $6,5 billion. The agreement has already been unanimously approved by the board members of both companies, and now it's just a matter of time before the deal is officially announced.

MuleSoft states that it will continue investing in the company's *application network* vision with **Anypoint Platform** , and that it will strengthen the new " *Salesforce Integration Cloud* ." This will allow all companies to expose any data, regardless of where it resides, to drive deep and intelligent customer experiences in a personalized 1:1 journey.

Together, the companies will further accelerate the **digital transformation** of their customers.

Salesforce's CEO is very optimistic about the acquisition:

> “Every digital transformation begins and ends with the customer,” said Salesforce CEO Marc Benioff in a statement. “Together, Salesforce and MuleSoft will enable customers to connect all information across the enterprise across all public and private clouds and data sources, radically enhancing innovation.”
