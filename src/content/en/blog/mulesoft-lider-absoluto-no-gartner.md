MuleSoft , [a](/en/mulesoft/) global leader in connectivity solutions between systems and APIs, has just achieved another major milestone. The company has been recognized as a **leader in Gartner's Magic Quadrant** for **Enterprise Integration Platform as a Service** ( [iPaaS](/en/What-is-IPAAS/) ). The company has also been named a leader in Gartner's Magic Quadrant for **Full Lifecycle API Management for the third consecutive time** , becoming the only vendor recognized as a leader in both reports.

[Click here to view the full reports.](https://www.mulesoft.com/lp/reports/gartner-magic-quadrant-leader)

Digital transformation demands connectivity between applications. Based on this premise, MuleSoft is uniquely positioned as a key resource for digital transformation. Using APIs to unlock data in virtually any system or application, MuleSoft customers can connect their technology building blocks in a standardized way. As a result, digital assets become connectable and reusable, creating a scalable application network built for change.

### In the words of Mark Dao, product director at MuleSoft:

“ *The speed of innovation is the soul of the company, because by creating APIs it is possible to unlock access to the data and capabilities of each application. Furthermore, integrations allow connectivity between these applications. Together, these APIs and integrations build our clients' application networks. With the breadth of capabilities of the AnyPoint Platform* ™ *, it is possible to unlock data trapped in your systems, gaining new levels of visibility into your application networks, which facilitates self-service and collaboration between business and IT* .”

### According to Dao:

"*The Anypoint Platform*™  *It is built from the ground up to allow for reuse, so users and developers can collaborate to drive innovation across the company.*. "
It's important to clarify that Gartner's research is based on the results of numerous studies conducted with organizations that use the tools. Those with the highest ratings are placed in the Magic Quadrant at the following levels: visionaries, niche players, challengers, and leaders. Leader is the highest level in the quadrant.
