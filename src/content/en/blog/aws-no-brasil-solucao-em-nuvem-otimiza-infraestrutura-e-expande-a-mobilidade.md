Digital transformation continues to accelerate within companies, and the use of cloud technologies and solutions like AWS is gaining increasing traction, becoming synonymous with agility and enhanced application security.

In the pursuit of optimized results, with agility, cost reduction, and an efficient security policy, technology leaders and managers are transforming the core business of companies amidst the process of application development and infrastructure migration.

Companies seeking cost reduction continue to invest in cloud computing, as solutions like AWS deliver excellent resource optimization and foster innovation in the process of corporate globalization.

The key differentiator is using the cloud as a competitive advantage to have a more agile and secure infrastructure, capable of standing out from the competition.

Another important factor was the maturation process of 5G, which further expands the possibilities for new business opportunities, since through the Internet of Things (IoT) it is possible to invest in vehicle automation, new techniques for the medical field, the financial sector, and data analysis.

See how [AWS](/en/Cloud-computing%3A-5-reasons-to-use-AWS-in-your-company./) can help in the process of migrating your infrastructure to the cloud and how companies can benefit from taking this important step in digital transformation.

## The financial sector is expanding its scope of action through the AWS cloud.

The financial sector has cloud solutions as one of its main investments for service improvement. After the Covid-19 pandemic, bank branches across Brazil experienced a reduction in customer traffic, as many customers began conducting their transactions through mobile devices.

Currently, most banking transactions are already [carried out via mobile phones](https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/radioagencia-nacional/economia/audio/2022-07/56-das-transacoes-bancarias-foram-realizadas-pelo-celular-em-2021) , and with the growing adoption of Pix (Brazil's instant payment system), banks continue to invest heavily in cloud infrastructure.

Through the mass adoption of 5G, it will be possible to take banking operations to a new level of quality and security, investing in a faster, lower-latency infrastructure compared to 4G.

By investing in IoT with a focus on mobile devices, banks will have the opportunity to open up new business opportunities, offering greater quality and security for their customers.

It's worth mentioning that the Brazilian government has already passed a law that [reduces charges](https://noticias.r7.com/brasil/bolsonaro-sanciona-lei-que-reduz-encargos-de-antenas-de-internet-29062022) for investments in IoT. AWS already partners with major telecommunications companies in Brazil and will soon be part of Vivo's 5G infrastructure.

## Why should companies choose AWS and implement a cloud solution?

We mentioned above some of the paths that cloud solutions have taken and how they are strengthening to provide basic services that serve citizens. Developing applications and having an infrastructure focused on cloud computing is becoming an increasingly natural process for companies in various sectors. If it's still not clear, here are 3 reasons to migrate your infrastructure to the cloud.

### 1 – Invest in mobility with the cloud.

Developing applications with a focus on mobility is undoubtedly one of the great benefits for those who want to invest in cloud applications.

Therefore, companies that invest in digital transformation with AWS can make their products and services more dynamic. Financial institutions expanded their scope of operations and improved services with a focus on mobility during the pandemic.

### 2 – Reduce costs with your infrastructure

By implementing a cloud infrastructure, it's possible to outsource server maintenance, thus having a skilled team like that of [Vertigo Tecnologia](/en/us/) , a specialist in AWS solutions.

Furthermore, the cost of a cloud infrastructure is lower than maintaining an on-premises environment, saving not only money but also reducing the need for physical space that usually needs to be adapted.

### 3 – Increase security with a cloud solution.

By hosting on AWS servers, those responsible for company security can define the frequency of backups and have them performed automatically.

In addition to the [security](/en/Cloud-Computing%3A-5-Tips-to-Increase-Security-in-an-AWS-Environment/) of files in the cloud, another important factor is the elimination of physical damage to the database infrastructure. It's common to see cases of fires, floods, or physical damage to local servers, but by having a server in the cloud and with backups configured, companies ensure the preservation of their information.
