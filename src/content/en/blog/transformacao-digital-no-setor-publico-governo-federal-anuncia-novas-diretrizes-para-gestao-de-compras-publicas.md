Proof of this is the intermediation carried out in IT projects by Cofiex, on which we dedicated a recent article, which you can read [here](/en/digital-transformation-in-the-public-sector/).

With the pandemic, the search for technological solutions has increased considerably within companies, and this trend has taken an exponential growth path. The impacts of this demand have affected various sectors, and the [public sector](/en/Why-public-institutions-need-digital-transformation./) is no exception , seeking to modernize its systems to streamline processes and better serve society. Not surprisingly, there is a rigorous and lengthy list of guidelines to be respected when adopting ideal solutions and partners.

In this article, you will learn about the new guidelines for managing public procurement, which marks a new era for the acquisition of digital products and services by institutions. Additionally, you will also find out about the impact on hiring qualified personnel for the implementation of digital transformation in the public sector.

## Understand the new guidelines for public procurement management.

The Ministry of Economy's Management Secretariat published a new ordinance on July 20, 2021, regarding the governance of Executive branch procurement in general. There is now a comprehensive plan to guide the steps involved in public procurement processes.

The ordinance includes documents, guidelines, and competencies, and seeks greater administrative participation in the processes.

See what Cristiano Heckert, Secretary of Management at the Ministry of Economy, said about the new regulation:

*"This rule that is now being formalized is about how the agency organizes itself to make good hires. How it organizes itself in terms of planning procedures and personnel, so that it is able to make good hires."*

The deputy secretary, Renato Fenili, added that the standard is not at an operational level and also covers all the guidelines and key elements of governance in procurement. Check it out:

*“It (the standard) encompasses all the guidelines, the main elements of governance in procurement. It's not at the operational level. That's why we didn't create a normative instruction. And it says the following: for a public body to perform well in procurement, it has to implement certain governance tools. One of them is the annual procurement plan. Another is a sustainable logistics plan, and another is competency management.”*
