The Global Partner Summit 2019/2020, which this year had the theme "Success Through Synergy," held virtually by Liferay, awarded and recognized Vertigo Tecnologia as Liferay Partner of the Year Latam for its performance in the sale and technical delivery of solutions on digital platforms, focusing on customer needs and improving their business results.

This first virtual edition featured important participation from Bryan Cheung, CEO of Liferay, Brian Kim, COO of Liferay, and the Vice Presidents of Sales, Carolina Moreno and Edmund Dueck.

## Global Partner Summit

Given the current pandemic situation, [Liferay](/en/Liferay/) decided to organize, for the first time, a 100% online event in a single day for its entire global partner ecosystem, thus facilitating accessibility and achieving greater reach. The ceremony brought together approximately 300 specialized companies from around the world.

Regarding the long-standing partnership with Vertigo, Flávio Moitinho, Sales Manager of [Liferay](https://www.liferay.com/pt/) in Brazil, commented: “They have followed Liferay's development and have always been by our side, offering the most modern digital experience solutions for various market segments such as industry, government, financial services, consumer goods, and more. The cloud is one of the main global trends in online experience management, and represents a priority area for Liferay. Vertigo is prepared to follow this trend with us.”

The event was presented by Karen Newnam, Liferay's Global Director of Alliances, and Darren Sepanek, Global Head of Partner Marketing, who affirmed the company's objective of always recognizing the partners who best achieve the goals, with total synergy between the community and a focus on solutions.

Therefore, the purpose of the award is to honor the partners who have exceeded expectations in driving Liferay forward and delivering customized solutions that help corporations meet the demands of their users, improve their operations, and generate revenue.

The achievement of winning Vertigo as Partner of the Year Latam was announced by Anne Durey, Public Relations Specialist at Liferay, who highlighted: “Vertigo has been part of the partner community since 2010 and is an important part of all the success we have achieved here in Brazil.” Emanuel Di Matteo, General Director of Liferay Latin America, who was also present at the announcement, congratulated the team: “I am sure that this partnership with Vertigo will prosper for many years to come!”

This recognition positions Vertigo as a global benchmark for large companies in the market seeking to achieve their digital transformation.
