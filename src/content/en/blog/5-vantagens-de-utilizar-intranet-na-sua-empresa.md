In an era where information and time are precious commodities for a company, knowing how to use technology is certainly a differentiating factor. In this post, we will discuss the benefits of having an intranet.

An intranet is a company portal accessible only to authorized users, and it can be used by different types of companies in various sectors. Its primary purpose is to fulfill two objectives:

1) Solve corporate communication problems;
2) Facilitate access to information.
Here are 5 reasons why you should start using an intranet in your company:

## **Different levels of access**

Although the premise of an intranet is to make information available and share it with everyone, you can create access levels according to each user's profile. This means that the information a manager has access to may not be the same as that of an analyst, and so on.

Furthermore, it's also possible to create access levels according to a specific project. This way, important company information is stored with much greater security and practicality.

## **Centralized information**

The information is made available to everyone who needs it, making processes more dynamic. Furthermore, these documents, which previously generated piles of paper, can now be kept up-to-date much more easily.

This results in more accurate and timely information in the hands of those who have the power to decide, helping in faster reactions, since there is a central point that brings together this information.

## **Ease of access**

You might be thinking that storing information in the cloud is great, but what about when you're away from your computer, doing an external task, or traveling?

It was to resolve this type of situation that some platforms now have mobile versions. In the case of [Fluig](/en/partnerships/fluig?utm_source=BlogVertigo&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=5VantagensIntranet) , for example, it's possible to know which actions need to be performed from the task center, which can be accessed via smartphone or tablet. In other words, the employee can check information, approve requests, request reimbursement, and update documents from anywhere.

## **Integration of all areas of the company.**

An intranet is capable of building a new organizational culture, based on the exchange of information and collaboration among everyone.

Departments that rarely interacted can now collaborate on all projects without misunderstandings. Collaborative social networks, such as [Fluig](/en/partnerships/fluig?utm_source=BlogVertigo&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=5VantagensIntranet) and [Liferay](/en/partnerships/Liferay?utm_source=BlogVertigo&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=5VantagensIntranet) , allow everyone to participate, enhancing knowledge and stimulating interaction not only horizontally but also vertically.

## **Latest version in hand**

By using an intranet, your employees can access process data, update it (when authorized), and send it to the colleague who will finalize the request. This way, all your documents are available and up-to-date for everyone, saving considerable time and reducing communication errors. Furthermore, the intranet is not limited to publishing static text and graphics, but also audio and video, which can be a powerful tool for teaching, communication, and learning.

It's important to remember that corporate networks should never be complicated; any employee with even basic internet skills should have no difficulty navigating the intranet. When creating your intranet, keep in mind not only the investment in good software, but also how your employees will interact with the new tool. Therefore, during this transition from offline to online, it's beneficial for the company to focus on training and, above all, encouraging everyone, explaining that this will facilitate the organization's tasks.
