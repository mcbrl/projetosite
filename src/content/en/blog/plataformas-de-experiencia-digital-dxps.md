**Digital Experience Platforms (DXP Platforms)** are an evolution of **Portal Platforms** and the still traditional **Content Management Systems (CMS)** . They are a technological response to the growing demand for personalization in the user experience and also for greater end-to-end management of the customer's online journey.

Every time you access your bank's online banking or your credit card's mileage program, you have access to exclusive, fully personalized information. Possibly, when you access these companies' portals, even without being logged in, the information displayed to you may have been adapted to your user profile. This level of customization is only possible thanks to the evolution of technologies that have changed the way companies communicate with their users virtually.

Digital Experience Platforms (DXPs) offer valuable functionalities for companies undergoing **Digital Transformation.** Therefore, it's important for IT, Marketing, and Innovation leaders to understand what DXP Platforms are, what they do, and how they help create a competitive advantage for businesses.

In this article you will get a general overview of this technology. We will talk about its benefits and applications. Stay with us until the end and get a sense of what this technology can do for your business.

## What is a Digital Experience Platform?

According to the definition by Gartner – a leading consulting and research firm – a Digital Experience Platform, or DXP, is an integrated set of technologies based on a single platform. This technology offers diverse target audiences consistent, secure, and personalized access to information and applications across multiple digital touchpoints.

Organizations use DXPs to create, deploy, and continuously improve websites, portals, mobile devices, and other digital experiences. DXPs manage the presentation layer based on an individual's role, security privileges, and preferences. They combine and coordinate applications, including content management, search and navigation, personalization, integration and aggregation, collaboration, workflow, analytics, mobile support, and multichannel support.

Furthermore, DXPs emerge as a differentiator for companies that want to add value to their business through improved user experiences and technological innovations.

## A new way of interacting with the customer and enhancing the brand.

The evolution of how customers interact digitally has brought about a need to reshape how companies interact with their audience. Companies are increasingly seeking to provide a better user experience and simpler, more effective ways to solve their problems.

Social media and digital channels have increased the importance of having positive user opinions about brands. In the digital environment, information sharing occurs much faster and more efficiently, forcing organizations to improve how they communicate on these platforms and to establish their own unique identity in the eyes of their users.

Users now expect companies to offer the solutions they seek in a user-friendly way, accessible with a simple touch on their mobile phone. Convenience and practicality are key concepts in this new hyper-connected world. This has led companies to converge their services to meet these expectations and align with their own business needs and objectives.

## The importance of DXPs in your business.

Digital Experience Platforms (DXPs) are inherently designed to integrate all business stakeholders, providing users with a pleasant and secure experience.

Liferay , [a](/en/Liferay) leading company in Gartner's Magic Quadrant for DXP Platforms, has the motto *"Make the real digital transformation happen* ." This is because using a DXP platform can cause a real transformation of a company's business operations. A DXP platform helps to put the customer at the center of the strategy and to have a much more complete view of the customer profile, their expectations, and behaviors.

In a presentation titled “More Than Meets the Eye: Partnering Strategies for Real Digital Transformation,” Forrester analyst Liz Herbet states that:

> "True Digital Transformation encompasses both the experience level and the core operations."

In other words, it's not enough to just have an eye-catching website or a mobile app. Transformation is necessary at every stage and process, focusing on user experience, improving how solutions are met, and refining how they are presented.

## The advantages of using a DXP

When we think about the benefits of a DXP platform, the obvious mental association is increased revenue. This is because when a company manages to provide a better digital experience for its users and obtain valuable analytical information about them, it tends to increase its competitive advantage, better retain its customers, and sell more.

However, the use of a DXP goes beyond that. It's also a way to connect all of a company's communication channels: social media, apps, and portals. The idea is that these connections strengthen the brand's identity, since each channel complements the others.

Digital Experience Platforms also help provide important business information through **analytics,** bringing a new source of information about the target audience. The data can be analyzed by the company, strengthening the decision-making process.

**Digital transformation** commonly leads to the optimization of a company's processes and resources. Often, the company becomes more flexible, making work processes less resistant to internal changes, resulting in outcomes that are more aligned with business objectives.

## How to start implementing a DXP

A DXP platform can be a powerful tool to support a company's digital transformation process. However, this process needs to be based on business processes and models that are consistent with the robust proposition that such a tool can offer.

For this reason, a company maturity analysis is recommended to ensure that the adoption of this platform aligns with the company's current situation and strategy.

Concluding that your company is ready to take this step towards Digital Transformation, it's time to plan the project scope to identify the requirements the tool needs to meet the company's business demands.
