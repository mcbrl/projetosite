## The reality of computing has changed.

Adopting [DevOps](/en/DevOps-and-Kubernetes%3A-an-efficient-and-complementary-duo./) allows for faster communication between development teams, enabling them to respond to customer needs more easily.

The bottleneck that plagued both sectors for years can now be overcome through the adoption of best practices, which further increase the agility of processes and the analysis of delivery results.

**See how Cloud Computing and DevOps are defined:**

Cloud computing is centralized in nature and provides DevOps automation with a standard, centralized platform for testing, deployment, and production. However, in the not-too-distant past, the distributed nature of some enterprise systems did not lend itself well to centralized software deployment. Using a cloud platform solves many problems with distributed complexity.

The automation that DevOps offers is cloud-centric. Many public and private cloud providers already have DevOps support built into their platforms, including integration and continuous development tools. These existing integrations reduce the costs associated with on-premises DevOps automation technology and also provide robust, centralized governance and control.

At this point, cloud computing plays an important role, as many development teams involved in this process find that governance facilitates problem-solving and reduces the frequency with which problems occur. All these processes are easier to control through the cloud.

Cloud-based DevOps reduces the need for accounting for resources used; furthermore, the cloud enhances usage-based accounting and tracking of resources used by application, developers, data, etc. By leveraging cloud resources, tracking development resource costs becomes easier.

## Advantages of applying Cloud Computing and DevOps

Combining cloud computing and DevOps effectively offers numerous advantages, including increased team efficiency, strengthened security policies, and enhanced agility and flexibility.

In addition to these advantages, there are other interesting points we can highlight that your company will benefit from:

- Optimized infrastructure processes;
- Quality assurance through automated code validation;
- Say goodbye to redundant code and applications, and ensure backups are done correctly and securely.
- Improvements to the communication channel and integration and orchestration between the development and infrastructure teams;
- Mature and collaborative development stages. Teams are able to maintain a constant exchange of information, thus avoiding rework and noise in the project.

Applying Digital Transformation by aligning the benefits of Cloud Computing and DevOps generates benefits that go far beyond time and money, thus achieving a much higher level of maturity and empowering your teams.

## Because professionals are migrating to the cloud through DevOps.

There is a great need for simplification and acceleration of development processes in the IT market. It is not uncommon to see large companies in the field experiencing difficulties when completing projects or launching products, simply because their IT teams cannot keep up with the dynamism that application development demands.

IT managers often refine application development processes, transitioning through the waterfall model to DevOps, but they know that this alone doesn't solve all the problems.

The time and cost involved in setting up a physical structure ultimately delays the development process, even when an agile methodology is applied. This type of action leaves teams in standby mode while resources are allocated.

Therefore, DevOps becomes much more powerful when applied alongside Cloud Computing, considering that the resource allocation process becomes much more agile, allowing the project to gain in scalability, security, and agility.
