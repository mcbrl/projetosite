## Democratization of innovation

The need for integration is growing across all business sectors, particularly in the areas of analytics and data science and customer support.*customer care*).
The technology services of the future will be assembled and composed by the people who actually use them. Tools that enable businesses to specialize in integrating applications, data, and processes through highly intuitive development will be in greater demand.
Solution architects, for example, play a key role in enabling corporate digital transformations, becoming trusted advisors to management teams and increasingly guiding IT purchasing decisions.
Although executives remain in charge of drafting the overall budget, this is increasingly becoming the responsibility of architects, so that senior leaders do not have to be involved in every technology decision.
The best way to achieve democratization is to train employees in API integration, as this not only helps them thrive in their careers but also benefits your business.
API-based connectivity is the most important skill and concept for creating a connected Customer Experience and bringing innovation to the market. As the application network expands through the proliferation of APIs, it becomes possible to accelerate Digital Transformation initiatives.

## Portfolio of strategic solutions

Hyper-specialization has created a large volume of applications, forcing companies to deal with this proliferation of tools. Dozens of platforms are used to manage functions such as finance, customer support, and more. Each of these is supported by a variety of highly specialized applications.
Therefore, it will be necessary to develop integration strategies to extract maximum value from the existing legacy. The norm is that building reusable integration assets will save time and money on future projects, thus generating greater commercial value.
This modular business model allows organizations to leave behind rigid, traditional planning and embrace proactive agility. This new business thinking fosters greater innovation, reduced costs, and better partnerships.

## Automation

Automation is the number one rule for enabling innovation and increasing productivity. However, it can bring challenges to innovation. Several professionals report difficulty overcoming obstacles associated with connecting IT systems, applications, and data without hindering automation initiatives. The answer to this obstacle is APIs.
Each API can be treated as a discrete, independent component that represents a part of a complete business process. To conduct activities, an API negotiates contact between two systems to provide the requested data or services.
The increasing complexity caused by an explosion of interdependent systems, dynamic data, and rising expectations demands a new approach to software. In 2021, we will see more intent-based systems and a new programming model will take over: the declarative model.
In this model, an intention is declared – a desired goal or end state – and the software systems connected via APIs in an application network will autonomously pursue the result.

## API Security

Security is a very important factor in generating customer loyalty, and is therefore one of the main technology investments an organization can make. After all, attacks on web applications are one of the leading causes of breaches.
With more companies migrating to distributed architectures, technology teams need a scalable way to make security fail-safe while managing an increasing number of microservices and containers, which brings greater complexity.
Companies should act as if every person and service (internal or external) could have malicious intent, and therefore implement security protocols to adequately protect their services, applications, and data. Failure to do so will result in more breaches, widespread disruptions, and inflated customer concerns. Prevention is better than cure.
APIs, for example, can be a vulnerability for intrusions. We've already mentioned in another article that, according to Gartner, in 2022, [APIs will be the most frequent attack vector.](/en/best-security-practices-for-using-APIs/) for data breaches of corporate web applications.
When APIs lack adequate security, they can be used to expose data, their communication can be intercepted, and back-end servers can experience downtime due to DoS attacks.
It's essential to implement best practices to keep your integrations and data under strict key protection. Here's how to ensure this security:

- Multifactor authentication;
- Token-based accreditation;
- Digital signatures;
- Public-key cryptography;
- Digital certification.

## Microservices

Microservices emerge as a fast way to build a new Customer Experience by exposing data and functionalities of a specific business entity. With this approach, organizations can quickly adapt to changing customer requests and demands, as well as offer services that create a competitive advantage.
By bringing together various microservices to solve business needs, the much-desired generation of added value is created. However, an organization can only realize the benefits of a microservices architecture by eliminating the custom code and complexity that come from managing all the disparate services.
Just as important as microservices is the [Service Mesh](/en/Why-API-Gateway-and-Service-Mesh-are-complementary-patterns./)A service mesh, or "service mesh," is an infrastructure layer embedded in an application that documents how services interact with each other. Its main goal is to make communication secure, fast, and reliable.
Companies deploying microservices for production will, in some way, need this pattern to scale. Despite being a recent approach, Service Mesh is growing in organizations that use microservices, not for what it adds, but for the complexity it removes. 
This approach allows technology teams to replace all unreliable and incompatible code within an organization, providing infrastructure freedom and cloud connectivity for those teams.
Eventually, Service Mesh is expected to permeate every workload executed by a team, so the technology will become better and easier to use as it matures.
