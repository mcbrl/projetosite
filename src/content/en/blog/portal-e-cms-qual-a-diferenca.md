It's very common for people to be unaware of the difference between CMS or WCM and portal tools when evaluating products designed to enable an online presence (website, blog, intranet, etc.). And, as we know, choosing the right tool is crucial for a company's success. Therefore, it's very important to understand this difference even before starting the search for companies specializing in the development of these types of web applications.
With that in mind, we decided to define the two and point out the peculiarities of each. Follow along!

## **What is CMS?**

A CMS is a web content management system designed to bring a business online with relative ease. At a basic level, a CMS will offer features such as easy content editing, version control (content revisions), media management, workflow management for content approval and publishing, template modification, and an easy-to-use dashboard. Among the best-known options on the market are WordPress, Joomla, Drupal, MovableType, and Tumpler.

## **What is a portal?**

A portal is a software platform used to build websites and web applications. It offers several native and ready-to-use functionalities such as: blogs; wikis; user permissions; forums; document storage and versioning; approval workflows; search tools; connectors to various platforms; and other features. Portals offer several resources that make them an excellent way to create and manage such applications:

- Creating websites by adding portlets or gadgets to specific pages;
- Content based on users' authority levels;
- Public and private pages;
- Community pages.
- Integration with CRM platforms such as Salesforce and SugarCRM.
- Connectivity with other ERPs such as IBM, Microsoft, and Oracle.
- Easily integrates with bus tools: [mulesoft](/en/partnerships/mulesoft?utm_source=BlogVertigo&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=PortalxCMSQualDiferen%C3%A7a), [WSO2](/en/partnerships/wso2?utm_source=BlogVertigo&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=PortalxCMSQualDiferen%C3%A7a) and others.

One example of a portal platform is [Liferay](http://www.liferay.com/pt?utm_source=BlogVertigo&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=PortalxCMSQualDiferen%C3%A7a) , an open-source solution that has [been a market leader for the sixth consecutive year](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/liferay-lider-em-portais-horizontais-pelo-sexto-ano/) . It brings together a set of tools to develop, produce, publish, and manage both the content and the graphical interface of portals very effectively. One of Liferay's important aspects is the platform's stability and security for transmitting and storing information; companies like [Azul](https://www.liferay.com/pt/products/liferay-portal/stories/azul-linhas-aereas?utm_source=BlogVertigo&utm_medium=Post&utm_campaign=PortalxCMSQualDiferen%C3%A7a) , Avianca, Smiles, Cisco, and Mapfre are using the Liferay Portal solution.
