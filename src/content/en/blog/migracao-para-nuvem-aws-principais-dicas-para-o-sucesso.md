Migrating to a cloud infrastructure is always a challenge for infrastructure managers, whether we're talking about [AWS](/en/AWS-in-Brazil%3A-Cloud-solution-optimizes-infrastructure-and-expands-mobility./) or another solution. Therefore, it's important to understand the necessary steps to ensure the entire process is structured and delivers satisfactory results.

Even if your company isn't in the cloud, it can already adopt cloud-native practices so that the change is made through a solid culture, building the foundations to, perhaps in the future, take the final step of implementing the cloud.

We've prepared this article outlining the first steps to migrate to the cloud, as well as some guidelines to ensure the process is done responsibly. Happy reading!

## What are the first steps for AWS migration?

As digital transformation continues to shape the IT market, cloud migration emerges as a crucial step to enhance the agility, efficiency, and scalability of IT operations.

However, this journey requires a strategic and planned approach to ensure satisfactory results. Therefore, there are essential elements to migrating a cloud infrastructure, involving initial planning and continuous optimization.

**Define your Goals and Needs**

Before diving into migration, it's crucial to have a clear understanding of your goals and needs. Determine which parts of your system you want to migrate, the benefits you hope to achieve, and the potential obstacles that may arise in the process.

**Planned Migration Strategy**

Based on the initial assessment, it's time to create a comprehensive migration strategy. Establish a priority sequence for the parts of the system to be migrated, define a realistic timeline, and identify which [AWS](/en/Cloud-Computing%3A-5-Tips-to-Increase-Security-in-an-AWS-Environment/) tools and services will be best suited to assist in the process.

**Infrastructure Ready for Migration**

Before kicking off the migration, it's crucial to ensure your current infrastructure is prepared. This may involve upgrading operating systems, enhancing databases, and conducting a thorough review of security configurations.

**Start the Migration with Precision**

AWS offers a range of options and services to simplify migration. One path to consider is leveraging the AWS Application Migration Service, which automates the application migration process. Alternatively, you can rely on support from specialized partners who bring in-depth knowledge and experience to ensure an efficient migration.

**Continuous Monitoring and Optimization**

While in the migration process, it's essential to monitor the performance and security of the parts of the system being migrated. Make adjustments as needed and optimize your cloud infrastructure to ensure the migration is completed successfully.

## How do I know my level of maturity in cloud computing?

Vertigo Tecnologia offers [Cloud Native Assessment](/en/cloud-native-assessment/) , which indicates the maturity level of your company and also where it should begin its implementation strategy.

Through this quick test, IT managers will be able to get a basic idea of ​​the company's cloud maturity level and know how to take the first steps towards modernization.

Furthermore, it's worth mentioning that, to assess the current state of your infrastructure before transitioning to a cloud-hosted platform, you can follow a few steps. See below:

**Understand your workloads.**

Conduct a thorough inventory of all your ongoing activities, encompassing applications, databases, and physical or virtual storage systems. This approach will provide a comprehensive overview of what is needed for the migration.

**Analysis of Performance and Capacity Requirements**

Assess the current performance and capacity needs of your existing operations. This includes analyzing the usage of resources such as central processing unit (CPU), memory, storage, and bandwidth. This data will help determine the resources required in the AWS cloud infrastructure.

**Safety Assessment and Compliance with Standards**

Ensure that your current infrastructure complies with current security and regulatory requirements. This may include reviewing security policies, access control systems, data encryption procedures, and adherence to specific industry regulations.

**Identifying Links and Integrations** 

Recognize the interdependencies and connections between your ongoing activities. This will ensure that all connections and integrations remain functional during the migration to the AWS cloud environment.

**Analysis of Structure and Expansion Capacity** 

Evaluate the current configuration of your activities in terms of structure and question whether it is compatible with the cloud platform. Consider the possibility of redesigning or optimizing the architecture to take advantage of the resources and benefits offered by AWS, such as automatic scalability and high availability.

**Conduct Migration Tests**

Before embarking on the full transition, it is advisable to conduct preliminary migration tests in test or pilot environments. This will allow for the early identification of potential problems and the possibility of adjusting the migration strategy, if necessary.

Furthermore, it is crucial to consider exploring supplementary resources, such as the guidance provided by [AWS](/en/Cloud-computing%3A-5-reasons-to-use-AWS-in-your-company./) and assistance from AWS migration specialists. These resources can provide targeted guidance and best practices for evaluating your current infrastructure.

## Poorly planned migration to the AWS cloud: what are the risks?

When a poorly planned migration to the AWS cloud platform occurs, several setbacks can arise, so check out some of them to avoid.

**Security Challenges** 

A migration without a solid plan can lead to security vulnerabilities, including data loss and unwanted exposure. It is crucial that security and compliance requirements are thoroughly assessed before initiating the migration.

**Excessive Spending** 

A poorly planned architecture can generate considerable expenses in computing resources. It is essential to align cloud activities with needs and avoid a hasty and inadequate transition.

**Compromised Performance**

A poorly managed migration can lead to performance issues, such as slow processing and downtime. It is essential that performance and capacity requirements are carefully examined before initiating the migration.

**Impact on business**

Inadequate planning can negatively impact operations, resulting in the inability to access vital data and applications. Conducting migration tests in controlled environments before a full migration is an important step.

**Integration Difficulties** 

A disorganized transition can lead to complications in integrating migrated workloads with other applications or systems. Identifying interdependencies and connections is crucial before initiating the migration.

To avoid these risks, it is essential to meticulously plan the migration to the AWS cloud platform. This includes assessing goals and requirements, preparing existing infrastructure, configuring and initiating the migration, monitoring and optimizing the entire process, and leveraging additional resources such as AWS prescriptive guidance and assistance from AWS migration specialists.
