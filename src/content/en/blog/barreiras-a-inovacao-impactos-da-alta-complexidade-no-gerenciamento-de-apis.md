The technology market has developed at an accelerated pace in recent decades. Disruptive innovations emerge constantly, generating new business opportunities and transforming how companies operate. However, despite all the technological advancements, there are still barriers that prevent some companies from innovating and being competitive. These barriers can create a technological debt that takes years to overcome. One of these barriers is the high complexity in API management.

APIs are fundamental for system integration, allowing different applications to exchange information securely and efficiently. However, API management can be extremely complex, generating a high technological debt for companies.

In this article, we will discuss the impacts of high complexity in API management and how this can affect business innovation and scalability. We will also present solutions to reduce complexity and enable companies to use APIs efficiently and reliably.

## How does a lack of API management impact innovation?

Poor API management can hinder innovation in a company in several ways. Firstly, it can lead to unnecessary duplication of effort, as different teams may end up working on similar projects without realizing it. This can result in wasted time and resources, delaying the delivery of important projects.

Lack of control over APIs can lead to data fragmentation, making it difficult for teams to access information relevant to their projects. This can lead to flawed decisions and delays in project execution, which can negatively impact company performance.

It is also important to emphasize that APIs can be vulnerable to cyberattacks and, without adequate controls, companies may allow unauthorized individuals to access their APIs, which can result in security breaches, loss of sensitive data, and legal penalties.

To ensure innovation and competitiveness in the market, companies need to guarantee proper management of their APIs, focusing on the security, performance, and interoperability of the solutions developed.

## What are the impacts of high complexity on API management, and how can businesses scale reliably?

The high complexity in API management can have several negative impacts on a company's business, mainly on its ability to innovate and adapt to market changes, consequently causing a technological debt. Among the main impacts are:

### Access control and API management

Lack of access control can lead to security problems such as data leaks, and can also harm the quality of service provided to customers. To ensure security, companies need to implement granular access control, allowing only authorized users to access APIs, and adopt a comprehensive approach to API management that includes defining clear security and access policies, as well as using advanced access control technologies such as two-factor authentication and data encryption.

Furthermore, companies need to carefully manage their APIs to ensure they are only used for authorized purposes.

### Governance between those who consume and those who provide information.

Governance between those who consume and those who provide information is another challenge related to the complexity of API management. This is because different teams may have different expectations regarding the quality and availability of APIs.

Without proper governance, APIs can be misused, causing problems such as resource overload and negatively impacting the end-user experience.

To address these challenges, companies need to adopt a collaborative approach to API management, involving both development and business teams throughout the process.

### High cost to use resources

The complexity involved in managing APIs can increase development and maintenance costs, which may make some solutions unfeasible, especially for smaller companies.

API management can be a complex task, especially when a company needs to deal with multiple APIs from different vendors and teams. This can lead to technology debt, which is a debt that accumulates over time due to a lack of system updates or maintenance.

The high complexity in API management can have significant impacts on a company, such as difficulty in reliably scaling the business. This can occur due to performance or availability issues, which can affect the user experience and, consequently, customer satisfaction.

Furthermore, such complexity can increase operating and maintenance costs, reducing the company's profit margin. This can make it difficult to implement new projects or expand the business. To reliably scale the business, efficient API management is essential.
