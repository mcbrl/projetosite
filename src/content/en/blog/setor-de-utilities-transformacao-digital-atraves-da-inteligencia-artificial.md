## Understand what the Utilities Sector is.

The utilities sector includes companies that provide services in the gas, electricity, and water industries—so-called essential services for society—and are known for their stability, reliability, and technology.

Utility companies can be either public or private, each subject to special regulations, primarily aimed at controlling service prices.

Society depends directly on the services provided by these sectors, whether at home, at work, or on the street. Because of this dependence, there is a consensus among consumers and the companies themselves on the need for a modern structure to provide good service in an agile and efficient manner.

Until a few years ago, the main means of communication with call centers was through phone calls, which often resulted in endless waiting lines and delays in resolving simple problems.

With the advancement of technology, the landscape has changed, and companies in the utilities sector are increasingly adopting advanced technologies.

## Hyper-automation and artificial intelligence integrated into chatbots

The use of artificial intelligence in the utilities sector should aim to enhance the employee experience, not eliminate their interaction within the project. Development teams and support staff should work together with automation, promoting a culture of accessibility and opportunities for continuous improvement in integrations.

When we talk about hyper-automation, we go beyond a simple solution that interacts automatically at your command; the concept is deeper than just assigning repetitive manual tasks. It's about automating any business process using a combination of RPA (Robotic Process Automation) and other technologies such as Artificial Intelligence.

Therefore, there are various levels of complexity when we talk about bot integration. Depending on your customer's pain points, a major advantage of chatbot solutions is their ability to learn more about the business and improve how they interact with customers as their use increases.

Chatbot solutions are increasingly focused on providing inclusive and helpful customer service. The utilities sector heavily utilizes automation in customer service, representing a growing trend in the use of artificial intelligence for this purpose.

## The role of chatbots in the Utilities Sector

Through artificial intelligence, it is possible to create customer service chatbots to automate order request processes, expedite document delivery, send negotiation proposals, provide duplicate invoices, and offer various other functionalities.

We can take as a practical example of the use of chatbots in the utilities sector the [results obtained by Neoenergia](https://www.neoenergia.com/pt-br/sala-de-imprensa/noticias/Paginas/assistente-virtual-ganha-novos-servicos-negociacao-debitos-whatsapp.aspx) . The adoption of integrations with artificial intelligence allows the customer to quickly request more than 70 services, such as power reconnection, and has already provided more than 7 million service requests in 13 months of use.

Another major demand from users in the utilities sector is the need to negotiate debts through [channels such as WhatsApp and Messenger](/en/Messaging-APIs-are-great-allies-for-your-omnichannel-strategy./) , something that is already a reality for many companies in the industry.

Users are gaining increasing autonomy in decision-making, as artificial intelligence tools are becoming quite accessible to a large part of the population. Chatbot solutions are becoming increasingly intuitive and integrated into users' daily lives, proving to be powerful tools within the utilities sector.
