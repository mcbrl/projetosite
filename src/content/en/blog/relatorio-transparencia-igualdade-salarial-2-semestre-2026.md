In accordance with the provisions of **[Law 14.611](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2023/lei/l14611.htm)** , of July 3, 2023, Vertigo publishes the Transparency and Salary Equity Report between Women and Men for the second half of 2026.

This topic is very significant and extremely important for society as a whole, and we are happy to practice equal pay and uphold the respect that women deserve. Without the contribution and talent of these professionals, we would not have achieved the success and reached where we are today.

## **Vertigo's numbers**

According to the report, the median contractual salary for women is equivalent to 90,1% of that received by men, while the average monthly remuneration for women is equivalent to 82,9% of that received by men.

Vertigo's active workforce is comprised of 41,0% women and 59,0% men.

Across major occupational groups, women's pay relative to men's pay is distributed as follows:

- Executives and Managers: average monthly compensation of 69,0% and median contractual salary of 76,2%.
- Professionals in Higher-Level Occupations: average monthly remuneration of 83,6% and median contractual salary of 88,9%.

Check out the **[transparency](/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Relatorio-Igualdade-SalarialLote_2026_2.pdf)** and equal pay report for men and women. The data refers to the first half of 2026.

**Source:** MTE – eSocial, Monthly RAIS (June/2026) and Emprega Brasil Portal (August/2026)
