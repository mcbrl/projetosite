The leakage of confidential information is one of the main problems faced by companies today. With easy access to technology and the growing need for collaboration, information is more exposed than ever. The result is the exposure of confidential data that can harm not only the company's image, but also lead to serious financial consequences.

One of the main causes of data breaches is the lack of governance over sensitive organizational data. This is directly associated with high employee turnover. When professionals leave the company, whether through dismissal or voluntary departure, they take with them knowledge and information that can be used against the company. Even with confidentiality agreements and other protective measures, the possibility of data breaches remains a real threat.

The impacts of leaked confidential information go far beyond the loss of credibility and reputation for the company. The financial consequences can be equally disastrous. In addition to the cost of correcting the problems caused by the leak, companies can also be held liable for data breaches and be fined by regulatory bodies.

In this article, we will explore the impact of leaked confidential information on increased company costs, discuss how information exposure can lead to serious financial consequences, and how companies can protect themselves from these risks. We will also discuss best practices for data security and the importance of employee awareness and training to prevent the leakage of confidential information.

## How can leaks of confidential information increase costs?

With the advancement of technology and the increase in data storage, information protection has become a priority for companies in all sectors. Confidential information, such as customer data, research projects, marketing strategies, and financial information, is extremely valuable and needs to be protected against leaks.

However, leaks of confidential information still occur frequently. When this information falls into the wrong hands, companies can face serious problems, including lost revenue, reputational damage, regulatory fines, and increased costs.

The financial impact of a data breach can be enormous, causing companies to face direct and indirect costs associated with data recovery and the restoration of security systems, in addition to the costs of regulatory fines and lawsuits resulting from the breach. There may also be expenses associated with lost business and damage to brand reputation, which can affect long-term revenue.

As mentioned, data breaches can increase regulatory compliance costs. Companies may face fines and financial penalties if they fail to comply with data protection regulations, which can result in additional expenses to remedy breaches and implement appropriate security measures.

The financial impact of a data breach can be significant, but the long-term effects on brand reputation and customer trust can also be devastating. When a company is responsible for a data breach, the loss of customer trust can affect the company's revenue and future growth.

According to the IBM Cost of a Data Breach Report, the average cost of a data breach in Brazil is over R$4,77 million. The same report indicated that the average time to identify and contain a data breach is 277 days, which can further increase costs for the company.

Therefore, it is crucial that companies adopt a proactive approach to protecting their confidential information, implementing robust security policies and educating employees on security best practices.

By adopting a well-planned data security strategy, companies can reduce the risk of information leaks and protect their brand reputation, regulatory compliance, and long-term financial results.

## What are the impacts related to the leak of confidential information?

Information security is a major concern for companies today, as the leakage of confidential information is linked to numerous problems. Understanding these impacts is crucial to grasping the seriousness of the situation. See below:

### 1\. Difficulty in knowing what the real security needs are.

Many companies struggle to understand the true security needs of their information, which can result in system vulnerabilities. A lack of proper risk analysis can make a company a target for attacks and intrusions.

A lack of investment in information security can be one of the main factors contributing to the leakage of confidential information. When a company is unwilling to invest in protecting its data, it becomes more vulnerable to attacks and potential leaks.

### 2\. Difficulty in protecting sensitive employee information due to high employee turnover.

Another major problem linked to the leakage of confidential information is the difficulty in protecting sensitive professional information due to high employee turnover. When a professional leaves the company, they may take valuable information with them that could harm the business.

The company needs to adopt measures to protect sensitive information, such as implementing security policies, access controls, monitoring suspicious activities, among other actions.

### 3\. Difficulty in maintaining a secure environment between legacy systems and newer platforms.

Many companies face challenges in maintaining a secure environment between legacy systems and modern platforms. This can make the system more vulnerable to attacks and intrusions.

The company needs to adopt security measures to protect its systems, such as implementing firewalls, recurring updates, data encryption, among others.

### 4\. Difficulty in containing the attack within the service before it is escalated to the entire operation.

Another difficulty faced by companies is preventing the attack from escalating beyond the service, that is, from reaching the entire operation. When the attack is detected late, the damage may already have been done, which can lead to financial losses and damage to the company's reputation.

Therefore, it is necessary to adopt security measures to detect and contain attacks in their early stages, such as implementing security monitoring, log analysis, penetration testing, and other initiatives.
