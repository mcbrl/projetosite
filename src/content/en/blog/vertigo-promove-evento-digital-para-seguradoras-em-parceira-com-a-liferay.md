On October 20, 2021, the first edition of **Liferay Insurance Day** took place , a digital event focused on the insurance segment.

The meeting, promoted by Vertigo Tecnologia in partnership with Liferay, discussed the power of self-service portals for insurance companies, helping to improve the customer experience and address the challenges of digital transformation in the insurance sector.

The event was attended by representatives from some of the largest companies in the industry, such as Prudential, Zurich, Mongeral, Sulamerica, Porto Seguro, Europ Assistance, Suhai, among others.

## Speakers provide an in-depth analysis of the market and present solutions for the segment.

Our **CEO, Bruno Badini** , opened the event by talking about the importance of the partnership between Vertigo and Liferay, as well as contextualizing Vertigo Tecnologia's role in the market and how it can be the right-hand partner of major leaders in the journey of Digital Transformation for insurance companies.

The event featured speakers **Gustavo Figueiredo, CTO of 88i Seguradora Digital** , and **Bárbara Silva, Solutions Architect at Liferay**.

**Gustavo** **Figueiredo** spoke about digital transformation in the insurance sector. As one of the main points of his talk, he emphasized the importance of an interconnected value chain between DevOps, APIs, and people, as a critical success factor for innovation in the industry.

The speaker also drew a parallel between startups that "were born digital," comparing them to traditional companies in the market that have been operating for decades and need a different strategy to change their traditional business practices and adapt them to the digital world.

According to Gustavo, everything depends on a stage of [digital maturity](/en/projects/DevOps-maturity-index/) within each company, but the main point is to have the user at the center of their strategy.

Next, **Bárbara Silva** gave a presentation on DXP, Liferay's digital experience platform, showing how the tool can support insurance companies in creating better digital experiences for users.

The speaker emphasized that, when using [Liferay DXP](/en/dxp--cms--or-portal%3A-which-is-the-best-tool-for-creating-impactful-digital-journeys/) , the customer is at the center of the experience, as the platform integrates with legacy systems, providing personalized experiences through information gathering.

"The platform is intuitive and eliminates the need for the operator to know programming codes. Through it, it is possible to create filters to enhance experiences, merging information to generate personalized dashboards," revealed the speaker.

At the end of the event, Vertigo and Liferay opened a space for guests to ask questions about the platform and understand how it helps in the business innovation journey and improves customer experience.
