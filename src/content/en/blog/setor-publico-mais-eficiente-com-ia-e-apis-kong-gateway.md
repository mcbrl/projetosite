The adoption of new technologies like Kong Gateway in the public sector is no longer an option: it's a necessity. The growing public demand for more accessible, faster, and more efficient services is pressuring government agencies to evolve their systems.

In this scenario, technologies such as Artificial Intelligence and API integration play a central role, especially when organized under a robust, secure, and scalable architecture.

This is where Kong Gateway, a Vertigo Tecnologia partnership, comes in, offering one of the most efficient solutions for managing and exposing APIs in the public sector.

Let's understand the challenges faced by the public sector and how the use of these technologies can reduce costs for public coffers and optimize services for the benefit of the population.

## **The main challenges facing the public sector in the digital age.**

Despite the technological advancements of recent years, the public sector still faces structural obstacles that hinder the delivery of quality digital services. Identifying these pain points is the first step in understanding where and how technologies like AI and APIs can generate real value.

### **Legacy systems and lack of interoperability**

Many public systems were built decades ago using monolithic technologies, without any concern for future integration. As a result, different agencies are unable to communicate seamlessly. The lack of interoperability leads to rework, hinders data sharing, and impairs the efficiency of public policies.

### **Limited citizen experience**

Public services often require citizens to navigate between portals, applications, or even in-person services to resolve simple issues. The lack of integration between channels and databases compromises the user experience and contributes to digital exclusion.

### **Security and Compliance Risks**

With increasing digitization, the risk of sensitive data leaks also increases. Many systems lack robust authentication mechanisms, access control, and traceability, which goes against guidelines such as the General Data Protection Law (LGPD). Furthermore, audits are difficult to perform in environments with weak API governance.

## **How Kong helps solve these challenges with gateway APIs.**

A modern architecture demands control, scalability, and security. [**Kong Gateway**](/en/Kong-Gateway%3A-6-tips-to-improve-API-performance./) is an API Gateway solution that acts as an abstraction layer between internal systems and external users, centralizing API traffic securely and efficiently. In the context of the public sector, this translates to greater agility, transparency, and resource savings.

### **What is Kong Gateway and how does it work?**

Kong is an open-source API Gateway capable of routing, authenticating, logging, and controlling requests to various services, whether legacy services, microservices, or external APIs. Its lightweight design and compatibility with modern architectures (such as [**Kubernetes**](/en/how-to-use-custom-metrics-in-kubernetes-horizontal-pod-autoscaler-hpa/) and microservices) make it ideal for complex and distributed environments, such as those in the public sector.

Among its main functions, the following stand out: authentication control (OAuth2, JWT, mTLS), rate limiting, response caching, observability (logs and metrics), and plugins for security and performance.

### **Kong Konnect and centralized API management**

Kong Konnect is Kong's managed platform that offers a unified view of the API lifecycle. For public bodies, this means the ability to centrally manage hundreds of APIs, with full visibility into consumption, performance, and security.

Furthermore, Konnect allows for API publishing and versioning, application of standardized security policies, complete auditing with traceability, and the creation of public and private API catalogs.

### **API Security with Kong**

Kong offers a range of native security plugins, essential for protecting sensitive data. Among the most used are authentication via OAuth2, JWT or certificates, rate limiting to prevent abuse, encryption in transit, and integrable WAF (Web Application Firewall) plugins. These features allow public bodies to guarantee the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data processed by their APIs.

## **Which segments of the public sector benefit most from the use of AI and APIs?**

AI technologies and APIs can benefit virtually all [**segments of public administration.**](/en/Solving-public-sector-pain-points-with-Liferay-DXP/) However, some sectors have the potential for more immediate and measurable impact.

### **Public health**

The integration of systems between hospitals, primary healthcare units, pharmacies, and agencies such as the Ministry of Health can be facilitated with APIs. This allows unified access to electronic medical records, online scheduling with AI for symptom screening, and real-time epidemiological monitoring.

### **Education**

With APIs and AI, it's possible to connect enrollment systems, academic records, and performance evaluations. Solutions include customized learning platforms, systems that alert about dropout risk, and integration between state education departments and universities.

### **Public safety and justice**

Data integration between police, courts, and prisons is critical. APIs enable the rapid sharing of warrants and incident reports, the use of AI for predictive crime analysis, and the traceability of judicial processes.

### **Social assistance and benefit programs**

APIs allow for cross-referencing income data, CPF (Brazilian taxpayer ID), work permit, and other records. This contributes to the automatic identification of fraud, faster granting of benefits, and the use of AI for predictive analysis of social demand.

## **How to build modern architecture with Kong in the public sector**

Implementing Kong does not require a complete overhaul of existing systems. It allows for a modular and progressive approach, adaptable to the reality of each organization.

### **Exposure layer via API Gateway**

Kong acts as the secure gateway for all APIs exposed by internal systems. It ensures that only authorized users and applications access the services, and facilitates the monitoring of all traffic. This layer is essential for standardizing data input and output, consistently applying security policies, and reducing response time through caching and load balancing.

### **Microservices or progressive modularization**

Even with legacy systems, it's possible to begin a progressive modularization. APIs can be exposed with Kong while the main system continues to operate normally. This allows for gradual integration with new systems, reuse of existing data without rework, and reduced risk during migrations.

### **Native observability and resilience**

Kong collects logs, metrics, and events, enabling the use of tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, or Datadog. This provides complete visibility into API traffic volume, errors, latency, and unauthorized access attempts. This information helps anticipate failures, optimize performance, and ensure audit compliance.

### **AI connected via APIs for real-time decisions**

Once the APIs are organized, it's possible to integrate AI models that operate on this data. Examples include classifying benefit requests based on history, triaging customer service with conversational AI, and recommending public policies based on demand prediction.

## **Strategy for the adoption of Kong and AI in the public sector.**

Digital transformation in the public sector requires strategic planning. The use of Kong Gateway can begin with small pilot projects and be expanded based on concrete results.

### **Diagnosis and prioritization of strategic APIs**

The first step is to identify which APIs have the greatest impact on the services provided. Criteria include access volume, social impact, and regulatory urgency.

### **Creating gateways by domain or department.**

It is possible to implement gateways by functional domain (education, health, security, etc.), which promotes autonomy, but with unified security standards. This allows for scalability and simultaneous control.

### **Capacity building and governance**

No technology works without skilled people. It is essential to create multidisciplinary teams with a DevOps culture, API governance committees, and clear security and versioning policies.
