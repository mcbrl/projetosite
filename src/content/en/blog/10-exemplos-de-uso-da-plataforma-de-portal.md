After presenting [best practices for creating a portal](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/as-melhores-praticas-para-criacao-de-portal/)Now is the time for you to discover which business solutions a Digital Platform can provide.
In this article, you'll see 10 examples of how to use a Digital Platform. They can inspire you when looking for a Portal solution that meets your business needs. Read on!

## Why is creating a portal important for your company?

As companies continue to seek solutions to address communication and collaboration issues, whether between partners, clients, or suppliers, corporate portals have emerged as the modern solution to these challenges. They are excellent choices due to their characteristics:

- To enhance your audience's digital experience, making it easy for them to access information and services;
- Reduced paper costs, transparency in information, ease of creating business indicators, and more;
- They can be accessed via any device, anywhere with extranet or intranet access, offering speed, relevance, and ease of deployment;
- To provide research and collaboration through interactive channels such as forums, chats, and gamification (game dynamics) with the help of secure role-based access;

## Features that make the Liferay platform an excellent choice.

Different forms of portals include intranets, extranets, knowledge management systems, collaboration channels, and others. They offer value to both internal users and stakeholders.
There are several options available today, both open source and proprietary, such as Oracle, IBM, Microsoft, WordPress, Joomla!, and so on. Among these options, we chose the Liferay Digital Platform to work with our clients because:

- Scalability;
- Stability;
- A clear roadmap for product evolution over the next few years;
- Offer integration with other market systems and ERPs (IBM, Oracle, Microsoft);
- To be customizable;
- It should be easy to manage for those who are not in IT;
- Professional support for the tool and more.

## 10 examples of how to use the Digital Platform

### 1 – Portal to boost employee engagement

The idea is to develop an agile, lightweight, and easy-to-use system. The portal solution provided by Liferay (Web CMS) includes a document repository, microsites, and mobility, all within a secure, SSO-based environment.
It is also interesting to add customized performance monitoring features, integrated with sales and HR tools.
**Benefit:**
Thus, a single information repository and constant content and management updates improve employee adoption and motivation.

### 2 – Collaboration Portal

You can also build an integrated, Liferay-based portal for key stakeholders with SSO-based access and 360° collaboration. This can include interactions such as alerts, emails, content, live chat, and surveys.
In a more elaborate step, it can also include technology capabilities such as predictive data research, workflow and inventory management, CRM, and integration with SAP.
**Benefit:**
This will provide employees with a richer experience for exchanging ideas and collaborating, as well as streamlining day-to-day operations.

### 3 – Extranet Portal

Liferay, based on SSO, provides multi-geographic support. If you have a parallel mobile website for product catalogs and supplier management, for example, it's possible to integrate BI, CRM, search engines, and also integrate with Google Analytics and custom databases for customer brand promotion efforts.

### 4 – Support Portal

You can create a portal to manage knowledge in a way that allows users to self-solve problems and also manage corporate knowledge. This can be achieved with a service layer, analytics integration, CMS, product exposure, and learning systems.
You can build an interactive, user-friendly, and highly customizable support portal to make the user experience more immersive.
**Benefit:**
Self-service tools can make resolving queries faster and provide more effective support to your customers and business partners.

### 5 – Learning management portal

By adopting an open-source architecture with Liferay, you can lower the total cost of ownership (TCO) for clients. You can create a portal that offers global collaboration tools between students and staff, including calendars, alerts, a document repository, and search. Furthermore, you can integrate with Google Apps, social media, discussion forums, etc.
**Benefit:**
You can customize the content using the single sign-on concept and still have scalability in this solution and interoperability between the systems you want to connect.

### 6 – Search portal

A multi-geographic search portal helps in business management, facilitating user management and instance management. This is done while also providing a collaboration platform in the form of activity feeds.

### 7 – E-commerce portal

The integrated Liferay solution can also be integrated with an e-commerce solution, offering features such as live chat, a library, and other customer service options.
**Benefit:**
For both B2B and B2C, the portal can be integrated with the company's enterprise resource planning (ERP) software, CRM, etc. This integrates the product catalog, improves customer service and support, and consequently enhances the user experience.

### 8 – Knowledge Management Portal

It is possible to activate a Liferay-based knowledge management system with features such as user entitlement, case submission, workflows, multilingual functionality, powerful search, collaboration channel, and instant response capability.

### 9 – Customized portal for sentiment and trend analysis

Another interesting use is to tie the portal to a middle-tier server and data management system to capture fundamental social signals. This architecture can help uncover valuable information from unstructured social media data.
By analyzing sentiment and trends, the company improves its ability to predict customer behavior, identify primary influencers, and design specific solutions centered around them. Well-executed data analysis from the portal can help uncover business opportunities and weaknesses.

### 10 – Vertical portal for financial services

Using LDAP (Lightweight Directory Access Protocol), a specific type of protocol used to update and search directories running over the TCP/IP layer, and legacy web apps, the Liferay solution handles all knowledge and administration aspects. It's possible to create a highly responsive, performance-oriented financial portal dashboard.
From there, it's possible to improve financial management capabilities with multifaceted research, express access to reports and dashboards. All this anytime, anywhere, and via any connected device (desktop, tablet, smartphone, etc.).
