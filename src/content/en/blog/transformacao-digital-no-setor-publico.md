## The Federal Government will mediate IT projects with Cofiex.

[The Digital Transformation](/en/Why-public-institutions-need-digital-transformation./) process in the public sector is gaining momentum through the **Brazil More Digital Program** , a government initiative to encourage technological innovation within public bodies. Proof of this is that on April 20, 2021, the IDB (Inter-American Development Bank) announced the release of up to [US$1 billion](https://www.gov.br/governodigital/pt-br/noticias/linha-de-credito-de-us-1-bilhao-do-bid-apoiara-estados-e-municipios-na-digitalizacao-de-servicos-publicos) for federal, state, and municipal government agencies that have projects to invest in technology.

But how does the **"More Digital Brazil" program** work in practice? Stay with us until the end of this article, as we will explain this great initiative in detail and how your institution can benefit from this project.

### What is Cofiex and what is its role in public sector projects?

#### Understand how states and municipalities will benefit.

Cofiex (External Financing [Commission](http://www.sigs.planejamento.gov.br/sgs/) ) is composed of different bodies within the Federal sphere, and its Executive Secretariat specializes in matters relating to the international economy. The Commission's objective is to defend Brazil's economic and financial interests ( [SAIN](https://www.gov.br/fazenda/pt-br/orgaos/sain) ).

Cofiex's role is to examine and authorize proposals for public sector projects and programs, as well as to intermediate funds through investments from other countries.

With the goal of integrating public bodies with each other, the Digital Government Secretariat will work with Cofiex to mediate and recommend projects that assist in digital transformation for the benefit of the population. This is expected to result in significant savings in public spending, as well as gains in the integration of systems and projects between states and municipalities.

Through the [Cofiex Dashboard](http://painel-cofiex.economia.gov.br/) tool , you have access to information on projects financed by banks with the guarantee of the Federal Government.
