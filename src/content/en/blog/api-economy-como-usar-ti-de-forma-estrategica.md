You surely know the importance of Information Technology for your company. But have you ever stopped to think about whether your IT team is being used strategically? Often, companies believe that IT should focus solely on internal business activities and in a focused way, when in fact, they should also focus on what is external to the organization, especially the new business models that emerge through technology. The world's largest companies use Information Technology to stay on top. That's what we want to talk about, and this idea is directly related to the concept of APIs, which is transforming the reality of companies, whether they are focused on B2B or B2C. Below, learn more about **the API Economy**.

## **API Economy**

The concept of the *API Economy* is simple and shouldn't be limited to the IT department. In fact, it relates to the entire company's business. Thus, API can mean new partnerships, business models, new sales channels, new communication channels, and so on.

[If you still don't know what an API is, check out our content and understand it in a simple way.](/en/What-is-API-Understand-it-in-a-simple-way./)

Every data transaction between two or more companies aims to generate benefits for the parties involved. The idea is to provide, consume, integrate, and add value to data (in some cases, also products and services) through application **programming interfaces** that create economic value, that is:

- **SAP with Salesforce \[Data Integration\];**
- **Banco do Brasil with Azul \[Miles\];**
- **Brazilian Postal Service with Mercado Livre \[ZIP Code Lookup\];**
- **[Buscapé](http://www.buscape.com.br/) with B2W \[referring to the website\];**
- **[Ford with Google](http://www.ford.pt/ServicosFord/SYNC-Bluetooth-Support/SYNC-com-app-link) \[Developing Android applications for Ford Sync\];**
- **[Take off](http://www.decolar.com/) and companies in the areas \[Referral to company website\];**
- **[Hotels.com](http://www.hoteis.com/) e [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.br/) \[Hotels from all over the world being offered\] and so on.**

## Summing it Up

In the context mentioned earlier, all these partnerships occurred, from a technical standpoint, due to the availability of APIs. These interfaces facilitate collaboration and information exchange between organizations. This generates benefits for platform users. In other words, the end consumer is the one who benefits.

Nowadays, information is everything. The more a company understands that information is the key to the success of its products and services, the more it will stand out in the market. In this context, the agent who can actively help in this process is the IT professional; that is, it is necessary to invite them to think strategically about the challenges facing your company.

It's important to highlight that large companies, such as Google, Facebook, TAM, Ipiranga, Itaú, and others, already work with the idea of ***​​the API Economy*** . However, there are still other sectors that resist adopting this concept, believing that it will create vulnerabilities in the data exposed. Companies in the healthcare sector share this concern due to the critical nature of their operations; however, Amil is a company that is differentiating itself in this market.

## **New context**

APIs not only improve how companies exchange information, but also offer greater security and speed to the process of creating new business models. This allows solutions to reach the market in a short amount of time. The goal is for the information to be used by the company as an advantage; therefore, **APIs** need to be managed and viewed as a new business model.

Of course, an API strategy isn't a binary decision (to use yes or no). There's the concept of **public and private APIs** , but when should you use one over the other?

[Learn more about public or private APIs.](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/api-publica-ou-privada-como-saber/)

There are **free APIs** (Google has some, although it charges in certain circumstances), some with a fixed fee, or others with revenue sharing from that API ( [How to monetize an API](/en/API-monetization-strategy) ). There is no right or wrong, nor more or less modern. What exists are certain information and services that you want your partners to have access to. But other information, you want to keep only for yourself.

In short, your organization needs to discuss the various elements of an API strategy, and then you need a set of governance mechanisms to implement it. In other words, bring your IT department closer and talk about new business models and how IT can help you in this process.

## **Conclusion**

Therefore, your company will be better prepared for this new economic scenario. After all, the digital world is causing enormous transformations in the business world. So, if your IT department is focused only on internal matters, you're already falling behind in the **API economy** . You need to start building an ecosystem, and **APIs are the ideal way to do that**.

As a result, you should look for the following benefits:

- **Expand the company's business and encourage innovative processes;**
- **Explore new and potential clients;**

- **Improve information sharing.**
