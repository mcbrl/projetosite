Adopting a cloud computing strategy requires an analysis of the best solutions available on the market, as well as a change in the business model.

Migrating infrastructure to the cloud has brought numerous possibilities for companies. Beyond optimizing results, it has also generated a new way of working through hybrid models.

With the increased use of cloud computing services, new solutions have emerged, and IT companies have strived to improve their offerings.

We've prepared 5 cloud computing trends for companies that want to implement the digital transformation of their services and optimize results.

Good reading.

## Why implement cloud solutions in my company?

Companies are seeking solutions that generate new business opportunities, increasing the security and scalability of their infrastructure.

When we talk about cloud infrastructure, we associate it with a business model that enables cost reduction and increased efficiency.

It's worth mentioning that the benefits of adopting a cloud infrastructure far outweigh the risks, thus justifying the investment in cloud-native solutions.

Cloud solutions like [AWS](/en/AWS-in-Brazil%3A-Cloud-solution-optimizes-infrastructure-and-expands-mobility./) provide various ways for your company to enjoy the advantages of a cloud solution while simultaneously protecting itself from the risks inherent in taking the step towards digital transformation.

Check out 5 trends for implementing cloud solutions in your company.

## 1 – Kubernetes, implement containerization

Organizing your code into containers provides greater agility and the option of code portability. By organizing your code structure within Kubernetes clusters, for example, it's possible to migrate to cloud-native services efficiently.

Furthermore, development teams can efficiently perform various tests and have more secure ways to perform backups, making the process much more fluid.

## 2 – Internet of Things (IoT)

Along with the arrival of 5G, the implementation of IoT services is gaining significant momentum. This requires a large data storage capacity and the ability to analyze that data.

Through fast and secure environments, companies can incorporate big data solutions for information analysis at a viable and effective cost.

Having cloud-based infrastructure becomes essential for securely storing this information and making it available on various devices.

Now, it's possible to achieve scalability with cloud-native solutions, having a dynamic data allocation space and optimizing company resources.

## 3 – Use of Software as a Service (SaaS)

With the advancement of digital transformation, the use of cloud-based software is keeping pace due to its practicality, agility, and mobility.

When companies implement the software as a service (SaaS) model, they become accessible from any device, requiring only an internet connection.

When migrating to the cloud, user access to this type of solution becomes more dynamic, generating greater business opportunities.

## 4 – Hybrid Cloud

The hybrid cloud is responsible for generating greater flexibility for companies, solving digital security and privacy problems.

By combining public or private clouds, it is possible to create unified management, orchestration, integration, and portability of workloads.

[Solutions like AWS](/en/Cloud-Computing%3A-5-Tips-to-Increase-Security-in-an-AWS-Environment/) provide greater flexibility for developing security policies by combining local and remote storage.

## 5 – Edge Computing

Edge computing operates in such a way that devices are placed as close as possible to perform application processing.

In this way, there is a gain in agility in sending requests to an infrastructure that is far away, which could even be on the other side of the planet.

By using this cloud computing service, it is possible to reduce latency, thus generating resource savings and greater scalability for applications.
