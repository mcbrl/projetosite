Have you heard of Terraform and its features? It's no secret that many companies have struggled to manage their cloud infrastructure at some level.

HashiCorp's Terraform is an open-source tool that enables the creation and modification of multiple versions of Infrastructure as Code (IaC) through secure and efficient processes.

Widely used in [DevOps culture,](/en/DevOps-tools%3A-5-tips-for-you-to-use-in-2022/) one of the tool's main characteristics is the user's freedom of choice, as it is not tied to any provider. Therefore, we are talking about a multi-platform solution.

We prepared this article to show what needs Terraform solves, as well as the challenges of automating your cloud infrastructure, and to present the main providers compatible with the solution.

Good reading!

## Terraform is the right-hand man for many clouds.

Terraform is a right-hand tool that helps multiple clouds, providing a single workflow for all of them.

Therefore, your company's cloud infrastructure, managed by Terraform, can be hosted on various servers, such as Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform, and many others.

Having freedom of choice is important because it provides flexibility and adapts to various business models. The tool's objective is precisely to facilitate and provide scalability for companies.

[HashiCorp's solution](/en/partnerships/hashicorp/) is a tool that delivers infrastructure in code, providing greater ease and agility for the infrastructure team to maintain optimal configurations.

## Can my cloud infrastructure run from anywhere?

One of the main advantages of Terraform is the ability to run your infrastructure in code from different environments, thus providing freedom to choose the provider that best suits your business model, whether it's AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, or other providers.

Cloud providers typically offer their own native infrastructure, each with its own unique characteristics in how code is executed.

As a consequence of the growth in the adoption of [cloud-native solutions](/en/Cloud-native-solutions-for-the-financial-sector%3A-the-challenges-of-digital-security./) , the use of hybrid clouds should increase, making it more efficient to adopt common tools and workflows to manage your company's infrastructure, regardless of the hosting location.

## What does Terraform solve?

The adoption of infrastructure as code has grown considerably over the years.

Therefore, it is a powerful solution that helps solve several problems that hindered infrastructure management in the past.

Setting up a cloud infrastructure means having an automated and scalable environment at your fingertips. This allows you to create and connect servers, and install and configure packages.

Provisioning and configuration should be automated, as the goal is to perform this task in a specific environment definition file.

Through Terraform, it's possible to have your infrastructure as code ready to help teams aligned with DevOps culture generate valuable business for their clients with greater agility and security.

Therefore, implement this type of automation to achieve significant gains in project deliveries and reduce the frequency of errors.

### Easily recreate the environments.

If it becomes necessary to replicate your environment, this can be done continuously and easily. Therefore, if your settings deviate from the ideal standard, problems are easily detected and resolved.

Through IaC, teams can easily create and destroy environments. This is a significant advantage, as with just a few commands you can restore your infrastructure to the desired state.
