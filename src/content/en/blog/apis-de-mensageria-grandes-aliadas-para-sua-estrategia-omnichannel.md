## Advantages of an omnichannel strategy combined with messaging.

[Omnichannel](/en/initiating-its-omnichannel-strategy/) is a strategy that uses a company's different communication channels in an integrated way, that is, through the simultaneous and integrated use of different communication channels.

In this way, it's possible to strengthen the relationship with the customer, whether online or offline. For example, if you are in a physical store, it's possible to access your data through an app via an API and synchronize your information. After that, you can choose between taking the product in person or receiving it at home later.

See how your company can improve customer service by integrating messaging APIs.

- **Customer loyalty** – From the moment your customer receives good service, the chances of keeping them loyal to your brand increase significantly. One of the main factors in customer dissatisfaction is poor service. Whether due to incorrect actions by the service representative or failures in communication channels that make the journey tiring, stressful, and sometimes generate misunderstandings about needs, resulting in a terrible experience.

- **Increased sales** – A satisfied customer is more likely to buy from your company again. Understanding that human relationships need to be clear and provide a unique experience for your customer is crucial. [Omnichannel strategies](https://materiais.vertigo.com.br/ebook-omnichannel-abordagem-urgente) They have the ability to make this a reality.

- **It improves your company's branding.** – As the omnichannel experience provides a positive image of the company, the trend is for comments praising customer service on social media and positive reviews to be made in apps or on Google My Business. In both cases, these are examples of "social proof" that encourage new customers to purchase their products.

- **Increased revenue –** As seen previously, if the customer journey is satisfactory and positive, the tendency is for them to place new orders/contracts. Furthermore, if the navigation during the service introduces them to other products, this strategy tends to convert more easily. In the end, your company's revenue will be higher, and the investment in omnichannel will translate into an excellent financial return.

The goal is to ensure your customer faces no barriers between making a purchase, clarifying doubts, understanding the product, and closing deals smoothly.
