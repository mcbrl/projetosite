Traditional companies designed their corporate IT based on legacy processes, systems, and products created in a pre-cloud era. Although these companies occupy prominent positions in their markets, they now compete with companies born in the cloud (such as SaaS models). But the combined adoption of **DevOps** and **Containers** is here to change this scenario. Read this article to the end and **check out our material with incredible case studies about Docker** . Perhaps it's what you need to boost your company's IT. Happy reading!

*Cloud-native* companies and products make full use of cloud economics, typically opting for technologies (often *open source* ) that have greater scalability. Furthermore, they choose resources whose provisioning is simplified and inexpensive (both in production and development).

The competitive disadvantage imposed by an obsolete **technology stack** is immense. There are disproportionate costs for customer acquisition, application maintenance, and provisioning, and there are even more obvious factors in a long list. Risk aversion and aversion to change, coupled with the inability to innovate at a pace compatible with market demand, will be fatal in the long run. The endless cycle of legacy IT obsolescence needs to be broken.

***What path should a corporation take to modernize its processes and tools without jeopardizing its operations?***

### Digital Transformation through DevOps and Containers

At Vertigo Tecnologia, we help our clients build their **Innovation Engine** by adopting the practices of cloud-born companies. This **Digital Transformation through DevOps** accelerates processes, deploys the right tools, and empowers the client to create their innovation pipeline. This breaks the limitations of legacy IT and opens new possibilities for value creation.

Containers not only support the **Innovation Engine** , but also modernize the maintenance and delivery processes of legacy applications. **Containers** are also essential for rapidly deploying **delivery** ***pipelines*** *and "infrastructure as* code" models.

The combined adoption of DevOps and Containers allows, above all, a gradual evolution process with short, concrete steps, resulting in a clear ROI (return on investment) at each stage.

### In the field, the MTA

**The Docker MTA (Modernizing Traditional Applications)** approach is an ideal entry point into the adoption and use of *containers . This is because it demonstrates, in the short term, the financial and operational gains that a* *container-* based application delivery process provides.

Unlike traditional IT vendors, who recommend a high initial investment (like Red Hat with its OpenShift), the MTA approach not only results in valuable learning but also delivers an operational environment already in place. *production-ready,* with negligible investment. Moreover, the MTA model applies equally to legacy applications, not just to *greenfield* of microservices.

MTA implements **the** following at the contracting company:

- **A modern environment for code versioning;**
- **Automation of application build and delivery (CI/CD, part of the basic toolset for DevOps);**
- **A basic infrastructure for container orchestration (*Swarm Mode, Kubernetes*);**
- **Migrating a legacy application as a containerized service, with building and delivery done by *pipeline* automated.**

The main value of MTA is to get all our clients' professionals on the "same page," sharing the same vision for the future of application development and the new processes and roles that are being established. In addition, it demonstrates the practical benefits of *containerization*.
