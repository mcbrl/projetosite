When we think about a **digital experience platform** , we generally associate this term with two fundamental concepts: **customer experience** and **digital transformation**.

This happens for a simple reason: there has been a market shift underway for some years now. Currently, the focus has moved away from simply offering a product and has become something more sophisticated, where creating a good customer experience has become the goal of companies.

With the dynamic pace of technological advancements, organizations have envisioned the possibility of connecting with their audience through various online channels. This adds agility, but also demands organization and a transformation of company standards.

In this content, we will present the connection between **customer experience** and the **digital experience platform** . With this, we aim to dispel the clouds of doubt that often arise when organizational decision-makers consider the best time to migrate to a digital experience platform.

## What is a DXP?

The first thing to do, before deciding to migrate to a Digital Experience Platform (DXP), is to understand the concept behind this type of technology.

The family tree of a DXP begins with CMS – Content Management System, passes through WEM – Web Experience Management and lands in [OMNICHANNEL](/en/initiating-its-omnichannel-strategy-a/) . We will deliberately not dwell on explaining the previous concepts, but it is worth noting that these tools accompany both the evolution of available technological resources and the demands that made them necessary.

That being said, it's time to better explain the concept of a Digital Experience Platform.

A DXP is software that comprises a set of integrated technologies. This technology offers diverse target audiences consistent, secure, and personalized access to information and applications through various digital touchpoints.

With this tool, organizations can **create, deploy, and continuously improve websites, portals, mobile devices** , and other digital experiences. DXP manages the presentation layer based on an individual's role, security privileges, and preferences . They combine and coordinate applications, including content management, search and navigation, personalization, integration and aggregation, collaboration, workflow, analytics, mobile and multichannel support.

With all these features, a DXP creates the perfect scenario for organizations to exponentially improve their communication channel with their customers, providing incredible digital experiences through technological innovations.

## When should your company use a digital experience platform?

We are in the era of Digital Transformation, where both companies and users are increasingly consuming technology in a simple and dynamic way. Therefore, it is natural that there is a migration process towards models that use digital platforms to leverage their businesses.

Although it may seem like an obvious move at first glance, the company needs to understand that this is a broad process and is not exclusively linked to the IT area.

It is the role of company decision-makers to assess the maturity of their business in order to decide whether they are prepared for a complete transformation of their operations or whether the focus should be solely on marketing and brand promotion.

Having clarity about your priorities is not an easy task, which is why many companies turn to research and specialized consulting firms to meet these demands and obtain these answers from a more strategic perspective.

Understanding the target audience's profile, analyzing their online behavior and their buying journey, are fundamental factors when developing a communication strategy that provides the customer with an experience that generates satisfaction and engagement.

When deciding to implement a DXP, the company needs to consider the business objectives that the platform aligns with, evaluating whether the company has diverse and heterogeneous target audiences, traffic volume, and/or needs to develop digital businesses on the platform.

A DXP supports, in addition to the Portals layer, business processes such as providing services to the user, integrating intranet and extranet.
