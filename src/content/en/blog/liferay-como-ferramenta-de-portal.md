## Advantages of the Portal Platform

A system can be either proprietary or open source, and the licensing method will define the freedom the company has to modify, redistribute, and compile the product. Proprietary software, such as Windows, cannot be modified or redistributed, while [free software](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/5-vantagens-de-usar-software-open-source/) , which usually has a lower cost, can be easily adapted to the user's needs. In the case of intranet and web systems, the advantages are even greater. Among them, we can highlight:

### Simplified Interface Development

Some platforms simplify the development of internal and external websites, especially those that allow users to authenticate to access personalized services.

### Secure Single Sign-On (SSO)

Access your content and applications from a single place. You can launch all your different systems together with a single login.

### SOA

Some portals are developed using the SOA concept, which is an architecture designed to make the best use of your available services. Integrate your HR, accounting, sales, and any other important data source.

### Workflow

It's possible to incorporate workflow into any portlet by customizing a workflow API. This allows for a better end-user experience; saves your development team time by reusing administration tools; and enables more seamless integration with workflow engines.

### Search Tool

The success of a portal depends heavily on how content search appears to the user and how easy it is to find information within it. This feature also helps build the portal's accuracy, relevance, and reliability when employees need it most. Content indexing and advanced search options (wildcards, AutoSuggest) are important items for the current needs of corporate search. Popularizing the use of metadata to be added as content, so that the search engine can determine if one result is more relevant than another, is another important step. Making the search box visible on every page view is important to encourage the use of this tool.

### Installation and maintenance costs

Some portal tools, being open source, become a solution that requires less investment both for installation and maintenance.

## Liferay in the construction of portals & intranets

A [intranet](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/5-vantagens-de-utilizar-intranet-na-sua-empresa/) It has become a highly valuable tool for companies seeking to unite employees and partners in the pursuit of new solutions. It's important to emphasize that the centralization of information is also vital for those implementing an intranet. It allows employees to easily access company information, business rules, newsletters, and other relevant data. A good intranet, therefore, should have simple navigation and focus on efficiency and usability at all stages.
In this scenario, the [Liferay](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/liferay-lider-em-portais-horizontais-pelo-sexto-ano/) It allows companies to create their portals or intranets using a single tool. It enables modifications to be made directly to the source code, creation of portlets, making the system more suitable to their needs. Furthermore, it's possible to create multiple websites within the platform, integrate blogs, databases (whether Oracle, Microsoft, or IBM), communication protocols between departments, content approval rules, a document library, Salesforce integration, and much more.
Companies can benefit from the high level of customization offered by this tool. Furthermore, administrators can edit the intranet interface to maintain the visual identity of each website; in other words, you can have 10 websites on a single platform, and they don't have to be identical.
