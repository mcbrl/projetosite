Time is running faster and faster, and with it new technologies emerge while others become obsolete. Therefore, the need for any company to become digital is only growing!
This digital acceleration is quite recent, since less than a decade ago organizations were focused on improving the *data mining* and virtual collaboration in their [Traditional IT.](/en/Traditional-IT-vs.-Modern-IT%3A-The-main-changes-in-relation-to-continuous-delivery./)
For companies that are now entering the market and whose foundation is based on concepts related to [DevOps,](/en/What-is-DevOps/) The journey has been smoother because they don't have many obstacles like complex and often highly bureaucratic and rigid corporate networks. For those that have been established for some time and are based on traditional IT concepts, the transition can be more difficult.
With Digital Transformation, companies are migrating from *data centers* Static and traditional, moving towards a more dynamic world with *cloud-native*, *private cloud, multicloud*, heavily based on technology platforms such as [Kubernetes](/en/Docker--Kubernetes--Mesos--container-orchestration./)Some of the reasons for this transition are:

- Seeking resource optimization;
- The need for greater scalability and resilience;
- Simplified maintenance;
- Access to new technologies.

Many organizations are replacing traditional resources with collaborative tools, solutions as a service, and management applications to accelerate Digital Transformation. However, obstacles in traditional IT still hinder innovation, such as the following:

## Obstacles to automation

Traditional IT has insufficient infrastructure maturity to support business digitization. Automating processes requires... **To streamline, optimize, and automate every step of a business.**.
It is necessary to include automation throughout the entire value chain, whether in employees' daily work routines, customer acquisition, sales closing, product manufacturing and shipping, financial accounting, or metrics monitoring.

## Outdated view of the role of IT.

Unfortunately, many companies still don't see their IT as a competitive and strategic differentiator for their business. For these managers, Digital Transformation is a fad or something fleeting, restricted to certain segments of the company or only part of the business.
Having an IT department isn't enough to become an agile or digital company. If your innovation area is isolated from the rest of the organization, while the company culture and management still have an outdated mindset, be prepared: your company runs the serious risk of falling behind.
So, how can corporate IT transform from a bottleneck into an enabler of innovation? Read our article and find out!

## Difficulties in the transition between realities

With the transition to the world *cloud native,* Nothing is thrown away; there's no need to start from scratch. There should be a bridge from legacy systems (databases) to modern services (web applications or...). *screen*).
There are several obstacles that an organization needs to overcome when transitioning from traditional IT to modern IT. These include:

### Provisioning

In a static world, the IT team knew and was familiar with all the servers they had (they even had names! :-D), while the dynamic world is more heterogeneous and ephemeral.

### Safety

In traditional IT, everything is protected within the four walls of the physical data center. With open-source development, there is a consequent decrease in the perception of trust. Therefore, it is necessary to have more control over the identity of each service. 

### Networks

Previously, all network information was based on the server that was running. In modern IT, the internal IP address changes all the time and is no longer valid; it is also necessary to have the service identity.

### Orchestration

Dedicated and homogeneous infrastructure is transformed into a dynamic infrastructure, with greater support for different technologies.
The challenges of transforming your corporate IT may seem daunting at first, but we can assure you the results are worth it! Therefore, we're here to help you through this process. 

## How to begin?

### Mapping of services and systems

To truly begin Digital Transformation, it's necessary to understand what the company already has internally. **Knowing exactly what value your services, systems, and devices are capable of delivering.**...as well as empowering teams to better explore existing possibilities.
Often, what causes a network to become slow, for example, is the poor allocation of resources due to a lack of strategic vision regarding the technological environment. The development of *assessments* Global systems allow for knowledge of all IT tools and their resources.

### Operating capacity

After mapping the resources (systems, services, and devices), it is necessary to determine if the best conditions are being offered for them to function at the desired speed and efficiency.
A modern, high-performance IT system depends on a **good data network with high availability and speed**.

### Integrated visibility

When a user opens a support ticket, it's most likely because they can't resolve the situation on their own, as they can't see where the problem lies. Therefore, support needs to have a 360-degree view of the IT infrastructure, with the solution at hand for any type of problem.
A large portion of incidents are related to high network resource consumption. To have a comprehensive map of what is being consumed, the best approach is to use a technological solution specifically designed for this purpose and that allows... **To collect accurate and intelligent information about the consumption of applications and devices.**

### monitoring and optimization

To reach a [**A complete view of your IT.**](/en/Why-observability-and-the-future-of-systems-monitoring/) This should become routine to ensure improved data traffic and application response times. Decision-making by IT managers, network administrators, and developers is facilitated when the necessary visibility is available.
This allows for the necessary corrections to be made, from properly allocating resources and isolating the root causes to strengthening security and ensuring user satisfaction.

## Solutions

A transformation *cloud-native*When implemented in a thoughtful and gradual manner, it has reduced and controlled risk. Success also depends on the involvement of everyone in the organization and its partners.
To conclude this process, it is important to develop and implement the right strategy with the most appropriate solutions for the identified obstacles. These are:

- **Infrastructure as code** It allows the same infrastructure to be replicated in different environments and platforms;
- O **provisioning** ***self-service*** It enables the reuse of infrastructure code blocks;
- **Provisioning in governance** It allows the development professional to establish business rules to decide what can and cannot be provisioned;
- **Centralization of access to "secrets"** (passwords, access keys) brings more security to applications;
- A **Orchestration** It helps when there are more services to *deploy* than virtual machines.

## Final Thoughts

*The cloud-native* approach requires investment and a new way of thinking, with new development paradigms. While many conventional concepts remain necessary, such as good design and automated testing, making this transition opens numerous doors to innovation for your corporate IT. Some of the main benefits gained include:

### Improved User Experience

Transforming the customer experience is the most visible process among the initiatives that are part of Digital Transformation. Investing in this area improves knowledge about the consumer, revises the buying journey, and increases revenue.

### Simplified operational processes

Through process digitization, employee training, and performance management, it's possible to obtain benefits, since automation allows companies to focus their teams' efforts on more strategic activities.

### Business model diversification

Organizations are not only changing how their functions operate, but also redefining how teams interact with each other and even the scope of the company's activities. In other words, it is even possible to seek new business models through... *cloud-native*.
Your company's transformation process can be executed with rapid results that justify this decision and its continuation. Corporate IT can cease to be the enemy of transformation and become its main catalyst.
A **Cloud Native Transformation** It accelerates the development of solutions and simplifies their maintenance. Your company begins to develop a new culture, practices, and tools that, combined with autonomous teams and software supply chain automation, promote frequent changes in a more agile and secure way.
