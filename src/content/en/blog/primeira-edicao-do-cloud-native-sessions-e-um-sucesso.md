Last Wednesday, April 27th, the first edition of Cloud Native Sessions took place, a free digital event aimed at discussing the Cloud Native journey and its benefits for businesses.

The digital meeting featured Richard McElroy, Partner Sales Manager LATAM at HashiCorp, who spoke about the challenges of the Cloud Operating Model and presented tools aimed at companies seeking cloud solutions.

Vertigo Tecnologia's CEO, Bruno Badini, hosted the event, which lasted about an hour and brought together approximately 40 guests from the technology sector representing various companies.

## Market overview and implementation of cloud-native solutions were the main topics discussed.

Richard began his presentation by discussing three important aspects that are highlighted when a company wants to implement the transition to the cloud:

- Timing to marketing
- Security and governance
- Operational efficiency

Within the topic discussed, the speaker also talked about success stories involving HashiCorp's direct involvement through the implementation of cloud-native solutions.

Richard mentioned in one of his reports that his work at the Toronto Stock Exchange resulted in an approximate efficiency gain of nearly 75% after provisioning cloud infrastructure.

### Existing parallels between traditional infrastructures and cloud infrastructures

During the event, a scenario was discussed that drew a parallel between a traditional data center and a dynamic data center, that is, one provisioned in the cloud.

In this way, he highlighted that companies that choose to migrate their infrastructure to cloud-native solutions demonstrate gains in speed, security, and governance compared to traditional infrastructures.

### Each company has its own level of cloud-native maturity and specific needs.

Throughout the presentation, Richard highlighted that each company has its own unique characteristics when it comes to using a cloud-based operating model. It's important to conduct a [technical analysis](/en/us/) to understand the company's level of cloud-native maturity, as well as to develop a strategy for initiating cloud solution implementations.

## Challenges: main customer objections to adopting cloud solutions

> *"What are the main objections that on-premise customers still have to entering a multi-cloud world, and what are the main arguments for reversing the situation?"* asked one participant.

Answering one of the questions asked by the participants, Richard commented on the challenges that HashiCorp and companies in the sector face in reaching the segment of customers who are afraid of migrating to the cloud.

> *“The migration movement already exists; at some point, the company will have to go to the cloud. It’s important to understand the need, the company’s ‘go-to marketing’ strategy. In other words, what are the challenges this company faces? What is its offering? Is it a transformative company, or does it want to reach that level?”* the speaker asked.

Richard concluded by saying that many objections stem from fear and not knowing how to take the first step, and reinforced the importance of the [partnership with Vertigo](/en/partnerships/hashicorp/) , which offers all the know-how to implement solutions according to the client's maturity level.

Bruno Badini added that many companies have not yet begun to discuss the move to cloud migration, increasing the risk of substantial losses when using local data centers.

> *“Even companies just starting out on the cloud-native journey are ready to begin thinking about their infrastructure as code through open-source solutions. This gives them the possibility to grow and eventually acquire an enterprise solution and the necessary support* ,” revealed Bruno.

At the end of the event, Vertigo held a raffle for a Fast Consulting session for participating companies. The winner will receive one hour of consulting to improve processes and identify action points that can optimize results within their business.
