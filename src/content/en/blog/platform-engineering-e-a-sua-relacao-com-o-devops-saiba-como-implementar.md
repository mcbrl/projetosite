When we talk about Platform Engineering, we highlight the practice of reducing the time spent on manual operations by the application development team, representing a [natural evolution of DevOps](/en/Veecode-platform/).

In this context, for example, the use of Platform Engineering and [Kubernetes](/en/DevOps-and-Kubernetes%3A-an-efficient-and-complementary-duo./) go hand in hand, since container orchestration is directly linked to the automation of processes related to hosting, creating, and running applications and websites.

On the other hand, it's also worth mentioning that implementing a platform that optimizes infrastructure development and maintenance processes doesn't literally mean there will be no manual operations in your team; it simply means the development team doesn't need to worry about that process.

Therefore, see how the set of DevOps practices can evolve into a new development culture within the company, leading to resilience, cost reduction, and greater autonomy, provided it has a solid and well-disseminated foundation among the teams. Happy reading.

## What is Platform Engineering?

Platform Engineering is directly linked to DevOps and is a platform composed of tools, technologies, and a set of practices that allow programmers greater autonomy to develop applications quickly and efficiently.

Therefore, Platform Engineering is based on a set of techniques and tools that automate tasks such as infrastructure provisioning, application deployment, and performance monitoring.

Included within this entire package is the core culture of DevOps practices, such as continuous integration and continuous delivery [(CI/CD)](/en/What-is-a-CD-ROM-pipeline-Understand-it./) , the use of containers and their orchestrators like [Mirantis Kubernetes](/en/partnerships/Mirantis/) , and the automation of infrastructure configuration and management, such as the use of [Infrastructure as Code](/en/partnerships/hashicorp/) (IaC) tools.

## How does Platform Engineering complement DevOps culture?

When we talk about Platform Engineering, it's strongly linked to DevOps, since both share the goal of automating processes, reducing dependence on manual operations in software systems, and providing greater autonomy for companies.

Therefore, we can say that Platform Engineering is an extension of DevOps, which focuses on automating operational processes through software and aims to provide greater autonomy for teams.

Therefore, with Platform Engineering, the goal is to create an environment where developers can focus on creating features and solving problems instead of spending time on routine operational tasks such as infrastructure provisioning, deployment, and monitoring.

## The role of Kubernetes within Platform Engineering

Kubernetes effectively complements Platform Engineering, enabling more automated and efficient management of infrastructure and applications, reducing the need for manual operations.

Through containerization using Kubernetes clusters, companies can [replicate their entire infrastructure](https://materiais.vertigo.com.br/case-privacy-dynamics) in an automated way, generating cost reductions and better time optimization.
