We live in a fast-paced world where more and more information is requested, and we are forced to know our customers even better. That's why we need data on everything, but data doesn't always generate information. How do we transform data into information? In this post, we'll talk about creating a single customer view and also provide a link to a video where the CEO of Janrain speaks authoritatively on the subject. It's important to clarify that for this strategy to work, two areas need to be well aligned: marketing and IT.

Three questions help illustrate this challenge:

1.  What are your strategies for gaining relevance and credibility with your consumer?
2.  Is your business and IT structure geared towards collecting relevant consumer information, or are you accumulating more and more data without a clear purpose?
3.  Is your customer information gathered in one place?

Gathering customer information can be a complicated task because this data is stored in different departments, cities, and even countries. New information is generated at each point of contact (whether or not it's a sale), and changes occur frequently. Yes, creating a holistic view of your customer journey can be a huge challenge, but this can be solved if the right IT architecture is used. That's why it's extremely important for IT and Marketing to work together closely.

Below you can access a video where Larry Dreves, founder and CEO of Janrain, talks about how to make customer data relevant to the business. He also discusses the benefits of managing this holistic view of the customer using cloud-based tools. This single view of the customer is coveted by many marketing professionals in different companies and segments. But how do you make it a reality? Go ahead and watch this video!
