**Digital transformation** is one of the hottest terms in the corporate world right now. The term refers to a movement by which companies use technology to innovate, reinvent their business models, and grow exponentially. This action implies structural changes, in terms of infrastructure and software, but also profound cultural changes.

There is no checklist to follow to navigate the path of **Digital Transformation.** But there are technologies that make up these digital journeys, such as APIs, Analytics, IoT, Cloud, Social, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Mobile, among others.

Gartner predicts significant investment in Digital Transformation by 2020. The consulting firm says that 58% of CEOs intend to implement strategies to align business and technology.

Already **IFS World** – a company specializing in software – points out, in a study conducted in more than 16 different countries, that 90% of companies have allocated budgets or have a strong desire to invest in Digital Transformation, Internet of Things technologies, APIs, and Big Data & Analytics.

The concept of **Digital Transformation** emerges at a time of heightened consumer empowerment. You, me, and possibly even your grandmother, are much more discerning when buying products and services. We research and ask many questions before deciding which product or service to buy.

The internet has given us control over the purchasing process. The world is changing very rapidly, and future consumers will be even more demanding than today's. Soon, the generation of digital natives – the so-called Generation Z – will be the majority of the economically active population, and this group does not differentiate between online and offline life. With these considerations, the guiding principle of Digital Transformation emerged: **Customer Experience**.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/Plataforma-Digital-Portal-x-Intranet-x-Mobile-min-1024x683-1.jpg)

### **Far beyond Customer Experience**

Have you ever noticed how easy it is to use certain types of services? Uber, for example, puts a car in front of you after a few clicks on your smartphone. With this action, the user knows the price before requesting the ride, with no surprises.

Another revolutionary example is insurance companies that charge by the hour, not by the month. It works like this: You have a car, which you use to commute to and from work. The rest of the day it stays in the garage. Imagine being charged only for the time your car is on the road? Incredible!

We are not only drawing attention to the technologies that enabled the existence of these services, such as the popularization of smartphones and improvements in geolocation systems.

Digital transformation implies a shift in business logic, making services more flexible and better suited to the customer.

According to **Gartner** , Customer Experience is the new battleground. It's where companies will effectively fight each other for their customers.

**Digital transformation** involves new ways of working, new lines of business, and a deep understanding of the digital economy, making it strictly necessary to thoroughly understand the consumption habits of the target audience. This will likely involve disrupting some current ways of working and their existing lines of business.

Now that we've given a general overview of what it is, let's move forward a bit and discover how to position your company in the digital age.

## **Starting your Digital Transformation**

If you're just starting your digital transformation, there are two things to consider to increase the chances of success in your journey.

First, ensure you have the ability to capture and retain customer information across existing channels, whether through your products or your digital channels. This will allow you to leverage the collected data to conduct in-depth analyses and truly understand your customer, as well as reinvent your offerings to help solve your consumers' problems.

Secondly, don't treat digital transformation as a regular project. These two things are very different. If your success is measured by deadlines and costs, you're setting yourself up for failure. Many initiatives have gone off track due to a false perception of failure, stemming from traditional ways of measuring project success.

A significant cultural shift is necessary, and this implies defining different KPIs. These transitions happen over time and should be evaluated in other ways, such as:

- Increased level of customer engagement.
- Increasing the number of offers that meet customer needs.
- Establishing new business lines that leverage digital technologies, to name a few.

Some organizations will develop a business integration model between consumers, suppliers, and employees to create or exchange goods, services, and social interaction. Others may interact with [**digital platforms**](/en/mobile-intranet-portal-digital-platform/) that will interface with legacy systems from other institutions.

Regardless of the configuration, the strategy should be designed to integrate business and IT needs, as well as establish a vision of collective leadership. Companies must decide what makes sense for their organization and long-term business objectives.

## **Stages of Digital Transformation**

Time to get more practical. Now that you have an overview of the concept discussed in the text, let's look at the steps of **Digital Transformation**.

**As we mentioned at the beginning of this content, there is no single right way to do this. For our instructions, we will use Gartner** 's concepts . The company outlined four steps for a company to become digital. Let's look at them below.

### **1 – Build and explore partnerships**

Future technologies often work in a complementary/additional way to each other. Famous technology companies already operate in this manner. Spotify and Facebook are examples of this. The music streaming app allows login to its platform using a social network account.

Creating business ecosystems or a marketplace should be a top priority for CIOs. It's essential to have an environment that fosters positive interaction between business stakeholders, whether suppliers, customers, or internal systems. A good [API strategy](/en/How-can-an-API-strategy-help-you-switch-ERPs/) can be crucial for this.

### **2 - Plan the IT**

Here we are again with the word "integration." Digital transformation is about exchange between areas. Having the infrastructure and development teams working together is essential for this. [Implementing DevOps can certainly help with this](/en/What-is-DevOps/).

When planning, it is necessary to be aware of which business and IT strategic planning scenarios are likely to occur and to review the implications, limitations, interdependencies, and risks of the strategy as a whole.

### **3 – Deliver value**

In this regard, [**digital platforms**](/en/mobile-intranet-portal-digital-platform/) are the main players. CIOs should pay close attention to them. They are one of the key tools for creating value for employees, customers, and partners.

They need to have a user-friendly and attractive interface for customers. Creating such an environment is not simple if you don't have [the right tools](/en/Liferay/) . CIOs should create a multidisciplinary team focused on strengthening the digital platform strategy and identifying potential monetization tactics and risk impacts.

### **4 – Evolve the business architecture**

Being aware of changes is essential nowadays, and it will be even more so the more ingrained technology becomes in our lives. CIOs must focus on technological growth, but also on ensuring that business architecture evolves to support governance, trust, strategy, reputation, security, and economics.

## **Have you already started your Digital Transformation?**

According to [Stephen Powers](https://go.forrester.com/blogs/solve-the-digital-transformation-puzzle/) , vice president of Forrester – a research firm – **Digital Transformation** is about leveraging digital assets and ecosystems to continuously improve customer outcomes while simultaneously increasing operational agility. The result is a reinvention of your business model with technology at the center of everything.
