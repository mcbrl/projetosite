## See 4 types of cloud

What is the best solution for my situation?

It depends on the context in which your company operates, as there is no single "best solution," but rather the solution that best addresses the problem within your business model.

As part of the Digital Transformation process in technology companies, innovative leaders are increasingly connecting with cloud-native solutions to better understand their business and how to improve the quality of their projects. Check it out.

### **1 – Private Cloud**

A private cloud refers to technology services that are developed and run over an internal or external network (internet) for specific users.

The solution offers many advantages, such as scalability and elasticity, customization, and control of dedicated resources in a locally hosted computing infrastructure.

Companies that prioritize security benefit from private clouds, as they offer a higher level of security and privacy, ensuring that sensitive data cannot be accessed by third parties.

### **2 – Public Cloud**

The public cloud is a model in which the computing infrastructure and services of third-party providers are shared across a public network.

Through this model, companies do not need to maintain an internal cloud infrastructure, as the service provider is responsible for its management and configuration.

In this way, companies that choose this solution can reduce investment costs in their own hardware and infrastructure. Another major advantage is that a public cloud is generally configured more quickly and easily.

### **3 – Hybrid Cloud**

The hybrid cloud gets its name from the combined use of private and public cloud platforms.

The combination of these cloud solutions works together on-premises and off-premises to generate a company's cloud computing services.

The advantage of a hybrid cloud environment is that it allows teams to benefit from the advantages offered by both types of solutions, according to the specific needs of each case.

Therefore, companies that opt ​​for a hybrid cloud can improve their workflow and enhance existing resources efficiently.

For example, it is possible to keep confidential data safe from threats within a private cloud infrastructure, and also add greater processing capacity, network bandwidth, or storage to a public cloud to meet temporary access spikes.

### **4 – Multiple Cloud**

Multi-cloud usage refers to operating any combination of private, public, and hybrid clouds.

Nowadays, there are several cloud-native solutions and services on the market, offering a variety of functions that address different security needs, containerization, customized processing capacity, and more.

Due to the many options and situations that teams face, the sheer number of solutions has led companies to adapt in order to better plan and configure their cloud environment.

To avoid overloading IT teams, who often have to work extra hard to overcome emergency situations, the use of multiple cloud solutions has emerged as a relief and a great help for technology professionals.

## Why adopt Cloud Native solutions?

There are many answers to this question, but when we talk about cloud architectures, we need to mention some relevant points.

Cloud Native is a method for developing applications that allows the use of containerized environments, generating efficiency for teams, financial savings, and greater control over delivery deadlines.

Furthermore, cloud-native solutions combined with DevOps methodology provide more efficient code, eliminating redundancy in application development, all along with the ability to perform backups correctly and securely.

The work routine, as well as the integration between the development and infrastructure teams, will be done naturally, generating better working conditions for both sides through collaborative and seamless development.
