Have you ever heard of the concept of **a Smart *City*** ? The idea involves sustainability, energy, waste management, traffic improvements, integration between public systems, provision of civil services, and so on. Basically, the concept of a "smart city" is to allow the evolution of the use of available resources, interpreting data and transforming that data into useful information for those who live in that city. Many cities around the world are already following this concept. In Brazil, we also have initiatives of this nature. Rio de Janeiro, for example, stands out with the [Carioca Digital](http://carioca.rio.rj.gov.br/) project , implemented by the City Hall in partnership with us.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/shutterstock_196087262-Converted.jpg)

## Smart City: Understand more about the concept.

We don't want to coin an academic concept, but the idea of ​​creating a smart city encompasses a huge number of factors/services that improve the lives of residents. Basically, everything, from a large project to small adjustments, can and should fit together to help create a **Smart** ***City** .* However, it is very important to emphasize that, like any Smart City project *, where the citizen is the focus* , it is very complicated to develop all the useful functionalities at once. In this case, the most important thing is: Think big, but start small and quickly scale your results.

- **Map the services most used by citizens;**
- **Divide the project into phases;**
- **Start version 1.0 of your project. Yes, it won't be perfect from the start, and you will improve it over time.**

It is clear that, to have a project of this magnitude, technology must be the foundation for the development of these solutions. This factor is important and crucial, since many cities have grown in a disorganized way, making it a challenge to provide essential services (such as public transport, energy, water, health, education, etc.) in an intelligent and integrated manner. To facilitate this integration between these different services, there is a project called [the Digital Highway.](http://www.serpro.gov.br/linhas-negocio/catalogo-de-solucoes/solucoes/outras-solucoes/infovia-brasilia) It is the first step for those who want to start providing smart services to citizens.

Around the world, several cities can be cited as examples of Smart *Cities* . Some of them are Las Vegas, New York, Atlanta, [Boston](https://pilot.boston.gov/) , [London](http://www.london.gov.uk/sites/default/files/smart_london_plan.pdf) , and others. In Brazil, there are some projects underway in several cities. However, [Carioca Digital](http://carioca.rio.rj.gov.br/) (Rio de Janeiro) is one of the most significant due to the range of services offered to citizens. The system integrates, in a single place, personalized data from the departments of transportation, education, health, housing, etc.

##  **Carioca Digital** 

The Rio de Janeiro City Hall ( [IplanRio](http://www.rio.rj.gov.br/web/iplanrio) ) created this project in partnership with us. [Carioca Digital](http://carioca.rio.rj.gov.br/) is a living portal, meaning it is constantly being updated with new services that will make life easier for Rio residents. The latest service added is related to the Department of Motor Vehicles (Detran). It is now possible to change the actual offender of a traffic ticket through Carioca Digital. This saves time when transferring points for the transfer of the vehicle.

This **Services Portal** was created in 2014 to integrate all the services of the Rio de Janeiro City Hall in one place, and is therefore constantly evolving. In this way, citizens can access the portal, register, and find a range of useful information for their daily lives. It is possible to report a vehicle violation, check traffic fines with the possibility of changing the actual offender, obtain permits and licenses, access electronic medical records from the Unified Health System (SUS), view their child's report card grades, find information on events at the city's cultural venues, check their credit balance in the Nota Carioca program, and more.

The key advantage is that this information is personalized. Upon logging in *,* the user has access to their personal information. The portal is committed to not disclosing the information of registered users.

## **Benefits**

- **For public officials:**
    - Cost reduction related to the volume of paper stored;
    - Reduction in the cost of sending letters to notify citizens about their requests;
    - Speed ​​in obtaining and disseminating information;
    - Creating a dashboard to track the number of requests submitted, approved, and rejected;
    - The ability to digitally track all stages of the process to improve performance;
    - It allows for a reduction in the number of employees serving the public, allocating them to more critical tasks.
- **For the Citizen:**
    - The possibility of carrying out the procedure digitally, at any time or place.
    - A single point of access for interacting with various city services;
    - Speed ​​in obtaining information;
    - Sending material through the portal in a simple way;
    - Increased transparency in the process steps.

## **Conclusion**

The idea behind [Carioca Digital](http://carioca.rio.rj.gov.br/) and other ***smart city*** projects is to offer greater well-being and quality of life to citizens through technology and the advantages it provides. Therefore, there is a wide range of possibilities for initiating a smart city project. The goal is to enable citizens to carry out activities that truly require their presence, thus generating savings for public funds.
