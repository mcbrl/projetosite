On February 7th and 8th, Vertigo Tecnologia held the first edition of Sales Kickoff, an internal meeting that brought together the company's leaders to discuss the 2023 Strategic Plan.

The event, held in Rio de Janeiro and led by C-Level executives Bruno Badini (CEO), André Fernandes (CPO), and Vinicius Siani (CTO), was a valuable opportunity to integrate the teams and, together, discuss the company's vision, goals, and future plans.

Furthermore, each area of ​​the company had the opportunity to discuss team performance and action plans for the remainder of the year through dynamic demonstrations, with practical explanations and considerable technical knowledge.

## 2023 will be a year of many growth opportunities.

During the meeting, the results from the areas in the last year were presented, along with the changes underway for 2023, the development of new products/solutions, the identification of opportunities for improvement, and the establishment of concrete action plans. 

The outcome of the meeting was extremely positive, and all participants left the event motivated and confident that the path to achieving their goals is being charted in an accessible and responsible manner.

Vertigo thanks everyone for their participation and dedication to the event, because the company's success does not depend on numbers, but on the people who are our greatest asset.
