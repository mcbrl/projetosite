The use of Platform Engineering is a practice that is becoming increasingly popular and can bring many benefits to companies in different sectors.

Perhaps you haven't asked yourself this yet, but what exactly is Platform Engineering and why use it?

In summary, this is an approach that seeks to optimize the development and maintenance of technological platforms, such as systems, applications, and infrastructures. To achieve this, agile methods, automation, continuous integration, and other techniques are used to increase the efficiency and quality of work performed by IT professionals.

But why use Platform Engineering? How can it add real value to your company? There are several reasons why you should start thinking about using this approach. First, this practice can reduce the costs and time required to develop and maintain systems and applications. Furthermore, it can increase the security, scalability, and reliability of platforms, ensuring they can support a large volume of users and transactions without failures or interruptions.

## Overview of Platform Engineering

Platform Engineering is a systematic approach to building and managing software platforms, which are sets of shared technologies and services that support application delivery. This approach is based on software engineering principles and aims to increase the efficiency and quality of software development, as well as reduce associated costs and risks.

Software platforms are increasingly important in companies that develop and deliver software, as they enable component reuse, process standardization, and resource optimization. [Platform Engineering](/en/partnerships/Veecode-platform/) helps to create and maintain these platforms effectively, allowing development teams to focus on the specific functionalities of the applications.

Platform Engineering involves several activities, such as defining the platform architecture, selecting technologies and services, implementing components, integrating systems, and automating processes.

Furthermore, there is ongoing platform management, including monitoring, quality control, troubleshooting, and evolution. This ensures that the platform continues to meet the needs of applications and end users, as well as adapt to changes in the business and technological environment.

In short, this is an essential and [complementary approach to DevOps](/en/Platform-engineering-and-its-relationship-with-DevOps%3A-learn-how-to-implement-it./) for companies that want to create and manage applications efficiently. With a [team specializing in Platform Engineering](/en/us/) and a systematic approach, companies can increase the efficiency and quality of software development, as well as reduce associated costs and risks.

## Benefits of Platform Engineering for your company

In this section, we will explore some of these benefits and how they can help your company achieve better results.

### Agility and Scalability

One of the main advantages of Platform Engineering is the agility and scalability it provides. By using a well-designed and structured platform, it's possible to reduce the time needed to develop and launch new products and services. Furthermore, the platform also allows the company to grow faster and more efficiently, without the need to invest in additional infrastructure.

### Standardization and Compliance

Another important benefit of Platform Engineering is the standardization and compliance it offers. With a well-defined platform, it's possible to ensure that all products and services developed by the company follow the same standards and guidelines. This helps guarantee the quality of the products and services offered, as well as simplifying the management and maintenance of the platform.

### Process Automation

Platform Engineering also enables process automation, which helps reduce the time and cost required to develop and launch new products and services. Automation eliminates manual and repetitive tasks, increasing the efficiency and productivity of the development team.

### continuous improvement

Finally, Platform Engineering also helps the company achieve continuous improvement. With a well-structured and documented platform, it's possible to identify opportunities for improvement and implement changes more quickly and efficiently. This helps ensure that the company is always up-to-date and offering high-quality products and services.

## Challenges and Considerations

When choosing to use an engineering platform, it is important to consider some challenges and factors to take into account.

### Implementation Complexity

Implementing an engineering platform can be complex, both in terms of time and cost. It's important to carefully assess the complexity of the implementation process and ensure your team is prepared to handle any potential challenges. Therefore, having a partner specializing in Platform Engineering, such as Vertigo Tecnologia, is crucial.

### Team Training and Adoption

Adopting an engineering platform may require your team to undergo intensive training. It's important to assess the time and resources needed to train your team and ensure they are prepared to use the platform effectively.

By considering these challenges and factors, you can make an informed decision about whether an engineering platform is the right choice for your company.

## **Which companies can use Platform Engineering?**

Platform Engineering can be used by various companies across different sectors. If you own a company looking to improve the efficiency and scalability of your processes, then Platform Engineering may be the ideal solution for you.

Below, we list some examples of companies that can benefit from Platform Engineering:

- **Technology companies:** Companies that develop software, applications, and systems can use Platform Engineering to improve the quality and efficiency of development, as well as facilitate the integration of different systems and technologies.
- **E-commerce companies:** Companies with online stores can use Platform Engineering to improve user experience, increase sales conversion, and integrate different payment and logistics platforms.
- **Financial services companies:** Companies that offer financial services, such as banks and brokerage firms, can use Platform Engineering to improve the security, efficiency, and scalability of their systems.
- **Telecommunications companies:** Companies that offer telecommunications services, such as telephony and internet, can use Platform Engineering to improve service quality, scalability, and the integration of different technologies.
- **Transport companies:** Companies that offer transportation services, such as airlines and logistics companies, can use Platform Engineering to improve operational efficiency, the integration of different systems, and information security.
