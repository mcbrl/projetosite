Just like technologies such as Cloud Computing and Big Data, APIs are evolving rapidly and spreading to almost every region of the planet, as they enable the development of such solutions. Based on some research, we decided to show in today's post some numbers that prove the accelerated growth in the use of APIs and explain why this technology is evolving so quickly.

## The number of APIs available

In a study conducted in 2012, GetElastic and Monetate estimated that, following the growth in the use of APIs, [By 2016, we would see around 30 APIs.](http://www.getelastic.com/the-rise-of-shopping-apis-infographic/)Furthermore, they stated that 100 new APIs would be launched each week. Another study conducted by... [ProgrammableWeb](http://www.programmableweb.com/apis/directory) It showed that only 2 APIs were added to its directory in 2015. Today, it has more than 18 APIs, while the directory... [ApiHound](http://apihound.com/apifinder) It has over 50 APIs.
As you can see, there is some confusion between the data presented by these studies. This is because they only take into account publicly available APIs, thus not reflecting the growth of private APIs. [\[Public or Private API, how to choose?\]](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/api-publica-ou-privada-como-saber/)Furthermore, many organizations only allow access to their API through premium accounts, making them less visible than public APIs.
Some experts even claim that the growth of private APIs surpasses that of public APIs. Research also doesn't take into account the fact that many developers prefer to use third-party APIs instead of building an in-house solution. In short, clear research on API usage is still lacking. But their growth over the last few years is, in fact, a reality.
As an example, we can cite the case of [MailChimp API](http://blog.mailchimp.com/10m-api-calls-per-day-more/), which in 2007 had only 102 users. But at the beginning of 2016, its most recent version reached the mark of 80 million users. Another example is of [Netflix API](http://techblog.netflix.com/2011/02/redesigning-netflix-api.html)In 2010 alone, this API, provided by Netflix, grew approximately 37 times compared to the previous year. These were just two of the many APIs whose use experienced enormous growth.

## API and its Consumer Market

APIs are no longer seen as complicated or as services developed only by experienced programmers who had to spend years training at NASA. Applications such as [IFTTT](https://ifttt.com/), [Zapier](https://zapier.com/) e [Pipefy](https://app.pipefy.com/login) They are interesting because they have already opened up API functionality to non-developers; yes, they are tools that have APIs, but you don't need to be an expert to worry about the technical aspects, but rather about developing the business solution. If you've ever seen someone cross-posting on Instagram, Facebook, and Twitter at the same time, then you've probably seen IFTTT in action.
While it's true that many extremely popular applications make use of APIs, they generally do so in a way that the average consumer probably doesn't notice. Zapier and IFTTT are among the first to transparently use APIs in a way that the average internet user can understand.
Just as Myspace enabled a generation of amateur programmers by allowing the use of HTML to create personalized profile pages, IFTTT and Zapier are creating amateur API consumers by encouraging them to create their own recipes or Zaps.
What makes these two applications important is that they are growing at the same time as the concept of the Internet of Things.

## B2B Products and Services

Looking at the B2B market, the projection is that APIs will continue to grow and have an increasingly significant impact within each company. A common growth technique for startups and other growing companies is to create integrations with other organizations that are already established or already have a strong following among a specific audience.
Integration, and in this case, APIs, continue to be a key factor for B2B organizations! The idea of ​​"connection/bridge" is fundamental for many digital businesses. For example, 41,4% of respondents surveyed by SmartBear said that integration with existing tools is the first point they evaluate, as they estimate their potential use through APIs.

## The growth of the API economy by technology 'sectors'.

Every sector of technology depends on APIs in some way. Below, we list some of the main areas that have been impacted by APIs in recent years:

### Internet of Things [\[API and Internet of Things\]](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/api-e-internet-das-coisas-entenda-essa-relacao/)

APIs are considered the 'interconnecting' technologies that provide the interface between a wide variety of devices (such as glasses, refrigerators, watches, among many others). In a recent study conducted by SmartBear, [44% of the API providers](https://smartbear.com/resources/ebooks/state-of-api-survey-report-2016/) surveyed said that the Internet of Things will drive significant growth in the use of APIs over the next two years.

### Social Media

Applications like iAlien and TweetDeck, aimed at Reddit and Twitter users, could only be developed thanks to the availability of APIs from these two social networks. This is why, in the coming years, these and many other social networks will continue to provide APIs for developers to build new applications for users and promote their expansion worldwide.

### Bank services

APIs are also contributing to the evolution of personal banking services by creating faster processes and better integration with other services. Furthermore, they are contributing to the emergence of digital wallets. Block.io [,](https://www.block.io/) for example, uses APIs to create a wallet that stores its users' Bitcoins, Dogecoins, and Litecoins in one place. [Nubank](https://www.nubank.com.br/) is another application that uses APIs. [GuiaBolso](https://www.guiabolso.com.br/) is another application...

### Conclusion

What we can conclude is that the world of APIs is growing by leaps and bounds. The number of APIs may not be growing at the rate predicted a few years ago. However, their use shows no signs of stagnation. With the progress of mobile technologies and the Internet of Things, there is no doubt that APIs will continue to mature and more and more companies will use them as a key factor for the growth of their businesses.
