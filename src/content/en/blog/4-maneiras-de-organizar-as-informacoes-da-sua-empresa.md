4 Tools to Organize Your Company's Information. Investing time in data management to obtain relevant information is becoming increasingly necessary, a current challenge. Another much-discussed point is where to store this data and how to find it quickly, easily, and in an up-to-date manner. Below are some of these options and how they can help you with this task:

## **Worksheets**

Using spreadsheets can greatly simplify some internal tasks and facilitate day-to-day decision-making within a company. For example, organizing the sales process can involve a lot of research and planning, conversations with potential clients, and other things. By using spreadsheets, the sales team can better organize this data and break down the process into stages.
This makes it easier to track the results of these efforts and update the most relevant data whenever there is a change. This is just one example, but it can be replicated in any sector according to the activities, ranging from employee records to the company's social media strategy.

## **Dropbox**

Dropbox is a cloud [\-](https://dropbox.com/) based file organization and storage system. In practice, this means you can store all your most important information and documents and access them from any device, anywhere in the world. Furthermore, you can share them with others, making documents public or allowing specific people to view them via secure links.

## **Intranet**

It's true that creating an intranet goes beyond the solutions mentioned above; it's more suitable for those who want to have company information available in one central location and be able to make important decisions quickly. But what is an intranet? Basically, it's an exclusive company portal, a very broad channel that serves as a corporate "meeting point" for sharing information and data.
A portal of this type is generally used to manage projects, share important updates, collect vital data, and set future goals. Beyond organization, an intranet can also have a social impact within the institution, as employees can communicate better, collaborate to achieve common goals, and develop professionally.
