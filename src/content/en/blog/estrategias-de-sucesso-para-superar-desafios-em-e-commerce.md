If you want your e-commerce business to be successful and your operations to be secure, you need to choose the right platform that perfectly fits your current and future needs.

## Website with optimized performance.

Make sure your website pages load quickly, as this impacts usability and search engine ranking. A 100-millisecond delay in page load time can reduce conversion rates by 7%. If the wait is two seconds, the bounce rate can increase by more than 100%.
Google and other search engines consider page load time as a ranking factor for SERPs. Therefore, choose a high-performance e-commerce platform to address this issue, such as \[platform name\]. Often, users face a poor experience on websites due to disparate systems. In other words, creating a seamless connection between different systems is mandatory in... Therefore, it is better to opt for advanced technological platforms that are flexible enough to connect different components.

## High availability and scalability

Scalability is a major problem in e-commerce because it's often overlooked by website owners when choosing a specific platform. However, with time and use, they realize this factor and understand the challenges hidden behind it.
To solve this problem, you should consider flexible approaches that help you respond to market demands more quickly than your competitors.
Spikes in online requests and traffic can bring down a website by overloading its server, causing potentially fatal problems for your business. Closely linked to website speed is its scalability.
Therefore, analyze the requirements of your field to ensure that the technology you invest in is agile, flexible, scalable, and robust.

## Data security 

One of the challenges of e-commerce is ensuring the security of customer information and transactions. When buyers place an order, they are placing their trust in your business. The owner is expected to keep that information secure.
There is a responsibility to these customers, and when failures occur, it can lead to a deterioration in the trust and authority of your brand.
Therefore, it is necessary to take the best measures to avoid vulnerabilities. You should check with your provider to ensure that everything possible has been done to guarantee the security of your customer's information.
In addition, look for more ways to protect your website: implement SSL connections and create robust data storage, encrypting sensitive data, for example.

## Mobile optimization

Your e-commerce site must be optimized for mobile devices. There's no longer room for non-responsive content in the market. If your site is slow, it may not appear, especially for searches from mobile devices. Therefore, you'll certainly lose customers to the competition.
One way to solve this problem is to adopt an approach *mobile-first* Liferay Commerce makes your website fast and user-friendly. It creates new opportunities, monetizes your business, and connects you with your partners and customers.

## Product information management

Importing information, updates, and product maintenance are some of the most time-consuming aspects of online retail. In an offline store, the customer can see a physical product, handle it, and read its packaging. But for e-commerce, the retailer must offer at least an image, description, and list of product specifications.
To perform this update in a planned and optimized way, you need to choose an advanced e-commerce platform with a robust API. This API should allow you to send product information from your ERP directly to your online store without requiring any manual data entry.
Liferay Commerce provides this ease by creating a single point of access for your systems, enabling a scalable solution, as well as user-friendly development, ease of integration, and customization.

## Implementation of SEO practices

Following SEO best practices is essential to ensure the success of your online business. Customers visit your website from various traffic sources, such as Facebook, Instagram, email marketing, search, and more.
Evaluate the product pages you want people to focus on and optimize them first. Use keywords in your product title, description, meta description, alt images, and subheadings.
Additionally, check if your platform is SEO-friendly, which allows your products and their descriptions to be found online more easily when someone searches for them on search engines.
