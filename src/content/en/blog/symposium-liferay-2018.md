On October 18, 2018, the **5th edition of the Liferay Symposium Brazil took place at the Renaissance Hotel in São Paulo** . The event brought together marketing and technology leaders from various companies to discuss the theme "The Next Step for Digital Innovation," featuring lectures, case studies, and immersive experiences with Liferay's new products.

With three content tracks, the event offered the audience the opportunity to deepen their technical and business knowledge. The technical track covered the new features of the recently launched Liferay DXP 7.1 and also presented the new **Liferay DXP Cloud, Liferay Commerce, and Liferay Analytics products** . The business track addressed Digital Strategies and success stories.

Vertigo was the Platinum sponsor of the event, reinforcing its leading role in the Liferay partner ecosystem. Year after year, Vertigo consolidates its position as the specialist with the largest number of successful Liferay implementation, migration, and support case studies. It is the only Brazilian company at the top of the Liferay partner hierarchy and the oldest partner in Latin America.

In the "Digital Strategies" track, Vertigo's CEO, Bruno Badini, spoke on the topic " **Digital Transformation on Stage or Real Innovation."** The presentation addressed the trade-off many technology leaders face when dealing with Digital Transformation. On one hand, there's the imperative need to innovate; on the other, the overwhelming daily demands of keeping operations running. These leaders often have to divide their time between "brain space," budget, talent, and strategy to decide where and how to invest resources and energy.

Badini addressed the dichotomy of bimodal IT, in which the company is, on the one hand, pressured by the market to seek innovation – to guarantee competitiveness and, in some cases, even survival. On the other hand, there is pressure for operational efficiency, meeting KPIs, and the functionality of legacy systems.

In all cases, the company needs to be prepared to embrace innovation. This presentation addressed the **5 pillars** for a company to be prepared for real innovation to happen. In a thought-provoking tone, a discussion was raised about the role of the Digital Transformation leader in bridging the gap between rhetoric and practical results for the company.
