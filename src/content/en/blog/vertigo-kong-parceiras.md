Vertigo Tecnologia and Kong Inc. have partnered to offer the Latin American market their API management solutions for modern multi-cloud and hybrid application architectures. As a leader in the global segment, the solution... [**Kong Enterprise**](/en/Kong-Enterprise-2020-general-availability%3A-the-platform-that-controls-the-complete-service-lifecycle./) It is present in more than 190 companies, with over 150 million downloads. 
Built on an open-source foundation, Kong Enterprise is a complete API management platform that manages the entire service lifecycle, allowing you to secure, connect, monetize, and orchestrate your APIs and their... [microservices](/en/What-are-microservices/).

“By joining forces, Vertigo Tecnologia and Kong Inc. will help large and small companies in Latin America accelerate their journey to microservices and any future architectures,” said Mia Blank, Director of Global Channel Sales at Kong Inc. 

A specialist in Digital Transformation, with expertise in developing complex business solutions for over 20 years, Vertigo Tecnologia will provide its clients with solutions for modernizing application infrastructure and IT management. This includes the adoption of DevOps delivery pipelines and systems integration through Kong's modern architecture, transforming Corporate IT into an agent of change with agility and greater market competitiveness. 

“We are very excited about this partnership with Kong Inc. Through it, our customers will now have access to a *world-class* platform for managing and delivering APIs and microservices in hybrid and multi-cloud deployments, Service Mesh, and emerging technologies. We believe that Kong's approach to APIs and microservices is the way of the future. Together with Vertigo's products and services, we are very excited to be able to deliver even more value and agility to the market,” says Bruno Badini, CEO of Vertigo.

The partnership between Vertigo and Kong will enable all companies seeking the Digital Transformation of their businesses to accelerate the transition with the most modern hybrid application architecture available.
