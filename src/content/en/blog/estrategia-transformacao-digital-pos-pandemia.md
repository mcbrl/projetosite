The pandemic has certainly transformed and boosted the use of APIs to accelerate digital transformation in companies.

Extremely necessary for business automation, APIs enable data integration between corporate systems. Adopting their use leads to the integration and organization of your company's data, as well as alternative revenue streams within your own business.
