Does your company already serve customers via chatbots on social media?

Providing excellent customer service is a key factor in your business's chances of success, whether in physical or online interactions.

The current pandemic context has only consolidated a trend that was already growing in the country: online sales. More than 10 million Brazilians made their first online purchase during this period, according to a survey by the company NuvemShop.

Given this entire scenario, online customer service, whether through websites and/or social media, is gaining increasing prominence, functioning as an important sales tool.

The consolidation of this trend has led customers to search for brands of interest on social media and, from there, follow the sales funnel until purchase. In many cases, this entire process is already being monitored with the help of chatbots.

Therefore, having a presence on social media is essential, as it's the showcase for your business. There you can speak directly and present your products and services.

Now that you know a little about the importance of online customer service, you're probably wondering if your company is ready to serve customers through digital channels, right?

Then this article is for you. We've prepared 5 tips to boost your business with the help of chatbots. Happy reading!

## Why are social media platforms great customer service channels?

Social media, once used exclusively for entertainment, has attracted the attention of organizations as a powerful sales channel.

Companies of all sizes have the opportunity to communicate with diverse audiences and help guide customers through the purchasing process. The ease and popularity of social media are one of the main arguments for corporations to adopt its use, but that's not all.

Through [integration with chatbots](/en/Messaging-APIs-are-great-allies-for-your-omnichannel-strategy./) , it's possible to automate the customer service process and provide support to each customer who gets in touch. Imagine a large flow of messages being answered manually at the same time?

There would certainly be a waiting list and many opportunities would be lost, but with the use of chatbots, this entire approach can be done efficiently.

Furthermore, having a good social media plan aligned with your company's website makes it possible to create campaigns to attract leads that match your business's ideal customer persona.

## Here are 5 tips to provide excellent customer service via chatbots.

### 1 – Don't keep your customer waiting.

What determines the difference between automated customer service via chatbots and social media? Certainly, one of the most relevant factors is the speed of response and problem resolution.

When customer service was handled via email, call center, or solely through online waiting lists with human agents, response times were longer and not always available 24 hours a day. However, with chatbots on social media, the situation has changed.

When users experience unsatisfactory service, delays in response, or no one gets in touch, the chances of your company receiving negative feedback increase.

This negative experience will be passed on to others, negatively impacting sales and generating less revenue. Therefore, focus on providing the best possible response in a short amount of time.

### 2 – Interact with your audience but don't delete comments.

It is essential to implement *community management* for your company's social media channels. Chatbots may not always have the necessary autonomy to respond to some comments, making manual interaction necessary.

Just as serious, or even worse, than not responding to comments is deleting them simply because they contain criticism or dissatisfaction with your services. Using a negative response to reverse the situation is a very powerful strategy.

Through this practice you have the chance to solve your client's problem, but if that's not possible, you can demonstrate that you are providing the best solution for them or even apologize for something.

Companies that do this demonstrate their true value to customers.

### 3 – Your chatbot needs to understand the customer persona.

We know that social media offers the possibility of a more relaxed communication style, without so much formality, and that in some cases it can even include GIFs and memes.

However, not all companies can adopt this type of language, as their business model does not allow it. It is not very common to see law firms using informal language for their communication.

Certainly, if a law firm starts communicating only through memes or very informal language, it will be viewed with apprehension by its clients. Therefore, its social media strategy and chatbot settings need to understand the client persona.

The chatbot can be respectful and adopt a more sober language when addressing this type of customer. Now, if your business is a clothing store for young people, your bot can be designed to communicate in the most appropriate way.

The important thing is to maintain professionalism, not to confuse freedom with a lack of business rules.

### 4 – Teach chatbots how to fulfill requests.

Implementing chatbots is a great solution for offering 24/7 customer service and providing requested information in a practical way, without the need for a human agent.

There are bots that are capable of learning new commands [through artificial intelligence](/en/utilities-sector-digital-transformation-through-artificial-intelligence/) , especially when we talk about solutions with a higher degree of complexity.

iFood, for example, uses chatbots in its customer service strategy on social media; another company that does this is Uber.

It is possible to implement service request functions directly through the social network's chatbot, without the need to open the application.

### 5 – Create redirects to conversion pages.

The time comes when the customer needs to move through the sales funnel, whether to complete the purchase, access a product catalog, or download materials from your company's website.

Configure your social media chatbot to convert leads and direct them to a conversion page.

It's important to have this type of strategy well-defined, since chatbots can perfectly handle approaching customers in an assertive way.
