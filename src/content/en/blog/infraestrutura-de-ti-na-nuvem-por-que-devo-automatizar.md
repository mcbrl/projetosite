## What is the purpose of your IT infrastructure?

Answering this question may sound redundant or even seem irrelevant, but it is fundamental to defining your strategy.

Your IT infrastructure is a key component of your business strategy.

Through it, it is possible to define how processes will be organized, and having an organized sector will have an impact on several fronts, such as:

- Modernizing customer service
- Problem solving
- Automation of methods
- Feasibility of services
- Provision of resources
- Implementation of innovative practices

Therefore, the search for a business model that positions your IT infrastructure as an automated operation becomes a strategy that can optimize routines with precision and agility.

Vertigo Tecnologia has IT tools that are powerful allies in maintaining an automated work environment, thus implementing routines with greater integration between areas and fewer chances of errors.

## Advantages of automating your IT infrastructure

One of the main goals of provisioning a cloud infrastructure is to make it reproducible as code, giving DevOps teams more freedom to plan and allocate resources within workflows.

HashiCorp's Terraform is a tool in Vertigo Tecnologia's solutions portfolio that enables this movement to transform your company.

By implementing infrastructure as code, it's possible to have an implementation and automation methodology that allows the use of tools to create, modify, and manage the infrastructure in a secure and modern way.

In this way, it is possible to have higher quality services, reduced costs, and significant gains while maintaining safety and quality.
