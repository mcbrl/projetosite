Do you know the advantages of using **open source software** ? As the business world and government organizations embrace the open source philosophy, it's becoming clear that the benefits of adopting this concept aren't limited to just price. After reading this, you'll probably wonder why you haven't already started **enjoying the advantages of using open source software**.

Open source is an alternative business philosophy, not just the provision of free software. It's important to note that while the **program's code may be free, it's not necessarily free of charge** . This type of application **can be modified and distributed** **freely to any user.** Let's better understand the advantages of using open source software.

In our content, you will learn about 5 advantages of using open source, and learn a little more about the concept.

## **Advantages of using open source software in your business.**

### **1 – Increased reliability**

In proprietary software, the client receives promises from vendors regarding the high level of security involved in the application. However, there is no way to be certain of anything. On the other hand, if they have open-source software installed on their machine, it is possible to examine the coded code. This is a way to be sure of the product's reliability level, after all, you will know where your information is going. Therefore, **the code is open for viewing**.

### **2 – Greater Interoperability**

Because they are products of different companies, proprietary software tends to be inflexible in its interaction and compatibility. Therefore, those who value the interoperability of software with computers, systems, and users will certainly adapt easily to the advantages of the open source mindset.

When using open-source software, there is greater **freedom to integrate it with other systems** such as ERP (SAP, Oracle, IBM, and others) and databases.

### **3 – Much more extensive sources of support**

In this respect, open source software far surpasses any proprietary software: there are thousands of fan communities. Lovers of the open source philosophy are constantly debating, offering suggestions, and assisting new users in improving the software.

Linux is an example of an open-source system that boasts a wealth of excellent documentation. All of it is free and available across the web. One of the great advantages of using open-source software is the large number of users, thus increasing **community support**.

### **4 – Freedom of customization**

The concept of open source involves maintaining authorship of the original source code. Despite this, it's possible to develop new applications that better suit your needs. **Software customization happens more quickly** , mainly because it avoids the bureaucratic aspects of proprietary software.

### **5 – Reducing the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)**

In most cases, we can say that Open Source **is cheaper than proprietary software** . This is because it has no licensing costs. But what we want to show you in this post is that the advantages of adopting open source in your business are numerous and go far beyond price!
