The integration of systems through APIs began in the United States in the mid-2000s. This business model was spearheaded by giants we know today, such as eBay, Salesforce, Amazon, and Twitter.

Integrating interfaces has been a way to boost digital transformation within companies and has created opportunities for new partnerships.

## The explosion of APIs in the 21st century

You, the reader of this article, probably lived through the technological transition during its greatest leap in a short period of years. Remembering what happened two decades ago isn't so distant if we consider the technological advancements we've had since then. Internet use, for example, began to take its first steps in Brazil in the mid-2000s.

To book a family trip, the standard method used to be by phone or in person at a travel agency, something that is now done easily, quickly, and securely through apps on your smartphone.

Companies have had to adapt and adopt the increasingly frequent use of technology in their operations. If you are still unsure about how an API works, read our article and [understand in a simple way how they function](/en/What-is-API-Understand-it-in-a-simple-way./) , as well as their application in essential services such as Google Maps and Uber.

Imagine a pandemic similar to COVID-19 two decades ago, impacting the global economy and healthcare systems at the beginning of the century or even in the 90s. Technology has always been a great ally not only for e-commerce or the entertainment market, but also for healthcare.

## Use of APIs during the pandemic

The use of APIs during the Coronavirus pandemic has increased considerably due to the scenario the world is facing.

Businesses that were closed to in-person customers had to reinvent themselves and resort to technological solutions to avoid shutting down, such as delivery systems and digital customer service channels, including digital platforms, conversational interfaces, and chatbots.

APIs have certainly been a valuable tool for science amidst the acceleration in vaccine research and production, assisting healthcare professionals in the fight against COVID-19.

Apple [and Google have partnered](https://www.apple.com/br/newsroom/2020/04/apple-and-google-partner-on-covid-19-contact-tracing-technology/) to create an app that helps monitor and track data, aiming to assist health authorities in combating the pandemic. The two giant competitors joined forces for a greater purpose, developing a powerful API that enabled interoperability between Android and iOS devices, providing relevant information to public health authorities' apps, thus helping them to develop preventative and support measures to combat COVID-19.

In the midst of the pandemic, the world has powerful allies in APIs (Anti-Pathological Imaging), and if the Covid-19 outbreak had occurred two decades ago, we would certainly have had greater difficulty in implementing actions to combat the virus.

## Your life could be saved by an API.

Healthcare is becoming increasingly integrated with cutting-edge technologies and driven by Artificial Intelligence (AI).

A set of APIs is capable of working precisely to assess your health in real time, such as heart rate, blood oxygen level, sleep monitoring, and even contacting emergency services in case of a sudden illness such as a heart attack or a domestic accident like a fall in the bathroom.

It is public knowledge on various news websites that an emergency API contained in the Apple Watch has already saved the lives of its users in different situations, such as a man who had fainted in the bathroom and another who suffered a bicycle accident; in both cases, the API contacted emergency services.

Imagine that after a tiring day you involuntarily doze off at the wheel of your car? A danger! There are already APIs in specific cars that detect if you start to doze off, causing the car to stop safely and send a medical assistance alert. Not bad, right?

These were just a few examples of how an API can save and assist people's lives. Undoubtedly, the functionalities often found in your smartphone, watch, or car will continue to improve, enhancing their existing role and learning new features.

When offering a product or service, whether it's a portal, app, or customer service system, it's essential to utilize the concept of APIs integrated into systems to bring a wide range of possible solutions to your application.
