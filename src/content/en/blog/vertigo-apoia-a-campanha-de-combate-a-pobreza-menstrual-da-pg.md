Have you ever heard of period poverty? Today, Vertigo Tecnologia is dedicating a special space within our technology-related topics to address this super important issue. It's about the ["Girl Helping Girl"](https://pg.descubrapg.com.br/always-menina-ajuda-menina) campaign , focused on initiatives that help women and girls in vulnerable situations across various regions of Brazil to access sanitary pads and other essential hygiene products.

The P&G Social Accelerator's Always campaign against Menstrual Poverty is an initiative of Procter & Gamble with other partners and is supported by Vertigo Tecnologia.

Menstruation is a natural process that most women go through, generally from the beginning of pre-adolescence until they reach maturity.

In addition to bleeding, there is increased fatigue, concern about leaks and personal hygiene, and even the use of pain medication during the menstrual cycle. However, not all women have access to these types of care and good health practices.

Learn more about what period poverty is and see how Vertigo and P&G are working to combat this alarming situation in our country.

## What is menstrual poverty?

Menstrual poverty refers to the lack of resources many women face to achieve dignified and adequate menstrual hygiene, including access to sanitary pads.

When someone finds themselves in this situation, it is very common to lack basic necessities, have difficulty accessing appropriate hygiene facilities, and also lack knowledge about how to take care of themselves properly.

Menstrual poverty affects women in situations of poverty and vulnerability, and is very common among homeless people, low-income individuals, and those in socio-educational programs, for example.

### School absenteeism becomes a problem.

When menstrual poverty affects adolescent girls, the psychological and health impacts are not the only problems. School absenteeism is also a growing consequence, hindering the development of thousands of girls.

In a scenario where public education is already so far behind private education, the aggravating factor of school dropout due to menstrual poverty makes the situation even worse.

Approximately [45% of women affected by menstrual poverty](https://pg.descubrapg.com.br/always-menina-ajuda-menina) believe that their education has been severely impacted by a lack of basic resources to manage their menstrual cycle.

So, what can be done? There are several initiatives aimed at helping women in vulnerable situations and affected by menstrual poverty. Vertigo Tecnologia contributed directly to one of these actions; check out more details.

According to the [study](https://www.unicef.org/brazil/comunicados-de-imprensa/no-brasil-milhoes-de-meninas-carecem-de-infraestrutura-e-itens-basicos-para-cuidados-menstruais) commissioned by Always from the Locomotiva Institute:

- 2,9 million current students in elementary, middle, or high school, aged 16 or older, are affected by Menstrual Poverty, with 2.4 million of them experiencing it recurrently.
- 77% of Brazilian women have already had to resort to alternative methods to stop bleeding.

## Vertigo embraces the fight against period poverty with technological support for the P&G campaign.

Vertigo Tecnologia specializes in the development and implementation of digital experience portals, as well as the modernization of IT applications and operations to maximize gains.

**It was through our background in portal projects and operations for the multinational Procter & Gamble that we received the invitation to develop the website for the** [**"Girl Helps Girl"**](https://pg.descubrapg.com.br/always-menina-ajuda-menina) **campaign , within P&G's Discover platform, the company's promotional portal managed by the Vertigo digital agency team.**

Launched in March 2022, the campaign aims to benefit women affected by menstrual poverty and already has the support of several influencers to increase engagement on social media.

The Vertigo team also developed a new page for [P&G's Social Accelerator](https://www.aceleradorapgsocial.com.br/) , which is focused on attracting social projects.

The page aims to raise funds through organizations that wish to contribute to the cause of menstrual poverty in partnership with the Always brand, which specializes in the production of sanitary pads. The website launch event was attended by the press and P&G leadership.

### Always has already donated more than 3 million sanitary pads to the cause.

In 2021, more than 2 million sanitary pads were donated to the Intimate Dignity program in partnership with the Government of São Paulo and distributed to public schools in the region.

In addition, 200 sanitary pads were also donated to the Instituto Educadoras do Brasil and distributed in communities in the northern region of the country.

Several institutions, such as the Red Cross and Casa José Coltro, among others, have benefited from social actions to combat menstrual poverty. This information is presented in an infographic on the Menina Ajuda Menina (Girl Helping Girl) page, as shown below:

## Would you like to help fight menstrual poverty through social projects? Find out how!

P&G's Social Accelerator [will select up to 5 projects](https://www.aceleradorapgsocial.com.br/) to receive funding, via P&G and partners, in support of the campaign to combat period poverty.

Each beneficiary will receive up to 50 reais in cash and a donation of 100 sanitary pads to help women through their initiatives.

Projects must be legally constituted or affiliated, and must possess all the documents listed on the project page.

If you don't have a project selected, but would like to support the initiative, visit the [Always Girl Helps Girl](https://pg.descubrapg.com.br/always-menina-ajuda-menina) page to learn how to contribute to the fight against menstrual poverty.

Don't miss out and help those who need it most!
