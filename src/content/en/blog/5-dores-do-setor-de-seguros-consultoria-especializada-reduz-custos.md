The Brazilian insurance sector is undergoing a profound transformation. Despite billions of dollars invested in technology and a consistent growth trajectory, insurers still face persistent operational pain points that drain resources, limit agility, and compromise competitiveness in a market increasingly contested by insurtechs and digital players.

These pain points are not merely isolated technological challenges. They represent structural barriers that directly impact operational efficiency, increase unnecessary costs, and hinder sustainable growth. Fragmented legacy systems, disconnected digital experiences, costly regulatory compliance, inefficient manual processes, and difficulty in scaling partner ecosystems are recurring problems that require more than just tools: they demand specialized expertise and strategic vision.

In this article, we explore the five main pain points in the insurance industry and show how specialized consulting can transform each of them into concrete gains in efficiency and cost reduction, with a measurable impact on the business bottom line.

## **Why do the challenges faced by the insurance industry require specialization?**

The insurance sector operates in an environment of extremely high regulatory and technological complexity. Standards such as Open Insurance, LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law), and the **[Insurance Legal Framework](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2024/lei/l15040.htm)** create layers of requirements that go far beyond general technical knowledge. At the same time, the architecture of critical financial systems and the need for integration with complex partner ecosystems demand specific and in-depth experience in the segment.

A generalist consulting firm may deliver tools and systems, but it will hardly understand the strategic nuances of the insurance business, the dynamics of underwriting, claims management, the complexity of distribution channels, or the specific governance challenges. The difference between implementing technology and generating real transformation lies precisely in this sectoral expertise combined with execution capacity.

Failing to address these pain points leads to measurable and growing consequences: loss of competitiveness against more agile competitors, operational costs that erode margins, difficulty in meeting increasingly tight regulatory deadlines, and, most importantly, a customer experience that drives customers away instead of building loyalty. The cost of inaction is always greater than the investment in structured transformation.

## **1 – Legacy systems and fragmented technological infrastructure**

Many insurance companies still operate with core systems developed decades ago that function in isolation, do not communicate with each other, and require expensive and specialized maintenance. This is one of the most persistent and costly pain points in the sector, creating a vicious cycle of technological dependence.

### **The problem: when technology becomes a brake on growth.**

Legacy systems create rigid information silos. The underwriting system doesn't communicate with the claims system, which in turn isn't integrated with the CRM, digital channels, or customer service platforms. Each new business need becomes a complex point-to-point integration project, with a high risk of failures, long lead times, and increasing costs.

The lack of modern APIs and the inability to adopt microservices-based architectures prevent the insurer from launching new products quickly, connecting to digital ecosystems, or responding agilely to market changes. Meanwhile, the cost of maintaining these infrastructures consumes an increasingly large share of the IT budget, without generating strategic value or competitive differentiation.

### **How specialized consulting solves**

A consulting firm with in-depth industry knowledge does not propose a complete and immediate replacement of critical systems, which would be operationally unfeasible and financially risky. Instead, it works with progressive and pragmatic modernization strategies, creating layers of abstraction that allow legacy systems to coexist temporarily with modern architectures, ensuring operational continuity throughout the transition.

Implementing **[robust APIs, intelligent gateways](/en/APIS-insurance-and-finance-sector-innovation-with-Vertigo-technology/)** , and microservices allows different systems to connect in a standardized and decoupled way. This enables faster integrations, reduces critical dependencies, and creates the flexibility needed to adopt new technologies without compromising operational stability. The result is an infrastructure that finally becomes a strategic asset capable of supporting innovation, and not a technical liability that limits growth.

### **Impact on efficiency and costs**

Progressive and well-structured modernization drastically reduces the time needed to launch new products, from cycles of months to weeks. Maintenance and licensing costs can fall significantly, as modern systems are more efficient and require fewer dedicated resources. Furthermore, the technological agility gained allows the insurer to respond quickly to regulatory changes, market opportunities, and competitive moves, transforming IT from a cost center into a growth engine.

## **2 – Fragmented digital experience and inconsistent customer journey**

In a market where customer experience has become the main competitive differentiator and selection criterion, many insurance companies still struggle to offer truly integrated and seamless digital journeys across all touchpoints.

### **The problem: disconnected channels that destroy value.**

A customer starts the quote process on the website, tries to continue through the app, needs to call the call center to clarify doubts, and, at the end of the journey, discovers that no one has a complete view of their history and preferences. Each channel operates with different information, distinct technologies, and disconnected processes, creating frustrating and discontinuous experiences that inevitably lead to abandonment and loss of business.

The lack of a unified and consolidated view of the customer prevents any real attempt at personalization, hinders the identification of cross-selling or upselling opportunities, and generates unnecessary friction at critical moments. The result is a digital conversion rate far below its potential, a growing and costly volume of customer service calls, and an experience that drives customers away instead of building loyalty.

### **How specialized consulting solves**

Building truly integrated digital journeys requires much more than technology: it demands a profound redesign of processes with an obsessive focus on user experience. A specialized consultancy maps the entire customer journey at each touchpoint, identifies friction points and drop-off points, and implements centralized platforms that guarantee a single view, clear governance, and a consistent experience across all channels.

These platforms allow customers to move freely between digital and physical channels without ever losing context, while the insurer obtains consolidated data in real time to personalize offers, anticipate needs, and reduce friction. The true integration between marketing, sales, after-sales service, and customer support becomes fluid, data-driven, and genuinely customer-centric.

### **Impact on efficiency and costs**

Truly integrated customer journeys significantly increase digital conversion, reducing reliance on more costly channels such as call centers and in-person service. The reduction in the volume of inquiries and complaints can easily exceed 30%, freeing up valuable resources for strategic and higher value-added activities. Furthermore, the measurable improvement in customer experience directly impacts indicators such as NPS, increases retention rates, and substantially reduces the cost of acquiring new customers.

## **3 – Complex and costly regulatory compliance**

The insurance sector is among the most heavily regulated in the country, and each new rule or regulatory update represents not only significant operational challenges, but also substantial legal and financial risks that can compromise operations and reputation.

### **The problem: reactive compliance that consumes resources.**

Insurance companies must simultaneously comply with a complex web of regulations: Open Insurance, LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law), the Insurance Legal Framework, SUSEP (Superintendence of Private Insurance) regulations, Central Bank resolutions, and other sector-specific requirements. Many companies still approach compliance in a predominantly reactive manner, with permanently overburdened teams executing intensive manual audit, adaptation, and control processes.

This manual and reactive approach not only consumes critical time and scarce resources, but also exponentially increases the risk of non-compliance, which can result in heavy fines, regulatory sanctions, and irreparable reputational damage. Furthermore, each new regulation becomes a lengthy and costly compliance project that paralyzes other strategic company initiatives.

### **How specialized consulting solves**

A consultancy with proven experience in financial sector regulation implements the strategic concept of compliance by design: the technological architecture is designed from the outset to meet current regulatory requirements and have the flexibility to absorb future changes. This includes complete and auditable data traceability, granular and dynamic access controls, automated audit logs, and robust API governance.

Intelligent automation of regulatory processes allows the insurer to respond quickly to new regulations, drastically reducing the time and cost of compliance. API gateways with configurable and versioned policies transform compliance from a one-off, reactive effort into a continuous, proactive, and much more efficient process, freeing up teams to focus on prevention and continuous improvement instead of putting out fires.

### **Impact on efficiency and costs**

The significant reduction in legal risks and the prevention of fines for non-compliance represent immediate savings and protection of brand value. Teams previously entirely dedicated to manual audit and control processes can be strategically reallocated to value-generating activities, such as emerging risk analysis and new product design. Furthermore, the speed gained in adapting to new regulations becomes a clear competitive advantage, allowing the insurer to launch regulated products and capture market opportunities before competitors.

## **4 – Low operational efficiency and manual processes that do not scale.**

Manual and repetitive processes drain critical resources, consume valuable team time, and create a structural barrier that prevents insurers from scaling their operations efficiently and profitably, severely limiting their growth potential.

### **The problem: operations that limit growth.**

Predominantly manual underwriting, document analysis without effective automation, claims management dependent on intensive human intervention even for highly repetitive tasks. These processes not only consume a disproportionate amount of time, but also generate avoidable errors that translate into costly rework, operational delays, and customer dissatisfaction.

The absence of intelligent automation creates an unsustainable equation: the insurer cannot grow significantly without proportionally increasing its workforce, which erodes margins and limits scalability. Furthermore, the lack of consolidated and structured data severely hinders strategic decision-making, as critical information about operations, risks, and opportunities is scattered across spreadsheets, emails, and non-integrated systems.

### **How specialized consulting solves**

Intelligent automation goes far beyond the simple, superficial digitization of forms. It involves the strategic use of artificial intelligence for sophisticated risk analysis, RPA for highly repetitive processes, and optimized workflows that eliminate bottlenecks and accelerate critical flows. A truly specialized consultancy accurately identifies which processes generate the most value when automated and implements solutions that balance advanced technology with appropriate human control.

Real-time operational data analysis allows managers to make decisions based on consolidated, up-to-date, and reliable information, identifying opportunities for continuous improvement, anticipating bottlenecks before they impact operations, and proactively responding to performance deviations. This transforms operations from reactive to predictive and from costly and inefficient.

### **Impact on efficiency and costs**

Insurance companies that implement intelligent and well-structured automation consistently report reductions in the time required for critical processes such as underwriting, document analysis, and claims processing. The drastic decrease in operational errors reduces rework, significantly improves customer satisfaction, and frees up teams for strategic activities. The ROI of automation is measurable and robust, and in many cases begins to be clearly perceived in the first few months of implementation.

## **5 – Difficulty in scaling the strategic partner ecosystem (B2B2C)**

Many insurance companies rely heavily on partners such as brokers, banks, fintechs, and retailers to distribute their products and reach new markets, but they face critical structural challenges in scaling this complex model while maintaining governance, quality, and operational efficiency.

### **The problem: uncontrolled expansion that dilutes the brand.**

Each partner operates differently, often with completely inconsistent digital experiences that weaken the insurer's brand perception and create confusion in the market. The lack of clear visibility into how partners actually serve end customers generates significant compliance risks, hinders consistent service quality management, and makes it impossible to guarantee a uniform brand experience.

Enabling new partners becomes an extremely slow and disproportionately costly process, requiring customized development on a case-by-case basis, complex integrations, and intensive dedicated support. This drastically limits the insurer's ability to expand its distribution network quickly, seize market opportunities, and compete effectively with players who can rapidly scale their indirect channels.

### **How specialized consulting solves**

Truly scalable white-label platforms allow each partner to operate under their own brand and visual identity, while maintaining the rigorous standardization and clear governance defined by the insurer. A consultancy with proven experience in complex B2B2C models implements validated and proven effective frameworks that dramatically accelerate the onboarding of new partners, from months to weeks.

These platforms offer truly centralized management, with complete, real-time visibility into usage, digital service quality, and the performance of each partner, while ensuring automatic compliance with LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law) and stringent cybersecurity standards. The result is a rapidly scalable ecosystem with total control, where the insurer maintains strategic governance without sacrificing the operational agility necessary for growth.

A concrete example of this approach is the implementation of complete white-label portals that allow partners to offer an end-to-end digital experience to their customers, with appropriate autonomy for customization within safe and well-defined limits. This substantially strengthens the B2B2C relationship, reduces dependence on more costly direct channels, and significantly accelerates portfolio expansion with quality and consistency.

### **Impact on efficiency and costs**

The reduction in the cost of enabling new partners can easily exceed 60%, since the replicable and tested framework completely eliminates the need for expensive custom development for each case. The scalability achieved allows the insurer to grow its distribution network exponentially without losing governance, compromising the end-customer experience, or proportionally increasing operational costs. Furthermore, the consistent and high-quality experience significantly strengthens the brand, increases loyalty, and improves margins through the indirect channel.
