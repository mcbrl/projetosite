## **Key challenges faced by companies offering investment portals.**

Creating and maintaining an efficient investment portal goes far beyond visual design. There are technical, operational, and strategic barriers that need to be overcome for the platform to fulfill its role. Among the most common are:

### **Inconsistent user experience**

One of the main reasons for abandoning financial platforms is frustration with the user experience. Confusing interfaces, excessive clicks to perform a simple operation, and a lack of personalization undermine investor confidence. When the digital journey is not intuitive, the customer tends to seek alternatives in the market.

### **Faulty integration between systems and data.**

Many investment portals need to connect to legacy systems, internal databases, ERPs, CRMs, and external APIs. When these integrations are weak or poorly structured, the result is duplicate information, outdated data, and slower processes, compromising both internal efficiency and customer experience.

### **Limited scalability for high demand.**

With the growth of the customer base and the increased use of digital platforms, it is common for portals to suffer from instability or slowness during peak times, such as when markets close or results are released. An architecture without scalability planning can quickly become an operational bottleneck.

### **Security flaws and regulatory risk**

The financial sector is among the most targeted by cyberattacks. Furthermore, companies need to comply with strict regulations, such as the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law). A vulnerable website exposes not only sensitive data, but also the institution's reputation and customer trust.

Through advanced segmentation and behavioral analysis capabilities, a DXP Platform allows you to offer content, recommendations, and features aligned with each user's profile and history. This increases engagement and strengthens loyalty.

### **Native integration and APIs**

The best DXP platforms have APIs and connectors that facilitate integration with internal and external systems. This ensures that information such as balances, transaction history, and performance reports are updated in real time, without overloading operations.

### **Guaranteed scalability**

A modern DXP architecture is designed to grow along with demand. Whether during peak access periods or a gradual increase in the customer base, the platform maintains performance and stability, ensuring that investors can access the portal without interruption.

### **Built-in security and compliance**

In addition to multi-factor authentication, end-to-end encryption, and audit logs, a robust DXP platform allows you to configure security policies aligned with current regulations. This reduces legal risks and protects data integrity.
