Technology, for the most part, is created and developed with the goal of improving our lives. This was the case when the telephone was invented, for example. The **Portal Carioca Digital** was created with a similar objective: to optimize time and effort.

It's a consensus that bureaucracy is one of our country's problems. Public services aren't known for their efficiency, and queues are horrible places. For this reason, the **Portal Carioca Digital** , from the city hall of Rio de Janeiro, sought to bring together as many services as possible that could be done online. In its initial phase, in 2013, there were 35 in total.

The tool that has been a real lifesaver for Rio de Janeiro residents has a name: [the Liferay Digital Experience Platform](/en/Liferay/) . It allows the creation of portals, intranets, and mobile solutions. If you want to learn more about digital platforms, [click here](/en/mobile-intranet-portal-digital-platform/).

Among the services provided by the **Carioca Digital Portal** is the opening of businesses. According to Fernando Ivo Cavalcante, Director of New Business Planning at **IPLANRIO** – the company responsible for IT in Rio de Janeiro – this process has shown great improvement in the capital. According to him, the process of opening a business went from 3 months to, in 57% of cases, just 10 minutes, without any need to visit public offices. Impressive, isn't it?

With the drastic reduction in bureaucracy came a reduction in paper consumption. Fernando Cavalcante points out that if all the paper saved from appeals against fines for preliminary defenses and delegation of offending defendants were stacked up, it would amount to one and a half Eiffel Towers per year! That's a lot of paper, and the environment thanks you.

**Bruno Badini – our CEO** – highlighted the work of the technical team. He spoke about the complexity, due to the heterogeneity of the systems, since the **Liferay platform** needed to communicate with numerous legacy systems of the Rio de Janeiro city hall.
