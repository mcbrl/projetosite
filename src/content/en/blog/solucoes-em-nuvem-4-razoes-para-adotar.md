And you? Are you already using the full potential of the cloud in your business? Check out 4 benefits that cloud computing can offer you and understand why it's essential for your company:

## 1 – Cost cutting

One benefit immediately noticeable when adopting cloud solutions is resource savings. Since, in some cases, you can even reduce or eliminate new IT infrastructure costs, investments in data centers, databases, machine-to-machine system installations, among others, can be reduced or even eliminated from the acquisition plan. Everything is handled by the cloud service provider. There are several companies that can help you with this: [Amazon](https://aws.amazon.com/pt/application-hosting/), [Locaweb](http://www.locaweb.com.br/default.html), [umbler](https://www.umbler.com/br), [equinix](http://www.equinix.com.br/) And more!
Another relevant point is the scalability of this type of solution. As the company grows, adding new users and increasing storage and processing capacity is a matter of expanding the service, not acquiring new physical machines and licenses for those purchased machines. Everything is accessed and provided via the web, which greatly reduces costs and implementation time.

## 2 – Optimize processes

With lower, more predictable and controllable costs, the company can improve its processes since it has technological scalability at its disposal without needing to make large investments, compared to purchasing physical equipment. This ease of cloud computing is mainly helping small and medium-sized enterprises, which until then had to make do as best they could with limited technological resources, and even large companies are not left out of this benefit, since they need to streamline many of their processes.
The mobility offered by Cloud Computing, depending on the service, allows users to access the solution on any device (desktop, smartphone, tablet, etc.). Thus, a sales executive can expedite order processing while in transit, stopped at the airport, or even in front of a client. A developer can access the dashboard to check if their... [APIs are live.](/en/Increase-your-sales-using-APIs./)Much more time is saved, the customer receives the service more quickly, and the company earns more.

## 3 – It makes IT more strategic.

Instead of worrying about maintaining systems, the IT department assumes a more consultative position within the company. In other words, it has more time to propose new solutions for the business and doesn't need to focus solely on tactical activities, those that require technical expertise.
Since updates, storage, backups, and other tasks are the responsibility of the cloud service provider, the IT manager and their team can focus their activities on developing new applications. This represents a new paradigm, elevating the role of the IT department within the company.

## 4 – It provides greater security for the information.

When everything is handled internally, concerns about data security are significant. After all, potential attacks and the loss of confidential information occur within the internal infrastructure, which is detrimental to the company. By contracting a cloud computing service, these concerns are reduced and become part of the service provider's scope of work, as they are obligated to provide updated tools and practices that guarantee information security.
For example, if a company suffers an incident (a fire, for example), its IT infrastructure can be seriously compromised. With cloud infrastructure, this problem doesn't exist, as the information is stored in the provider's processing center and operations don't need to stop due to power outages or the loss of a data center.

## Conclusion

With technological scalability, enhanced security and mobility, an IT team focused on creating strategic solutions, and significant cost reductions, the company becomes much more competitive.
In other words, cloud computing improves productivity, streamlines and optimizes processes, and provides greater communication and market knowledge capabilities. Companies that invest in cloud computing are much better prepared to compete and move towards growth.
