An **intranet** should offer certain functionalities to maximize its potential and justify the investment. These applications will benefit the company's productivity and are therefore important. In this post, you'll discover what your [intranet](/en/5-advantages-of-using-an-intranet-in-your-company./) shouldn't be without!

## Intranet as a content management system

Content management systems (CMS) are platforms that allow content publication. This is one of the functionalities of [Liferay](/en/Liferay-or-WordPress/) and [Fluig](/en/Increase-your-company%27s-productivity-by-using-your-intranet-correctly./) , for example. This ensures that content production is done as on a website. The result of this type of approach includes greater integration and adherence to internet use, since the posted content will become more interesting and focused on the interests of employees.

[Learn here what a CMS is.](/en/Portal-and-CMS%3A-what-is-the-difference/)

An intranet can have its own content management system and, in addition, allow integration with external CMSs. Project announcements, newsletters, and market news are some of the possible uses of the intranet's CMS.

## Search Tool

The success of a portal depends heavily on how content search appears to the user and how easy it is to find information. This feature also helps build the portal's accuracy, relevance, and reliability when employees need it most. Content indexing and advanced search options (wildcards and AutoSuggest) are important items for the current needs of corporate search. Popularizing the use of metadata to be added as content, so that the search engine can determine if one result is more relevant than another, is another important step. Making the search box visible on every page view is extremely important to encourage the use of this tool.

## Integration with corporate services

Your intranet should integrate with other corporate services, whether through APIs (especially private ones), web services, or by directly querying a database. This is the case, for example, when accessing information from areas such as sales, marketing, trade marketing, HR, finance, and others. This allows the activities performed to become much more efficient, integrated, and faster.
Integrating APIs with the intranet could allow for greater monitoring and tracking of specific activities.

## Mobile Friendly

Remember that the screen size is smaller, so initially, start with what is essential for this platform. Adding everything at once can be confusing and cause significant disruption for those developing or using a mobile device. If your company has external teams, such as logistics or sales teams, mobile optimization will become even more indispensable for the intranet. In this way, it can become a new form of access.

#### Items that are relevant in the mobile version and that can greatly help with engagement on your intranet:

- **Custom Content** – They include manuals, access policies, and other files of that nature.
- **News feed** – Providing access to *blogs* e *feed* news, helping employees stay informed about the latest events within the company.
- **Contact list** Having a list of all contacts with names and numbers can help users explore the native functionalities of... *smartphones*, such as calls, messages, emails, etc.
- **Database** Extremely important for sales teams. *marketing* and for operations managers. In this way, they will be able to analyze orders and even check sales history.

## Service Request Center

Your **intranet** should also include a direct service request feature, such as support. Another important point is to consider integration with existing process flows. Besides ensuring greater integration and adherence when using the intranet, utilizing these direct requests will increase productivity because, over time, some steps will be automated by the tool itself.

## Metrics & Monitoring

It's important to understand the performance of your Intranet (or Portal) and detect performance drops. This is crucial because this Portal can be considered mission-critical for the company (which isn't always easy). To do this, it's important to offer metrics and data in a simple and direct way: number of visits per page, *portlet* loading time , memory usage, alerts, etc.

## Permission hierarchy

It is one of the fundamental items in any intranet. These **Permission hierarchies** It's not just about creating and editing content, but also about viewing the portal's pages.
**The intranet system** It is responsible for issuing permissions. It establishes who can access certain areas or content. Furthermore, it protects important data and limits access to specific areas. It also reduces the need for an extra communication system in certain situations. This is the case, for example, with documents or areas related to senior management.
With a permission hierarchy system, managers, for example, will be able to use the **Intranet** Just like other employees, but with access to certain areas restricted to others.

## Conclusion

The presence of all these items in **Intranet** This will ensure good employee participation and provide greater integration and use of the Portal. Having an Intranet is an investment that is becoming increasingly necessary for those who want to have up-to-date, centralized information in one place.
