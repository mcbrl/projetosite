Does your company implement DevSecOps practices in API development? When it comes to digital security, developers need to adopt various behaviors to ensure applications are reliable and less vulnerable to exploits *.* Using security practices based on DevSecOps culture optimizes project progress from the beginning of development, through the various stages, and throughout the API lifecycle.

Cybercriminals are constantly searching for vulnerabilities in APIs that could affect service functionality; it's no wonder that, from time to time, it's necessary [to reassess the security level](https://www.cisoadvisor.com.br/aplicativos-com-cinco-anos-podem-apresentar-falhas-de-seguranca/) of applications.

Below are some tips that can help your company implement a DevSecOps strategy in API development and stay aligned with digital security best practices.

## DevSecOps: What does it solve?

DevSecOps makes security a central pillar for application development and [API integration](/en/partnerships/Veecode-platform/) within the enterprise.

First and foremost, we need to understand that DevSecOps is not a tool or software; it's a culture that emerged from DevOps, with the two being interconnected and encompassing various areas.

Therefore, teams that previously worked separately now operate in an integrated and collaborative manner, bringing their contribution to the central focus on application security.

In this way, it is possible for everyone involved in the project to be aligned and consider safety to be the company's top priority.

## DevSecOps for APIs

Vulnerabilities in APIs can cause enormous financial losses for companies, in addition to exposing confidential data and damaging the corporation's image. Therefore, nowadays, thinking about how to make your code more secure is paramount.

Check out some tips to increase the security level of APIs and integrate applications.

### 1 – Apply observability

Through [observability,](/en/Applying-observability-in-Kubernetes/) it is possible to constantly monitor each phase of application development, making it a highly important DevSecOps practice.

Thus, the response time of this continuous monitoring will be dynamic and efficient amidst the adjustments that are normal during the development phase, but which can sometimes go unnoticed and lead to future security breaches.

### 2 – Have a team of DevSecOps experts.

It may seem obvious, but having a team of DevSecOps specialists is fundamental to the successful implementation of the practice.

The team responsible for digital security actions will conduct research on best practices for risk reduction, developing secure ways to automate manual tasks, among other actions.

Thus, the company will have a foundation for creating DevSecOps strategies that can be disseminated to the rest of the teams through ongoing training.

### 3 – Utilize DevSecOps solutions

Through powerful tools, implementing DevSecOps becomes much simpler. [HashiCorp Vault](/en/partnerships/hashicorp/) , available in Vertigo Tecnologia solutions, is an excellent option for those who want to increase their security level.

Having password access control with strict security policies is one of the key features of HashiCorp solutions.

When teams from different areas have unlimited access to project information, it creates a greater opportunity for errors, the implementation of malicious code, and even data leaks.

Make use of tools that automatically manage passwords and limit access to only authorized individuals.

### 4 – Implement CI/CD pipeline

When it comes to automation, DevSecOps fits perfectly. Through a [CI/CD pipeline](/en/What-is-a-CD-ROM-pipeline-Understand-it./) , it's possible to achieve a rapid response through process automation and continuous integration.

Therefore, several testing phases will be carried out during the API lifecycle until the final version of the application is released.

It's worth emphasizing that the key to success is recurring application testing; only in this way will it be possible to reduce the risk of failures.
