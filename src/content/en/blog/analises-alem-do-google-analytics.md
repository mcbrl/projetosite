New technologies are everywhere, and those who know how to transform their data into information will come out ahead! Beyond giving buyers a voice, these technologies are providing increasingly more interactions, thus raising the expectations of users of these digital platforms.

According to research by Adobe Systems, [offering a better digital experience is no longer just a priority](https://blogs.adobe.com/digitaleurope/digital-marketing/the-adobe-2016-digital-trends-report/) ; it has become the primary focus of marketing and IT departments. To deliver this, managers need to utilize data analytics platforms. Today, the most well-known tool is [Google Analytics](https://www.google.com/analytics/) , which provides, in a general way, information about how visitors arrived at the company's website, what they do on the platform, and how long they stay there.

But it's a mistake to think that Google Analytics should be the definitive or central solution for analyzing existing data. There are several other market solutions that deliver valuable information to both IT and Marketing departments, such as Dynatrace, New Relic, IBM, Adobe, and others. Here at Vertigo, for example, we are partners with Dynatrace. We chose this tool because we consider its functionalities to be lacking in other tools. This type of tool can help managers see, in more detail, the experience of their visitors across various digital platforms (website, mobile app, kiosk, etc.).

Next, we'll talk about 3 solutions it offers. Check it out:

## 1\. User Experience Management

Within the Dynatrace platform, it's possible to use the [UEM](https://www.dynatrace.com/platform/offerings/user-experience-management/) (User Experience Management) tool, which allows for in-depth monitoring of users across all digital channels. Yes, it's possible to discover which line of code is causing problems in the application. This allows you to understand, through precise data, how each user's experience works, whether they are logged in or not, in order to improve satisfaction and performance. With UEM, managers can also:

- Manage the user experience 24/7, on any device or application;
- Review individual visits and understand what is affecting the user experience;
- Obtain detailed information for the development team;
- Obtain real-time indicators for the sales and business area;
- To proactively identify and resolve technical problems;
- Transform any user action into a business metric.

## 2\. Application Monitoring

Another tool available on the Dynatrace platform is [Synthetic Monitoring](https://www.dynatrace.com/platform/offerings/synthetic-monitoring/) , which simulates visits to websites and mobile applications, allowing you to compare this data with market standards and even those of your competitors. With it, you can easily detect and analyze problematic components before they affect the user experience, whether in test or production environments. This tool can also be used for:

- Check the performance of the hosting provider and website components;
- Perform hundreds of tests in just a few seconds;
- View the response time for critical pages;
- Compare the company's website performance with that of its competitors;
- Analyze the user experience of Web 2.0 applications.

## 3\. User Behavior Analysis

With the [User Behavior Analytics tool](https://www.dynatrace.com/capabilities/user-behavior-analytics/) , managers can better evaluate and understand user behavior as they access websites or applications. They can compare this user behavior with conversion and bounce rates across all channels. This analytics tool also allows:

- Knowing which landing pages are the most popular;
- See which pages users are leaving from and eliminate technical problems;
- Identify the most clicked areas of the website using heatmaps;
- Analyze each customer's journey through the website;
- Monitor performance rates and failures in real time.

All three of these tools provided by the Dynatrace platform allow companies to have a clearer view of their users' experience. Google Analytics is an extremely powerful, free, and widely used tool, but it doesn't allow you to know, for example, where the error is within a page where the "buy" button doesn't work, an application that is negatively impacting the visitor experience, which applications are being used by pages, and how the company's website performs compared to its competitors.

These are just some of the pieces of information that can serve as business metrics. Hence the importance of managers using, in addition to Google Analytics, the tools found on the Dynatrace platform, for example. This combination gives them a complete view of user experience and behavior and can help them better develop their strategies to achieve success in the digital world.
