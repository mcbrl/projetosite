Customer experience is one of the most discussed topics today, and this discussion is increasingly moving beyond the exclusive domain of Marketing. Professionals from other areas, such as IT and Sales, are joining in to contribute to these discussions on how to monitor the Digital Experience. This is no coincidence, as these areas have recognized the importance of ensuring customer and visitor satisfaction. Given a more demanding customer profile, experience can be the best strategy for attracting, retaining, and converting consumers.

However, many managers don't know how to implement this strategy, and even fewer know how to monitor the results. That's today's topic.

In this article, discover how the digital experience is important for your business and how Digital Metrics systems have evolved.

## **The Importance of Digital Experience**

The first thing managers think about when a website/app/application starts operating is how they will attract visitors/convert sales/generate engagement with that application. But they forget an important detail in the process: without a good shopping experience, the customer may not return because they are with another provider that offers a good experience.

Conversely, if the company focuses on providing a positive experience from the first contact, naturally guiding the customer to their first purchase and subsequently offering adequate customer support, it will create a cycle that can significantly increase future purchases.

> Let's ask a question: if you had to focus on conversion or customer experience (CX), which would you prefer to focus on?

If you said conversion, I think you were mistaken in your answer!

Certamente, a conversão é importante, mas ela é algo momentâneo e pontual. Você pode ter conversão sem pensar em Customer experience (CX), imagine que só você venda aquele produto/serviço, você pode até não enxergar o valor desses conceitos agora, mas se um concorrente chegar pensando em CX, você vai sentir o peso disso sendo refletido diretamente nas suas vendas.

Quando você pensa em CX você naturalmente está pensando em conversão também, e projetando qual a melhor experiência que você pode ofertar para conquistar aquele visitante e o tornar em um cliente pagante do seu produto/serviço. Um negócio não é um negócio sem vendas/conversão. 

Enquanto conversão é importante, CX é ainda mais crítica. Feito corretamente, a boa experiência do clientes (CX) impulsiona as vendas contínuas com clientes novos e existentes – tanto para cross-sell e upsell – e também impulsiona o engajamento com a aplicação e com a marca, o que acaba por impulsionar as vendas.

Sim, o marketing deve levar visitante e transformá-los em fãs, e levar os fãs e transformá-los em clientes pagantes. Mas a jornada não termina aí – continua por todo o ciclo de vida do cliente. Cada interação com suas aplicações conta./

The digital experience is so important that it can be considered a fundamental piece in building a customer loyalty strategy. In many cases, companies that fail to provide a good digital experience for their customers undermine their entire loyalty strategy and end up having to focus more on attracting new customers, which is much more expensive and laborious.

Ultimately, the company can increase its customer base by retaining them for longer, boosting sales, and generating a positive market image. In other words, the digital experience is also fundamental for developing a strong competitive advantage over the competition.

## **Monitoring beyond the purchase journey.**

Many companies measure a website's digital performance by only considering the buyer's visit (buyer's journey) through the sales funnel. This leaves out important information such as:

- The impact that system slowness has on preventing purchases;
- Maximum time my application can take to load a page;
- His level of satisfaction with the business;
- time spent using the application;
- Abandonment rate at the time of purchase.
- The reason why he abandoned it;

Many managers, whether from IT or Business, are unaware that systems that track and monitor System Performance have evolved, making it possible to measure performance results in Infrastructure, Systems, and Business areas. Today, a company can measure visitor satisfaction levels using software like Dynatrace and know exactly the health of its applications.

## **Conclusion | Digital Performance** 

Technological advancements have enabled the development of monitoring systems with sufficient capacity to track not only sales and customer sentiment results, but also the results generated by all sectors of the company.

**Finance, Marketing, HR, IT, Sales, Purchasing,** etc., all integrated to generate a report detailing the business's **Digital Performance**.

Contrary to what many people believe, Digital Performance Management is not the same as traditional website and system performance management. It's much more than that. The difference, in fact, lies in its ability to measure customer experience at a more comprehensive level and understand the role of each system in that customer's digital experience journey.

> The focus is more directed towards: How will the systems and applications provide a good digital experience for the Visitor/Customer/User?
