You've certainly heard of various solutions for publishing and managing web content, including free platforms for creating websites and online portals, right? But, generally, those looking for this type of solution don't know the technical details necessary to make the most informed choice. That's what we'll try to summarize in this article, so you can learn about the... **Open Source Portal platforms**.
The type of development methodology is what will make the difference when choosing a platform. Here, we will discuss the main characteristics of three of these systems: Liferay, Drupal, and Joomla. Although they are different solutions (portal platform and CMS – Content Management System), they are considered some of the best alternatives available on the market.
Understand each of them better to make the most informed choice.

## **Drupal**

According to the website [Alexa](http://www.alexa.com/)Drupal is one of the most popular CMS (content management system) solutions in the world, powering 1% of websites globally. It's one of the most flexible solutions on the market, used for everything from small content portals to personal blogs and small business websites.
Drupal is characterized by its versatility, offering solutions for a variety of different uses. However, managing the system requires a very steep learning curve for end users. Therefore, it is best suited for advanced users who want a very interesting platform capable of meeting all kinds of needs. Drupal's popularity is what classifies it among the... **3 Open Source Portal platforms**.

## **Joomla!**

O **Joomla** It is also quite popular among CMS users in web development. Used for a wide variety of applications, it has become popular largely due to the ease of developing institutional websites, blogs and personal pages, as well as portfolios, galleries and more.
Joomla natively supports only MySQL databases and is based on the MooTools JavaScript framework. A drawback is that it doesn't allow full customization of page addresses. This is the main criticism against this content management system.

## **Liferay** 

Liferay is unique in that it positions itself as a **Portal Tool and not as a CMS. This open-source tool is the right choice for companies that need a flexible, powerful** *,* and easy-to-manage enterprise content publishing solution.

Liferay offers its numerous features. Liferay has native support for major databases (Microsoft, IBM, Oracle, and others) and operating systems. This opens up the possibility of publishing portals in multiple languages ​​and multiple sites, managing users based on rules and groups, drag-and-drop applications, and framework support. *workflow.* These are just some of the characteristics that make it... **Liferay** The best choice when it comes to creating corporate portals.

The best platform for your needs is the one that best aligns with your business objectives.
