*By: Felipe Cortês*
Amid the coronavirus pandemic, companies are facing several problems, most of them in the digital realm. What used to be easily remedied is now costly: the **the digital and sustainable transformation** Within companies, it was already fundamental, and now it's becoming mandatory. One of the pillars of digitally transforming companies is... [**DevOps**](/en/devops/).

DevOps plays a fundamental role in digital transformation, directly impacting business growth and the quality of developed systems. Some of the benefits that DevOps provides are [:](/en/devops/)

### **1\. Process optimization**

DevOps streamlines processes, making work flow faster and more productively. This optimization increases team productivity and performance.

### **2\. Improvement in quality**

The goal of DevOps is to enable teams to communicate better and understand that new, frictionless processes are viable for achieving a single objective, enabling new processes and directly impacting the improvement of project quality.

### **3\. Automation of processes and methodologies**

In DevOps, automating processes and methodologies leads to the elimination of rework, teams have more time to pursue continuous improvement and innovation within the company, and it drastically reduces resolution time in case of unforeseen events.

### **4\. Increased motivation**

Keeping employees motivated is fundamental to a company's growth. DevOps creates an environment conducive to continuous development, *feedback* , and encouragement of collaboration, thus fostering team integration and facilitating problem-solving.

With all these benefits, you must be thinking: "What an incredible tool!". Well, [**DevOps**](/en/devops/) is not just a tool, nor a product: it is primarily a culture. The DevOps culture brings all the benefits mentioned, but organizational buy-in is fundamental for this to happen.
