DevOps culture is increasingly embedded within the context of companies seeking to implement or strengthen their Digital Transformation strategies. Various sectors utilize this methodology, and of course, the financial sector is no exception.

As one of the areas with the greatest challenges in the adoption of digital security, the financial sector has been undergoing constant changes in the way it behaves and communicates with its clients.

With the growing demand for Digital Transformation in the sector, several fintechs aligned with agile methodologies, such as DevOps, have emerged in the field of financial operations. The term fintech refers to technology and innovation applied to the development of financial service solutions, which explains the direct competition between traditional banks and fintechs that has emerged in recent years.

This competition has fueled the search for intelligent solutions within the financial sector, and the [DevOps culture](/en/DevOps-and-Kubernetes%3A-an-efficient-and-complementary-duo./) is more present than ever as a growth tool in this scenario.

We prepared this article to show how DevOps is an opportunity to make companies in the financial sector increasingly prepared to compete for a prominent place in the global market and offer better services to their clients. Enjoy your reading!

## Traditional financial market vs. Fintechs

The financial market was one of the most impacted by Digital Transformation. In Brazil, large banks have always monopolized the sector, centralizing a large portion of customers within their networks. With the advent of fintechs, a considerable share of the market has been divided, and the digitalization of the sector continues to grow every day, with established names such as Nubank, PagSeguro, PicPay, among others.

In the not-too-distant past, resolving issues with your credit card bill or even just asking questions required a long and painful journey through telephone customer service centers, which wasted time and didn't always guarantee a resolution.

Currently, we can make payments and clarify doubts through omnichannel customer service right in the palm of our hand, via applications developed for the financial sector. This leap in customer service came with the arrival of fintechs.

Although conventional banks like Itaú dominate the market, fintechs have begun to attract many clients from the traditional market, using modern methodologies for application development and transforming the way clients have their requests fulfilled.

## Digital Transformation via DevOps is the way to level the playing field.

Financial institutions have awakened to the need to resolve technological inconsistencies that have always been present in the traditional market. To do this, it has been necessary to embrace innovation in both application development and infrastructure, with resources capable of meeting demands in a competitive manner.

Traditional banks have already begun implementing technological solutions to keep pace with fintech technology. This requires an architecture that includes:

- Dynamic scalability;
- Fault resistance;
- Quick adaptation;
- Connectivity with a wide variety of devices, data, and systems.

Before the arrival of fintechs, the financial market already adopted technological solutions. It wasn't a market disconnected from the digital world, but the use of traditional IT models was common, which are generally complex and expensive to operate and maintain.

Outdated systems and methodologies lead development and infrastructure teams to spend excessive time on maintenance and basic operations.

With the arrival of DevOps, the financial sector began to incorporate Digital Transformation in a more holistic way, focusing on developing innovative ways to add value to the company.

## Discover the advantages for companies in the financial sector that adopt DevOps.

Adopting a DevOps culture means bridging the gap between operations and development teams and eliminating bottlenecks, fostering collaboration and improved communication between both groups.

From this perspective, developing [cloud-native](/en/Cloud-native%3A-do-you-know-the-difference-between-the-types-of-clouds/) projects to operate with greater scalability, adopting solutions like [Mirantis Kubernetes](/en/Kubernetes-solutions-with-Mirantis/) and many others that are [trending in the IT market for 2022](/en/digital-transformation-gartner-reveals-strategic-technology-trends-for-2022/) , are important steps within the Digital Transformation process.

Adopting DevOps brings several benefits. Here are some that are already present in various companies in the financial sector:

- Greater agility and automation;
- Modern organizational culture;
- Encouraging experimentation with new solutions;
- Greater clarity and transparency in decision-making;
- Increased cooperation between infrastructure and development teams;
- Improved application performance;
- [Implementation of observability](/en/Applying-observability-in-Kubernetes/) during the course of the project.
