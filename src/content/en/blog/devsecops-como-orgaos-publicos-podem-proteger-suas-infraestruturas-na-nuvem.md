Has the public sector adopted DevSecOps best practices? Attacks on the infrastructure of public institutions have become more frequent, and the proximity of elections has boosted the [growth of DDoS attacks](https://www.convergenciadigital.com.br/Seguranca/Ataques-DDoS-dobram-no-Brasil-por-conta-das-eleicoes-61539.html).

According to the Global Threat Hunting System report by NSFocus, the current scenario shows that approximately 225 websites, including government and news agencies, educational institutions, and telecommunications operators, are under the threat of cyberattacks.

Several municipal websites, the Santa Catarina State Department of Education, and the Federal Government portal are some of the recent targets of cybercriminals who are directing DDoS attacks to disrupt and compromise the operation of services.

Therefore, what is the role of DevSecOps in relation to [Digital Government](/en/digital-transformation-in-the-public-sector/) and other areas of activity in the digitization of public services?

## Why is DevSecOps important for digital security?

When an application begins development, it's important that the security team is involved in the project design process, as they will have the specialized knowledge to close any vulnerabilities that could be exploited within the code.

In DevOps-based projects, continuous integration (CI) and continuous delivery (CD) cycles are followed, thus making it possible to address security policies from a compliance perspective.

Therefore, public institutions that choose partners specialized in developing projects under DevSecOps best practices tend to have a solid cloud infrastructure to support them.

## What cloud solutions can increase the security of my infrastructure?

Large companies are migrating their local infrastructure to the cloud, and the public sector is following this trend due to the need to modernize services.

In addition to cost reduction, the public sector has the opportunity to optimize its infrastructure and invest in digital security.

Solutions like [AWS (Amazon Web Services)](/en/partnerships/aws/) provide greater scalability to support your demands, as well as a secure and stable environment.

Furthermore, it is possible to implement DevSecOps strategies with [HashiCorp](/en/partnerships/hashicorp/) solutions :

**API-driven:** Automation and the use of CI/CD are enabled through the use of APIs while preparing policies for encoding, protecting, and controlling access to secrets.

**Secrets management:** application data remains secure through centralized storage, access, and distribution of dynamic secrets.

**Identity-independent security:** any trusted identity provider can be used. After all, it is scale-independent, does not require complex firewall rules, and does not require frequent updates.

**Integration:** Secrets are requested for any system through a consistent, audited, and secure workflow. Support is available for public clouds, private data centers, and a wide variety of endpoint systems.

## Benefits of DevSecOps for the public sector

By implementing DevSecOps practices, institutions will be able to share responsibility between operations and development. Furthermore, these cycles will be short and frequent, resulting in agility and scalability.

By implementing this methodology, it will be possible to have greater security and continuous, integrated infrastructure through the automation of security policies as an integrated responsibility.
