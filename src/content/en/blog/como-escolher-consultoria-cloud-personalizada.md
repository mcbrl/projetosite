Cloud computing has moved beyond being a trend and has become a cornerstone of innovation and scalability in modern businesses. For leaders like CEOs and IT managers, making strategic decisions about the cloud journey has become a critical mission. But with so many options and approaches available, how do you choose a cloud consulting firm that is truly aligned with your company's specific needs?

In this article, you will understand how to select a customized cloud consulting service, the advantages of a cloud-native approach, and why the right choice can accelerate results, reduce costs, and increase the security of your operation.

The technical and strategic criteria that differentiate a traditional consulting firm from one specialized in cloud computing will be presented, along with a case study of Vertigo Tecnologia, which exemplifies the real-world impacts of efficient consulting.

**The role of customized cloud consulting in the era of digital transformation.**

Cloud migration involves much more than simply moving servers. It requires strategy, alignment with business objectives, and compliance with the technical and regulatory requirements of each sector. It is in this context that customized cloud consulting becomes essential.

### **Understanding cloud consulting**

[**Cloud consulting**](/en/solutions/cloud_native/) is a specialized service that supports companies in planning, implementing, and optimizing cloud solutions. Effective consulting operates from the initial diagnosis to post-migration support, promoting governance, scalability, and security.

### **Why customization is essential**

Every company has a unique infrastructure, culture, and business model. Customized consulting doesn't apply generic solutions, but designs tailored architectures to address the real challenges of the business, including compliance, performance, costs, and expansion.

### **What differentiates a strategic approach?**

While some consulting firms still adopt traditional methods, the most modern ones embrace a cloud-native mindset, which prioritizes automation, containerization, [**DevSecOps**](/en/Process-automation-in-the-public-sector%3A-the-impact-of-DevOps-on-government-modernization./) , and intensive use of infrastructure as code (IaC). These elements ensure flexibility and speed in delivery.

## **Traditional cloud consulting vs. cloud native consulting: what really changes?**

Before hiring a consulting firm, it's crucial to understand that not all offer the same type of service. Many still operate with a focus on on-premise environments, while others are native to the cloud universe.

### **Differences in technological focus**

Traditional IT consulting firms typically work with physical infrastructure, local networks, legacy systems, and ERPs. Cloud-focused consulting firms, on the other hand, work with cloud platforms, Kubernetes, microservices, pipeline automation, and emerging technologies.

### **Differences in delivery and scalability**

While traditional consulting firms deliver projects in long cycles with many manual steps, cloud-based consulting firms utilize agile practices and robust automation to accelerate time-to-market. This results in greater scalability and lower operational costs.

### **Differences in governance and security**

Modern cloud consulting firms incorporate DevSecOps practices, continuous monitoring, state-of-the-art encryption, and intelligent identity and access management. In contrast, more traditional approaches still rely on physical firewalls and manual control processes.

## **Criteria for choosing a customized cloud consulting service.**

Selecting the ideal consulting firm requires more than just evaluating a portfolio. It's necessary to understand their technical capabilities, the methodologies they use, and their compatibility with the company's objectives.

### **Evaluate the specialization in cloud native.**

Verify that the consulting firm is proficient in technologies such as Kubernetes, managed services on AWS, Azure, or GCP, as well as DevOps methodologies and [**CI/CD pipelines**](/en/What-is-a-CD-ROM-pipeline-Understand-it./) . These skills are fundamental for creating modern, secure, and scalable architectures.

### **Look for relevant success stories.**

Companies with proven experience tend to have greater technical maturity and the ability to handle complex challenges. Investigate real-world case studies, especially those involving automation, security, and cloud replicability projects.

### **Customization and service capabilities**

A good consulting firm doesn't just deliver what's requested, but proposes solutions with a future-oriented vision. Evaluate whether the partner understands your business model, maps risks, and delivers a project that goes beyond technical migration.

## **Vertigo and Privacy Dynamics Case Study: Excellence in Cloud Infrastructure Automation**

To illustrate how specialized consulting can make a difference, we highlight the work of Vertigo Tecnologia with the North American startup Privacy Dynamics, which specializes in data anonymization.

### **The Privacy Dynamics Challenge**

Privacy Dynamics needed to securely and efficiently replicate its containerized application in the [**AWS cloud**](/en/Cloud-Computing%3A-5-Tips-to-Increase-Security-in-an-AWS-Environment/) , meeting the demands of clients requiring self-hosted solutions. The previous model involved manual configurations and poor scalability, which limited the growth of the operation.

### **The solution proposed by Vertigo Tecnologia**

Vertigo initiated the project focusing on automation and scalability. Technologies such as Kubernetes, Terraform, and VKPR (Vertigo Kubernetes Production Runtime) were adopted. The consultancy implemented modern practices such as:

- Automatic provisioning of production-ready Kubernetes clusters.
- Creating a replicable and secure structure, adapted to each end customer.
- Implementation of automation pipelines for application installation and updates.

### **Concrete results achieved**

Vertigo's performance directly impacted Privacy Dynamics' operational efficiency. Key results include:

- Significant reduction in the time to provision new customers.
- Complete automation of the cloud infrastructure deployment cycle.
- Improved governance through the use of Infrastructure as Code.
- Expanding the customer base without a proportional increase in the technical team.
- Incorporating DevOps culture and advanced security practices.
