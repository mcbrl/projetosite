## What is Messaging?

Before we move on, we need to explain what a messaging service is. It's a smart solution that allows you to send and receive messages quickly and securely, which is a basic need in today's world.

When we talk about the exchange of important information within the business sector, we look at the interaction between the company's systems, which enables the integration of different services vital to the functioning of projects.

Many people mistakenly believe that messaging services are simply about exchanging messages. Did you know that the possibilities go far beyond that?

For example, a major advantage of implementing integrations between messaging APIs, offered by Vertigo in partnership with Take Blip, is the necessary infrastructure for sending and receiving important documents from various departments.

## Digital Transformation through Integration of Messaging APIs

Companies that are aligned with [Digital Transformation](/en/digital-transformation-strategy-post-pandemic/) certainly rely on a reliable messaging service as part of their daily work routine.

The solution is also a tool that helps the company fulfill its obligations, such as paying taxes required by regulatory bodies.

Furthermore, messaging services can be easily integrated with various ERP systems. When API integration is done, the sending of invoices follows this flow: the ERP exports the invoice to the messaging channel, which in turn sends the XML file to the indicated agency.

Through the partnership between Vertigo Tecnologia and Take Blip, implementing Digital Transformation by contracting a messaging service is an action that will bring many benefits to the entire company.

The teams will optimize their time across various tasks, resulting in a lower error rate in completing their daily tasks and facilitating the daily work of team members.
