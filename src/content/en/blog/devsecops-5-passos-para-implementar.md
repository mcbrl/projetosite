At a time when the pandemic has forced large companies to rethink their business strategies, many are seeking to implement DevOps and DevSecOps culture as part of their Digital Transformation journey.

In this process, concern for security is a crucial and decisive step for the success of the entire operation. It is at this stage that we begin to [explore DevSecOps](/en/Discover-the-difference-between-DevOps--DevSecOps--and-NetOps./).

In short, DevOps aligns development and operations teams, while DevSecOps adds the security component to this equation.

Therefore, when planning your [Digital Transformation strategy](/en/digital-transformation-strategy-post-pandemic/) , in addition to a solid foundation in the DevOps methodology, it is essential to be concerned with the integrity of your company's data, that is, with the security factor.

We've prepared this article with 5 steps to help you successfully implement the DevSecOps methodology. Happy reading!

## What is DevSecOps?

Before learning the 5 steps that can help you implement a DevSecOps strategy efficiently, it's necessary to understand what this methodology means.

Implementing DevSecOps means developing security policies alongside the agility of DevOps, from the initial phase of the project lifecycle to its final steps, ensuring greater reliability and delivery with the lowest risk.

In this way, development teams are able to implement best security practices for building applications, providing a solid foundation for customers. Similar to DevOps, solutions should be inclusive at all stages, ensuring security and privacy throughout the project.

## What are the benefits of DevSecOps?

Of course, by now you should know that DevSecOps' main benefit is the security of your applications, but how does this actually happen? Let's go into more detail.

By implementing this method, it is possible to share responsibilities between the operations and development teams, preventing an overload of tasks and thus increasing the quality of the work performed.

Development cycles become shorter and more frequent, providing greater speed and scalability.

Concern for the project's privacy policy is important, but when implementing DevSecOps, security and infrastructure are integrated and continuous from the project's earliest stages.

Another major advantage is the possibility of automating security policies as an integrated responsibility.

## Here are 5 steps to implement a DevSecOps strategy in your company.

### 1\. Invest in your team's training.

A key factor in the success of large companies is having an internal development program.

Conducting security workshops and keeping your team members up-to-date on trends is crucial for increasing the chances of successfully adopting a DevSecOps strategy, as well as encouraging professional growth.

### 2 – Encourage knowledge sharing.

Create smart practices and encourage the development team to build code with security as a guiding principle.

Through this practice, security teams will consequently have information to share, feedback to give, and visibility regarding threats will always be within the field of vision of everyone involved in the project.

Knowledge sharing is a behavior that needs to be incorporated into the company culture.

### 3 – Implement automated security testing.

Map out the security flaws and vulnerabilities in your project. After identifying and correcting them, dedicate efforts to developing automated security solutions to repeat and automate error correction.

Code the solutions so they can be included in the unit testing for newly added features. This way, security requirements are implemented from the start of the development process.

Implement automations with specialized security teams, exercising utmost security and caution, and always performing implementations first in test environments.

### 4 – Allocate security professionals from the start.

The sooner security professionals get involved in the project, the better. Ideally, the DevSecOps project should be incorporated during the DevOps implementation phase.

The security process, as already mentioned, needs to be continuous and throughout the entire development process. If security is considered late, the chances of a serious security breach occurring are much higher, and often the work will be even greater, causing delays in the application's delivery.

### 5 - Conduct regular audits

Conduct scheduled security audits across all the infrastructure that currently supports your software projects.

Conduct constant tests, simulate intrusions and scenarios that push your security team to their limits, ensuring that all possible failures are considered.

In this way, it is possible to develop policies to prevent security breaches.
