After four years dedicated to leading Vertigo's Technology & Innovation sector, André Fernandes is leaving his position as CTIO to assume the newly created Product area of ​​the company. This change, motivated by Vertigo's exponential growth in recent years, is part of the company's business strategy aimed at investing in intellectual property assets. Still a member of the company's executive board, now in the role of CPO (Chief Product Officer), André will focus on developing new digital products with global market appeal, such as the Vertigo iPaaS and the FIVE Academy, among many others to come.

In this context, we are very pleased to announce the arrival of Vinícius Siani, who joins Vertigo as the company's new CTO. Vinícius brings with him 27 years of experience in managing teams, projects, and the entire production chain, the last 15 of which were dedicated to Vale in various global leadership positions (Brazil, Canada, Africa, and the Middle East).

As the new CTO, Vinicius will have challenging missions:

- To contribute to the creation of the strategy for offering Vertigo products to the global market;
- To bring Vertigo's value proposition to the executive level of our clients;
- To structure the new Business Unit for professional service and support;
- To consolidate Vertigo's delivery standard with integrated portfolio and project management;
- Implement results-based management throughout the operation, rewarding and celebrating competence, thus providing a challenging and growth-oriented environment for the team;
- Increase the level of technical support for the sales team.

> *Hi everyone, I'm very happy and motivated to join the Vertigo team. It's easy to see how competent and committed you are, which is why you've brought the company to its current level. I hope to contribute to the team and help Vertigo grow even more and reach new heights. Count on me!*
> *Hugs!*  **Vinicius Siani – CTO of Vertigo Tecnologia**
