## A favorable scenario for new Digital Transformation strategies.

Over the past few months, much has been said about how the pandemic accelerated the Digital Transformation process amidst the adaptation scenario the world had to undergo. It is true that with the advancement of vaccination, the overall assessment of the pandemic scenario has become more optimistic, but this does not mean that the situation will normalize anytime soon, especially in the business sector.

Therefore, it is worth highlighting that technology continues to work for the benefit of society, creating innovative solutions to enable remote jobs and provide solutions for its clients.

More than ever, paying attention to technology trends is necessary for companies and their leaders to keep pace with the speed of Digital Transformation in an increasingly globalized world, through innovative solutions.

See the 12 technology trends mapped by Gartner for the year 2022.

## Check out the technologies that will stand out in 2022, according to Gartner.

**1 – Data Fabric**

The term Data Fabric can be seen as an architecture and a suite of data services capable of providing consistent resources across [multi-cloud environments](https://materiais.vertigo.com.br/seguranca-em-um-mundo-multicloud-ebook) . This robust architecture standardizes data management practices in the cloud, on-premise, and at edge devices. Key advantages of Data Fabric include visibility and insight generation, access and control, and data protection and security.

This data network can benefit from analytics to learn and recommend where data should be used and changed. This can reduce data management efforts by up to 70%.

**2 – Cybersecurity Mesh**

A cybersecurity mesh is a flexible architecture capable of integrating widely distributed and diverse security services.

This architecture allows best-in-class autonomous security solutions to work together to improve overall security while bringing control points closer to the assets they are designed to protect. It can quickly and reliably verify identity, context, and policy adherence in cloud environments.

**3 – Privacy-Enhancing Computing**

Privacy-enhanced computing protects the processing of personal data in untrusted environments, a factor that is becoming increasingly critical due to evolving privacy and data protection laws, as well as growing user concerns.

A focus on privacy-promoting computing utilizes a variety of privacy protection techniques to allow value to be extracted from data while meeting compliance requirements.

**4 – Cloud native platforms (Cloud-Native Platforms)**

Cloud-native platforms are technologies that enable the construction of new, resilient, elastic, and agile application architectures. This allows users to respond to and adapt to a variety of situations within the digital world.

Cloud-native platforms improve upon the traditional approach of migrating an infrastructure to the cloud, which often results in an inefficient process, fails to leverage the benefits of cloud computing, and adds complexity to maintenance.

You can learn more about cloud-native solutions through this [link](/en/solutions/cloud_native/).

**5 – Composable Applications**

Combinable applications are developed from business-centric modular components.

Combinable applications facilitate the use and reuse of code, accelerating time to market for new software solutions and unlocking corporate value.

**6 – Decision Intelligence**

Decision intelligence is an approach aimed at improving organizational decision-making, and is a fundamental part of the Digital Transformation process. It models each decision as a set of processes, using intelligence and analytics to inform, learn from, and refine decisions.

Decision intelligence can be a valuable tool to help humans make decisions and potentially automate them through the use of augmented analytics, simulations, and AI.

**7 – Hyperautomation**

Hyperautomation is a business approach developed to quickly identify, examine, and automate as many business and IT processes as possible.

This approach allows for scalability, remote operation, and business model disruption. It involves automating any business process by combining RPA (Robotic Process Automation) and other technologies such as Artificial Intelligence.

**8 – AI Engineering**

AI engineering automates updates to data, models, and applications to accelerate the delivery of an AI solution. Combined with strong AI governance, this practice can operationalize AI delivery to ensure its continued business value.

**9 – Distributed Enterprises**

Distributed companies reflect a *digital-first* , remote-first business model to improve employee experiences, digitize customer and partner touchpoints, and build product experiences.

Distributed businesses better meet the needs of remote employees and consumers, who are increasing the demand for virtual services and hybrid workplaces.

**10 – Total Experience**

Total experience is a business strategy that integrates employee, customer, and user experience. This strategy aggregates multiple experiences across various touchpoints to accelerate growth.

A complete experience can generate greater trust, satisfaction, loyalty, and advocacy from customers and employees through the holistic management of stakeholder experiences.

**11 – Autonomous Systems**

Autonomous systems are self-managing physical or software systems that learn from their environments and dynamically modify their own algorithms in real time to optimize their behavior in complex ecosystems.

Autonomous systems create an agile set of technology resources that are capable of supporting new requirements and situations, optimizing performance, and defending against attacks without human intervention.

**12 – Generative AI**

Generative AI learns about artifacts from data and generates innovative new creations that are similar to the original, but do not replicate it.

Generative AI has the potential to create new forms of creative content, such as video, and accelerate R&D cycles in fields ranging from medicine to product creation.
