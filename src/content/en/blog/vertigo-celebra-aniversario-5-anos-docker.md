[March is the month we celebrate Docker](/en/docker/) 's anniversary ! In this edition, the event was led by our CTIO, André Fernandes, who gave a lesson on the system to an audience eager for knowledge about container technology, which is still new to many people, as we could see at the event.

Docker's anniversary celebration began with a lengthy explanation of Docker's history, which started as a company – called DotCloud – a cloud-based PaaS solution for other companies. Docker was then a spin-off that took on a life of its own, rebranding the company that created it and surpassing the original dotCloud solution itself (which was sold to another German company).

![](/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/41000592532_0c2ee5bd97_b-768x576-1.jpg)

Currently, Docker positions itself as a producer of tools for building and managing containerized clouds. The products responsible for each action mentioned are Docker Community and Docker Enterprise, respectively.

The presentation focused on Docker CE, Docker EE, and Kubernetes products. The event's content was relevant for both beginners and more experienced users. Those just starting out in the world of containerization enjoyed very didactic content regarding the entire application ecosystem.

This was the case for *Bruno, 32, a developer who is starting his own projects. He was excited about the advantages of containers.*

> “ *The container will generate enormous flexibility. I will structure my application in an easier way, making the image run more simply, having the advantages of a simpler, much faster deployment,” Bruno stated.*

Those with more experience were able to share their expertise and update themselves on the technology. This was the case with Hilton, 36, who is already quite knowledgeable about the technology and emphasized the need to adopt Docker.

> *"Docker is essential for businesses, especially monolithic ones! The main difference with Docker is how much it abstracts the hardware and operating system architecture. We need that. Both in large companies and in companies that are just starting out."*

![](/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/41042548431_15874525da_k-768x576-1.jpg)

After the presentation, it was time to get hands-on. The audience accessed [Play With Docker](https://labs.play-with-docker.com/) – where they could “play around” a bit with Docker Community, which is a cloud building tool.

There was also testing with the Docker Enterprise version, through a link exclusive to the event, in which participants could work with the application that allows managing containers in a cloud.

At the end of the workshop there was a question and answer session regarding Docker systems, where giveaways were distributed to the audience.
