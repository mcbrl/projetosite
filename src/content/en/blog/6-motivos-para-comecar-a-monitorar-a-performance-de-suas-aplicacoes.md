There's no point in computerizing your entire company only to introduce a poor, slow, or difficult-to-use system. To effectively anticipate this type of scenario and avoid unnecessary work and headaches for your team, APM – **Application Performance Monitoring** – emerges as a good alternative. Through it, it's possible to analyze how your team is using corporate applications. With this, it's possible to better understand if these applications are offering problems or solutions.
But the advantages don't stop there. We've listed the main reasons to implement this APM concept in your company.

## 1\. Better understanding of how the software used by your company works.

Application performance is fundamental to understanding what's working and what's not. APM makes it possible to understand the flow of applications, how they work, and how users are using your technology. This is essential for your IT department to better understand the demands of your business and identify bottlenecks that can be costly in terms of productivity and even business profitability. Imagine if you lose revenue because of an application that isn't working well?

## 2\. Reducing the time and costs associated with support.

**Application Performance** Monitoring (APM) has immense potential in reducing support costs for fixing specific failures. With the right optimizations and configurations, it's possible to reduce the team dedicated to bug fixing and validation, and thus allocate that personnel to other more strategic IT activities.

## 3\. Encouraging proactivity in your IT department

APM will help your organization stimulate [a proactive environment](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/5-acoes-para-aumentar-a-colaboracao-na-sua-empresa/) Among IT professionals, this is because when support calls decrease due to frequent failures and errors that need to be addressed one at a time, your team has time to work on continuous improvements, that is, on the evolution of your applications.
With this technique, you will see an increase in your team's proactivity, as they will be focused on anticipating problems and keeping systems running at their maximum potential. This change in approach can also impact revenue generation, as your IT team can be closer to the Business area to develop new solutions.

## 4\. Better visibility of the possibilities offered by your software.

Visibility is key when discussing application performance. [data quality](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/o-governo-os-cidadaos-e-os-dados/)  Collected data is as important for building knowledge about the technologies used as it is for addressing situations that may arise from their use. This visibility is as important for understanding what is happening within your applications as it is for knowing how the end user behaves when interacting with your tool.
With the right metrics and indicators, it's possible to find automation opportunities and foster innovation. Furthermore, it's possible to generate new revenue streams.

## 5\. Efficient resource management

More assertive resource management will be a natural consequence of APM. This is because, through the collected data, it will be easy to safely filter which resources are needed. *with* quality *hardware* Resources need to be allocated. Furthermore, it's possible to determine the best way to solve the problem and optimize the system.
Resizing resource usage is an effective way to save money without compromising business productivity. In other words, it's possible to reallocate part of the IT budget to new projects. The savings result from good monitoring and offer gains comparable to those made possible by... [cloud computing](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/solucoes-em-nuvem-4-razoes-para-adotar/).

## 6\. Ensuring user satisfaction with application performance.

Satisfied users, who better understand how the tools available to them work, are productive users. Application performance makes the team's life easier, making their systems more efficient based on user experience.
Efficient systems offer quality and are excellent for keeping employees and customers happy, with accurate deliveries and success rates.
