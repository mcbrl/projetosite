This is our inaugural post, and I'm very happy to have you as our reader. Thank you so much! In this first post, I have two topics to discuss:
1) We turned 16! That's no joke;
2) We'll tell you what topics you'll read about on our blog.
We were founded in 1999 and today, in 2015, we're celebrating our 16th anniversary! We have so much to share and so much to learn. In that time, we've worked for clients of all sizes and across various sectors. Sixteen years of history, hard work, dedication, people, projects, and friendships. And since we've decided to share what we've accomplished during this period, we'll use our blog as a point of contact.
Our intention here is not to coin new academic concepts, but rather to help people who have questions about technology applied to the business world. We want to help people solve their problems today, right now! Sharing good ideas is part of Vertigo; we do it with our clients, and now we're going to do it with you too!
But what exactly are we going to talk about? Our focus will be on IT solutions. More specifically, we'll discuss clearly and objectively how to achieve business objectives using IT. And to do this, we'll explain how we deliver these solutions through Portals, Intranets, Systems Integration, APIs, SOA, BPM, and Mobile. We have extensive practical experience from projects and consulting in all these areas, and here are some of our clients:
