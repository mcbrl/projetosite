We are pleased and satisfied to announce that Vinicius Siani, current CTO of [Vertigo Tecnologia](/en/careers/) , is joining the company as a partner.

After 8 months on Vertigo's executive board, Vinicius was responsible for restructuring the customer service business unit, resulting in the creation of the Solutions Engineering and Project Delivery areas.

She also served as director of the People and Management area, leading strategic projects such as the company's first performance evaluation program.

In the business area, she has contributed to Vertigo's internationalization project by providing technical support to the sales team, reviewing the recruitment strategy, and constantly training the customer service team to deliver highly complex projects in different countries.

“Since my ‘relationship’ with @Vertigo, I’ve had a different level of alignment, both in terms of values ​​and business mindset, with the board (André and Bruno). At the end of 2021, after achieving so much in such a short time, with this synergy that only grows, it was natural to take this step. I am very happy and fulfilled! Eager for what lies ahead! To infinity and beyond! More than ever #IAmVertigo, #GoVertigo,” Vinicius emphasized.

Vinicius will continue to assist Vertigo in implementing results-based management and maintaining an organizational environment of constant learning, growth, and professional development.
