Recently, Gartner Inc. analyzed and defined the key technologies for 2024 within the corporate landscape.

When we talk about a scenario where the speed of technological evolution defines competitiveness, artificial intelligence (AI) emerges as a driving force for organizational growth and security. In this era of constant innovation, companies have the opportunity not only to adapt, but also to gain maturity regarding AI-powered innovations.

From solutions that add value to operations to those that enhance resilience in the face of changing customer expectations and business models, AI has become the backbone for many organizations seeking to thrive and excel in the market.

In this article, we'll learn more about the technologies that are trending for 2024 and understand how they can help your company.

Below are the main [technological trends for 2024](https://www.gartner.com.br/pt-br/tecnologia-da-informacao/insights/principais-tendencias-tecnologicas) , according to Gartner.

## AI Trust, Risk and Security Management (AI TRiSM)

According to Gartner, AI TRiSM, short for Artificial Intelligence Trust, Risk, and Security Management, plays a crucial role in the governance of advanced AI models, addressing fundamental issues such as reliability, fairness, security, robustness, transparency, and data protection.

By 2026, implementing TRiSM controls in AI-powered applications within enterprises will result in a significant improvement in the accuracy of their decisions, effectively eliminating 80% of flawed and illegitimate information.

## Continuous threat exposure management (CTEM)

Continuous Threat Exposure Management represents a pragmatic and systemic approach to the constant adaptation of priorities in optimizing cybersecurity.

By 2026, organizations that align their security investments with the guidelines of a CTEM program will experience a remarkable two-thirds reduction in security breaches. This strategy provides an agile and proactive response to threats, establishing a high standard of cyber resilience.

## Sustainable technology

Sustainable Technology represents a model of digital solutions designed to drive environmental, social, and governance (ESG) outcomes, promoting long-term ecological balance and respecting human rights.

By 2027, it is projected that 25% of information technology directors will have their compensation tied to the impact of their sustainable technology initiatives. This approach reflects the growing importance of aligning technology leadership with sustainability goals, reinforcing the commitment to responsible practices and a positive impact on the world.

## platform engineering

According to Gartner, [Platform Engineering](/en/Veecode-platform/) is a discipline dedicated to building and operating internal self-service platforms. Each platform is conceived as a distinct layer, developed and maintained by a specialized product team, with the purpose of meeting user needs through interfaces with tools and processes.

By 2026, it is anticipated that 80% of software engineering organizations will establish platform teams, functioning as internal providers of reusable services, components, and tools. This approach aims to optimize application deployment, promoting operational efficiency and maximizing the value delivered to end users.

## Enhanced development with AI

AI-Augmented Development refers to the incorporation of AI technologies, including generative artificial intelligence and machine learning, to assist software engineers in the process of creating, testing, and delivering applications.

It is projected that by 2028, approximately 75% of enterprise software engineers will be using AI coding assistants, representing a significant increase from less than 10% recorded at the beginning of 2023. This evolution highlights the rapid adoption and integration of artificial intelligence in software development, driving efficiency and innovation in the sector.

## Industry Cloud Platforms

According to Gartner, the approach consists of driving specific business outcomes for the sector by integrating core Software as a Service (SaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), and Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) services into a comprehensive product offering with combinable capabilities.

It is predicted that, by 2027, more than 50% of companies will adopt cloud-based industry platforms to drive their business initiatives. This increase represents a significant rise compared to the less than 15% recorded in 2023. The growing adoption of these integrated platforms highlights the importance of [cloud solutions](/en/Cloud-Computing%3A-5-Tips-to-Increase-Security-in-an-AWS-Environment/) for accelerating transformations and achieving operational efficiency in the business environment.

## Smart applications

Smart Applications refer to applications designed for consumers or businesses, enhanced with artificial intelligence and data from transactions and external sources.

It is predicted that by 2026, approximately 30% of new applications will incorporate AI to power adaptive and personalized user interfaces, marking a notable increase compared to the less than 5% observed currently. This evolution highlights the growing trend of integrating artificial intelligence to deliver more dynamic user experiences tailored to individual needs, according to Gartner.

## Democratized generative AI

Democratized Generative AI refers to the ability to create new, fluid content, such as images, speech, text, and more, and its widespread availability, which democratizes access to information and skills, emerging as one of the most important trends of this decade.

Looking ahead to the future, by 2026, it is estimated that over 80% of companies will have incorporated [generative AI-](/en/new-Genai-stack-for-developers/) based application programming interfaces , in addition to having implemented generative AI-enabled models and/or applications in production environments. This increase is substantial compared to the less than 5% currently recorded, highlighting the rapid adoption and significant transformation driven by generative AI in enterprise environments.

## Increased connected workforce

The Augmented Connected Workforce represents a strategy aimed at optimizing the value provided by the human team, establishing a connective network that enhances the use of intelligent technology, workforce analytics, and skills development, with the goal of accelerating and scaling talent growth.

By 2027, it is anticipated that 25% of IT directors will incorporate Augmented Connected Workforce initiatives to halve the time required to achieve competency in essential roles. This approach aims to optimize team efficiency and adaptability, providing an agile response to market demands through the strategic integration of technology and human skills.

## Machine clients

Machine Clients refer to non-human economic actors that carry out transactions for the purchase of goods and services in exchange for payment.

It is predicted that, by 2028, the rise of machine-customers will result in the obsolescence of 20% of digital stores designed for human interaction. This phenomenon highlights the rapid transformation in the online shopping landscape, driven by the growing presence and influence of automated economic agents, signaling a significant shift in e-commerce dynamics, according to Gartner.
