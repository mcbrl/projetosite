The Internet of Things works with a simple yet ingenious concept: everything is always connected and can be controlled, managed, and analyzed through computers or mobile devices such as *smartphones* and *tablets* . Smart light bulbs, fully automated homes, and even connected factories can be managed remotely. The future looks promising, and the technologies already available are becoming increasingly cheaper.

But for the Internet of Things to be possible and for us to have this multitude of smart objects, good *software* development is necessary to transform these ideas into reality. To increase possibilities, creating programs that are easily expandable and can adapt to new demands is increasingly essential, and this can be done through APIs.

What makes IoT communication different from traditional communication is the "internet" component in the equation. This makes the Internet of Things more complex from both a business and technical standpoint. APIs simplify the Internet of Things and play a crucial role in accelerating the adoption of this new technology.
In this post, you'll understand how using APIs is key to working with the Internet of Things, and the possibilities this relationship brings to your business. Keep reading!

## The first step to entering the world of the Internet of Things.

This initiative requires a large number of software developers to program devices and build connected experiences using data flowing from sensor to sensor. With the first connected objects becoming increasingly cheaper, the investment in smart homes, which was once very high, is becoming more accessible, and consumers are seeking solutions that support this scenario. Therefore, many companies recognize the importance of investing in APIs: they enable this future integration and allow businesses to take their first steps – and open up future possibilities – in the world of the Internet of Things.

This new market is not expected to remain a small slice of investment for long: according to [research by IDC (International Data Corporation)](http://www.idc.com/downloads/idc_market_in_a_minute_iot_infographic.pdf) , it should exceed US$7 trillion in 2020. Therefore, businesses are seeking to keep up with this trend by preparing their platforms to better understand the different possibilities and get ahead in this development.

## An environment conducive to the Internet of Things.

With initial investment in APIs that enable connectivity between various objects and systems, companies can create an environment ready for the Internet of Things to be better leveraged and for various current challenges to be overcome. The current climate encourages developers to experiment with this medium and better understand how they can take advantage of its capabilities, so that it is possible to create intelligent and profitable solutions from these platforms. 

This environment is expected to grow in the coming years to support the billions of objects and devices that are expected to be connected by the end of this decade. To achieve this, the use of APIs facilitates the creation of interfaces that allow connections between a network with high data transfer rates and the various necessary receivers. Some API management platforms (Mulesoft, WSO2, Red Hat, and others) already offer [cloud-](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/solucoes-em-nuvem-4-razoes-para-adotar/) based options , and this growth is expected to be constant.
