Vertigo Tecnologia is the first company in the Liferay partner ecosystem in Latin America and the second in the world to achieve the DXP Cloud Specialized Partner Seal. This recognition positions the company as a global benchmark for large companies in the market seeking to create digital experiences for their digital transformation with the support of cloud solutions.

“Vertigo is one of Liferay’s oldest partners in Latin America. They have followed Liferay’s development and have always been by our side, offering the most modern digital experience solutions for various segments in the region, such as industry, government, financial services, consumer goods, and more. The cloud is one of the main global trends in online experience management and represents a priority area for Liferay; Vertigo is prepared to follow this trend with us,” comments Flávio Moitinho, Sales Manager of Liferay in Brazil.

“It was quite challenging to achieve the DXP Cloud Specialized Partner Seal, but it was certainly a great source of pride for the entire team when we received the news that we were the first Latam partners to reach this milestone. It is our duty as a partner to continue investing heavily in the quality of our commercial approaches, as well as in the excellence of our technical deliverables, and we couldn't miss this technological advancement from Liferay. Our clients win, the Liferay ecosystem wins, and obviously, we win too,” commented Bruno Badini, CEO of Vertigo.

## **
DXP Cloud Specialized Partner**

The recognition of Vertigo Tecnologia is part of Liferay's global strategy to encourage the adoption of cloud solutions. "As the market increasingly seeks to migrate to the cloud, Liferay has been investing in qualifying its ecosystem for new opportunities. Vertigo stood out for its pioneering spirit in this initiative," he emphasized.u Moitinho.
To receive the certification, the Vertigo Tecnologia team underwent specific training, both technical and sales-related, focused on cloud solutions. Furthermore, they met the requirements of their specialized pitch for DXP Cloud, following a rigorous evaluation by the global team of Liferay's Cloud division. 
“This shows that Vertigo Tecnologia is ready to offer cloud solutions with the highest degree of expertise, providing clients with the most modern technology in the Liferay stack for managing cloud experiences,” added Moitinho.

## **
DXP Cloud**

Liferay DXP Cloud is a solution *enterprise* which simplifies the implementation, management, and scalability of Liferay DXP with Platform as a Service (PaaS) functions. Operating with Liferay DXP Cloud significantly reduces the need for IT teams to maintain and manage their own infrastructure, allowing them to focus on delivering more value and new business services.
Specifically built for our leading digital experience platform, Liferay DXP Cloud brings native features that help optimize Liferay DXP performance.
