## The use of e-commerce encourages Digital Transformation in the hygiene and beauty sector.

At some point, the COVID-19 pandemic will be controlled. When that time comes, will consumer habits remain the same?

Concern for health and hygiene has never been higher than in current times, which has consistently changed consumer behavior regarding products.

The exponential increase in sales via e-commerce, according to global research, has recorded growth in online commerce, increasing the number of orders and revenue.

Nationally, a survey by the Brazilian Association of Electronic Commerce (ABComm) indicated an increase [in e-commerce consumption](https://g1.globo.com/economia/noticia/2021/02/26/com-pandemia-comercio-eletronico-tem-salto-em-2020-e-dobra-participacao-no-varejo-brasileiro.ghtml) due to the quarantine, and this trend continued for months. The health and pharmaceutical sector saw a 6,3% increase in consumption.

At this point, the adoption of technologies such as [messaging services and chatbots](/en/Messaging-APIs-are-great-allies-for-your-omnichannel-strategy./) are strong allies in bridging the gap between the company and its customers. Through API integrations, present in [Takeblip's solutions](/en/partnerships/take-blip/) , there are ideal options for those who use e-commerce.

## Hygiene and beauty companies are becoming more connected with digital solutions.

Last year, [Unilever launched a campaign](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/home/marketing/2020/09/22/bonito-e-mais-conectado.html) that involved thousands of people, including celebrities and micro-influencers, promoting its brands.

In an interview with the website Meio & Mensagem, Eduardo Campanella, vice president of marketing for the company, sees this type of action as opportunities generated through digital platforms. See:

***"Traditional consumers have started to enter the digital platform and discover new products*** . "

Eduardo noted a 60% increase in the number of consumers who came to beauty salons weekly, or in person, seeking clarification on how to sanitize products upon arriving home.

According to the vice president of marketing, the sale of products via apps and the use of delivery drivers will continue to be a strong trend, as they provide greater security and convenience for people.

## The pandemic was a driver of increased demand for beauty products.

Pierry Coni, head of the famous beauty brand L'Occitane en Provence Brazil, reinforces the idea that the pandemic accelerated sales and relationship structures, but emphasizes that not all companies were prepared for this leap. See what she said in an interview for the website Meio & Mensagem:

***"Most companies were not prepared for such a sharp increase in the importance of digital sales channels. Consumers, on the other hand, expect a seamless purchase from start to finish, and that hasn't changed."***

As a result, e-commerce is returning to pre-crisis levels, but the Digital Transformation process of online sales structures will inherit a higher quality service when life returns to normal.

Several companies in the hygiene and beauty sector are going through this process of restructuring their sales channels, portals, and services.
