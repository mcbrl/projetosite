## Check out 3 of the most common mistakes that compromise the security of your data.

1.  **Manual safety procedures:** Many companies perform security processes, such as backups or updates, manually. This type of practice can lead to failures or even intentional errors on the part of employees.
2.  **Managing non-existent secrets:** The inappropriate use of shared passwords through chats or unencrypted text documents. The more people involved in password handling processes, the greater the likelihood of leaks, whether accidental or intentional.
3.  **Not allocating security professionals from the start of the process:** The sooner we plan a digital security strategy, the greater the likelihood of success.

Involve DevSecOps professionals from the project's inception. This type of approach is important to ensure that security implementations are carried out holistically, considering each stage of the project—beginning, middle, and end. The security process needs to be continuous, and security failures during the project will negatively impact service quality and delivery deadlines.

## It's worth remembering: data privacy is now the law.

First and foremost, companies need to face a new reality and adapt to new laws and changes in how they manage data security.

With the LGPD (General Data Protection Law) in effect in Brazil, there is a need for mechanisms to protect confidential information.

Failure to comply with the requirements leaves companies subject to sanctions:

- Warning, with a deadline for adopting corrective measures.
- Fines for non-compliance, which can reach 2% of revenue (limited to R$ 50 million).
- Partial or total suspension of its activities.
