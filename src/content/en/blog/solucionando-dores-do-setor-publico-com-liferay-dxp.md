Digital transformation has become a priority for the public sector, and platforms like Liferay DXP are gaining increasing prominence in this scenario. With the growing demand for more accessible, efficient, and secure digital services, governments and institutions face the challenge of modernizing their technological infrastructures without compromising security and user experience.

In this way, digital experience platforms (DXP) play a fundamental role, enabling the creation of integrated, scalable, and citizen-centric digital environments. Liferay DXP is one of the most robust solutions available, offering flexibility, security, and an approach geared towards personalization and operational efficiency.

Vertigo Tecnologia specializes in implementing Liferay DXP, helping public agencies modernize their systems and provide an enhanced experience for both citizens and public servants.

## **What is the importance of creating modern institutional portals?**

Institutional portals are the gateway to digital public services. Citizens and public servants use these spaces to access information, make requests, and interact with government services. However, many of these portals are still outdated, unintuitive, and unable to meet the needs of modern users.

Modernizing institutional portals is essential to ensure efficient and accessible service, as well as providing a secure and reliable environment for data storage and processing.

Liferay [**DXP enables the creation of**](/en/4-steps-to-modernize-public-sector-portals-with-Liferay-DXP/) modern, adaptable, and customized institutional portals, ensuring:

- **Unified experience**Citizens can access different services in a single digital environment, reducing the need to navigate through multiple platforms.
- **Accessibility and usability**Intuitive and responsive interfaces ensure that anyone, regardless of the device used, has easy access to services.
- **Efficient content management**Liferay allows non-technical teams to update information quickly and securely, ensuring that users always have access to up-to-date content.
- **security and compliance**Robust access management, permissions, and authentication ensure the protection of sensitive data and compliance with regulatory standards.

With these capabilities, the platform becomes an essential component in the modernization of digital services, providing a reliable and efficient environment to meet the needs of the public sector.

## **How can Liferay DXP solve the pain points of the public sector?**

The public sector [**faces**](/en/Digital-transformation-in-the-public-sector%3A-digital-processes-will-be-mandatory./) complex challenges in modernizing its digital services. Issues such as legacy systems, information security, integration between different platforms, and the need for customization are some of the main barriers faced by government agencies. Liferay DXP acts as a solution to these challenges, bringing advanced and flexible technology to meet the sector's demands.

### **1\. Integration with legacy systems**

Many public agencies use outdated systems that hinder communication between different departments and platforms. Liferay DXP allows integration with these systems through advanced APIs and connectors, enabling a gradual transition to a more modern environment without compromising existing operations.

### **2\. Safety and compliance with regulatory standards**

Data protection is one of the main challenges for public administration. Liferay DXP offers support for multi-factor authentication, refined access control, and compliance with regulations such as the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law), ensuring that information is stored and processed securely.

### **3\. Process automation and service digitization**

Bureaucracy and manual processes are obstacles to the efficiency of public services. With Liferay DXP, it is possible to automate workflows and digitize internal processes, reducing the need for paper and speeding up the delivery of services to citizens.

### **4\. Creating personalized experiences for different user profiles.**

Citizens and public servants have distinct needs when accessing digital services. Liferay allows for audience segmentation and customization of displayed content, ensuring that each user quickly finds the information and services most relevant to their needs.

### **5\. Multichannel experience**

The diversity of devices used by citizens demands that digital services be accessible across different platforms. [**Liferay DXP**](https://materiais.vertigo.com.br/ebook-modernizacao-de-portais-no-setor-publico) supports a multichannel experience, enabling access to services via computers, smartphones, and tablets, ensuring responsive and intuitive navigation.

With these features, Liferay DXP stands out as a complete solution for modernizing the public sector, enabling the implementation of innovative digital strategies that improve operational efficiency and user experience.

## **Segments of the public sector that can use Liferay DXP**

The versatility of Liferay DXP allows its application in different segments of the public sector, meeting the specific needs of each area and ensuring greater efficiency in service delivery.

### **Public Administration and Government Portals**

Government agencies responsible for administrative services can use Liferay DXP to develop unified portals that centralize essential services, such as document issuance, information retrieval, and access to social programs. Customization and integration facilitate citizens' access to information in an organized and efficient manner.

### **Education and Public Educational Institutions**

Universities, schools, and educational institutions can use the platform to create digital environments geared towards students, teachers, and administrators. Liferay enables the construction of educational portals, knowledge repositories, academic management systems, and spaces for communication between students and faculty.

### **Public Health**

Hospitals, clinics, and healthcare facilities can [**benefit from Liferay DXP**](https://materiais.vertigo.com.br/ebook-modernizacao-de-portais-no-setor-publico) to create platforms that optimize appointment scheduling, access to electronic health records, and management of health programs. The platform's security ensures the protection of patient data, while customization enhances the user experience.

### **Justice and Public Safety**

Justice and security agencies can use Liferay to create institutional portals that allow for process tracking, document requests, and integration between different departments. The automation of legal and administrative processes reduces bureaucracy and improves operational efficiency.

### **Transportation and Urban Mobility**

The transportation sector can implement Liferay DXP-based solutions for managing public transport services, issuing digital tickets, providing information on urban mobility, and establishing communication channels with the public. Accessibility and integration with existing systems facilitate the modernization of services.

### **Finance and Public Revenue**

Agencies responsible for tax collection and financial management can use the platform to offer services such as issuing payment slips, checking outstanding debts, and requesting payment plans. Digitizing these processes improves the taxpayer experience and reduces the burden of in-person services.
