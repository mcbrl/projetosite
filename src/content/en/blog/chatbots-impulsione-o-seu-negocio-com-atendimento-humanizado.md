Have you ever wondered about the importance of chatbots in generating excellent customer service and making your customer journey special?

This trend is hotter than ever, and is the focus of many corporations around the world. According to a study by Gartner, [85% of companies](https://www.gartner.com/en/webinars/4007544/the-gartner-2022-predictions-hyperautomation-inclusive-of-rpa-low-code-) will increase or maintain hyper-automation strategies as early as 2022.

Therefore, thinking about Digital Transformation strategies that have a solid foundation in the architecture of the customer service system becomes fundamental to promoting fast and efficient service via chatbot.

## Many companies need to relieve pressure on their customer service departments.

Perhaps you've already had unpleasant experiences in call centers, spending several minutes talking to someone without any guarantee that your problem would be solved.

Any manual process tends to be more time-consuming, even more so when it depends on human effort. After all, we are not machines and we get tired, sick, and are susceptible to errors or misunderstandings when talking to the customer. The sum of each of these factors results in a service that is almost always slow and inefficient.

We have several examples of significant reductions in customer response time, such as the case of the TV operator SKY, which saw an [88% reduction](https://exame.com/bussola/chatbot-da-sky-reduz-tempo-de-resposta-em-88-e-aumenta-personalizacao/) in waiting time for customer service.

## What do companies gain by humanizing their customer service?

We already know that using chatbots reduces customer service wait times and that they can operate 24 hours a day if needed. But let's get to the point: having a bot operating doesn't mean it's working ideally for your business; you need to go beyond simple implementation, you need a strategy.

By making chatbot integration a project to humanize customer service, the company tends to improve the quality of its approach and increase customer retention.

Humanizing your chatbot can also be a good argument for creating a virtual assistant with empathy to meet customer needs.

It's possible to program your bot to match your company's tone of voice, in some cases even giving it a personalized appearance, like virtual mascots, to create a closer connection.

## Here are some tips for humanizing chatbots.

**1 – Create a persona and tone of voice**

Defining an identity that resembles human characteristics is a powerful tool for solidifying and building closer relationships with customers, which also leads to constructive and spontaneous feedback.

Whenever it's possible to create a human-centered interface, it will help to connect with the audience through empathy.

It is crucial that, at the beginning of the chatbot development process, the company defines its ideal customer persona. This allows for the creation of specific scenarios to generate empathy and credibility with the target audience.

Develop clear communication that aligns with your company's culture.

**2 – Define the conversation flow**

Conduct simulations of customer service interactions that resemble a conversation between two people, considering that a conversation occurs with phrases from both parties, so that the context makes sense.

Map out all the possible outcomes within the dialogues and prepare the chatbot to understand the necessary directions for each point. Developing short, direct flows that don't require many steps to resolve a problem can be a good solution.

**3 – Create personalized interactions**

Using [artificial intelligence](/en/utilities-sector-digital-transformation-through-artificial-intelligence/) is a possibility that improves the customer service experience. Through it, we can identify and memorize important customer information such as their name, address, birthday, and preferred products, thus enabling a personalized conversation.

The chatbot can wish you a happy birthday, ask if the delivery address is the same as the one used for your last purchase, call the customer by name, and even suggest products based on their search history.

**4 – Define a customer service policy for dissatisfied customers.**

Often, when we receive a complaint, we write a detailed and substantial text. Since this is about humanizing your chatbot, it's ideal to identify when this happens, or even when there's profanity, and direct the interaction to a human agent, as some situations can be complex.

**5 - Feedback channels need to be sensitive.**

It's common for customers to complete their purchase or service interaction without providing feedback on their journey up to the end of the conversation. Therefore, a friendly and interactive approach is important to encourage your customer to contribute to this crucial step, which helps improve your chatbot.

However, making complaints almost never requires any kind of incentive; that is, if the customer receives poor service or is dissatisfied, they will provide that feedback. Therefore, it is very important that the tone of the conversation when making complaints is more serious.
