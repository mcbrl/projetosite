Investing in digital security has become a major priority for the financial sector, as it is a favorite target for hackers and fraudsters looking for vulnerabilities within its applications.

It's almost impossible to think of large banks and financial institutions without them using [Cloud Native solutions](/en/solutions/cloud_native/) in some way; however, this doesn't mean that all the functionalities of this powerful technology are being used in the best way.

Hacker attacks are not the only problem for banks; they also need a network infrastructure that can handle simultaneous access and peak hours so that transactions can be carried out quickly and securely.

There are many scenarios and situations that need to be constantly tested and updated to ensure user safety.

We prepared this article to show how cloud-native solutions and a focus on digital security are vital for the financial sector. Enjoy your reading.

## Innovation and accessibility have opened doors, some of them dangerous.

As digital transformation progresses, the adoption of digital banking services has grown, adding to the impact of the pandemic which forced the population to resolve banking bureaucracy online.

According to a survey by the Brazilian Federation of Banks (Febraban), there was a [90% increase](https://canaltech.com.br/seguranca/golpes-financeiros-aumentaram-com-popularidade-das-contas-digitais-na-pandemia-202144/) in the number of accounts opened from 2019 onwards compared to the previous year.

With great ease come great danger.

The phrase, adapted from Uncle Ben's line in Spider-Man, makes a good analogy about the risks we are willing to take in exchange for convenience.

In addition to scams involving unauthorized access to users' cell phones or malicious links that steal their data, there are increasingly more attacks and attempted intrusions aimed at obtaining confidential account holders' information.

We can already see commercials from major banks on television, reinforcing the message that they respect and invest in data privacy, a narrative that is becoming increasingly important when choosing your financial institution.

If there is an increase in the number of users, an overload of accesses on bank servers is to be expected, which requires robust solutions to ensure the full functioning of banking systems.

## Key digital security challenges for financial institutions

When using financial institution apps, you are accessing an application that communicates with databases. These interactions are carried out through API integration.

There is no doubt that API integrations are advantageous, but if they have flaws and are hacked, they become an entry point for actions that jeopardize confidential data between clients and can damage the image and finances of institutions.

Here are [some](/en/How-digital-transformation-contributes-to-open-banking./) digital security challenges that companies in the financial sector need to face:

- **Protecting environments and data:** Implement standardization and automation, granting the minimum privileges to reduce unauthorized connections and access.
- **Security of the CI/CD process:** Integration of security verifiers for containers, as part of the process of including them in the registry.
- **Centralization of user access control and identity resources:** Implementing two-factor authentication is an important step towards securing microservices in banking operations.
- **Introduce secure API gateways:** Security increases control and visibility over the authorization and routing process. By reducing the number of exposed APIs, banking organizations can reduce attack surfaces.
- **Automation of service and system configuration management resources.**Keep security policies up-to-date and eliminate any manual errors that may arise.

With the growth of cloud solutions, we have entered an era where digital security for these platforms is rapidly increasing. This new process is already changing how organizations are creating and managing their applications.
