A fundamental process in the implementation of new projects and during operations, provisioning ensures that servers are prepared to fulfill their function within the scope of the infrastructure.

The steps involved in server provisioning typically include:

- Select one or more servers from those available;
- Installing software such as operating systems, applications, etc.
- Define and configure your resources.

It's hard work that, although it can be made easier, usually requires many hours from the managers and IT technicians involved. Imagine a situation where your [e-commerce](/en/e-commerce-sales%3A-is-your-website-prepared-for-peak-traffic/) The company is experiencing excellent sales volume, but needs more capacity to continue operating well during a known peak in traffic. Slow speeds and downtime, in this scenario, mean dissatisfied customers, fewer sales, and reduced profitability. With the consumer at the center of digital transformations, this situation only worsens.
It is necessary to make automated provisioning a fundamental and as efficient a part of the process as possible. When demand grows, the company must keep up in a scalable way, being able to implement adjustments quickly.
In other words, automation is essential for retail companies to achieve IT optimization and Digital Transformation in practice. Modern and dynamic IT environments need to be able to scale quickly. A common starting point for the operational model... *cloud-native* It allows the operations team to shift their focus from provisioning servers based on homogeneous infrastructure sets to workflows that enable on-demand changes across a variety of clouds and service providers.
In a country where e-commerce is projected to grow ever more rapidly, it's natural that retailers need not only to be present in an online and multichannel environment, but also to integrate the solutions offered to best serve the offline market. This brings advantages for both the customer and the business.
Therefore, all the changes that the retail sector is making, and those that are yet to come, require an infrastructure prepared to keep up with them: always available and offering simple integration with security and connectivity solutions.
One of the solutions, in this regard, is *Infrastructure as Code (IAC)* Infrastructure as Code, or Infrastructure as Code, is a holistic approach to IT infrastructure automation. Keep reading our article and discover its benefits and how it can become a competitive advantage in the retail market.

##
Getting to know IaC

The main goal of a shared service for infrastructure provisioning is to enable the delivery of reproducible infrastructure as code, providing DevOps teams with a way to plan and provision resources within CI/CD workflows using familiar tools.
Infrastructure as Code is an implementation and automation methodology that allows teams to use tools to create, modify, and manage infrastructure in a secure and repeatable way, without losing control, security, quality, and availability. It can be used for physical, virtual, or other resources. [*cloud-native*.](/en/Discover-the-6-pillars-for-building-cloud-native-applications./)
Organizations undergoing Digital Transformation end up putting more pressure on their software application delivery and support teams. The digital experience is the primary interface between customers and businesses in [B2B and B2C companies](/en/the-value-of-APIs-for-retail/). 
Modern digital interactions are designed responsively and built in the cloud to provide rich and personalized experiences. This prioritization pattern forces a shift in the software delivery model and is felt most acutely by IT.
For self-service IT, decoupling the creation process from the provisioning process greatly reduces the time required for any application to go live, since developers no longer need to wait for approval, as they can use a pre-approved template.
This methodology can be used in an extremely strategic way within a technological resource planning process. In short, the approach *IAC* It is nothing more than delivering an agile infrastructure using simple and objective coding, without the need for numerous steps and processes to prepare an environment, without losing control, security, quality, and availability.
This trend is revolutionizing how online retail companies handle the infrastructure of their projects. It is capable of driving consistent value delivery, ensuring availability in digital operations.
As e-commerce environments become more complex and IT teams need to work with tighter deadlines and without making mistakes, infrastructure as code has become a great way to ensure agility and efficiency in online retail.
Furthermore, it is an excellent ally for retailers when investing in the innovation of their virtual environments and in the development of new projects to improve their products and services.
Infrastructure as Code involves important concepts related to IT automation: provisioning, configuration management, and orchestration. Let's understand more:

##
Provisioning

*Provisioning IaC* environments means automating configuration in a scalable way. Tasks such as creating and connecting servers, installing and configuring packages, provisioning, and configuration should be automated. The goal is to do all of this through a single environment definition file.

##
Configuration management

Infrastructure as Code involves more than just provisioning environments; it also involves maintaining them in the desired state. Configuration managers perform checks at regular intervals, saving manual work and helping to standardize your IT environment.

## Orchestration

Your digital retail IT likely requires managing and maintaining many applications spread across multiple devices. *datacenters* and infrastructure. In addition, of course, to deployments in public, private, and hybrid clouds. The more complex an IT system is, the more complicated the management of all its variable components becomes; therefore, orchestration is necessary.
Furthermore, it's possible to control these orchestrations with comprehensive automation solutions. This allows you to monitor and connect them all, as well as easily run more advanced and autonomous systems.

##
Benefits

Having an operational model *cloud-native* This is an inevitable shift for companies that want to maximize their Digital Transformation efforts. Corporate IT needs to evolve from ITIL-based control points focused on cost optimization to a focus on self-service with speed optimization.
Investing in connectivity technology is vital for the current and future challenges of the retail sector. In a world preparing to leverage artificial intelligence, the key step is ensuring that your systems, services, and APIs are always available, which will bring direct benefits to businesses.
This is possible with Infrastructure as Code, designed to help teams deliver new business and more customer value quickly. This type of automation, in addition to increasing delivery speed – greatly benefiting those who need quick responses – also reduces the incidence of errors that could be caused by a system administrator operating manually.
This operational efficiency and control of complex environments – whether due to the sensitivity of the systems, their size, or their indispensability – makes this type of infrastructure an important investment for retailers.
In addition to these, there are other advantages we can highlight:

##
Successful application deployment

With automation, it's possible to go through the allocation/provisioning, creation for testing, and deployment phases in a reliable and coded manner. This reduces the chances of human error and, at the same time, increases efficiency and productivity.
It's also possible to configure, from the start, the services needed for an online retailer and get fully functional applications. All this with a common and transparent approach that all members of the IT team can understand.

##
Safety

Define security and risk management policies for your online store, enforce them, and troubleshoot using them as automated steps across your entire infrastructure. Security takes precedence over IT processes, and automation increases proactivity.
Standardizing security processes and workflows facilitates compliance and auditing. You know exactly how everything works, and with that, you can verify consistency in the processes.

##
Management improvement

Apply *IAC* It provides compliance and governance without requiring a change in the overall team workflow, enabling collaboration and understanding for [DevSecOps](/en/devsecops/).
Without policy as code, commercial organizations resort to using a ticket-based review process to approve changes. This causes developers to wait weeks or more to provision the infrastructure. 
Centralized teams codify policies that enforce security and best practices throughout provisioning. *cloud-native*Ensuring that changes are compliant without creating a manual review bottleneck is essential for the retail sector since... *downtime* Delays in system updates can cause various problems for a company that fails to deliver what its customers need when they are contacted.

##
Productivity increase

The IT team is freed from numerous manual and repetitive tasks, which are now performed automatically. This allows employees to be redirected to more important business activities, such as differentiating the company from the competition. 
Furthermore, controlling the operation of the systems becomes more flexible. Developers will rarely need to work on their management and support. Therefore, the company can count on greater independence regarding the code, increasing the productivity of the entire team.

##
Financial and operational gains

It's possible to achieve a higher financial return thanks to control over the production environment, automation of deliveries, and predictability regarding resource variations. Furthermore, IT incidents and support calls are reduced, eliminating unnecessary expenses.
The automation of *IAC* It also reduces the rate of IT rework, as it allows for the necessary tests to be performed with each code release by the developer, ensuring that the application is functioning correctly and integrated with the main code. 

##
Conclusion

Predicting the future can be a complicated task, but we already know that some features are being improved for the future of automation. Certainly, there will be increasing autonomy and, therefore, more intelligence integrated into these systems, which could expand to encompass more parts of the software.
Imagine the possibility of a security risk being detected, reported, corrected, tested, and deployed, all automatically. Your system could self-recover, collect relevant information to uncover potential attacks, and notify the right professionals, all without you having to deal with it. *downtime*.
Therefore, it is necessary to step out of your comfort zone, change your concepts about IT management, and learn how to work with Infrastructure as Code. With *IAC* It's possible to eliminate repetitive and manual processes that consume your team's time. This allows them to be more productive, reduces errors, improves collaboration, and frees up more time to invest in more meaningful and complex tasks.
