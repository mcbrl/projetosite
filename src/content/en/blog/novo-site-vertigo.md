## New Vertigo Website

It is with great excitement that I announce to you all our new website. Even more modern, intuitive, and designed to bring you the best that the IT world has to offer.

This change paves the way for a new strategic approach for the company, appealing to the global market, in which we are investing in original and innovative digital products, such as the [Vertigo iPaaS](/en/What-is-IPAAS/) and Five Academy, among other products that you will soon be able to see on the market.

The new website is part of a broad rebranding of Vertigo, which aims to continue producing high-quality informative and educational content that, for years, has helped the entire IT community understand the challenges and seize the opportunities in the sector.

We will continue our mission to offer rich materials and practical tools free of charge to analyze your business on its path to digital transformation.

### **New product area and strengthened leadership.**

As we announced in May, my partner and founder André Fernandes stepped down as CTIO to take on the role of CPO, leading the company's newly created Products area, to leverage Vertigo's growth in intellectual property assets. Fernandes will focus on developing new digital products with appeal to the business world.

Among the actions taken, we celebrate the [arrival of the new CTO Vinicius Siani](/en/Vertigo-presents-a-new--recently-created-product-area./) , a professional with 27 years of experience in team management, to strengthen Vertigo's delivery and support areas and help it reach the global market.

### We are the right hand of innovative leaders.

We are always attentive to changes, as well as emerging opportunities and the pain points of our clients.

With the new communication strategy planned through studies on the IT market, we will be closer to our clients and continue working on the mission of Digital Transformation for large companies in the public and private sectors.

### Is your company already part of the Digital Transformation?

We are the ideal partners for bold leaders who want to test new business models. If you also seek to create impactful digital journeys for your customers and employees, then visit our new website and count on me and my team to help you in the digital acceleration process of your business.

**Bruno Tardin Badini**

CEO of Vertigo Technology
