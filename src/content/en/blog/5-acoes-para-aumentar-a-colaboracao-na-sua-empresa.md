The goal of this post is to show the importance of collaboration within companies. It's important to remember that this collaboration can happen through events, portals, mobile applications, bulletin boards, and so on. It's good to emphasize that a portal platform ([Learn more about Portal](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/portal-e-cms-qual-a-diferenca/)This can certainly be very helpful in this collaboration process.
Let's talk about 5 initiatives to increase collaboration within your company.

## 1\. Send questions to your team.

When you have an idea you'd like to present, or even a question that could benefit several people, go ahead and post it, but do so in a place where your team sees relevance. Sometimes a conversation triggers several responses at the same time, and that's important; actions can be taken. Sometimes it's important to say that it may not have a single interaction; the important thing is that you'll be able to find that idea or that question later! Don't ignore your company's habits; if this has never been done, start slowly and show the benefits.

## 2\. Be part of each team's actions.

Whenever someone from any team submits a report, answers one of the posted questions, or starts a conversation, see this as the beginning of an activity and find ways to encourage that action. You can filter the communication flow display by team or project so you can get a quick overview of the work being done. Be a new communication agent within each group!

## 3\. Talk to the company.

Use this channel to communicate good things happening within the company (good financial results, new clients, projects delivered on time, positive client feedback, and so on). Use your intranet for this; post a message on your intranet and share the positive results. Take the opportunity to do this in person as well.

[![](/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/Add-heading-1.png)](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/o-que-nao-pode-faltar-na-sua-intranet/?_fs=87bb9e17-c4e8-4975-b9a7-feb2e4aad195)

## 4\. Launch projects and initiatives.

This is obviously not a daily activity. Whenever necessary, use this space to invite people, create a digital workspace, and invite relevant professionals to interact. This initiative can be very helpful, since not everyone is looking at the same thing at the same time. You are in a digital space, so use it! Don't be limited by geographical location.

## 5\. Know your company

Use your intranet to learn more about who works with you! Connect LinkedIn to other company platforms—yes, that's possible—or create a space where people can talk about their projects. By doing this, you'll know which employees have already worked with a particular technology or project. This can greatly help you in future decisions.
The list above is not intended to be exhaustive; in fact, it's a very personal list. It's quite likely that some features haven't been highlighted. And you? Do you use social collaboration in your company? Which tool do you use? Comment below!
