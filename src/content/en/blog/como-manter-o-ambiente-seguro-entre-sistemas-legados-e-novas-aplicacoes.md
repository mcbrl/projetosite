Currently, companies face an increasing challenge regarding the security of their information systems. Managing environments that combine legacy systems and new applications can bring numerous risks, both to security and to the efficiency of processes. It is important to understand that information security is one of the main pillars for the success of a business, and any failure in this aspect can have significant impacts.

The growing demand for new technological solutions has led to an increase in the complexity of IT environments. This scenario can make it more difficult to protect critical and sensitive information, as well as affect system performance. Integrating legacy systems and new applications can be a complex process and requires meticulous planning.

Another aspect to consider is data security in relation to potential external threats. Using outdated systems can leave companies vulnerable to hacker attacks and other forms of intrusion, which can have serious consequences and directly impact the organization's image.

To meet these challenges, a strategic and efficient approach is needed. Security must be considered at every stage of the process, from project conception to system implementation and maintenance. Adopting best practices and security tools capable of guaranteeing data protection and environmental efficiency is fundamental.

Therefore, we will explore the security impacts between legacy systems and new applications, discuss how the use of DevSecOps can help address these challenges, and present some strategies to increase the security of the IT environment. With this, we hope to contribute to enabling companies to deal more efficiently and securely with the increasing complexity of their information systems.

## Security challenges between legacy systems and new applications.

With the increased use of new technologies, many companies need to deal with the integration of legacy systems and new applications in their IT environments. This integration can generate a number of security-related challenges, as legacy systems often have known vulnerabilities that can be exploited by attackers. Furthermore, new applications may have security flaws that have not yet been discovered and patched.

According to a report by Accenture, "The Cost of Cybercrime," the average cost of a cyberattack for Brazilian companies was R$ 4,6 million. This includes not only direct costs, such as damage to IT infrastructure and lost revenue, but also indirect costs, such as loss of customer trust and damage to the company's reputation.

Integrating legacy systems and new applications can create unnecessary complexity that hinders the identification of security threats and the implementation of preventative measures. This can result in greater exposure to security risks and the need for additional investments in technology and human resources to manage those risks.

Another common challenge is the lack of communication and collaboration between teams from different systems, which can lead to security gaps and a lack of coordination in detecting and responding to security incidents. To overcome this challenge, it is important to have an integrated security management plan that takes into account all systems in use and encompasses the collaboration of all teams involved.

Compliance with security regulations, such as the General Data Protection Law (LGPD) in Brazil and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Union, can be an additional challenge when integrating legacy systems and new applications. It is important to ensure that all systems comply with applicable regulations and that the teams involved are aware of their data security and privacy obligations.

Security between legacy systems and new applications is a critical issue for companies, which need to ensure that their data and information remain secure in a constantly changing environment. The impacts can be devastating, ranging from leaks of sensitive information to loss of customer trust and financial losses.

Investing in modern security solutions, such as DevSecOps and other advanced technologies, is essential to ensure the protection of the company's IT environment. By understanding the risks and acting proactively, it's possible to minimize negative impacts and maintain a secure and productive environment for everyone.

## How can you use DevSecOps to your advantage?

Due to the growing demand for increasingly secure IT environments, many companies are seeking alternatives to ensure the protection of their data and systems. It is in this context that **DevSecOps** emerges as an efficient solution for integrating security into software development and operation.

DevSecOps is a modern approach that aims to integrate security into all stages of the software lifecycle, from planning to release. This methodology allows development and operations teams to work together from the beginning of the process, so they can identify and resolve potential security flaws quickly and efficiently.

In a Forrester Research study, companies that adopted DevSecOps experienced a 60% reduction in the number of critical vulnerabilities compared to companies that did not use this methodology. Furthermore, the same study showed that companies that implemented DevSecOps were able to fix security flaws 11 times faster than those that did not.

Another advantage of DevSecOps is that it allows companies to automate a large part of their security processes, reducing the possibility of human error. This automation allows IT teams to focus on more strategic activities, which can increase team efficiency and productivity.

DevSecOps, therefore, can help companies integrate security into all stages of the software lifecycle, reduce the possibility of human error, fix security flaws more quickly, and increase the efficiency and productivity of IT teams.

## How to increase the security of your IT environment.

IT environment security is crucial for ensuring the protection of company data and the integrity of systems. Below are some practices that can help increase security in the IT environment:

- **Conduct a risk assessment:** Assessing risks is important to understand the threats that the IT environment may be facing. Based on this assessment, it is possible to define more effective security strategies to protect the systems.
- **Update regularly:** Keeping systems up-to-date is crucial to avoid vulnerabilities that can be exploited by hackers. Be sure to apply security updates as soon as they are available.
- **Implement encryption:** Using encryption to protect confidential data is a highly recommended practice. Ensure that all sensitive information is protected by end-to-end encryption.
- **Adopt a strong password policy:** Having a strong password policy helps protect systems against brute-force attacks and other intrusion techniques. Make sure all passwords are complex and are updated regularly.
- **Implement monitoring and access control:** Monitoring access to systems is important for detecting potential suspicious activity. Furthermore, access control helps ensure that only authorized individuals have access to the systems.

These are some practices that can help increase the security of the IT environment. It's important to remember that security is not a static process and must be continuously evaluated and improved to ensure the protection of systems. Investing in training and awareness of IT security among staff is fundamental. All employees should be educated on best practices, including the importance of creating strong passwords and protecting confidential information.
