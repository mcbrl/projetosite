**Productivity in the workplace** This is one of the main challenges for modern companies, especially for **knowledge workers.** We are experiencing an endless explosion of information, so what is actually important and should be prioritized? For example, [**latest official data from Google**](http://www.internetlivestats.com/google-search-statistics/) They mention 1,2 trillion searches per year, which is more than eight times greater than in 2005. 
An increasing amount of work time is spent "sifting through" information. [**Interesting McKinsey study from 2012.**](http://www.mckinsey.com/industries/high-tech/our-insights/the-social-economy) It was found that knowledge workers spent more than 60% of their time on activities unrelated to their roles, such as managing email, attending meetings, and searching for internal information. User-Centric Intranets aim to... **to improve employee efficiency,** reducing the time spent on these activities of having to sift through information. 

## **Reduce email overload.**

In 2012, you spent 28% of your work time managing emails. Today, that percentage is certainly even higher. In 2015, the number of business emails sent and received per user totaled 122 per day. This number continues to grow and is expected to increase further. [**On average, 126 messages were sent and received per day per user until the end of 2019.**](http://www.radicati.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/Email-Statistics-Report-2015-2019-Executive-Summary.pdf)Calm down, that's an average. 
Assuming that it takes, on average, at least two minutes to process each email, 122 emails a day represent four hours of work, or half a workday just to figure out what to do!
One of the **social intranet** It significantly reduces the number of work-related emails, replacing endless email threads with contextualized, easily indexable and searchable conversations or direct interactions via instant messaging.

## **Accelerate the search for internal information.**

The information explosion also invades the corporate world (finding the right template, the right policy, the right people, the right data, the right form, etc.). On average, at least 19% of work time is spent searching for internal information. And that document, is it in its final version? Is there another document after “V6\_Final\_FINAL\_06/16” or is this really the last version?
An intranet typically provides a single point of entry for company information, often including a **knowledge base** and facilitating the search through various search engines and information classification.
There are several tools that can help you in this process: Oracle, [Liferay](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/liferay-lider-em-portais-horizontais-pelo-sexto-ano/)Microsoft, IBM, [Fluig](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/4-maneiras-de-organizar-as-informacoes-da-sua-empresa/),

## **Simplify administrative processes.**

Internal processes can consume a significant amount of an employee's work time, especially for newcomers. This amount of time varies from company to company, but administrative processes such as vacation requests, expense reports, timesheets, and travel requests do consume time.
One of the **Corporate Intranet solution** It can provide a single point of entry for these routine business processes through integration with existing business applications. With this type of integration, business processes can be accelerated and facilitated.

## **Reduce the time spent in meetings.**

[**Internal meetings can cost up to 15%.**](https://www.themuse.com/advice/how-much-time-do-we-spend-in-meetings-hint-its-scary) of the average employee's time. This number is much higher for administrative and managerial positions. While meetings are necessary and important, implementing a collaborative intranet tends to improve efficiency. **team productivity** throughout the company.
Some meetings will naturally disappear, for example, those routine interdepartmental meetings scheduled for communication purposes, without a clear and precise agenda. Others will become more productive due to... **online collaboration** prior and contextualized.
As a result, some travel expenses will be reduced, as well as the time lost commuting to attend these meetings.

## **Efficient teamwork**

**Collaborative** **intranet solutions** facilitate all interactions and teamwork, including across various business functions that may be geographically distributed, improving **team productivity and the financial outcome of projects** . Some real-world examples:

- A complex RFP would require less time to be answered.
- Ideas coming from many different sources would accelerate innovation.
- A complex issue requiring support could be resolved in less time and with a higher quality response.
- Working remotely on certain solutions and having access to corporate content.

## **ROI of a Corporate Intranet through Productivity Gains**

The financial impact of **increased productivity in the workplace** The return can be enormous. In fact, that's the most compelling argument to justify the ROI of a corporate intranet. For example, if it's possible to improve the time spent managing email by just 15%, **productivity gains** **annual** They represent about 3% of their **annual employee costs** From the moment your intranet starts gaining adoption throughout the company, it might seem complex, but it's not! Imagine having a search tool, just like Google, but to find your information whenever you want it?
The most complicated part is justifying these gains to management. To do this, it's necessary to estimate the time spent on different activities within the company, as well as estimate what is expected to be gained with the new intranet. For example:

- To estimate the time spent on email management, collect email volume statistics from the IT team. Then, perform some time estimation calculations.
- To estimate the time spent searching for information, find some examples of the effort required to find specific information in real life and extrapolate.
- Select a few administrative processes and map out how long they take.
- Use a few examples of cross-departmental collaboration (responding to RFPs, launching new products) and estimate what is needed in terms of the number of meetings, travel, and email exchanges.
- Make assumptions about what could be improved through the use of an intranet, using a low, medium, and high impact assessment.
- Assess whether someone who should not have had access to document X did so due to an information management failure.

Using real-life examples of wasted and saved time within your company would help illustrate the value of the Intranet in terms of productivity gains and cost reductions linked to those gains.

## **Conclusion**

In conclusion, here's a rough estimate: productivity gains from a successful user-centric intranet are at least 30%. A more conservative average would be a 15% improvement. As with any new technology or feature, mass adoption of the intranet is essential to achieving the much-desired significant productivity gains.
