An API ( [click here to understand what an API is](/en/Understand-what-an-API-is./) ) is used to facilitate and expedite communication between systems and devices. In recent years, it has become fundamental for those who intend to create richer, more scalable, and dynamic systems. With the help of cloud computing, mobile applications, and other revolutions within IT, these interfaces have reinvented the way business partners exchange information with each other.

Although typically associated with the B2C market, APIs are also integrated into various corporate systems today. By offering an interface that gives developers and partners the opportunity to access their information, an organization can increase its market presence, customer base, and even its profits.

## Increasing the number of partners through APIs

Many people believe that the only way to acquire new customers is through marketing campaigns. However, APIs are gaining prominence in the list of strategies for acquiring new partners. Companies like Hotel Urbano, Ipiranga, NovaPontoCom, and Bovespa, for example, have seen a greater flow of users by creating a set of protocols and routines where developers can obtain and post information. Google Maps, on the other hand, allows people to use their data to build personalized maps. This has enabled the creation of dynamic tools ranging from violence maps to plugins with the addresses of supermarket chain stores. By giving developers the freedom to explore their data, new tools have been used and Google Maps traffic has expanded, keeping the service ahead of its competitors.

## Using APIs to increase consumer and developer engagement.

One of the main ways to achieve consistent profits is by increasing customer engagement. There are countless strategies to achieve this goal: some choose to invest in social media, others to use innovative advertising, and still others opt to promote APIs.

#### **[DROPBOX](https://www.dropbox.com/developers)**

One successful example is [Dropbox for businesses](https://www.dropbox.com/business) . This document storage application allows independent developers to create apps that, by integrating with the platform, generate greater engagement with it. The tools are promoted by the company itself, which helps it maintain its leadership in the competitive digital market.

To encourage developers to adopt your APIs, a good method of attraction is referral and affiliate programs. Sharing benefits with developers who access your systems motivates them to implement them in more services. In some cases, it may be useful to adopt a strategy similar to Dropbox, promoting the best applications that use data from your platform. This not only showcases the business opportunities that your interface offers, but can also be an opportunity to profit through a *traffic referral* program (recommending one website to another).

## Content distribution in search of more revenue.

**[Booking](http://www.booking.com/affiliate-program/faq.html)**

The success of many business models depends on the number of people who view and interact with a platform. One of the best ways to do this is through an API. The hotel search website Booking.com, for example, follows this strategy. It allows small websites to use its platform's data to search for available hotels on a commission-based sales model. Large e-commerce sites, such as Submarino, allow small partners to sell products on their platform. Stock verification and product promotion are, naturally, also done through APIs.

## Conclusion

The ways in which an API can increase a company's revenue are numerous. A company with a good platform can expand its business, increase its revenue, and have more engaged customers. ( [click here to see examples of companies that use APIs](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/entenda-o-que-e-uma-api/) )
