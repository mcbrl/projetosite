**The connectivity benchmarks report – released by MuleSoft – included the participation of leading technology countries.**   

The benchmark report aims to answer questions such as: "What are the main challenges facing IT organizations?", "What is the current status of digital transformation initiatives?", and "What is the role of IT in terms of evolution in this new, customer-centric era?" [MuleSoft](/en/mulesoft/) , provider of the leading platform for creating application networks, the Anypoint Platform, announced the conclusions of its 2019 Connectivity Benchmarks Report on the current state of IT.

## **The research structure that originated the benchmarks report**

The research was conducted based on a global data collection effort, with technology leaders from major powers such as the US, UK, Germany, Netherlands, Australia, Singapore, and China. The benchmark report reveals that while 97% of organizations are currently undertaking or planning to undertake digital transformation initiatives, integration challenges are hindering the efforts of 84% of them. Almost half of the respondents – the equivalent of 43% – reported that more than 1.000 applications are being used across all their companies, but only 29% of them are integrated with each other, leaving valuable data trapped in silos.

With 650 people interviewed, the research also reveals that IT is struggling to keep up with business demands, given that 64% of respondents said they were unable to deliver all projects last year. Furthermore, project volumes are expected to grow, with respondents predicting an average increase of 32% this year. This means that if Digital Transformation initiatives are not successfully completed, 90% of organizations believe their revenues will be negatively impacted.

The interviews were conducted online, using a rigorous multi-level screening process to ensure the participation of only candidates suitable for the research.

Among the many points raised, these were the main topics addressed in the report, the conclusions of which we will present in detail below:

- Digital transformation is paramount – everything to have a connected customer.
- The new role of IT: a business catalyst.
- The IT delivery gap widens as new technologies emerge.
- IT leaders prepare for the future.
- API Programs: The Key to Unlocking the Customer Experience

## **Digital transformation is paramount: everything needed to have a connected customer.**

In today's connected world, companies are increasingly competing on speed and agility, striving to meet the high expectations of their customers. However, an alarming finding from the report is that only 36% of IT leaders report being able to offer a complete connectivity experience to their customers. The vast majority of organizations (97%) are currently organizing and planning digital transformation initiatives – through mobile, cloud, and analytics technologies. Among their main goals are increasing IT efficiency (79%), expanding business (77%), and improving customer experience (71%).

In a market where digital strategies are the threshold of competition, organizations must prepare to face a new set of challenges if they wish to come out on top. In the future, they will use digital technologies to better understand their customers; to deliver cohesive experiences across all channels, within customer ecosystems; and to operate so efficiently that improvements can be delivered in real time. While this goal seems quite clear, companies are still struggling to overcome the traditional, fragmented model of operation.

To truly stand out in a market where every organization is digital, companies must go further, adopting digital tools and overcoming the most varied and persistent business challenges, striving to achieve digital maturity in line with market demands.

## **The new role of IT: a business catalyst.**

Digital transformation, which began as an IT initiative, has evolved into a business necessity as a whole, with a single objective: to improve the customer experience. And in this objective, IT and business leaders report being more aligned than ever in 2019, which is reflected in the numbers: 72% compared to 57% in 2018.

And this alignment between them is also demonstrated in the key performance indicators (KPIs): the two main objectives reported by organizations are to increase business efficiency (77%) and increase IT operational efficiency (79%). This shows that the courage to change is taking place in organizations, shifting the focus away from IT as a simple operational function, recognizing its central role in the business, and leading the organization towards a successful digital transformation.

## **The IT delivery gap widens as new technologies emerge.**

With new technologies, IT leaders are feeling more pressure than ever to rethink how things will be developed from now on. This is because, despite expectations of a 32% annual increase in IT demands, the majority (77%) of IT respondents expect a budget increase of less than 10%.

The growing demand for IT and the increasing need for IT support are reflected in the business through the growing number of projects that departments are involved in each year. And on average, IT leaders are being pressured to deliver 32% more projects year after year. However, only 36% of them reported managing to deliver all the projects they were involved in during the last year.

Therefore, in order to meet business needs, IT leaders are looking for new ways to streamline and manage company systems, data, and devices.

To achieve this digital transformation, the biggest challenges cited by respondents are infrastructure and legacy systems. Of IT leaders, 59% report that their legacy infrastructure hinders the introduction of new technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Big Data, and the Internet of Things.

## **The report indicated that IT leaders are preparing for the future.**

Decision-makers at the world's most innovative IT companies are increasingly looking for ways to make their teams more productive, reducing the learning curve and investing in cost-effective tools. Below we highlight some of the initiatives that IT teams are considering:

### Hire more talent or outsource contracts.

More than half (52%) of the IT teams surveyed plan to hire more talent to support their growing needs. However, 29% of respondents cite a lack of skills and experience within their IT teams as one of the biggest challenges to digital transformation. This suggests that, even though 44% of respondents believe they will be able to meet their demands through outsourcing, this appears to be an immediate solution with serious implications later on.

### Using DevOps to improve the efficiency of application production and deployment.

Half (50%) of IT decision-makers are using DevOps to improve the efficiency of application production and deployment. When done correctly, this allows developers to automate processes such as security, patching, configuration management, performance testing, and monitoring across multiple environments.

### Reusing software components to create new products and services.

According to the report, 46% of IT teams are increasingly reusing software components to improve the efficiency of application production and deployment. When the IT team creates reusable assets, it allows the company to increase speed and overall delivery capacity.

### Leveraging APIs to increase productivity, agility, and innovation.

APIs are the glue that connects to the digital world. For organizations to deliver Digital Transformation and innovation with the speed of market leaders, it is essential to discover, connect, and reuse software assets using APIs. Of those surveyed, 53% of those who have APIs in their business results matrix report increased productivity, 46% report greater team agility, and 40% report increased innovation—all indicators that serve to better connect and integrate the business.

## **API Programs: The Key to Unlocking the Customer Experience**

Companies are making progress toward connecting the customer to the experience through digital transformation initiatives. Technical and business leaders are becoming increasingly aligned in their business objectives. More mature organizations are partnering with customers to reinvent their digital strategies.

In this scenario, there are significant financial benefits to adopting APIs as a strategy for your company. Among those surveyed, 91% of those who report having public and/or private APIs are seeing significant business results, with increased productivity. 53% report a reduction in operational costs and 33% report an increase in revenue. For 36% of respondents, APIs generate more than 25% of the organization's revenue.

However, simply owning them is not enough to stay ahead of the market. It's necessary to develop and coordinate a complex API strategy within your business. For example, data shows that those who manage their APIs as products are more likely than their API-owning counterparts to demonstrate increased innovation – 49% versus 40% – and greater agility among teams to support IT – 58% versus 46%.

Furthermore, the research shows that participants who reuse software assets when developing new projects are more likely than their API-owning peers to show increased productivity – 63% versus 53% – and revenue growth – 41% versus 29%.

## **The closing thought**

With the IT landscape becoming increasingly complex, organizations can build their application networks one API at a time, providing businesses with an agile foundation for success in the digital age. In this sense, reusable APIs serve as the building blocks of the application network, enabling the creation of new business models and simplifying the expansion of a connected partner ecosystem. The MuleSoft Connectivity Benchmarks Report demonstrates the importance of adopting a comprehensive API strategy to generate desired business outcomes.
