Have you ever imagined having a platform capable of accelerating the modernization of your infrastructure without requiring your team to handle complex technical aspects or undergo lengthy learning cycles?

VeeCode Platform is a complete API Platform, built to deliver much more than isolated templates and tools. It combines Platform Engineering, an integrated DevPortal, support for dynamic plugins, observability, and scalability, centralizing what teams need to reduce technical barriers and gain autonomy. Our mission is clear: to simplify the transition to a cloud-native architecture, with an intelligent ecosystem of self-service tools, pipeline automation, and integrated governance.

## **Why is VeeCode Platform a strategic ally?**

In a scenario where [**DevOps**](/en/How-can-AI-optimize-DevOps/) culture demands collaboration, dynamism, and continuous delivery, relying on specialists at each operational stage is no longer sustainable.

### **Infrastructure as a product**

By adopting Platform Engineering as its cornerstone, VeeCode Platform delivers infrastructure as a product: ready to be consumed on demand, with automated provisioning and reusable components.

### **Immediate benefits**

- Reduced **cognitive load** of the teams;
- Standardization of environments and pipelines;
- Faster delivery of APIs and microservices;
- Scalability with less rework.

The result? More standardization, fewer technical barriers, and greater speed of innovation.

## **What is the VeeCode Platform?**

VeeCode [**Platform**](https://platform.vee.codes/) is a modular, open-source solution designed to accelerate IT modernization.

With rapid environment provisioning, integration with CI/CD pipelines, and support for Infrastructure as Code (IaC), it allows you to adopt DevOps and GitOps practices at scale.

### **Dynamic plugins for greater autonomy.**

Dynamic plugins adapt to your company's technical context, automating critical steps from environment configuration to API deployment. This frees up your team's time to focus on what truly matters: delivering reliable products to market.

## **DevPortal VeeCode: Everything your team needs, all in one place.**

VeeCode's DevPortal is the central cockpit for operations. It's where development, infrastructure, and security teams access API specs, **CI/CD** status , Pull Requests, performance metrics, documentation, and alerts.

### **Advantages of DevPortal**

- Centralization of data and tools on a self-service platform;
- Governance with access control and integrated security;
- Visibility across the entire API and microservice lifecycle;
- Genuine collaboration between teams, reducing operational silos.

With DevPortal, consuming APIs and managing microservices become simpler, more scalable, and more reliable.
