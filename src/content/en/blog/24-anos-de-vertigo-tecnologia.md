In the constantly evolving corporate market, Vertigo Tecnologia celebrates 24 years of existence with a history full of learning and achievements.

In this way, our C-Level executives, André Fernandes **(CPO)** , Bruno Badini **(CEO)** , and Vinicius Siani **(CTO)** , shared some of their vision involving the past, present, and future of Vertigo Tecnologia.

### The importance of technological trends and the austere management model.

Our CEO, **Bruno Badini,** highlighted the importance of staying up-to-date with the main technological trends in the market, which puts us at the forefront of the most in-demand solutions.

*"Throughout this entire journey, we have always stayed attuned to the main global technological trends in the corporate market. This has placed us at the forefront of the most in-demand offerings and solutions at any given time."*

*Our austere management model has allowed us to be very resilient and adept at navigating macroeconomic turbulence, such as the 2008 stock market crash, the Covid-19 pandemic, wars, changes in government, etc. All of this happened with a purpose and a non-negotiable premise of having our PEOPLE at the center of the business, which today is embodied in one of our main corporate values.”*

### Adaptability and innovation are essential to remain relevant.

In turn, **André Fernandes** , our CPO, revealed how Vertigo has distinguished itself over time, adapting to market changes.

*"We have always had an unusual ability to understand the contemporary problems of the corporate world and to discover how to solve them with the technologies available on the market (even if recently launched)."*

*This leading role helped us establish partnerships with innovative global companies at the moment they invested in Brazil as a market, positioning us as the best national partner for these brands.*

*It has been this way since our beginning, when the corporate world discovered Delphi and Visual Basic; when it evolved to distributed platforms in Java EE; when it adopted bus services and when it moved them to containers and microservices; when it went from mainframes to Linux and when it went from the data center to the cloud.*

*Today we are a consultancy that not only performs the technical work but also supports clients in their transformation process towards a modern, cloud-native, responsive IT environment.”*

### Plans and goals for the future

Our CTO, **Vinicius Siani** , shared Vertigo's exciting plans and goals for the future. The company seeks to delight its customers with disruptive solutions and offer an exceptional work environment for its employees.

*"Vertigo relentlessly seeks to delight its customers by generating value with the most disruptive technologies and to captivate its employees by being an exceptional place to work."*

*An amazing team that grows every day! We intend to grow even more while preserving these purposes and living our values.*

*Conquering the world by working well and being happy. Goals? Let's create new, even greater value-added offerings and expand our operations in Brazil and internationally as well.*

*To be great, in this way, to the point of becoming a world reference in what we do, is undoubtedly a dream! To infinity and beyond!”*
