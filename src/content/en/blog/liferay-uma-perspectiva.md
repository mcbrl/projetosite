When choosing a digital experience platform, such as [Liferay](/en/Liferay/) , for your company, several important factors should be considered. In many cases, for security reasons, managers like to learn more about the company responsible for the technology they intend to adopt.

With that in mind, we wrote this article that tells a bit of the story and inspiring journey of Liferay, from its creation, as well as the evolution of its products to the current versions.

Come along on this journey with us!

Founded in the early 2000s in the United States, one of Liferay's initial goals was to offer an enterprise portal solution. Currently, its mission [is to build a vibrant business, make its technology useful, invest in communities, and thus enable people to reach their full potential to help others.](https://www.liferay.com/pt/company/our-story)

Liferay has established itself globally over nearly two decades. It's worth noting that Brazil is the only country representing the company in Latin America, with branches in São Paulo and Recife.

For seven years, it has remained in Gartner's Magic Quadrant for Digital Experience Platform, occupying the highest level of the classification methodology, that of Leader.

The use of technology is becoming increasingly important in the market, and as a result, companies are transforming their business structures. With the Liferay Digital Experience Platform, you can get ahead and promote your company's digital transformation in a consistent and effective way.

## Liferay's timeline and its evolution.

It all started in a church in the Diamond Bar neighborhood of California, where one of the partners who helped Bryan Cheung create the company was a volunteer. Since the church had a large, unused space during the week, they saw an opportunity to use the free space for consulting with their clients.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/logo-liferay.png)

In 2004, the company transformed into Liferay Inc., focused solely on **corporate portals** and created to meet the main demands that arose in relation to the Liferay Portal. The first branch was opened in Germany in 2006. Expansion continued to the Asian continent in 2007 with the opening of a branch in China. In 2009 it expanded again to India and currently has a presence on every continent.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/liferay2.png)

In 2007, **Liferay Portal** was named Technology of the Year by InfoWorld magazine, and Liferay subsequently partnered with ICEsoft Technologies, enabling Ajax support within Liferay Portal.

In 2008, it dedicated itself exclusively to the jQuery UI project, adding one of the world's largest JavaScript libraries to its products. Also in the same year, it was recognized by Gartner as a Visionary Horizontal Portal company and signed a technology-sharing agreement with Sun Microsystems, which lasted until 2010, when Sun was acquired by Oracle.

Starting in 2009, the company changed its consulting model to a subscription model. From then on, the company began to release updates and hired developers to continuously improve the product.

After changing its model in 2012, Liferay released version 6.1 with improvements and new features compared to previous versions, including an enhanced *document library* , its own marketplace, and a dynamic data list.

In 2013, a partnership was formalized with TIBCO Software, facilitating the integration of Liferay with other systems. The goal was to provide adapters for the connectivity company CE Adaptadores, a TIBCO partner.

In 2016, the criteria for Gartner's Magic Quadrant for Horizontal Portals were changed, becoming the Magic Quadrant for Digital Experience Platforms, where Liferay remained in the **Leader** quadrant.

In the same year, it launched the Liferay Digital Experience Platform, successor to the Liferay Portal EE, which changed its previous strategy with the goal of increasing the product's reach.

Available in versions 7.0 (Community Edition) and DXP (Enterprise Edition), it has made significant changes to the portal's architecture, switching to OSGi (Open Services Gateway Initiative – a component system for the Java platform) and accepting more current front-end frameworks such as Angular and React.

Liferay DXP 7.1 is the latest version of the product and was released earlier this year. This update offers business users greater flexibility in building digital content, making web content creation more intuitive and eliminating the need for coding.

In this way, it can assist the developer more strategically in delivering this content, creating their own customizations according to the client's interests or importing them directly into Liferay DXP via APIs.

## The shift in perspective that elevated Liferay's standing.

During the development of the product and the company, Bryan Cheung realized that he had begun to attract the attention of more mature and established companies, which were not necessarily looking for the most robust technologies, but rather the most stable ones.

Therefore, instead of implementing a single, more robust version of its software, it decided to create customized software branches for each production environment.

Customers were satisfied with purchasing a specific version of the software, since they could always use the same version without having to migrate to a new product with each new version released.

In this way, there is no need to constantly mix future product developments and release new features for the same version, which increases the stability of the product itself. With these decisions, Liferay's revenue increased almost tenfold during the seven years following its creation.

With Liferay's digital transformation platform, you can manage and unify all the content of your website, your mobile applications, and your internal business structures.

Furthermore, it's also possible to provide enterprise portals, manage content and middleware, connecting your entire back-end system and presenting it to the user in a user-friendly format.

Currently, Liferay is one of the largest companies in the field of digital transformation and customer experience, with success stories in large companies such as Domino's, Allianz, HP, among many others.

You can use their newest product, Liferay DXP, to build your intranet, create user-friendly portals, and integrate with other systems if needed.

## How do Liferay products work?

Liferay products operate on an End of Service Life (EoSL) policy, providing support for the versions to which subscriptions were made, according to the customer's subscription level.

The Premium support period for a given Liferay product is equivalent to 4 years from the product's initial delivery date. In other words, the date the company made that version of the product available for licensing or sale.

During this phase, the customer will receive full support, according to their subscription level. This includes access to the Liferay Customer Portal and the complete knowledge base of its products, as well as access to all consolidated fixes and service packs for their versions and security alerts for critical patches.

After the Premium phase ends, the Limited product support phase begins, lasting for an additional three years. It offers the same support as before, but with support staff guiding customers to upgrade to a more recent version of the product if they are interested.

By using Liferay products, you open up a range of options for your company. With Liferay Analytics Cloud, for example, you can monitor the behavior of customer and user profiles. You can also integrate it with Liferay's digital commerce platform, and the responsiveness of Liferay DXP allows you to develop native or hybrid applications, providing unique mobile experiences.
