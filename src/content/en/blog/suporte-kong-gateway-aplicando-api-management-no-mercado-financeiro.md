## **Security and Compliance: Protection of Sensitive Data**

Security is a priority in the [**financial sector**](https://materiais.vertigo.com.br/ebook-setor-financeiro-como-blindar-api-com-o-uso-de-kong-e-inteligencia-artificial) . Inadequate API management can expose sensitive data and compromise the integrity of services. Kong Gateway offers advanced layers of protection, ensuring that transactions are secure and compliant with regulations such as LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law).

### **How does Kong Gateway enhance security in the financial sector?**

- Advanced authentication and authorization: support for OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, and JWT for secure access control.
- Data encryption: protecting information in transit and at rest with state-of-the-art encryption.
- Rate limiting and monitoring: prevention against DDoS attacks and other cyber threats.
- Detailed audits and logs: complete transparency for compliance with regulatory bodies.

With Vertigo Tecnologia, banks and fintechs can implement these security practices efficiently, ensuring that their [**APIs are protected**](/en/zero-trust-security-in-a-devsecops-strategy/) against fraud and data leaks.

## **Fast and Flexible Integrations: Open Banking and Beyond Banking**

The concept of Open Banking requires financial institutions to make [**open APIs**](/en/Public-vs.-private-APIs-in-the-financial-sector%3A-which-is-the-best-choice/) available for integration with third parties, ensuring greater innovation and competitiveness. The Kong API facilitates this process, allowing banks and fintechs to expose and consume APIs securely, with control and traceability.

### **Key benefits of the Kong API for Open Banking**

- Standardization of APIs: facilitates interoperability between financial institutions and partners.
- Permissions and consent management: complete control over third-party access to banking data.
- Resilient API Gateway: maintains service stability even during peak access times.

Beyond Open Banking, Beyond Banking is gaining ground, expanding integrations to ecosystems that go beyond the traditional banking sector, such as insurance, investments, and even retail. Vertigo Tecnologia structures these integrations so that banks and fintechs can quickly adapt to the new demands of the market.
