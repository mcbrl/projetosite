On June 21, 2022, the city of São Paulo hosted the first edition of Cloud Native Experience (CNE), an in-person event promoted by Vertigo Tecnologia and held at the Fogo de Chão steakhouse in the Jardins neighborhood.

A recurring theme in the actions promoted by Vertigo this year, the cloud scenario is proving to be a constantly growing market. According to Gartner, a reference on the global cloud market, it is estimated that by 2022, 70% of companies and organizations will have some application or strategy implemented in the cloud.

In this context, the meeting aimed to promote an enriching discussion about experiences related to Cloud Native practices and how this approach can offer agility to application development processes, with greater efficiency and cost savings.

The event was attended by innovative leaders from 12 major national and international companies, promoting a rich exchange of knowledge on IT operations management, as well as networking among participants from different sectors.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/EquipeVertigo-1024x614.jpg)

Vertigo Tecnologia Team – Bruno Badini (CEO), João Trevelin (Account Executive), Vinicius Siani (CTO), Thiago Morais (Marketing Manager), Luiz Fraga (Account Executive)

### Vertigo explains how Cloud Native practices enable companies to create and run applications in modern and dynamic environments.

Bruno Badini, CEO of Vertigo, opened the event with a warm welcome to those present and introduced Vinicius Siani, CTO of Vertigo, and João Trevelin, Account Executive, who gave a joint presentation on the topic "Now it's Cloud Native".

Among the topics discussed, the pair drew a parallel between the evolution of IT infrastructure, resilient architecture, the evolution of software design, and the framework of the Cloud Native journey.

According to Vinicius Siani, typical Cloud Native architectures can be understood through the use of technologies and management models typical of the cloud, including containers, infrastructure as code, provisioning automation, elasticity, resilience, orchestrators, microservices, etc.

João Trevelin highlighted the recurring concern of companies in reconciling security, connectivity, and agility in processes and deliveries, presenting the Cloud Native journey as a model that facilitates this balance.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Vinicius_Siani-1024x634.jpg)

Vinicius Siani (CTO of Vertigo Tecnologia) / João Trevelin (Account Executive of Vertigo Tecnologia)

### The challenges of building digital products was the topic of the second lecture at the event.

The second presentation of the day was given by José Augusto de Oliveira Jr., Engineering Director at Nubank, who shared his experience on the Cloud Native journey through the presentation "From Zero to Cloud Native Product".

According to José Augusto, the path to building a digital product involves correctly assembling the technical team, clearly defining OKRs, constantly focusing on the financial responsibility of projects, and developing a strong culture of belonging among the work teams.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Jose_Augusto-1024x634.jpg)
