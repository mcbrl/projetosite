## A story always linked to talent development and the Digital Transformation of society.

Last Friday, July 23, 2021, Vertigo Tecnologia celebrated its 22nd anniversary during a lively online happy hour. On that same date in 1999, founder André Fernandes, now CPO (Chief Product Officer), realized his dream of entrepreneurship at a time when technology was not yet a strategic priority for organizations.

Thus was born a consulting and training company, whose first address in a commercial space in Rio de Janeiro was less than 30 m² and had a team made up of young university students who shared the dream of impacting the digital journeys of companies and society.

Throughout its history, the company has accumulated stories of success and inspiration. The greatest of these, without a doubt, is that of Bruno Badini, CEO of Vertigo, who also began his journey in 1999 as an intern. In 2002, after a restructuring of the executive board, Bruno assumed the role of Operations Director and, in 2006, was promoted to CEO, a position he holds to this day. "Vertigo gave me the opportunity to be who I am. Immense gratitude for everything we have built together here," he emphasized.

One of the company's most striking characteristics is its constant investment in employee development. For Amanda Carvalho, an HR intern, Vertigo represents one of the largest and most powerful learning, development, and growth environments she has ever been a part of. For Daniela Barros, Agile Project Manager, the difference lies in the trust placed in people. "Vertigo represents the best vote of confidence I could have received," she revealed.

## Focus on the global market

Today, Vertigo presents itself to the market as a digital boutique, focused on helping companies in the public and private sectors during their digital transformation journey, acting in the management of cloud projects, systems integration, and the offering of digital products.

With the exponential growth of recent years, the company's strategic objective is to invest in its own intellectual property assets. To this end, in April it created the Products division with the aim of developing new digital solutions with appeal to the global market, such as the Vertigo iPaaS and the FIVE Academy, among many others to come.

## Digital Transformation at the heart of partnerships

Throughout our journey, Vertigo Tecnologia has established strategic business [partnerships](/en/partnerships/) to accelerate the delivery of our solutions. Large companies such as Kong, Hashicorp, Docker, AWS, Weaveworks, and [Liferay](https://www.liferay.com/pt/) , which recognized us as the only Platinum Service Partner in Latin America, as well as Partner of the Year Latam 2019/2020, are high-value partners for our business.

We will continue with our purpose of helping leaders transform the world and create intelligent businesses to build a better society through technology and its true purpose.

We thank all the partners who are with us or who have been part of this incredible journey!
