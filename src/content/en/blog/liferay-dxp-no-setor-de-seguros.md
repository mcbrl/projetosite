Digital transformation has gone from being a trend to an imperative in the insurance sector. With pressure for innovation, stricter regulatory requirements, and the rise of insurtechs, traditional insurers face a challenging landscape. In this context, Liferay DXP emerges as a robust and flexible platform capable of resolving critical operational pain points while delivering modern, secure, and personalized digital experiences.

## **Current overview of the insurance sector and the main challenges faced.**

Before investing in innovation, it is essential to understand the main obstacles hindering modernization in the insurance sector, especially with regard to technological integration and the regulatory framework.

### **System fragmentation and poor integration**

The insurance sector grapples with a considerable technological legacy, with diverse systems that don't communicate with each other, hindering the consolidation of a unified customer view. This fragmentation compromises efficiency and negatively impacts the insured's experience.

### **Pressure for compliance and safety**

Requirements such as LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law), SUSEP (Superintendence of Private Insurance), and other regulations make rigorous control over data and access essential. Many insurance companies still operate with structures that do not offer traceability or integrated security.

### **Difficulty in innovating quickly.**

Startups and insurtechs are able to launch new products in weeks. Traditional insurance companies, on the other hand, face long cycles and high costs for updates and integrations, which compromises their competitiveness.
