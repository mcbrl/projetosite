Let's talk about the magic that happens in the world of APIs, in two parts. By the end, you'll see that thinking about an API is more common than you might imagine. And thinking about the digital world without thinking about APIs might not be a good solution.
Companies that originally developed in the digital world have benefited early on from the advantages that technology offers for business growth, but this is not a privilege exclusive to them. The API Economy ([What is an API?](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/o-que-e-api-entenda-de-uma-maneira-simples/)This marks a universe of opportunities for hundreds of organizations in a wide variety of sectors.
Social media is booming, user habits are changing, and individuals are increasingly connected through mobile devices, creating a scenario that companies need to learn to navigate. In this sense, APIs can be true growth engines, enabling companies to develop new channels, expand their reach, and further spread their brand. The question is: Is your company ready for this?

## A little of history

In short, an API is a communication bridge between two or more systems.
This need for communication is inherent to us, to human beings, and we have gradually transferred it to machines. Machines have been improved since their invention; they were repetitive and mechanical. Over time, they acquired more and more functions, but they lacked the "intelligence" behind their systems, not as we see it today.
Englishman Alan Turing is largely responsible for developing a machine capable of performing intelligent work that previously could only be done by humans. Yes, he was responsible for creating the first computer. ## APIs everywhere

The use of APIs ([Understand what an API is.](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/o-que-e-api-entenda-de-uma-maneira-simples/)These technologies are more common than you might think, and they are present in the most routine user actions. They are not obvious because they don't operate in a visible way when you visit a website or use software, but their functions allow interaction between applications, providing access to some resource or simply enabling information.
When you enter a hotel's website, for example, and you see a [Google map](https://developers.google.com/maps/) Regarding the location of the place, it's an API that's behind it. When you want to calculate the shipping cost of a product in an online store, there's also a postal service API involved. It's important to note that APIs exist for both the B2B and B2C worlds.
Although many of the examples involve large companies, the use of APIs brings real and distinct competitive advantages that are available to any organization prepared to implement them.

## Potential to generate value

We know that data is abundant nowadays, but it's the combination of data that has the surprising power to generate value for the business. Creating an API means making data available so that other developers can create products associated with the company's service. When this is considered in the current context, where people avidly consume a wide variety of applications—because they have already realized that these programs make life easier—it becomes easy to visualize the potential reach that the brand can have.
Applications communicate with each other seamlessly, without the user even realizing it or, in some cases, being able to intervene. The possibility of connectivity between various systems or applications through APIs is what marks the intelligence of this interaction between interfaces. And users want interactivity, we all do!
Here are some examples of APIs that we use every day:

- APIs that are part of a website's functionality, such as the [Take off](http://dev.despegar.com/);
- APIs that are used in online software ([SaaS](https://blog.vertigo.com.br/o-que-voce-precisa-saber-sobre-saas/));
- APIs support mobile applications (Mobile Apps);
- APIs and the Internet of Things are directly related;

## Requirement to remain in the market

Because they are managed with the support of software tools, it is natural that new business models increasingly seek dynamic and intelligent alternatives, also supported by technology, to make the company prosper. Gradually, what was once a differentiator is becoming a requirement to remain in the market, hence the importance of understanding how the API Economy is gaining ground and that these transformations cannot simply be ignored.
Developing an API goes beyond simply establishing a presence in the virtual sphere: it's a strategy to achieve the company's established goals, and, just as with any strategy that values ​​discipline, it's necessary to consider a realistic market diagnosis and carefully define the objectives the company intends to achieve.
Managing the API is just as important as developing it; after all, abandoning the initiative will not contribute to achieving good results. Management is a way to discover where the company should or should not direct its efforts, better articulating and aligning the strategy according to the needs of the business.

## Conclusion

With the increased use of mobile devices and applications, the API Economy is quite developed, and there is still much to be done in this area! Of course, the company's segment and business model will influence the strategy it can develop, but preparing as early as possible to deal with these new perspectives is fundamental.
