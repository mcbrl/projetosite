Last Saturday (July 23rd), Vertigo Tecnologia celebrated its 23rd anniversary, an occasion that symbolizes the beginning of the company's entire journey, founded by our current CPO, André Fernandes.

Vertigo's journey to bring digital transformation to its clients began in a small 30 m² room in downtown Rio de Janeiro, and since then, the company has grown and now serves large organizations in Brazil and abroad.

Bruno Badini, our CEO, began his inspiring journey within the company while still an intern and university student. Over the years, he has become one of the key figures responsible for Vertigo's growth.

Throughout its 23-year history, Vertigo Tecnologia has multiplied its workforce and today has become a company with more than 200 employees spread across Brazil and the world.

Over the past year, the company has undergone profound transformations in its business and management models. Among these changes, we celebrate the arrival of Vinícius Siani, our current CTO, who joined Vertigo with the mission of implementing results-oriented management. After one year with the company, Siani continues to develop an organizational environment of constant learning, growth, and professional development.

## Vertigo believes that investing in training is the path to success.

Vertigo has grown and is now beginning to write a new success story, this time in Europe!

The brand's internationalization process has reached Portugal through the work of Luiz Ernani, our Global Partner, who leads the company's commercial expansion throughout Europe.

Recently, we expanded our vertical growth with our newest project, Vee Academy, an EdTech company that is the educational arm of Vertigo and invests in the training and retraining of professionals in the technology field.
